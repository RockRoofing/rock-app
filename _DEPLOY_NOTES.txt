DEPLOY NOTES - Merge Procurement Savings into the Procurement Schedule
=====================================================================
(This REPLACES the earlier "Procurement_savings_merged" package - use THIS one. The only
 difference is the Total Savings formula direction, see point 1.)

FIVE files, all EDITS (no new files). Just replace each one.

  pages/api/procurement.js                              (IHM -> schedule sync fields)
  pages/operations/project-management/procurement.js    (schedule columns + gating)
  pages/handover/form.js                                (IHM procurement item fields)
  pages/operations/projects.js                          (removes Procurement Savings tab)
  components/PreContractNav.js                          (removes Procurement Savings nav)

WHAT CHANGED

1. PROCUREMENT SCHEDULE - new columns per row, added BEFORE "Order Placed?":
     Budget Total | Budget Comments | Buying Total | Buying Comments | Total Savings
   - Total Savings is calculated automatically = Budget Total - Buying Total, matching the
     old Procurement Savings doc. So buying UNDER budget shows a POSITIVE saving in GREEN;
     an overspend shows negative in RED.
   - "Order Placed?" has been MOVED to the end of the row (just before Actions).
   - Attachments stay at the very end (in the Actions cell), as before.
   - A row CANNOT be marked "Order Placed = Yes" until the savings are complete
     (Budget Total, Budget Comments, Buying Total and Buying Comments all filled in).
     Trying to shows a prompt; a small "Savings needed" hint shows until they're filled.
     Same rule + fields are in the Edit/Add popup.

2. INTERNAL HANDOVER MINUTES (IHM) - each procurement item now also has Budget Total,
   Budget Comments, Buying Total and Buying Comments. These copy through to the Procurement
   Schedule automatically when the minutes are completed (same one-time sync as before -
   after that first copy, the live Schedule is master).

3. PROCUREMENT SAVINGS tabs REMOVED from the Pre-Contract navigation and from the Projects
   per-project tabs. (The old pages still exist in the repo but are unlinked - can be
   deleted later; harmless to leave.)
