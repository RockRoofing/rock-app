pkg345 - Portal users are the source of truth for who owns an activity
=======================================================================

REPLACES pkg302 through pkg344. Deploy this one only.

CHANGED FILES
-------------
  pages/crm.js
  pages/api/crm.js        returns the portal user list
  lib/crmImportMap.js     unchanged since pkg305


WHY YOU HAD TWO JAMESES
------------------------
The CRM's list of people was five hard-coded FIRST names, written when it was a prototype:

    Roman, Niall, James, Simon, Edita

Pipedrive stores full names - "James McVeigh". So anything imported said James McVeigh and
anything created in the CRM said James, and the Person Responsible filter listed both as if
they were different people.


HOW THEY ARE MERGED
-------------------
The portal user list is now the source of truth. Every name is resolved against it before
being shown or filtered, in this order:

    exact, ignoring case     "james mcveigh"  ->  James McVeigh
    unique first name        "James"          ->  James McVeigh
    unique last name         "McVeigh"        ->  James McVeigh

So the old short names fold into the real ones and the filter lists each person once.

IF TWO PORTAL USERS SHARE A FIRST NAME, a bare "James" is LEFT AS IT IS rather than guessed
at. Picking one of two people would be worse than showing it unresolved - you would have no
way of knowing it had chosen wrong. Verified with two Jameses on the list: full names
resolve, the bare one does not move.

A name that matches nobody - an old Pipedrive user who was never a portal user, say - is
shown as written rather than dropped.


THE DROPDOWNS NOW LIST REAL PEOPLE
-----------------------------------
Everywhere you pick a person - adding an activity on a deal, editing one there, or the
Person responsible cell in the table - now lists your actual portal users, with you first
and marked "(you)". The five prototype names are gone.


ONE THING TO BE AWARE OF
-------------------------
This resolves names ON DISPLAY. The stored records still hold whatever was written at the
time, and are only rewritten when you edit that activity. That is deliberate: it changes
nothing in your data and cannot go wrong.

If you would rather the stored records were tidied for good, I can add a one-off pass that
rewrites every activity's assignee to its resolved portal name. Worth doing before cutover,
but not urgent - the display is correct either way.
