pkg336 - Activities tab: open projects only
============================================

REPLACES pkg302 through pkg335. Deploy this one only.

CHANGED FILES
-------------
  pages/crm.js
  pages/api/crm.js        unchanged since pkg335
  lib/crmImportMap.js     unchanged since pkg305


WHAT CHANGED
------------
The Activities tab lists activities on OPEN projects only. The Open / Open + won / All
dropdown is gone - the filter bar is back to Person, Customer and Include completed.

Won and lost projects are excluded. That is the right call for a to-do list: once a job is
won or lost, chasing it here is not the job.


ONE THING I HAVE DELIBERATELY KEPT
-----------------------------------
The line under the count:

    "660 more on won or lost projects (not listed here)"

It is informational only now - no button, nothing to click. But it stays, because this is
exactly the rule that hid your activity for the last several rounds. An activity on a lost
project will still not appear here, and that line is the only thing that says so.

Without it the tab is silent about what it is holding back, and we would be straight back
to "it saved but I cannot see it". With it, the answer is on screen.

If you want it gone entirely, say so - but I would keep it.


THE OTHER FIXES FROM pkg335 ARE STILL IN HERE
-----------------------------------------------
  - a deal is no longer saved if its activities or notes failed to write, so they cannot be
    stripped from the deal without being safely stored (that was a genuine data-loss bug)
  - "Recount from stored records" under "Where are my activities?", which rebuilds the list
    and the kanban counts from what is stored - worth running once after deploying
  - the tab refreshes every 60 seconds and on window focus, so an estimator's progress
    reaches the salesperson without a reload
