WHAT WENT WRONG
================
The schema has fourteen field types. I gave the PDF a case for five of them and let
the rest fall through to JSON.stringify, which is why your J249 came out with

    {"title":"Sales Rep","name":"Colin Sanderson","company":"Renolit",...}

for the manufacturer contacts, the whole roof build-up as a bracketed array, the
scope drawings as 200-character blob URLs, and the entire procurement schedule and
live task list as raw objects. A default that dumps JSON looks like it works right
up until somebody fills the field in.

Every type now has its own case:

  Manufacturer contacts   Colin Sanderson | Sales Rep | Renolit | 07818 336897 | email
  Scope drawings          the file NAME. A blob URL is noise on a printed document
                          and is not what anybody reads
  Roof build-up           the name, the substrate, then one line per layer:
                            VCL: Renolit, Polythene, 0.25mm
                            Insulation: Kingspan, TT46, 130mm to 290mm, calc: No
                          Empty layers are dropped
  Live tasks              numbered, with owner and close-out date
  Procurement             numbered by package, with supplier, owner, budget, the
                          buying notes and any attachments by name
  Risk log                numbered, with mitigation and owner on their own lines


AND THE SCOPE OF WORKS WAS HTML
---------------------------------
Scope of works is a `long` field, but the form writes it from a rich-text editor, so
what is stored is markup. It printed as

    <u style=""><b>5th Floor roof area</b></u><div>Single ply membrane...

Now converted to text. Two details that took a second pass: breaking only on closing
tags ran the heading into the paragraph after it ("5th Floor roof areaSingle ply
membrane..."), and breaking on both put a blank line between every line of the
specification. A closing tag immediately followed by an opening one is collapsed to
a single break, so consecutive lines sit together and a deliberately empty line
still comes through as a paragraph break.


BOTH VIEWS THE SAME - TWO REAL FAULTS IN THE OPS VIEW
=======================================================
Checking the two against each other turned up that the Ops read-only view was wrong
in its own right, independently of the PDF:

1. ROOF TYPES SHOWED ONLY THE NAME. The renderer read r.type, r.area, r.system and
   r.notes. A roof type has none of those - emptyRoofType() in lib/ihmSchema.js is
   { name, substrate, rows[] } - so every field but the name came out empty and the
   entire specification, the layers the meeting sat and agreed, was not displayed at
   all. It now shows the substrate and one line per layer, the same content as the
   PDF.

2. SCOPE OF WORKS SHOWED ITS TAGS. Down the template-driven path a `long` field
   holding markup went through the plain text row, so the Ops view printed the HTML.
   It now renders as HTML when it looks like HTML, which is what the older
   non-template path always did.

Pre-Contract see the form itself, which has always rendered these correctly through
its own editors. Both sides now agree with each other and with the PDF.
