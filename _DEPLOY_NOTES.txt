REFERENCE COLUMN
----------------
Added between Invoice and Due date. It was already on the payload
(business-financials.js:836) and simply never shown - which is a shame, because the
reference is usually the application or project and is what actually makes a row
recognisable. Truncated with the full text on hover.

Header, body and footer cells all checked at 7.


ALSO IN THIS PACKAGE
--------------------
pkg628 - date pickers commit on BLUR, not on every change. The bills list sorts by
planned payment date, so scrolling the picker from August to September re-sorted
the table, moved the row and shut the calendar before a day had been picked. Enter
commits, Escape abandons. Same on expected payment dates.

pkg627 - BALANCE SHEET TAB. Reads every account from Xero's Balance Sheet report,
grouped by Xero's own sections, with no account codes assumed anywhere so it works
on any chart of accounts. Track what you want, set outstanding / per month / day /
from / to. "Clears in" turns red when the schedule will not clear the balance.

FINANCING & TAX column replaces "Vehicles / commitments" and carries both.
Payments stop when the liability clears - 12,000 at 5,000/month gives 5,000,
5,000, 2,000.

RECONNECT XERO before the balance sheet read - it needs
accounting.reports.balancesheet.read, which does not apply to an existing
connection.
