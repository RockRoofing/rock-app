WHAT IT SENDS
-------------
Each morning, everyone with activities DUE TODAY OR OVERDUE gets one email listing only
their own. Overdue first, in red, with how many days late; then due today, in green. Each
line shows the activity, the project and the customer, and there is a button through to the
CRM.

Nobody with nothing due gets an email. If nobody at all has anything, nothing is sent.


WHO GETS ONE
------------
Portal users with a pre-contract or admin role and an email address - the same people who
can be assigned an activity in the first place.

Names are matched the same way the CRM screen does: exact, then a unique first name, then a
unique surname. So an activity assigned to "James" still reaches James McVeigh. A first
name shared by two people is NOT guessed at - that activity is left out rather than emailed
to the wrong person.


WHAT IS EXCLUDED, AND WHY
--------------------------
  future activities        not due yet
  won or lost projects     matches the Activities tab; chasing a finished job is noise
  unassigned activities    nobody to send them to (the kanban card already flags these)

Verified all four exclusions plus the short-name matching against a worked example.


WHEN IT RUNS
------------
Hourly cron, sending in the 07:00 UK hour. Hourly rather than a fixed daily time so it does
not drift by an hour when the clocks change, which a UTC-pinned cron would.

TESTING:
  ...?dryRun=1   lists who WOULD get one and how many activities each, sends nothing
  ...?force=1    sends immediately regardless of the hour

Run the dryRun first - it is the quickest way to confirm the name matching is picking up
the right people before anything lands in anyone's inbox.


NOTE
----
It reads the same outstanding-activity list the CRM uses, which is kept current on every
write - so the email cannot disagree with what the Activities tab shows.
