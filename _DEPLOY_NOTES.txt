EVERY MONTH TIES NOW - BUT THE LINES WERE ALL NIL
--------------------------------------------------
Bank, Debtors, Retention and Creditors all read 0 on the actual months and the
entire balance sat in Financing & tax. Two causes, both found by testing the
matching against the real chart of accounts in your PDF.

1. THE STORED DATA HAS NO MONTHLY VALUES. It was fetched before the parser kept
   every column, so the totals came through - which is why the months tie - but
   the individual lines did not. Every lookup returned zero.

   The page now DETECTS this and says so, rather than showing a tidy table of
   zeros. One refresh fixes it.

2. MATCHING ON THE NAME ALONE WAS NEVER GOING TO WORK on your chart:

     "American Express Platinum Business - 2000" appears TWICE - once under
     "Cash at bank and in hand" as an asset, once under Creditors as a liability.
     Same name, opposite meaning. The asset matched nothing and the liability
     went into Creditors instead of Cards.

   Now matched on Xero's SECTION as well as the name:

     Amex (Cash at bank)        -> Cards
     Amex (Creditors)           -> Cards
     Capital On Tap             -> Cards
     Accounts Receivable        -> Debtors
     Retentions Receivable      -> Retention
     Other Debtors              -> Debtors
     Accounts Payable           -> Creditors
     CIS & PAYE                 -> Creditors
     WIP, Inventory, Prepayments, fixed assets, deferred tax -> Financing

FINANCING IS A DELIBERATE CATCH-ALL. WIP, inventory, prepayments, fixed assets and
deferred tax are real but are not separately forecast. Rather than invent a line
for each and leave them static, that line absorbs them and balances the month
exactly. One honest catch-all beats five stale lines.
