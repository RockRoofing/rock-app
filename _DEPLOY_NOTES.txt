CAN IT BE 1:00AM EXACTLY? ALMOST
---------------------------------
Vercel does not promise to-the-minute execution - a scheduled job can run a few minutes
late under load. Nobody can give you exactly 01:00:00.

What I can do is fire it at the TOP of the hour rather than anywhere within it, which is
what it does now. Expect 01:00 give or take a few minutes.

Previously it ran hourly and checked the time, so it could have gone at any point between
07:00 and 07:59. Now the cron itself runs at minute 0 of UTC hours 0 and 1, and the handler
sends on whichever of those is 01:00 in London:

    August (BST):   00:00 UTC = 01:00 UK   <- sends       01:00 UTC = 02:00 UK   <- skips
    January (GMT):  00:00 UTC = 00:00 UK   <- skips       01:00 UTC = 01:00 UK   <- sends

So it stays at 01:00 through the clock change instead of drifting to 02:00 in summer, which
is what a plain "0 1 * * *" would have done. Verified for both seasons.


FOR TESTING, DO NOT WAIT UNTIL 1AM
-----------------------------------
    ...api/cron/crm-daily-activities?dryRun=1
        lists who would get an email and how many activities each. Sends nothing. Run this
        first - it confirms the name matching is finding the right people.

    ...api/cron/crm-daily-activities?force=1
        sends immediately, whatever the time.

Both ignore the hour gate entirely, so you can check it properly in the next two minutes
rather than tomorrow morning.

If you move it outside 00:00-01:00 UK, the cron schedule in vercel.json needs the matching
UTC hours too - tell me the time and I will set both.
