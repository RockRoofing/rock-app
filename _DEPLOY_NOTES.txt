pkg724 - WIP adjustment on the Forecast P&L and Forecast Balance Sheet
=======================================================================

REPLACE (no new files):
  lib/forecastMonths.js
  pages/business-financials/forecast-pl.js
  pages/business-financials/forecast-balance-sheet.js
  pages/api/business-financials.js

Built on the exact pkg722 files - verified against the pkg722 zip before
starting, not against my working copy.

WHERE THE FIGURE COMES FROM
---------------------------
wip:lock:<YYYY-MM>, written when WIP is signed off in Commercial. The lock for
the LAST ACTUAL month is used.

If that month has never been signed off, the most recent earlier lock is used
INSTEAD and the page says so in amber, naming both months. It does not quietly
substitute. Sign off the right month in Commercial and the banner clears.

FORECAST P&L
------------
New "Work in progress" panel above the table:
  - the month, the signed-off figure, and a tick "include in the FY total"
  - excluded by default; automatically INCLUDED when the last actual month is
    the year end (November), where the journal is genuine FY revenue
  - the tick is an explicit override; "back to automatic" returns it to
    following the year end as it moves

In the table:
  - EVERY MONTHLY COLUMN IS UNCHANGED. Each month still reads exactly what
    Xero reports. Nothing has been taken off a month.
  - one new row, "less WIP in <month>", carrying the deduction in the FY
    column only, with dashes across the months
  - a "Net profit after WIP" row underneath
  - the five summary boxes use the adjusted figures

CONSEQUENCE, STATED ON THE PAGE: the FY total no longer equals the sum of the
monthly columns, and the gap is exactly the WIP figure. That is deliberate.
The panel says so rather than leaving it looking like an arithmetic fault.

Revenue only. The journal is Dr WIP, Cr Sales - there is no cost side.

FORECAST BALANCE SHEET
----------------------
Actual months are left EXACTLY as Xero has them - the asset really was on the
balance sheet on those dates, and re-basing onto Xero is what a closed month is
for.

The deduction lands on the FORECAST months only, applied once at the first one
after the last actual month and carried by the running balances from there. The
column heading for that month shows "WIP OUT".

Taken off reserves AND off the financing catch-all together, so both sides of
the sheet move by the same amount and "Out by" still ties. Bank is untouched -
WIP is not cash.

Driven by the same tick on the Forecast P&L. One setting, both pages.

Month headings on the BS now also show MANUAL in blue, matching the P&L.

WHAT TO CHECK
-------------
  - Forecast P&L: WIP panel shows the right month and figure. Untick / retick
    and the FY total moves by exactly that amount while the columns do not.
  - The amber "no signed-off WIP for <month>" warning is ABSENT. If it shows,
    sign that month off in Commercial.
  - Forecast BS: "Out by" still near zero on every month, including the one
    marked WIP OUT.
  - Net assets step down by the WIP amount at the first forecast month.
