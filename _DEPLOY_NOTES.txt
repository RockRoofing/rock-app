pkg323 - Activities added in a project were never being saved
==============================================================

REPLACES pkg302 through pkg322. Deploy this one only.

CHANGED FILES
-------------
  pages/crm.js            THE FIX
  pages/api/crm.js        unchanged since pkg322
  lib/crmImportMap.js     unchanged since pkg305


ROOT CAUSE - THE SAVE WAS FAILING SILENTLY
--------------------------------------------
The Activities tab was reading correctly. The activity was never being stored.

Opening a deal loads that deal's imported activities and notes and merges them INTO the
deal so the deal view can show them. The autosave then writes the whole deals list back -
including those merged copies, which already live in their own per-deal stores.

    crm:deals on its own                 6.4 MB
    request body limit                   8 MB
    if every deal were opened            29.9 MB

So after opening a few deals the payload crosses the limit and the save is rejected. And
the save was written as:

    catch (e) { /* ignore */ }

It failed silently, the header still said "Saved", and the activity sat in the browser
looking fine until the next reload took it away.

That is my fault twice over: merging imported records into the saved object, and swallowing
the error that would have told us.


THE FIX
-------
1. Imported activities and notes are now stripped before saving. They are flagged when
   loaded, so they are easy to identify, and they are already stored per deal - there was
   never any reason to write them back. The payload returns to the size of the deals
   themselves and the save succeeds.

   Verified: an activity you add is kept, the imported copies are dropped.

2. SAVE FAILURES ARE NOW VISIBLE. The "Saved" badge in the header turns red and says what
   went wrong - e.g. "Save failed (server 413). Your last change is not stored." No more
   silently losing work while the screen says it is fine.


WHAT TO EXPECT
--------------
Add an activity inside a project and it appears in the Activities tab, and survives a
reload. Same for notes, stage moves and field edits - all of which were at risk from the
same failing save, though an activity is where you happened to notice it.

WORTH CHECKING: anything you added in the CRM over the last few sessions may not have been
saved. Spot-check a couple of projects you know you edited. If work is missing, it was lost
at this point, and I am sorry - the silent catch is why it went unnoticed for so long.
