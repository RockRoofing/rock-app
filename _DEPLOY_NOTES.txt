WHAT WAS ALREADY CORRECT
-------------------------
The operative-facing screens already filtered to live and were not touched:
  Complete a Form (fill.js), Drawings, RAMS, Report Site Issue (issue.js),
  and Browse (which filters on the dashboard stage INPROGRESS).


WHAT WAS WRONG
--------------
1. ALL EIGHT CM SCREENS. Applications, Contracted Rates, Forms Completed, Missing Forms,
   Pre-Start, SRATs, Live Tasks and Variations all get their project list from the shared
   useMyProjects hook in lib/cmSiteApp.js, and that hook did not filter by status. So
   Complete and Archived jobs were appearing in every CM project picker.

   Fixed in the hook itself rather than in eight separate files, so it stays consistent
   and cannot drift apart later.

2. HOME SCREEN BADGE COUNTS. The RAMS / overdue-task / issue badges on the Site App home
   were counted across all statuses, so a completed job could still put a red number on
   the menu. Now counted on live projects only.

3. CONTRACTED RATES SCHEDULE. That picker filtered on whether a project has contracted
   rates but not on status, so finished jobs stayed selectable. Now live only.


ONE THING WORTH KNOWING - TWO DIFFERENT MEANINGS OF "LIVE"
-----------------------------------------------------------
The Site App draws projects from two different sources, and each has its own idea of live:

  Ops projects (ops:projects)  ->  status 'active'      (Live / Complete / Archived,
                                                         set in Ops > Projects)
  Dashboard rows (Xero)        ->  stage 'INPROGRESS'   (driven by the Retention Tracker:
                                                         INPROGRESS / DEFECTS / CLOSED)

Both are now filtered to their own version of live, which is the right answer for each.
But they can disagree: a job marked Complete in Ops but still INPROGRESS on the Retention
Tracker would drop off most screens while remaining on the two dashboard-fed ones
(Contracted Rates schedule, Browse).

If you want a single definition driving everything, that is a bigger piece - tell me which
of the two should win and I will make the other follow it.
