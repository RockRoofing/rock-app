1. RETENTION OUTSTANDING CARD
-----------------------------
Fifth card: the halves NOT yet ticked as Received, across the projects shown.
Red while there is something to chase, green at zero.

  Total Retention        gross - 1st Value + 2nd Value, ignores payment

2. THE 612 COLUMN WAS HIDING THE ANSWER
---------------------------------------
  dashboard.js:495   retention612Allocated: Math.abs(retention612)

retention612 is signed and summed - deductions negative, releases positive.
Taking the absolute value of the net made two completely different situations
identical:

                          OLD      DEDUCTED  RELEASED  HELD   RELEASED+PAID
  deducted only          10,000      10,000        0  10,000            0
  half released, paid     5,000      10,000    5,000   5,000        5,000
  half released, unpaid   5,000      10,000    5,000   5,000            0
  no 612 lines at all         0           -        -       -            -

The middle two both read 5,000 and could not be told apart. One is banked, the
other has left retention and become an ordinary debtor. That is the reason the
register would not reconcile to Xero.

"612 Allocated" is now three columns - 612 Deducted, 612 Released, 612 Held - all
sortable. Held is deducted less released and is the figure to compare against
Retention Owed. Hovering Released shows how many 612 lines were found, the date
of the earliest, and how much of the release sits on invoices now paid.

Computed from the stored invoice lines, so it works WITHOUT a resync.


3. THE HISTORIC DATA PROBLEM - HANDLED, NOT HIDDEN
--------------------------------------------------
You said older releases were often posted as a plain sales invoice with no 612
coding. That means 612 Released will UNDER-report on old projects, and a naive
zero would read as "nothing released" when the truth is "no evidence".

So a project with NO 612 movement at all shows a DASH in all three columns, not a
zero, with a tooltip saying no 612 lines were found and why that might be. A zero
only appears where there genuinely is 612 activity and none of it is a release.

The manual Received ticks remain the source of truth for what has been paid.
Nothing here overwrites them - the 612 columns are evidence to reconcile against,
which is the whole point.


4. WHAT TO DO NEXT
------------------
Run a LONG invoice sync before trusting these columns. The Sync from Xero button
sends months: 6 and the endpoint accepts up to 60 - and per the comment at
sync-invoices.js:186, lines outside the window are never re-fetched, so 612 data
on older projects is partial. Gas Lane is the worked example in that comment:
17,317 of retention reading as a few hundred.

Once a long sync has run, compare 612 Held against Retention Owed project by
project. Where they agree, the ticks can be trusted. Where 612 Released is a dash
on a project you know has been part-released, that is a project whose releases
were never coded to 612 - and no automatic method will ever find them.
