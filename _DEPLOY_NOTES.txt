THE CHANGE
===========
The W/C column in the pop-out is replaced by the date the row is actually about, and
the heading changes with the section:

    Missing / Upcoming    "Date needed"       the date it is needed for
    Completed             "Date completed"    the date it was done on - and for a
                                              diary that is its own Site Diary Date

Shown as "Mon 17 Aug 26" rather than a week commencing.


EVERY FORM TYPE HAS A REAL DATE - IT JUST WAS NOT BEING CARRIED
=================================================================
Only the Monday of the week was on the row, so a Pre-Start needed on the Thursday and
one needed on the Monday read identically. Each obligation now carries the day it
relates to:

    Pre-Start                 the start or return-to-site date that triggered it
    Start on Site Checklist   the first allocated day on site
    Works Area Handover       the finishing day before the 5-day gap
    Daily Site Diary          the allocated day
    Water Ingress Report      the visit day

And where it is done, the date it was done:

    Pre-Start                 when the minutes were sent
    Start on Site / WAH       the submission's own date, falling back to when it was
                              submitted
    Daily Site Diary          its Site Diary Date - which, since pkg842 matches a
                              diary on that date, is the same day it was needed for


ALSO
-----
The lists now SORT on the date being shown. Sorting by week while displaying a
specific date makes a list look unordered.

Where an obligation genuinely has no specific date, the cell falls back to "W/C
17 Aug 26" in grey rather than showing an empty box.
