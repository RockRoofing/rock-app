1. MCD BASIS - NO, IT WAS NOT ALLOWED FOR
-----------------------------------------
You were right to ask. The forecast never sent the flags at all, and the two
sides of the codebase DEFAULT THE OPPOSITE WAY:

  lib/applications.js:144        app.mcdOnVariations !== false    -> absent = TRUE
  pages/api/applications.js:194  project.mcdOnVariations === true -> absent = FALSE

So on a project with the boxes unticked - MCD on contract works only - the real
application charged no MCD on variations or materials, and the forecast charged
it on everything. Leaving the flags off did not mean "use the project setting",
it meant "discount everything".

Both flags now come from Edit Project Details and are sent on EVERY
computeApplicationSummary call in the modal, gathered into one `mcdBasis` object
so no call site can forget one. They are saved onto the forecast too.

  MCD 2.5%, retention 5%, 100,000 works + 20,000 variation:
    MCD on everything        sales 117,000.00
    MCD on contract works    sales 117,500.00

500.00 on one variation. It compounds across a job.

The override gross-up now derives its ratio from the certificate itself rather
than assuming MCD hits the whole account, so it is right on either basis.


2. FORECASTS NOW RECOMPUTE
--------------------------
Answering your earlier question - they did not, and now they do.

On every load, each saved forecast is recalculated against the CURRENT
applications. Its stored percentages are untouched; only the deduction for what
has already been claimed is recalculated, which is the one part that depends on
anything outside the forecast.

  Forecast saved when the prior application certified 40,000.
  That application is corrected and now certifies 70,000.

    stored figure       74,575.00   <- double counts 30,000 of gross
    recomputed          46,787.50

So entering the missing "Previously certified (gross)" on J240 Application 4 will
now correct both the actual AND every forecast that chained off it, on the next
page load, with nothing else to do.

A forecast whose revenue was OVERRIDDEN is left alone. You typed that figure
deliberately and recalculating it silently would be worse than letting it age.

Cash instalments are scaled by the same ratio, so the payment dates and the
monthly split you saved are preserved.

This is a read-time recalculation - nothing is written back, so the saved
forecast is never altered behind you. Reopening one still shows what you saved.

It needs the project register from pkg539 to join forecast keys ("L:J240") to
applications (keyed by Xero id) - that register is the only place holding both.
