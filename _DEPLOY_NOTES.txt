THE BANNER
----------
Above the cash rows, red, listing every forecast whose money has no date to land
on:

    3 forecasts cannot be scheduled - missing dates.
      J190 - Russell Hill (31/08/26 - 06/11/26) - no sales payment date
      J244 - Waungron (01/09/26 - 30/11/26) - 2 materials lines with no delivery date
      J240 - Market Drayton (05/10/26 - 17/12/26) - 1 labour payment with no date

    These are saved and look right above, but they contribute NOTHING to the cash
    flow - the money has no date to land on.

Three checks: revenue with no dated sales line, materials with a value but no
delivery date, labour with a value but no date. Superseded periods are skipped.


WHY THIS ONE MATTERS
--------------------
It is the exact failure that cost most of today. A forecast with no dates SAVES
correctly, RENDERS correctly on the Gantt, and contributes nothing anywhere -
there was no way to tell it apart from one that was working. Every session we have
found another variant of it by chasing a total that looked wrong.

DETECTED FROM THE SAVED SCHEDULES, not by fetching every project's calendar. If a
forecast has revenue and no dated sales line, that is proof enough that something
upstream did not resolve - whatever the reason.
