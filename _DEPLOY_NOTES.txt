pkg416 - variations excluded from average secured project value
===============================================================

Deploy after pkg415. One changed file: pages/scorecard-crm.js. No new files.


THE LOGIC, AS IT WAS
--------------------
Take the person's deals where status is won, value is above zero, and the close date falls
in the rolling 6 months ending with the month shown. Sum the values, divide by the count.
The comparison figure is the 6 months before that, non-overlapping.

Edita's card does the same across all estimators rather than one person's.

No stage filter at all - so yes, variations were counted as secured projects.


WHY THAT MATTERED MORE THAN IT LOOKS
--------------------------------------
A variation is extra work on a job you have already won. They are numerous and small.
From the export we looked at earlier, nineteen won variations averaged about £6,700
against real projects in the hundreds of thousands.

On a worked example: two real projects at £300,000 and £100,000, plus two variations at
£6,700 and £2,253.

    with variations       4 deals, average £102,238
    without variations    2 deals, average £200,000

The number was being halved, and it was drifting towards "what is a typical variation
worth" rather than "what is a typical job worth" - which is the opposite of what the card
is for.

Now excluded, on BOTH the estimator cards and Edita's. The two must agree about what
counts as a secured project or they cannot be compared with each other.


TWO THINGS I HAVE NOT CHANGED - YOUR CALL
-------------------------------------------
TOTAL VALUE OF WORK SECURED still includes variations. I think that is right: a variation
IS money secured, and that card is measuring revenue rather than typical job size. But it
does mean the total and the average now count different things, and somebody will
eventually multiply one by the other and get a surprise.

STRIKE RATE also still includes them. Same question, and it cuts the other way - a won
variation with no competitive process arguably flatters the strike rate.

Say the word on either and it is one line each.
