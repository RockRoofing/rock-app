THREE CHANGES
-------------
1. NEW forecast option: "Avg last financial year"
   Averages the code's actuals across the whole PREVIOUS financial year (completed
   months). Needs last FY to be synced in the P&L benchmark; if there's no prior-year
   data for a code it just shows the placeholder.

2. "Last full month" now uses the LAST COMPLETED MONTH even if it was 0.
   Previously it skipped months with no cost entry and jumped to an earlier month.
   Now a completed month with zero cost is correctly treated as 0.

3. Clear (x) on a manual Budget / Mo.
   When you've typed a manual budget figure, a small red x appears next to it. Click
   it to clear the manual value and revert that row's budget to the forecast figure.
