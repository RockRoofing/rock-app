WHERE DOES THE CIS GO / IS IT DOUBLE COUNTED?
---------------------------------------------
Confirmed with you: Xero applies the CIS deduction ON THE BILL. A Ł1,250 gross
labour bill splits into:
   Ł1,000  -> payable to the subcontractor (this is the bill's Amount Due)
   Ł250    -> WITHHELD ON THE BALANCE SHEET as a CIS liability (your 821-type
             control account) - NOT a bill in "Bills to pay".

So the Ł250 is NOT in your outstanding bills and NOT in Amount Due. Adding it as a
separate "CIS to HMRC" outflow on the 22nd is therefore CORRECT and NOT a double
count - it represents clearing that balance-sheet liability, which nothing else in
the forecast captures. (The only way it would double up is if you also entered the
HMRC CIS payment as a payable BILL in Xero - you don't; it's a liability
settlement.)

AUTO-TICK FROM ACCOUNT 321
--------------------------
Bills with any line on account 321 (CIS Labour Expense) now auto-tick as CIS - no
manual ticking needed. You can still UN-tick any bill (e.g. gross-status
subcontractors) and that choice is remembered and overrides the auto-detection.
Re-ticking restores it.

** IMPORTANT: re-sync Bills to Pay after deploying. ** The 321 detection is added
when bills are fetched from Xero, so already-synced bills won't show auto-ticked
until you run the Bills to Pay sync once. New/re-synced bills will auto-tick.

No extra Xero calls - the account code comes from line items already returned by the
bills fetch, so no timeout risk.
