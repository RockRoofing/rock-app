pkg757 - Cash is cash. Debt is headroom.
=========================================

DEPLOY OVER pkg756. Two files. Contains pkg756.

REPLACE:
  pages/api/business-financials.js
  pages/business-financials/cashflow.js

WHAT WAS WRONG
--------------
Invoice finance drawn counted toward opening cash. Cards did not. Two
liabilities, two different treatments - and the invoice finance one was counted
THREE times:

  1. inside the cash line          -180,000 sat in "opening cash"
  2. as overdraft drawn            odDrawn = max(0, -bankTotal), and bankTotal
                                   was negative BECAUSE of Bibby - so the model
                                   thought you had drawn 172,611 of overdraft
                                   while Metrobank held +7,389
  3. as invoice finance drawn      subtracted again in the net position box

Net of all facilities read -545,710. It is nearer -250,388.

NOW
---
OPENING CASH IS MONEY IN BANK ACCOUNTS. Nothing else. The advance was drawn and
SPENT - the spending is already in the Metrobank balance. What remains is a
liability, and a liability reduces what you can still BORROW, not what you hold.
Cards were always treated that way; invoice finance now matches.

On your figures:

  Cash in the bank              7,389   (was -172,611)
  Overdraft drawn                   0   (was 172,611)
  Overdraft headroom           60,000   (was 0)
  Invoice finance available     2,268   (was 59,557)
  Card headroom                82,223
  Max cash available          151,880   (was -30,831)

  Net cash position:
    Bank cash                   7,389
    less card debt            -77,777
    less overdraft drawn            0
    = Net cash                -70,388
    less invoice finance     -180,000
    = Net of all facilities  -250,388   (was -545,710)

Each debt appears ONCE, on the borrowing side, which is where it constrains you.

TWO MORE THINGS FIXED
---------------------
The summary boxes read Xero's bank total; they now use your typed balances
first, same precedence as everything else on the page. Card debt likewise.

Availability was measured against the drawn figure held on the Invoice Finance
page - a snapshot. With 180,000 typed against 122,711 on that page it read
59,557 when real headroom is about 2,268. It now uses the typed figure, and an
amber bar appears when the two disagree, naming both, so a stale Invoice Finance
page cannot quietly overstate your headroom again.

"Opening cash (all bank combined)" is relabelled "Cash in the bank".

WHAT TO WATCH FROM HERE
-----------------------
Two lines, and the gap between them. Cash below zero means you cannot pay people
that week. The dashed line closing on the cash line means you are running out of
things to draw on. Cash comfortably positive with the gap nearly shut is a worse
position than cash slightly negative with plenty of headroom - and only the two
together tell you that.
