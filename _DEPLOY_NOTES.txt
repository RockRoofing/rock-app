THE SECOND LINE
---------------
Dashed teal, under the gold cash line: bank + card headroom + overdraft +
invoice finance availability.

    closing cash   overdraft headroom   TOTAL AVAILABLE
        30,000              60,000            219,032
       -25,000              35,000            139,032
       200,000              60,000            389,032

THE OVERDRAFT HEADROOM MOVES WITH THE BALANCE. Drawn overdraft already shows as a
negative bank figure, so the headroom is the limit LESS what is used - counting
the full limit on top would count the drawn part twice. On a week the balance goes
to -25,000 the available line correctly tightens rather than staying flat.

Card and invoice finance headroom are held constant across the weeks. Their drawn
amounts do not change within the forecast, and modelling a drawdown you have not
decided on would be false precision.

INVOICE FINANCE COMES FROM THE IF PAGE - the position it publishes, which is the
one you reconciled against Bibby. Not a second calculation that could disagree.


DRAWN UNDER THE CASH LINE, AND DASHED
--------------------------------------
Deliberately. Available is what you could pay out if you drew everything; it is
not money you have, and it should not look like it. The caption says so: the gap
between the two lines is borrowing capacity, not cash.
