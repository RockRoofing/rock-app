V01 AND V02 - FOUND IT, AND pkg523 COULD NOT HAVE WORKED
----------------------------------------------------------
Project settings are looked up by TWO KEYS:

    const settings = allSettings[id] || allSettings[cp.jobNo] || {}

The tracking option id, OR the job number. Two records can exist for the same project, and
the dashboard returned whichever matched FIRST - so a variation written against the
job-number record is invisible the moment an id record exists.

That is V01 and V02: written against one record, V03 against the other.

pkg523 was the right idea in the wrong place. It compared two lists on the CLIENT, and the
dashboard only ever sends one - so the recovery could never fire. Resolved on the server
now, where both records exist.

BOTH ARE MERGED, on variation number. Neither is dropped, and where the same number
appears in both the richer record wins - the one carrying its workings.

    job-number record: V01, V02
    id record:         V03
    now returned:      V01, V02, V03

Nothing was ever lost. It was reading the wrong record.

TO CONFIRM ON YOUR DATA, console on any portal page:

    fetch('/api/variation-debug?jobNo=J205').then(r=>r.json()).then(console.log)

That reads the job-number record directly. Writes nothing.


CONTRACTED RATES VARIATIONS ARE NOW REAL VARIATIONS
-----------------------------------------------------
Turning schedule lines into a variation created a row of numbers and no paperwork - no
document, nothing to send, nothing to instruct, and nothing for the tracker's View to
show.

It now builds the same record the Variation Builder does: the schedule lines become the
variation's items at their contracted rates, with the standard clarifications, the
sub-contract ref, and who raised it.

And the SEND WINDOW OPENS STRAIGHT AFTER - the same window, the same email, the same
instruct button, the same instruction coming back. One send flow, so a variation raised
from the rate schedule is indistinguishable from one built by hand.
