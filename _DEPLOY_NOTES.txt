THE HIGH INVOLVEMENT BUG
------------------------
I took the percentage of `fundable` - the debt after the eligibility caps but
BEFORE credit limits. Bibby's "approved sales ledger" is their Approved Debt,
which is Sales Ledger less Non-Funded Debt - and Non-Funded INCLUDES Credit Limit
exceeded. So their base is the figure AFTER limits.

  base 1,375,463 (the inflated debt)  cap 481,412.05  Wates over by       0.00
  base   421,033 (before limits)      cap 147,361.55  Wates over by     768.07
  base   323,029 (after limits)       cap 113,060.31  Wates over by  35,069.31

Only the last reproduces their figure. That is why it showed 0 and why nothing
was ever over the cap - the cap was far too high.

Now based on the approved ledger after limits, and the label says so:
"Cap GBP 113,060.31 = 35% of the GBP 323,029.46 approved ledger."
