CHANGES

1. PAYMENT SCHEDULE - two valid ways to complete it:
   - The fixed "Application / Valuation / Payment day of month" boxes, OR
   - The manual monthly date table.
   If the manual table has ANY month filled in, the fixed day-of-month boxes are NOT
   required. If the manual table is empty, the three day boxes ARE required (as before).

   IMPORTANT: for any month row where you enter a date, that row must have ALL THREE
   dates (Application, Valuation, Payment). A half-filled month shows as incomplete,
   naming the month(s) e.g. "Complete all monthly dates for: Mar 2026, Apr 2026".

2. "STILL TO COMPLETE" BANNER at the top of Edit Project Details. It lists exactly
   what's outstanding for the project to count as complete, and updates live as you
   fill fields in. When everything's done it turns green: "Project details complete".
