DEPLOY NOTES - Markup: select, move, reshape and restyle any mark
=================================================================

ONE file, an EDIT - just replace it.
  components/DrawingMarkup.js

WHAT'S NEW (applies to RFI attachment markup AND the Design Drawings markup, on images
and PDFs)
  Every mark you add is now fully editable with the Select tool:
   - Click a mark to SELECT it (works when several marks overlap - click the one you want).
   - Drag its body to MOVE it anywhere.
   - Drag the round HANDLES to reshape:
       * Arrows / lines: drag either end to stretch, shrink or re-point it.
       * Boxes / circles: drag a corner handle to resize.
       * Text boxes: drag the corner handle to resize the box; drag the body to move it.
   - Restyle the selected mark on the fly: change its COLOUR (colour swatches), thickness,
     and (for text) size / bold / underline.
   - Double-click a text box to edit its words again.
   - "Delete selected" removes the selected mark; "Undo" removes the last one added.
  Remember to press "Save markup" to store your changes.

NOTES
  - Thin lines/arrows have an invisible wider "grab" band so they're easy to select.
  - Clicking empty space clears the selection.
  - Existing saved markups keep working and are now editable too.
