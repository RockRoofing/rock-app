WHAT'S NEW

1. DAILY ACTIVITY EMAIL (once per day, per project)
   When a new RFI is added, or a comment is posted, everyone with access to that project
   AND the Design portal (internal team + that project's customers) gets an email:
     "There have been new RFIs added for your project." and/or
     "There have been comments added to RFIs for your project."
   It is sent AT MOST ONCE PER DAY per project - if there's a flurry of comments/new RFIs,
   people get a single summary that day, not one per comment. (A scheduled job runs a few
   times a day and sends the day's summary the first time it sees activity, then not again
   that day.)

2. UNREAD FLAGS on the RFI tracker
   RFIs with new activity you haven't opened yet are flagged with an orange dot and a
   highlighted row, and the comments count shows "N new". This is PER USER - it clears for
   you when YOU open that RFI (not when a colleague does).

3. RFI PAGE IS NOW FULL WIDTH.

4. EVERY-3-DAYS OUTSTANDING RFI EMAIL
   Every third day, everyone with access gets an email listing the RFIs still outstanding
   (unresolved) for their project, as a table (RFI number, description, required-by,
   responsible). If a project has NO outstanding RFIs, no email is sent for it.
