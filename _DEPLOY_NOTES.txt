GOOD NEWS - IT WAS TREATING EACH MONTH AS A PERIOD ALL ALONG
--------------------------------------------------------------
Moseley Hall, 01/02/27 to 10/05/27, spread 40/30/25/5, EOM+35. The schedule the
forecast actually builds is:

    Feb 27  40%  ->  cash 04/04/27
    Mar 27  30%  ->  cash 05/05/27
    Apr 27  25%  ->  cash 04/06/27
    May 27   5%  ->  cash 05/07/27

Four payments, one per month, exactly as it should.

THE LINE UNDERNEATH WAS LYING. It read "cash on 05/07/27" - and that figure was
not from the schedule at all. It was the PERIOD END, 10/05, plus 35 days, computed
separately:

    const cash = paymentDate(cycling ? (term.startDate || refDate) : refDate, term)

The "val ... then N more" preview was only ever wired up for the Project
application dates cycle. Every other cycle fell through to that single made-up
date.

WORSE, IT LOOKED PLAUSIBLE. Period end + 35 happened to land on 05/07/27, which is
also the last instalment - so it read like a sensible answer while describing
something the forecast does not use.

Now it shows the real schedule:

    4 payments: 04/04/27, 05/05/27, 04/06/27...

Same treatment the labour line has always had, which is why that one was right.
