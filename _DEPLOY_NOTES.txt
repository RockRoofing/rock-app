  everything else as pkg771

WHAT IT DOES
------------
An "edit" link beside the project title at the top of a deal. Click it, type,
Enter to save or Escape to cancel. A blank name is rejected rather than saved.

WHY IT CANNOT DOUBLE-COUNT
---------------------------
I checked before building it. Every sales and scorecard report aggregates on
d.id - the title appears in them only as a display column, never as a key. The
rename patches the SAME deal in place, so no second record can exist.

It writes a plain note to the timeline, NOT a value-change entry. The Value
Changes report reads entries of type 'value', so a rename cannot appear there
and cannot move a figure.

Nothing else on the deal is touched: value, stage, activities and fields are all
unchanged.

THE OLD NAME IS KEPT
--------------------
The timeline records: Renamed: "old name" -> "new name", with who did it.

A renamed project is otherwise impossible to find later by the name everybody
first knew it as - which on a job that ran for a year is the name half your
emails and half your paperwork still use.

ONE THING TO BE AWARE OF
-------------------------
The CRM import is wipe-and-replace from a Pipedrive export. If you rename a deal
here and then run an import, the Pipedrive title comes back. Rename in both, or
rename here only on deals that no longer come down in the export.
