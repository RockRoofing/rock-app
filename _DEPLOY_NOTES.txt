pkg477 - application full width, three cards across
===================================================

Deploy after pkg476. One changed file: pages/applications.js. No new files.


THE LAYOUT
----------
    Summary          Retention          Certificate

Side by side, instead of Summary and Certificate paired with Retention as a full-width
band beneath them.

Retention sits in the MIDDLE deliberately - it is the bridge between what has been done
and what is being certified, and both neighbours reference it.

It is also now styled to match the other two: same card, same corner radius, same header
bar. It was built as a standalone panel and looked like something that had wandered in.


THE PAGE IS WIDER
-----------------
1280 was right for two blocks. With three it squeezed the tables until the figures
wrapped. Now 2000, still centred, so the columns do not stretch absurdly on a very wide
monitor.


IT COLLAPSES RATHER THAN CRUSHES
----------------------------------
The grid fits as many 340px columns as will go, so it drops to two and then one on a
narrow window instead of squashing three four-column tables into nothing.

Checked at the widths people actually use:

    1366px   3 columns, ~428px each
    1512px   3 columns, ~477px each
    1920px   3 columns, ~613px each

So a laptop still gets all three side by side, which is the point.
