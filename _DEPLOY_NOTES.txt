1. DEALS RESEARCHED
-------------------
Same figure as the Deals Researched dashboard, from the same source, so the two cannot
disagree. Default target 20/month - change it with the pencil on the card.


2. AVERAGE VALUE OF PROJECTS SECURED
------------------------------------
Rolling 6 months ending with the month shown, with the 6 months BEFORE that underneath:

    Average value of projects secured
    £182,400
    Previous 6 months: £150,000   ▲ £32,400 (22%)   from 14 won deals

Two decisions worth knowing:

  - the average is per WON DEAL, not per month. "What is a job worth to us now", not
    "how much did we win" - you already have Total value of work secured for that.
  - won deals with NO VALUE are excluded. A won deal worth zero is a data gap, not a
    cheap job, and leaving it in drags the average down for no reason.

Windows are exactly 6 months and do not overlap. Checked on the bench.


3. EMAILS SENT EXTERNALLY
-------------------------
Counts messages sent from that person's mailbox to at least one address outside
rockroofing.co.uk. Internal chatter is not the activity being measured.

Reads Sent Items directly, so it counts everything sent - not just mail the CRM managed to
file against a project. An email to a customer about a job that never became a project is
still work done.

Stored as a per-month tally, not the messages:

    crm:email-volume -> { "edita@rockroofing.co.uk": { "2026-08": 143 } }

Counting is not the same as keeping. Nobody needs 40,000 subject lines retained to know
143 emails went out in August.

A nightly cron (02:00) recounts the last TWO months only - a finished month does not
change. Re-running OVERWRITES those months rather than adding to them, so it cannot
double-count.

BACKFILL, once, after deploying:

    /api/cron/crm-email-volume?months=13

That gives you a year of history. It reads a lot of Sent Items, so use ?mailbox= to do one
person at a time if it times out.

A month with no figure shows a dash, not a red zero. A month we never counted is not a
month with no emails.

The mailbox is matched to the person by FIRST NAME against the address - "Edita" ->
edita@rockroofing.co.uk. That is the only join available: the scorecard knows people by
name, Graph knows them by mailbox, and nothing links the two. It works for your five; if
you ever have two people sharing a first name it will need a proper mapping.
