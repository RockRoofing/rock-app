pkg503 - timeline moved under the activity
==========================================

Deploy after pkg502. One changed file: pages/crm.js. No new files.


THE LAYOUT
----------
    DEAL                  WHAT HAPPENED?
    SUMMARY               (call notes)
    PROJECT DETAILS
    CUSTOMER CONTACT      SET THE NEXT ACTIVITY
    ORGANISATION          what / when / who

                          TIMELINE
                          email, notes, previous calls

The timeline is now inside the right-hand column, directly under the activity, instead of
below both columns. So the details stay beside it for its whole length rather than being
pushed above it with a tall empty space alongside.


THE DETAILS COLUMN IS STICKY
------------------------------
It stays on screen while you scroll the timeline next to it.

That matters more here than it sounds: the left column is much shorter than the right once
the timeline is in there, so without this the phone number would scroll out of view
exactly when you were reading back through what was last said - which is the moment you
want both.

Capped at 82% of the window height with its own scroll, for a project carrying all 34
fields.

The timeline gets a little more room too - 380px rather than 320px - now that it is not
competing with anything below it.
