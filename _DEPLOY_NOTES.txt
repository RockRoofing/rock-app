WHAT CHANGED
1. OVERNIGHT ALLOWANCES ARE NOW SAVED
   In the Accounts Weekly Labour Allocation picker, ticking an O/A box now saves it immediately
   (stored per installer, per day). Next time you open the picker, all previously-set O/As are
   already ticked - nothing is lost on close. The downloadable report also reads the saved O/As,
   so it always reflects what's recorded.

2. NEW "Review past O/A" TAB
   The Accounts pop-out now has two tabs: "Build report" (as before) and "Review past O/A".
   The Review tab lists every recorded overnight allowance and lets you filter by:
     - From / To date range
     - Installer
   so you can check when O/A applied on past dates. You can also remove an entry from here.
