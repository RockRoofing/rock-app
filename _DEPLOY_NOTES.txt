WHY IT WAS CUT
--------------
Budgets is twelve months plus five summary columns. The page sets a minWidth on
the table and nowrap on every cell, which is right on screen - you scroll
sideways. Print cannot scroll, so the right-hand columns fell off the sheet with
nothing to say they had.

Neither of those was being overridden in print. Now both are: the table
compresses to the page instead of being clipped.

WHAT CHANGED IN PRINT ONLY
---------------------------
  tables      min-width removed, width 100%, 7.5pt
  cells       nowrap removed so long labels wrap, tighter padding
  selects     hidden - a dropdown cannot be read on paper and the method column
              was costing real width
  inputs      kept, because they hold figures you need, but stripped of their
              box so they read as text rather than empty form fields

At 7.5pt across A3 landscape that is about 23mm per column against roughly 15mm
for a seven-figure number, so it fits with room to spare.

AND A BACKSTOP
--------------
The five widest sections - Budgets, 12-Month, Forecast P&L, Forecast Balance
Sheet and Cash Schedule - also print scaled to 72%. Small type is always better
than a missing column: a reader can squint, but cannot see a column that is not
on the page.

WHAT TO CHECK
-------------
Export Budgets on its own and confirm the FY total and Tracking columns are
present at the right-hand edge. Those are the two that were being lost, and they
are the two worth having.
