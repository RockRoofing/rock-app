pkg441 - "Complete all monthly dates for May 2026" with no May row
==================================================================

Deploy after pkg440. One changed file: pages/project/[id].js (literal [id] folder).


THE PROBLEM
-----------
Two different rules were in play and they had drifted apart.

  THE TABLE shows a rolling window: the last 2 months and 12 ahead. Today that is
  June 2026 to July 2027.

  THE COMPLETENESS CHECK looks at EVERY month that holds any dates at all, however old,
  and flags any with some of the three filled in and not all.

May 2026 has a partial set saved from an earlier session. The window has since rolled past
it, so the warning had no row to be fixed in. Unfixable - and an unfixable warning is worse
than no warning, because people learn to ignore the whole banner.


FIXED
-----
Any month holding dates is now shown, whether or not it falls in the window, sorted into
place. May 2026 will appear at the top of your table.

Past months that only appear because they hold data are labelled in orange - "past month,
part filled" - so it is obvious why a month from before the window is on screen.


HOW TO CLEAR IT
---------------
Two ways, both fine:

  FILL IT IN   complete the missing date(s) for May. The warning goes.

  CLEAR IT     delete all three May dates. The row removes itself entirely - that was
               already the behaviour, it just needed a row to do it in.

Which is right depends on whether May was a real application month. If it was, fill it in;
if somebody started typing and stopped, clear it.


WORTH CHECKING ELSEWHERE
------------------------
This will not be the only project. Any project where somebody part-filled a month that has
since scrolled out of the window will have had the same silent, unfixable warning. They
will all become fixable with this deploy - so if the "still to complete" count drops across
several projects over the next few days, that is why.
