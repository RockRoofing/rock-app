THE RETENTION SECTION
---------------------
A new block at the end of the application, after the works:

    Retention
    Retention on the final account is £5,850.00 at 3%. Each half is £2,925.00.

    [ ] 1st Half   £2,925.00
    [ ] 2nd Half   £2,925.00

Tick a half when it falls due and it is added to that application.

Each half is measured against the retention on the FINAL ACCOUNT, not on the work
certified so far - a release claimed at 90% complete is still half of the whole pot, which
is what the contract says.


IN THE SUMMARY
--------------
Two lines under Materials On Site, filled straight from the section above:

    1st Release Retention    £2,925.00      (grey and "not claimed" until ticked)
    2nd Release Retention    £0.00

The right-hand column always shows what the half is worth, so the amounts are visible
before anything is claimed.


ON THE CERTIFICATE
------------------
A "Retention Released" line appears between Retention and Total when something is claimed -
which is where it happens: retention comes off, the released half goes back on.

    Gross              £200,000.00
    MCD @ 2.5%           £5,000.00
    Sub-Total          £195,000.00
    Retention @ 3%       £5,850.00
    Retention Released   £2,925.00
    Total              £192,075.00


CLAIMED ONCE, NOT EVERY MONTH
-------------------------------
A half ticked on one application stays ticked on the next, but it does NOT appear again in
"This Cert" - the certificate only ever claims what has not already been claimed. The tick
box for a half claimed earlier is locked and labelled, because unticking it would not
un-claim the money, it would just make the certificate wrong.


THROUGH TO THE RETENTION TRACKER
---------------------------------
Retention Owed now reads: retention on the invoiced value LESS whatever has been claimed
back. Claim both halves and it goes to zero, instead of the register carrying on chasing
money that has been applied for.

Worked through end to end on a £200,000 job at 2.5% MCD and 3% retention: owed £5,850
before, £0.00 after both halves.


ONE THING WORTH KNOWING
-----------------------
There was already a "retentionReleased" in dashboard.js meaning something different -
retention DUE by date, once the PC and defects dates have passed. Mine is retention
CLAIMED, and it is called retentionClaimed for that reason. Due and claimed are not the
same thing and one quietly overwriting the other would have been hard to spot.
