DEPLOY NOTES - Cash Flow: labour payment options (weekly/fortnightly/EOM)
========================================================================

ONE file, an EDIT - just replace it.
  pages/project-cashflow.js

LABOUR PAYMENT now has three options (top of the application):
  - Weekly       - labour split across each WEEK of the period
  - Fortnightly  - labour split across each FORTNIGHT of the period
  - EOM + days   - single payment, end of the period's month + X days

  Weekly / fortnightly:
    * The period is divided into week (or fortnight) windows from the 1st day of the
      period.
    * The labour total for the period is split across those windows BY WORKING DAYS
      (Mon-Fri) in each window.
    * Each instalment is paid on that window's end date + X days (default 7, editable).
  The editor shows the resulting payment dates live.

  Labour "this period" is now the INCREMENT over the previous application (matching how
  revenue this-cert works), so weekly/fortnightly amounts reflect only the new labour
  in the period. The saved forecast stores the full labour payment schedule
  ([{date, amount}]) ready for the cash flow feed.

SALES terms unchanged. MATERIALS terms unchanged (still per line).
