STILL WRONG, AND HERE IS WHY
----------------------------
The actual months are still showing MY summed figure, not Xero's - net assets
1,083,146 at Jul against a real 56,460, and "Out by" still constant.

pkg662 IS deployed (the new note about ACTUAL months is on your screenshot), so
the fetch ran. What did not happen is the MATCH: my parser only looked for a
handful of exact labels at two levels of nesting. If Xero words the row
differently, or nests it one level deeper, nothing matched - and the page fell
back to my own sum silently, which is the very fault it was meant to fix.

I checked the date parsing first: "31 Jul 2026" parses correctly, so the columns
are not the problem.
