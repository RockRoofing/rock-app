pkg723 - Bibby availability in the cash flow chart tooltip
===========================================================

REPLACE:
  pages/business-financials/cashflow.js

No new files. Deploy pkg722 first if you have not.

WHAT CHANGED
------------
Hovering any week on "Projected cash balance" now gives a full breakdown
instead of two bare figures:

  Closing cash
  Total available
  -- Invoice finance --
  Bibby availability (gross)     <- the whole advance the facility supports
  less drawn                        against the ledger that week, BEFORE
  Still drawable                    deducting what you have borrowed
  -- --
  Card headroom
  Overdraft headroom             <- moves with the balance, as it should

Gross and net are tracked separately rather than one derived from the other.
Both are floored at zero, so once either hits the floor "net + drawn" stops
equalling gross and deriving it would quietly be wrong.

The dashed "total available" line still uses the NET figure and that has not
changed - money already drawn is in the bank and cannot be drawn twice.
