pkg502 - the Make Contact panel now shows everything
====================================================

Deploy after pkg501. One changed file: pages/crm.js. No new files.


WHAT WAS WRONG WITH pkg498
--------------------------
I hand-picked about fifteen fields I thought would be useful. That was the wrong approach
twice over: anything I had not thought of was simply missing, and anything added to the
deal in future would never appear here.


NOW BUILT FROM THE SCHEMA
-------------------------
Every field on the deal, in the same four groups the deal itself uses. 34 fields:

    DEAL              value, stage, status, owner
    SUMMARY           Value, Organization, Contact, Owner, Project Score
    PROJECT DETAILS   Glenigan Project ID, Site Location, Region, Size m2, Credit Score,
                      Credit Limit, Insured Credit Limit, Project Stage, Roofing Works
                      On-Site, Estimator Responsible, Systems Priced, Description of
                      Project Scope of Works, General Information, Sales Person, Project
                      Start Date, Project Type, Lead Source, Lost Reason
    CUSTOMER CONTACT  Name, Phone, Email, Job Role
    ORGANISATION      Address, Phone, Website, Email, Registration Number, Supply Chain
                      Approved?

Add a field to the deal and it appears here too, with no further work.


CONTACT AND ORGANISATION FALL BACK TO THE MASTER RECORDS
----------------------------------------------------------
Same resolution the deal view uses: a deal stores the contact's and company's NAME, while
the phone, email, address and registration number live on the Contact and Company records.
Anything typed on the deal wins; blanks fill from the master record.

Without that, most of those two sections would have shown a dash on most projects - the
same fault pkg457 fixed on the deal page.


ALL FOUR PHONE AND EMAIL FIELDS ARE LINKS
-------------------------------------------
Contact phone, contact email, organisation phone, organisation email - tel: and mailto:.
The panel is open because somebody is about to make contact, so any of the four should be
one click.


EMPTY FIELDS ARE STILL SKIPPED
--------------------------------
Deliberately. 34 rows of dashes is not "everything", it is noise on a phone call. A group
with nothing in it does not render at all.
