1) MARGIN PAGE  (new tab: Summary | Sales | MARGIN | Bills ...)
-----------------------------------------------------
Gross and net margins from the monthly P&L, completed months only.
  Gross margin = (Income - Cost of Sales) / Income
  Net margin   = (Income - Cost of Sales - Overheads) / Income

FOUR line charts, each with an amber dashed TREND line:
  - Gross margin - current FY
  - Net margin - current FY
  - Gross margin - 12-month rolling (each point = trailing 12 months aggregated)
  - Net margin - 12-month rolling

Filters: Financial year (Dec-Nov, same as Sales) + a From/To month range.
Below the charts: a table showing exactly how each month in the selected FY was
calculated (income, cost of sales, gross profit, gross margin, overheads, net
profit, net margin).

Data comes from your Xero benchmark sync (the same P&L figures the Summary uses).
The rolling charts only plot a month once a full trailing 12 months exists, so
early months won't have a rolling point - that's expected.
