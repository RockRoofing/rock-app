WHY
  The scheduling code itself checks out (correct UK-time gate, sends on the right day at
  or after the set hour, once per day). Since the manual "Send now" works, the automatic
  send failing points to something operational - most likely the cron job not being
  active, or the schedule day/hour/last-sent state. This package makes that VISIBLE.

WHAT TO DO AFTER DEPLOY
  1. Open the weekly report settings (where you set recipients + schedule) and click the
     new "Test schedule now" button. It saves your current day/hour, then runs the REAL
     scheduler and tells you:
       - whether it WOULD send right now, and if not, exactly why
         (e.g. "Not the scheduled day", "Too early", "Already sent today"),
       - the current UK day/time it sees, your scheduled day/time, and the last-sent date.

  2. Interpreting it:
     - If it says "Would send now" but no automatic email ever arrives on the scheduled
       day -> the CRON JOB isn't running. In Vercel: Settings -> Cron Jobs, confirm
       /api/cron/weekly-report is listed and enabled. Cron jobs only register on a
       successful deployment AFTER vercel.json contained them - if it's missing, redeploy
       from the latest commit (or check the project is on the Pro plan with crons on).
     - If it says "Already sent today" but nothing arrived -> the from-address/recipients
       issue, not the schedule. Use "Send now" and read that message.
     - If "Not the scheduled day / too early" -> it's simply not time yet; note it uses
       UK time and the hourly cron.

  You can also hit the cron directly in a browser to force a send and see the raw result:
     https://app.rockroofing.co.uk/api/cron/weekly-report?force=1
  (Shows JSON incl. who it sent to / any error / the diagnostic.)
