pkg457 - contact and company details showing on the deal
========================================================

Deploy after pkg456. One changed file: pages/crm.js. No new files.


WHY THEY WERE BLANK
-------------------
The data was there. Nothing was reading it.

A DEAL stores the contact's NAME and the company's NAME - that is all. The phone, email,
job role, address, website and registration number live on the CONTACT and COMPANY
records, which is where the Pipedrive People and Organizations exports put them.

The two side boxes on the deal only ever read the deal's own fields, so every one of those
showed "-" while the same information sat one click away under Contacts and Companies.


FIXED
-----
Both boxes now fall back to the master record, matched on name.

    Customer Contact   phone, email, job role
    Organization       address, phone, website, email, registration number

The DEAL WINS where it has a value. Anything typed on the deal is a deliberate override
for that job - a site contact's mobile is often not the number on the company record - so
it is never replaced by the master.

Matching ignores case and stray spaces, since the name is the only link between the two
and " Watkin Jones & Son Ltd " should still find it.

Each box says "Blank fields filled from the contact record" underneath when it has one, so
it is clear where a value came from rather than looking like it was typed on the deal.


A LIMITATION WORTH KNOWING
--------------------------
The link is the NAME. Rename a company and its deals stop finding its details - the same
fragility I flagged in pkg384 about linking by name rather than id.

It has not caused a problem yet and fixing it properly means giving companies and contacts
their own stable ids, which is a real piece of work rather than a patch. Worth doing at
some point; not worth doing in the middle of this.
