pkg445 - payment date picker was locked to the wrong month
==========================================================

Deploy after pkg444. One changed file: pages/project/[id].js (literal [id] folder).


YES, IT MAKES SENSE - AND YOU FOUND THE CAUSE
-----------------------------------------------
Your screenshot shows every month except August greyed out. That is a min/max on the date
input, and it was applied to ALL THREE columns:

    Application date   belongs to that month   correct
    Valuation date     belongs to that month   correct
    Payment date       normally the month AFTER, or later   WRONG

So on an August row the picker greyed out September, and a payment due 11 September - your
exact example - could only be typed by hand.


FIXED
-----
Application and valuation dates: unchanged, still limited to their own month. They should
be.

Payment date: now runs from the 1st of the row's month to the end of the month SIX after
it. Wide enough for any real payment term - 30, 45, 60, even 90 days - and still narrow
enough that the picker opens on the right month instead of on today.

    August 2026 row   ->  payment selectable 01 Aug 2026 to 28 Feb 2027
    December 2026 row ->  payment selectable 01 Dec 2026 to 30 Jun 2027

Year rollover checked, February and 30/31-day months included.


WHY A WINDOW RATHER THAN NO LIMIT AT ALL
------------------------------------------
Removing the limits entirely would open the picker on today's date every time, so a
January 2027 row would need scrolling back and forth. Keeping a window means it still
opens where you are working, and it also stops a payment date being set years out by a
mis-click.

If six months is ever not enough, it is one number in that file.


ALREADY FINE, CHECKED
---------------------
The four date fields on the application itself - the ones pkg444 made editable - have no
limits at all. Any date, any month, any year. Nothing needed there.
