================================================

Deploy after pkg488.

THE DEFAULT IS NOW OFF FOR BOTH
---------------------------------
    [ ] Apply the discount to variations
    [ ] Apply the discount to materials on site

So a new project discounts the measured work only, and the certificate reads:

    Gross measured work
    MCD
    Sub-total
    Variations & materials
    Sub-total
    Retention
    Total

Tick either box to bring that element back under the discount.


WHAT I DID NOT FLIP, AND WHY IT MATTERS
-----------------------------------------
The CALCULATION still treats a missing flag as "discount applies to everything".

Applications sent before this setting existed have no flag stored. Had I flipped the
calculation default, every one of them would have re-priced itself on the new basis - and
the PDF for a certificate the customer received last year would print different figures
from the one they have. Those are settled documents.

So:

    old application, no flag      whole account      unchanged, £199,536.75
    new application               measured only      £200,039.77
    project with a box ticked     whole account      £199,536.75

Verified on your J1 numbers.


DRAFTS FOLLOW THE PROJECT
-------------------------
A draft picks up a change to the setting; a sent application keeps the basis it was sent
on.

Without that, changing the setting would appear to do nothing on any draft already
started - you would have had to delete it and begin again to see the effect. Applications
pre-dating the setting are excluded from this and stay as they are.


ONE THING TO EXPECT
-------------------
Any DRAFT created between pkg488 and this deploy was stamped with the old default and will
now switch to measured-only when you open it, and the figures will change. That is
correct, but worth knowing rather than discovering.
