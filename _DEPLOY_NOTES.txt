THE DIAGNOSIS, CONFIRMED
------------------------
Re-saving app 1 did not fix it, which rules out a stale grossClaimedToDate and
leaves double counting. On save (line 1466):

    grossClaimedToDate: sum.grossCurrent + (prevGross - priorMeasuredGross) + revUpliftGross

sum.grossCurrent ALREADY contains that period's materials on site. Your app 1
override WAS the materials on site - so the same 62,000 enters twice, and app 3
deducts 158,000 where 96,000 has actually been claimed.

  contract        135,000
  claimed         96,000   (60,000 + 36,000, which is the cash)
  chain deducts   158,000
  app 3 offers    -23,000  instead of 39,000


1. "PREVIOUSLY CLAIMED (GROSS)" - A TYPED FIGURE WINS
-----------------------------------------------------
New override box beside Revenue this period. Blank follows the chain exactly as
now; type a figure and it is used directly.

Put 96,000 in and app 3 gives 39,000.

This is the honest fix. A revenue override cannot be reconstructed from
percentages - that is what "override" means - so where the chain cannot know, you
tell it. The applications screen already works this way.

Saved with the forecast, reloaded when you edit it, and RESET when you open a
different one - left out of the reset it would carry silently onto the next
forecast, which is the worst kind of stale figure because it looks deliberate.


2. OVER-CLAIM WARNING
---------------------
Red banner above the summary boxes when the prior exceeds contract plus
instructed variations:

    Already claimed GBP 158,000 against a contract of GBP 135,000 - over by
    GBP 23,000. That is why this period offers a negative figure: the excess
    unwinds here.

It should never have been possible to claim more than the job is worth without
being told. Surfacing later as a NEGATIVE revenue on the next application reads
like a broken calculation rather than an over-claim.
