WHAT HAPPENS
------------
The FIRST time a project moves into Negotiating, four activities are created, due today,
assigned to the Sales Person on the deal:

  1. Update Details information for Post Contract: Location, m2, Value, Credit Score,
     Credit Limit, Credit Insurance, Roofing Works on Site, Description of Project Scope of
     Works (Comprehensive list of all systems and types)
  2. Have we added the company to Top Service monitoring?
  3. Confirm provisional start on site date internally or with the Customer
  4. Have we confirmed a fully insured limit? Notify Director if full limit not obtained.


ONCE ONLY, AS ASKED
--------------------
The project records that it has been done, on the deal itself. Move it out of Negotiating
and back in and nothing is created a second time - even if the original four have been
completed or deleted in the meantime.

That marker is deliberately stored on the deal rather than worked out from whatever
activities happen to be present. Inferring it from the activity list would mean completing
them all and moving back in would look identical to a project that had never been through
the stage, and you would get a fresh set.

Confirmed the marker survives being saved - if it did not, the tasks would reappear after
a reload.


IF THERE IS NO SALES PERSON
----------------------------
The four are still created, unassigned, and the kanban card shows an amber
"No sales person assigned" flag. Better to have the work recorded with nobody on it than
not recorded at all.

Set the Sales Person afterwards and those four are picked up and assigned to them
automatically - the same way the estimator adopts the Tender return activity.


SALES PERSON NOW COMES FROM THE PORTAL
----------------------------------------
It was another hard-coded list of first names - Edita, Roman, Niall, James, Simon - the
same thing that gave you "James" and "James McVeigh" as two people. It now lists your
portal users, matching the estimator field and the activity owners.

I also had to fix the deal sidebar's dropdowns while doing this: they read their options
straight from the schema, so with the hard-coded list removed both Estimator Responsible
and Sales Person would have shown as EMPTY dropdowns on the deal. They now use the portal
users like the Add Project form does.
