NEW - "Overheads" row (below Water Ingress)
A new row lets you allocate staff to non-project time. Click a day (Day view) to open its
Overheads panel, then add one or more staff members, EACH with their own category:
  Holidays, Internal Time, Ops Support, Sick, Unpaid Leave, Paid Leave.
- Multiple staff per day, each on their own category.
- The day cell shows the headcount; week view shows the weekly total.
- You can change a person's category inline or remove them.
- A person can only be on ONE overhead entry per day (prevents double-booking).

Stored separately from project allocations (ops:overheads), so it doesn't affect project
labour numbers or clash checks.

NOTE: These overhead entries are not yet shown on the Weekly Labour Allocation report - if you
want them on the report too, tell me and I'll add them (kept separate for now since the report
is about site labour).
