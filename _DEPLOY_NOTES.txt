WHAT'S NEW
  The per-row Forecast method dropdown has a new option:
     "Make same as Budget / mo"

  When you pick it for a row, it stamps that row's Budget / Mo figure into every
  REMAINING month of the financial year (the current month + all future months) as
  the forecast value. Completed months keep their actuals.

  It's an ACTION, not a sticky mode - after it runs, the dropdown returns to the
  row's normal method (1/2/3). The filled months become editable overrides, so you
  can still fine-tune any individual month afterwards. The change is saved
  immediately.

  If a row has no Budget / Mo value set, the option does nothing (nothing to apply).
