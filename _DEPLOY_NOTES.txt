WHAT'S NEW ON THE RFI PAGE
  - New "Attachments" column in the RFI table showing a paperclip + count.
  - Open an RFI, and each attachment now has:
      * "View & mark up" - opens a viewer window showing the PDF (multi-page) or image.
      * "Download" - saves the original file.
  - In the viewer you can annotate the drawing itself:
      * Arrow, Line, Box, Circle, freehand Draw, HIGHLIGHT (marker), and Text boxes.
      * Choose the COLOUR, thickness and opacity of lines / writing.
      * Highlighter lays down a thick semi-transparent stroke for highlighting text/areas.
      * Select + Delete to remove a mark; Undo; then "Save markup".
  - Markup is saved against that attachment and shown again next time (an attachment that
    has marks shows a small "marked up" tag). Both internal staff and customers can view,
    mark up and download.

NOTES
  - PDFs render with pdf.js (same approach as the drawings pages); very large or unusual
    PDFs that can't render inline still offer Download.
  - The Highlight tool and Download button were added to the shared DrawingMarkup
    component, so the Design "Drawings" pages get them too.
