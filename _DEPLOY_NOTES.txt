DEPLOY NOTES - Project Concern invites: always UK time
======================================================

TWO files, both EDITS - just replace them.
  pages/api/concern-invite.js     (calendar invite now pinned to UK time)
  components/ProjectConcerns.js    ("UK time" label + help text)

THE PROBLEM
  Calendar invites used "floating" time with no timezone, so each attendee's calendar
  read the time in THEIR OWN timezone. Staff outside the UK saw the wrong time.

FIX
  The invite now sends the meeting time as UK time (Europe/London) with a proper
  timezone definition (handles GMT/BST automatically). Every attendee - anywhere in the
  world - now sees the meeting converted correctly to their local time from the UK time
  you chose.

  The concern page now shows a "UK time" label next to the time field and notes that
  attendees elsewhere will see it in their local time.

NOTE: this affects NEW/updated invites sent after deploy. Any invites already sent
before this will keep their old (floating) time until the meeting date/time is changed
and a new invite goes out.
