WHY THIS RATHER THAN ANOTHER FIX
---------------------------------
I have shipped several fixes for this and each one has been a guess at which of the rules
was excluding your activity. That is not working and it is wasting your time.

So instead of guessing again: open "Where are my activities?" and the tab now lists EVERY
activity you have added, and states for each one exactly where it stands.

    Bensham Lane - Chase drawings (2026-08-20)
        showing

    Round Silo - Call back Tuesday (2026-08-25)
        hidden: project is "won" (tab lists open projects only)

    Dwyer Road - Send quote (no date)
        hidden: person filter is "James McVeigh", this is "nobody"

It checks the rules in order - done, project status, person filter, customer filter - and
reports the first one that excludes it. If it says "showing" and you still cannot see it in
the table, that is a different problem entirely and I will know where to look.

One screenshot of that panel ends this.


WHAT IT TELLS US
----------------
  "showing"                       the rules are fine; the fault is in the table itself
  "project is won/lost"           pkg336 restricted this tab to open projects at your
                                  request, and that is what is catching them
  "person filter is..."           a filter is set that your activity does not match
  "marked done"                   it was saved as complete
  "project is not in the CRM"     the deal it belongs to is not loaded
