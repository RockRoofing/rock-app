WHAT CHANGED

1. NEW RFI -> IMMEDIATE email, one per RFI. Every time an RFI is raised, everyone assigned
   to the project (Rock Roofing team + customers) gets an email straight away, and it now
   says clearly "<Name> from Rock Roofing has raised a new RFI ...". One email per new RFI.

2. COMMENTS -> ONE email per project at the END OF THE DAY (not straight away). If there
   are several comments across the day, people get a single "new comments on RFIs for your
   project" email that evening. (The daily job now runs once at 18:00.)

3. SEND REMINDERS button (Rock Roofing users only), next to "+ Add RFI". It emails the full
   list of OUTSTANDING (unresolved) RFIs to everyone assigned to the project on demand.
   A note beside it reads: "Reminders are also sent out automatically every 3 working days."
   If there are no outstanding RFIs, it tells you and sends nothing.

4. AUTOMATIC OUTSTANDING REMINDER is now every 3 WORKING DAYS (weekends don't count), in
   addition to the manual button. Projects with no outstanding RFIs are skipped.
