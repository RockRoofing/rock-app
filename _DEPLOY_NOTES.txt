WHAT'S NEW
----------
A "Bills to pay" table at the BOTTOM of the Cash Flow page, in a compact scrollable
window (max ~340px tall) so it doesn't take over the page - just scroll through it.

Each bill row shows: Supplier, Ref, Due date, Amount, and an editable
"Planned payment date".

  - Set a planned payment date on any bill and the 13-week forecast above updates
    immediately (that bill's cash-out moves to the week you chose).
  - A planned date is highlighted ORANGE so you can see which bills you've moved.
  - Leave it blank (or click the x to clear) and the bill falls back to its Xero
    due date, as before.
  - Rows are sorted by planned payment date. Payment dates are saved per bill and
    persist across sessions and page loads.

HOW IT FEEDS THE FORECAST
   The forecast's "Bills out" now uses: planned payment date if set, otherwise the
   Xero due date. So the running cash line, lowest point and closing balance all
   reflect when you actually plan to pay.
