WHAT THIS IS
  A Notification Builder in the Admin centre (like the form/template builder). You create
  and edit email notifications there - dates, recipients, and email content are all
  editable. Two functions are supported: Commercial and Bookkeeping.

EACH NOTIFICATION HAS
  - Name, on/off toggle.
  - Function (Commercial / Bookkeeping) and Cadence (Weekly / Monthly).
  - Due day: weekly = a day of the week; monthly = a day of the month.
  - Send offset: 0 = on the due day; 1 = the day after; 2 = two days after ... (used for
    the "still not complete" follow-ups).
  - When to send: "Always" (a plain reminder on the due day) OR "Only if the tasks are
    NOT all marked Yes" (checks the relevant Weekly/Monthly task grid for that period).
  - Recipients: tick portal users AND/OR type in any other email addresses.
  - Email subject + body (free text).
  - "Send test" button (send to yourself or to the real recipients) to preview.

HOW SENDING WORKS
  The cron runs daily and, for each enabled notification, fires only on its due day (+
  offset). "Only if not complete" notifications send only when that period's tasks aren't
  all Yes. Each notification only sends once per period (no duplicates).

TO SET UP WHAT YOU ASKED FOR, create these (Admin > Email Notifications > New):

  COMMERCIAL - Weekly reminder
    Function Commercial, Weekly, Due day Thursday, offset 0, When "Always".
    Body: "This is a weekly reminder that the Weekly Commercial Tasks need completing by
    close of play today."

  COMMERCIAL - Weekly not-complete (Friday)
    Commercial, Weekly, Due day Thursday, offset 1, When "Only if not complete".
    Body: "The Weekly Commercial Tasks have not been marked complete. Please ensure the
    weekly tasks are completed ASAP."

  COMMERCIAL - Monthly reminder
    Commercial, Monthly, Due day of month = your tracker date, offset 0, "Always".
    Body: "This is a reminder that you need to complete the Monthly Commercial Tasks by
    the end of today."

  COMMERCIAL - Monthly not-complete
    Commercial, Monthly, same due day, offset 1, "Only if not complete".
    Body: "The Monthly Commercial Tasks have not been marked complete. Please ensure the
    monthly tasks are completed ASAP."
  COMMERCIAL - Monthly still-not-complete (+2)
    As above but offset 3 (i.e. 2 days after the first follow-up), "Only if not complete".

  BOOKKEEPING - the same four/five, with Function = Bookkeeping, weekly Due day = Friday,
  monthly Due day of month = 15, and the wording swapped to "Bookkeeping".
