WHAT THIS DOES

ROCK DRAWINGS (grid, like the Ops drawings layout)
  - Thumbnail cards; drawing STATUS shown below the thumbnail.
  - Status defaults to "In Review"; can be moved to "Approved" or "Construction Issue"
    (dropdown on the card, or inside the drawing).
  - Upload drawings (internal only). Customers cannot upload.
  - Click a drawing -> opens the drawing with a COMMENTS section below.
  - Both Rock and the customer can comment (rich text + @mentions, author + time
    recorded, customer comments tagged).
  - ON-DRAWING MARKUP: arrows, boxes, circles, lines, freehand, and text call-outs.
    Each has a changeable COLOUR, OPACITY and line THICKNESS. Undo, delete a selected
    shape, and Save markup. Both Rock and the customer can mark up.

CONTRACT DRAWINGS (table)
  - Upload PDF drawings; the app reads the TITLE BLOCK and fills in Architect,
    Reference, Project, Revision, Status and Date automatically where it can.
  - "Edit info" lets you check/correct any field (title blocks vary between architects,
    so treat the auto-read values as a best-effort starting point).
  - Click a drawing to open, comment and (for image drawings) mark up.

ACCESS (server-enforced)
  - Internal: upload, status, edit info, delete, comment, markup.
  - Customers: view, download, comment and markup only - no upload/delete, scoped to
    their projects.

HONEST NOTES (bones - refine after you walk it)
  - Markup draws on IMAGE drawings (PNG/JPG). PDF/DWG drawings open in a tab to view;
    on-PDF markup is a later refinement.
  - Title-block auto-read is best-effort and will need tuning once you try it on your
    real architects' drawings - that's expected; the Edit info fields are the safety net.
  - Tech Sub Builder, Leak Test Cert Builder and O&M auto-assembly remain "coming soon".
