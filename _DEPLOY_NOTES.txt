THE ANSWER
----------
160,831.50 of debt belongs to customers with NO INSURED LIMIT recorded in the
app. The rule is

    advance = hasLimit ? Math.min(rawAdvance, insured) : 0

so those customers are funded at ZERO. Bibby plainly do hold limits for them -
that is the bulk of the gap, and it is a data problem, not a maths one.

Reconciled from your export:

    Sales Ledger                      470,575.68
      less retention invoices           6,109.24
      less eligibility caps            43,433.81
    = Approved Debt                   421,032.63
      less NO insured limit set       160,831.50   <- the problem
    = Insurable Approved Debt         260,201.13
    x 60%                             156,120.68   the app's figure, exactly

Against Bibby: your Sales Ledger is within 2,248.82 of theirs (470,575.68 vs
468,326.86), which is close enough to be timing. The app over-deducts by
30,007.84 once High Involvement is entered - and 160,831.50 of missing limits
more than explains it.


TWO BUGS IN MY EXPORT, BOTH FIXED
---------------------------------
1. MIXED UNITS. "over insured limit" was in ADVANCE terms, "no insured limit set"
   in DEBT terms - and they were THE SAME EXCLUSION. 160,831.50 x 60% = 96,498.90
   exactly. A customer with no limit gets advance 0, so their whole raw advance
   already sat inside the over-limit figure. The summary could not be made to add
   up, which is why it looked wrong.

   Everything is now in DEBT terms down to Approved Debt, then the rate is applied
   ONCE, matching how Bibby lay it out. The summary now reconciles line by line to
   156,120.68.

2. NO WAY TO SEE WHICH CUSTOMERS. A single 160,831.50 with nowhere to go. There is
   now a BY CUSTOMER block - fundable debt, insured limit, "NO LIMIT SET", advance
   and debt not funded - sorted so the missing limits come first.


WHAT TO DO
----------
1. Deploy, export again, look at the BY CUSTOMER block. Every row marked
   "NO LIMIT SET" is money being funded at zero.

2. Those names almost certainly failed to match on the Bibby CSV import - the
   import reports "N matched a customer, M did not". Unmatched Bibby names are
   stored under the BIBBY spelling, so the app's customer never picks them up.
   Fix the names or set the limits by hand on the page.

3. Enter High involvement 35,069.31 and Funds in use 122,711.31 from their
   summary, or the app's Availability will stay wrong even once limits are right.

4. Then chase the 2,248.82 on the top line - almost certainly invoices raised
   after their statement date.
