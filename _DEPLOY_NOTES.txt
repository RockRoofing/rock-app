THE CHANGE
===========
Every card at the top of Forms Missing now opens a pop-out listing the forms behind
its number.

  The % card          every required form across all types
  Each form card      just that form type

Inside, they are grouped MISSING first, then Upcoming, then Completed - missing is
the reason anybody opens this page, so it should not be below a list of things that
are already done. Each row gives the week, the project, who is responsible, and the
form type where the view covers all of them.

The cards say "Click to see the forms" underneath. A card that does something should
look like it does; otherwise the only way to find out is to click one.


ONE DECISION THAT MATTERS
==========================
THE DRILL-DOWN READS ALL ROWS, NOT THE FILTERED ONES.

The cards are built from the API's own summary, which knows nothing about the page's
Status / Form / Responsible filters. Had the pop-out used the filtered list, pressing
a card reading 12 missing could have shown four - the number you pressed and the
number you got would disagree, and there would be nothing on screen to explain why.

So the pop-out always matches the card. The filters below carry on applying to the
table only.


ALSO
-----
x and Escape close it, never a backdrop click - the same rule as the other modals.
Where a form type has nothing required in the chosen weeks it says so rather than
opening an empty box.
