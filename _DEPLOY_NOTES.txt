1. THE OVERLAP - MY FAULT FROM pkg742
--------------------------------------
Making Bills to pay and Invoices owed drag-resizable meant changing them from
maxHeight to a FIXED height. The print stylesheet already forced
overflow: visible, so wide tables would not be clipped.

A fixed-height box with visible overflow does not grow - it spills its rows out
of the box and straight onto whatever comes next. That is why Bills to pay is
printing on top of the Forecast P&L and the Forecast Balance Sheet on your PDF.

Print now also forces height: auto, max-height: none and resize: none, so the
lists expand to their full length on paper instead of overflowing a fixed box.

should be - they had not finished when the print dialog opened. Those two are
the slowest because they each compose three endpoints. The note by the Download
