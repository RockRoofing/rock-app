ON THE VALUE - IT IS ALREADY NET
--------------------------------
Checked, because it is worth being certain rather than agreeing.

The figure is thisCert.total from computeApplicationSummary() - the certified
value for THAT application, after MCD and after retention. Not the gross, and not
the cumulative.

  gross this cert          40,000.00
  less MCD @ 2.5%           1,000.00
  less retention @ 5%       1,950.00
  = total this cert        37,050.00   <- what the chart uses

applicationTotal (100,000.00 in that test) is the gross cumulative figure and is
not used anywhere in the cash flow.

Nothing in this endpoint reads invoices - grep for "invoice" in
pages/api/project-cashflow.js returns nothing. It is applications only, as you
asked.

SO THE INFLATED FIGURES ARE NOT GROSS-VS-NET. The two causes I would still expect
in your August tooltip:

  1. A DRAFT WITH "PREVIOUSLY CERTIFIED (GROSS)" BLANK. With no previous figure
     the certificate computes as the whole cumulative value rather than the
     increment - so a 92,766.50 draft that should contribute nothing contributes
     92,766.50. These are marked "(draft)" in the breakdown. J240 Application 4
     is in exactly this state.

  2. THE SAME PROJECT APPEARING TWICE, once as "App N" and once as "forecast". A
     forecast is only superseded when an application's VALUATION date reaches the
     forecast's END date, so a forecast running past the application it overlaps
     stays in.

Both are now readable straight off the hover. Send me the August list and I will
fix whichever it is.
