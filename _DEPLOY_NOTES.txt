pkg344 - Change who is responsible, from either place

ON THE DEAL
-----------
Editing an activity now has a Person responsible dropdown next to the text and the date.
Your own name is first and marked "(you)", then the others, then "Nobody".

The activity header also shows who it belongs to without having to open it:

    ACTIVITY - James McVeigh
    ACTIVITY - nobody assigned

The second one is in grey, so an unassigned activity is obvious at a glance rather than
something you find out later when a filter hides it.


ON THE ACTIVITIES TAB
---------------------
Already there from pkg343 - click the Person responsible cell for the same dropdown, or use
the bigger box behind the pencil. No change needed.


A BUG THIS TURNED UP
---------------------
The deal's edit handler was:

    editActivity = (id, aid, text, due) => ...{ ...a, text, due }

No assignee. So editing an activity from the deal - even just fixing a typo in the text -
SILENTLY CLEARED whoever was responsible for it. Anything you had assigned would lose its
owner the next time it was touched.

Fixed: the assignee is kept when you do not change it, updated when you do, and only
cleared if you deliberately pick "Nobody".

Verified all four cases: kept on a text-only edit, reassigned, cleared on purpose, and the
old behaviour reproduced to confirm it was really doing that.
