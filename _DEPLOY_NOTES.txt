THE SUB-CONTRACT REF - it was never going to work
---------------------------------------------------
There is no "subContractRef" anywhere in the app. The field is called ORDERREF, resolved
from the handover, and Outstanding Invoices already prints it as [Sub-Contract Ref].

I was reading a name that does not exist, so it was always going to come back blank. Now
resolved the same way Outstanding Invoices resolves it, and it says "not set on the
handover" rather than "not set in Project Details", which was also wrong.


THE BUILDER IS NOW TWO PANES
------------------------------
    LEFT    every variation on this project, newest first, each with a DRAFT or SENT
            badge, its description and its value. Click one and it opens on the right -
            items, workings and clarifications all come back, so a draft can be finished
            rather than started again.
            "+ Raise new variation" at the foot.

    RIGHT   the one being worked on.

Saving an existing variation now REPLACES it rather than adding a second with the same
number, and the form no longer clears itself after a save - an edit you wanted to check or
send used to vanish the moment it was written.


VIEW ON THE TRACKER
-------------------
A View button on any variation that came from the builder. It shows what the CUSTOMER got:
header, priced items, total, clarifications - plus when it was sent and to whom, and a
Download PDF link.

"Show workings" underneath opens the build-up: every materials and labour line, the waste,
the mark-up and the totals per item. Hidden by default, so the top half can be read back
during a call without the customer's copy being cluttered with our costs.


REQUESTED BY
------------
A real dropdown of the CUSTOMER'S people only - it was also offering the company name and
our own QS, who do not request variations. "Someone else..." opens a text box for anybody
not on the handover.


THE WORKINGS WILL NOT CLOSE HALF DONE
---------------------------------------
It now names exactly what is missing:

    "This item still needs a description, a quantity, a unit, a mark-up %, at least one
     materials or labour line."

All five are required. Each of these ends up on a document going to a customer, and an
item with neither materials nor labour behind it is priced from nothing.


THE DOCUMENT
------------
    LOGO      now on it. It was looking for LOGO_URL, which is not set - it now serves
              /rock-logo.jpg from the site, exactly as the application PDF does.
    BLACK     no red anywhere. Red on a document going out reads as a warning.
    ROWS      double height, so descriptions are not stacked on top of each other.
    SPACING   more air between the last item and the total box.


THE EMAIL
---------
    "Hi Jack," - the first name of whoever is ticked to receive it.
    Signed off with your name, Rock Roofing Limited, your phone and your email.
    Replies go to you. That was already the case and is unchanged.


RAISE AND SEND
--------------
The button is now "Raise & send to customer" - or "Save & send to customer" when editing.
It saves, then opens the send window. A variation that is raised and not sent is one the
customer does not know about.

Sending now records WHEN and TO WHOM on the variation, which is what drives the DRAFT/SENT
badge in the left-hand list.
