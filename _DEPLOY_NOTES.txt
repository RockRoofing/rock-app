pkg788 - 612 on credit notes now reaches the project
=====================================================

  pages/api/sync-invoices.js        (changed here)

WHAT WAS HAPPENING
------------------
Credit notes WERE being fetched, and their 612 lines WERE being read and
negated. The problem was the matching.

A line is put against a project by the tracking category on its own line items.
A credit note raised against an invoice in Xero usually carries no tracking - it
just reverses part of something already coded - so trackingNames came back
empty, it fell to __UNASSIGNED__, and its 612 movement never reached the
project.

Hence the discrepancy on any job where a credit note has been applied.

THE FIX
-------
Two passes. Invoices are matched first, building a map of Xero invoice ID to
project. Untracked credit notes are then resolved through the invoice they were
ALLOCATED to, which the sync now keeps from Xero's Allocations.

  invoice    tracked                 -> project
  credit     tracked                 -> project (as before)
  credit     untracked, allocated    -> project via the invoice  (NEW)
  credit     untracked, unallocated  -> unassigned, as it should be

The sync response now reports creditNotesMatchedByAllocation so you can see how
many were rescued.

YOU MUST RE-SYNC
----------------
This changes how the lines store is BUILT. Nothing moves until you press Sync
Invoices - the existing store still has those credit notes filed under
unassigned.

ONE THING TO CHECK AFTERWARDS
------------------------------
The Deducted / Released split. A deduction is a negative 612 line, a release is
positive - and a credit note's amounts are negated on the way in. So a credit
note that REVERSES part of a retention deduction arrives as a positive, and will
be counted as RELEASED rather than as a smaller deduction.

The net position is right either way, and the outstanding card is unaffected.
But the two columns individually may read high on both sides. Look at a project
you know has a credit note and tell me whether Deducted and Released match what
you would expect - if not, I will split them on the transaction type rather than
the sign.
