WHAT THIS DOES - the full RFI feature (bones), inside a project's Design workspace:
  - Table of RFIs with: RFI number (auto-assigned RFI-001, 002...), Issued date (auto
    when created), Description (expands vertically as you type), Required-by date with a
    TRAFFIC LIGHT (orange = due within 5 days, red = overdue, nothing when fine or
    resolved - no green, as requested), Customer responsible (dropdown of the external
    customer users assigned to THIS project), Status (Open / Resolved), comment count.
  - + Add RFI: description, required date, responsible customer, attach files.
  - Click a row to open the RFI: full details, attachments, mark resolved / re-open,
    edit, delete, and a COMMENT THREAD.
  - Comments: rich text (Bold / Italic / Underline) and @mentions (type @ to pick a
    person - internal Design users or the project's customer users). Every comment
    records who wrote it and the date/time. Customer comments are tagged "Customer".

ACCESS (enforced server-side):
  - Internal (Designer / Post-Contract / Management / Admin): full - create, edit,
    resolve, delete, comment.
  - External customers: VIEW + COMMENT only, and only on their assigned projects. They
    cannot create/edit/resolve/delete.

NOTE: this is the working "bones" - deploy, try it, then tell me what to refine.
The @mention currently inserts a styled name token in the comment; wiring mentions to
notifications (email/alerts) is a later refinement if you want it.
