--------------------
1. THE PERSON AND CUSTOMER FILTERS NO LONGER PERSIST between visits. They reset each time

   setting, and remembering it costs more than it gains. Your TAB, COLUMNS and SORT are
   still remembered, because those genuinely are settings.
