1. NET CASH POSITION
--------------------
One box, beside Max cash available:

    Net cash position
    -74,656

      Bank cash                    2,500.52
      less card debt             -77,156.42
      = Net cash                 -74,655.90
      less invoice finance drawn -122,711.31
      = Net of all facilities   -197,367.21

      Drawing headroom does not change this - cash and debt rise together.

ONE BOX, NOT TWO. Two headline numbers measuring the same thing at different
scopes invites "which is the real one" and neither gets trusted. Both figures are
here with the arithmetic between them visible.

INVOICE FINANCE IS ON ITS OWN LINE because it is different in kind. Card debt and
overdraft are money owed with nothing behind them; IF is advanced against invoices
customers will pay and self-liquidates as the ledger collects. Netting it in
silently would overstate the problem, leaving it out would flatter it.

TRADE PAYABLES ARE DELIBERATELY EXCLUDED. Your 302,771 of bills is operational
debt, and the 13-week already schedules every one of them week by week. Putting it
in a headline box as well tells the same story twice, and the forecast is the
better place because timing is what matters.

WHY IT WAS NEEDED. The page said "Max cash available 191,532" and never said you
owed 197,367 against it. Read alone, that is strength. And drawing 46,188 of the
headroom raises cash to -28,468 while net of all facilities stays at -197,367
exactly - Max cash available measures how much borrowing is left, dressed as cash.
The sub-line on Max cash now says so too.


2. REFRESH BALANCES FROM XERO - REMOVED
---------------------------------------
Button, handler and its state are gone. Xero's Balance Sheet returns the BOOK
balance - only what has been reconciled - so it runs behind the real bank position
silently, which is worse than not having it.

Balances are typed in the panel below with the date they were read. The API
handler is left in place, and the Xero figures already fetched still drive the
per-account cross-check and the "Start from Xero's accounts" button - so nothing
is lost, only the misleading one-click refresh.
