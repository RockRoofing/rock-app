THE ACTUAL ANSWER
-----------------
Your app's permitted scope list does NOT contain accounting.transactions or
accounting.transactions.read. So invalid_scope was correct and unavoidable - the
name was never the problem, the app simply cannot request it. Four attempts spent
on the wrong question, which is on me. The portal list was the thing to check
first and I did not ask for it.

Of the scopes it IS permitted, two are already granted and already fail on
Overpayments:

  accounting.invoices.read           granted, Overpayments -> 401
  accounting.banktransactions.read   granted, Overpayments -> 401

That leaves ONE candidate worth trying: accounting.payments.read. An overpayment
originates from a payment, so of everything on the list it is the best fit. The
quick buttons on /connect are now just that one and its full variant.


HOW TO TRY IT
-------------
1. /connect -> click "accounting.payments.read" -> Reconnect Xero -> approve
2. Bills to Pay -> Sync bills

  "117 bills, 1 credit notes, 4 overpayments."   it worked
  "... Token HAS ... Granted: <list>"            granted but still refused
  "... DOES NOT HAVE ..."                        not granted
  invalid_scope from Xero                        not permitted either

Whatever happens you cannot get stuck: click "none", reconnect, and you are back
to the working connection.


IF accounting.payments.read DOES NOT WORK
-----------------------------------------
Then Overpayments genuinely needs accounting.transactions and this app cannot
have it. Two options at that point:

  a) See whether accounting.transactions can be ADDED to the app in the developer
     portal. If the list you sent is what is SELECTED rather than everything on
     offer, ticking it and reconnecting would fix this properly.

  b) Drop it. Say the word and I will strip the overpayment fetch out entirely.

Option (b) costs almost nothing, and it is worth being clear about that: without
any of this the app matched Xero's "Bills to Pay" screen TO THE PENNY at
307,471.06. The whole exercise nets off 4,699.57 across four records. The app is
arguably more useful matching the Xero screen exactly.
