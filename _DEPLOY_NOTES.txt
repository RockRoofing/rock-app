=======================================================================================

ONE file, an EDIT - just replace it.
  pages/operations/project-process.js

THE BUG
  When you reopened a card, it sometimes showed the OLD state - ticked items appeared
  unticked and chat messages were missing - until you did a full page refresh. The data
  was saving correctly all along; the card just wasn't re-syncing with the latest data
  when reopened.

THE FIX
  The card now re-syncs with the latest saved data whenever its contents change (not only
  when you open a different card). Ticking an item also refreshes the board immediately, so
  the checklist, chat and traffic light always reflect what's actually saved - no refresh
  needed.
