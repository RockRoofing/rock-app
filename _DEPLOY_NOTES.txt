WHAT WAS WRONG
--------------
The WIP deduction went straight onto reserves, so August read a 59,676 FALL in
net assets in a month that made 62,250. The number was right; nothing on the
page said where it came from.

Worse, the November accrual built in pkg726 was on the P&L but NOT on the
balance sheet at all - so the two pages would have ended the year apart by the
accrual's profit effect.

NOW
---
Reserves roll forward CLEAN - each month's own profit, unadjusted - and the two
WIP movements sit on their own lines underneath:

  Reserves (opening + profit)      clean roll-forward
  less last completed WIP          from the first forecast month onwards
  plus estimated WIP at 30 Nov     November only
  Reserves after WIP               ties to Net assets
  Out by

On your figures August now reads:

  Reserves (opening + profit)   118,710      (56,460 + 62,250)
  less last completed WIP      -121,926
  Reserves after WIP             -3,216

Same closing figure as before, but the 62,250 of profit is visible and the fall
is a labelled line rather than an unexplained step.

Both movements still hit the financing catch-all as well, so assets and
liabilities move with reserves and "Out by" keeps tying. The tie is now checked
against reserves AFTER the WIP rows, not before - that had to change with them.

No attempt to itemise WIP as its own asset row. That would mean pulling it out
of Xero's actual months, which needs the account name, which is the same
unresolved problem as the credit cards. Financing absorbs it and ties correctly.

The redundant "WIP OUT" badge on the August column heading is gone - the
movement has its own row now.
