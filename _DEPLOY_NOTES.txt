on their own dates; codes without one pay their predicted figure on the 28th,
which is the same default the 'oneday' mode already uses.


MONTHS WILL STILL DIFFER - AND SHOULD
---------------------------------------
    Sep  budgets 54,370   cash flow 59,733
    Oct          54,870             53,534
    Nov          55,861             50,997

A cost scheduled to pay on the 5th of the following month belongs in that month on
a cash flow and in this one on Budgets. Forcing them to agree per month would make
the cash flow wrong.

THE YEAR CANNOT DIFFER, because the same money leaves the bank either way. The
12-month now carries a check that says so if the totals drift more than 2% apart.
