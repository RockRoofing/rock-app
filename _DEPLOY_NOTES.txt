WHAT'S NEW - payment terms so the forecast knows WHEN cash moves:

  Each term is: a BASIS + a number of days.
    - "days"    = that many days from the reference date
    - "EOM +"   = end of the month the reference date is in, then + that many days
                  (the last day of the month counts as being in that month)

  SALES received  - term set at the TOP of the application (from the period end).
                    Default: EOM + 30.
  LABOUR paid     - term set at the TOP of the application (from the period end).
                    Default: 0 days.
  MATERIALS paid  - term set PER LINE (per supplier), from that line's delivery day.
                    Default: EOM + 30 from the delivery month.

  Each term shows the resulting CASH DATE live as you change it. The saved forecast
  now stores the terms and the computed cash dates (salesDate, labourDate, and a
  payDate per material line) ready to drive the Business Financials cash flow feed.
