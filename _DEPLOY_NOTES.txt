pkg463 - "more" now shows the whole email
=========================================

Deploy after pkg462.

CHANGED FILES (replace)
-----------------------
  pages/crm.js
  pages/api/crm.js
  lib/msGraph.js

NEW FILES: none.


THE CAUSE
---------
The sync never stored the full email. It stores Graph's bodyPreview - their own short
extract - capped at a further 400 characters:

    preview: (m.bodyPreview || '').slice(0, 400)

So "more" was expanding to the whole of what we HELD, which was the start of the email.
There was nothing more to show.


FIXED - FETCHED WHEN YOU ASK, NOT STORED
------------------------------------------
Pressing "more" on an email whose preview was cut fetches the full body from Outlook,
once, and caches it for as long as the page is open.

Not stored, deliberately. Keeping every full body would multiply the size of the email
store many times over for text almost nobody reads - and that store is already large
enough to need chunked saving.

Only fetched when it is actually needed:

    email of 90 chars    no "more" at all
    email of 250 chars   "more" expands instantly, no request - we already have it all
    email of 400 chars   "more" fetches the rest

So the common case costs nothing.


PLAIN TEXT, ON PURPOSE
----------------------
The body is requested as text rather than HTML. An email body is untrusted input from
outside the business - rendering its HTML would mean sanitising it properly first, and
plain text reads perfectly well in a card. Formatting and images are what "Open in
Outlook" is for.


WHEN IT CANNOT LOAD
-------------------
A message deleted or moved in Outlook since it was filed will not be there any more. You
get a plain message saying so with an Open in Outlook link, rather than a spinner that
never stops.
