pkg721 - Invoice Store Audit (READ-ONLY)
========================================

WHAT THIS DOES
Finds supplier bills sitting in the sales invoice store (invoiced:lines:*), by
matching Xero InvoiceIDs against the supplier bills already held in
costs:bills:* and bank:outstanding-bills.

It WRITES NOTHING. It does not touch dashboard:cache, so opening this page will
NOT flip the Cash Flow onto the contaminated source. Safe to run any time.

ACCESS
Admin only, same as the rest of Business Financials.

WHERE
Business Financials -> Store Audit (new tab in the nav bar).

WHAT TO LOOK AT
  "Supplier bills found in it"  - count of contaminated rows
  "Still outstanding"           - what the Cash Flow arrears row wrongly counts
                                  as money in. This should roughly account for
                                  the jump in the starting balance.
  "Full value"                  - what inflates invoiced totals, WIP and margin
                                  on the affected jobs.

Match column:
  ID    = matched on Xero InvoiceID. Certain. This IS a supplier bill.
  name  = matched on supplier name only, no ID on the row. Likely, but check
          before anything gets deleted.

If the count comes back ZERO, the store is clean and the cause is elsewhere -
tell me and I will stop looking here.
