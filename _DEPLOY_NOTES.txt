WHAT IT DOES
------------
Hover any segment of the green bar at the top of a deal and the tooltip now
reads:

  Received - 51 days
  Negotiating - 34 days (current, and counting)
  MC Unsecured - not reached
  1st Contact - no dated history

NO NEW DATA WAS NEEDED
-----------------------
The scaffold was already there - TimelineBar read deal.stageDays with a comment
saying "Not available from import yet. When persistence records stage-entry
timestamps we compute real values here."

It turned out those timestamps have been recorded all along. Every stage move
writes a history entry with type 'stage', a timestamp, stageFrom and stageTo. So
the timeline is reconstructed from what the deal already holds rather than
adding a field and starting the clock from today.

That also means it works retrospectively - deals moved months ago show their
real history immediately.

THREE THINGS IT GETS RIGHT
---------------------------
A stage entered more than once SUMS. Deals go backwards - Received to MC Secured
and back to Received - and reporting only the last visit would understate it. On
a test deal that was 25 days plus 11, shown as 36.

The Created date anchors the first stage, so the time before the first move is
counted rather than lost.

Where there is no Created date the stage before the first move is left BLANK,
not zero. Zero reads as a fact; blank reads as "we do not know", which is the
truth. The tooltip says "no dated history" for those.

deal.stageDays is still honoured if anything ever supplies it, so this cannot
fight a future import.
