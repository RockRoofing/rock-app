THE TAB
-------
New "Activities" tab alongside Pipeline / List / Companies / Contacts. One table of every
outstanding activity across the whole CRM, OLDEST DUE FIRST, with the columns you asked
for:

  Project | Activity | Due date | Person responsible | Customer company |
  Customer name | Customer email | Customer phone

Click any header to sort by it; click again to reverse. Blank values always sort to the
bottom either way, so a missing due date never heads the list.

Filters: Person responsible, and Customer company. They combine, and the count on the
right shows how many you are looking at out of the total.

Due dates are colour-coded to match the board - red and marked OVERDUE if the date has
passed, green if it is today. The overdue count is shown next to the filters.

Clicking a project name opens that deal.


WHERE THE DATA COMES FROM
--------------------------
Two sources, merged and de-duplicated:

  1. Activities imported from Pipedrive
  2. Activities added by hand inside the CRM

Email and phone are not on the activity or the deal - they are looked up from the Contacts
import, matched on the deal's contact person. So a customer will only show an email and
phone if that person exists in your People export. Anything missing shows a dash rather
than a blank cell, so you can see it is absent rather than broken.

Only OUTSTANDING activities are listed by default. There is an "Include completed" tick box
if you want the history, but the default is a to-do list, which is what a due-date-ordered
table is for. Completed rows appear greyed and struck through.

PERFORMANCE NOTE: the import now also writes a small flat list of just the outstanding
activities, so this tab does not have to read 5,000+ per-deal records to build the table.
About a thousand rows rather than 32,000.


*** EXPECT IT TO BE EMPTY UNTIL YOU RE-EXPORT ***
--------------------------------------------------
Every one of the 39,401 rows in your current activities export is marked Done, so there are
no outstanding activities to list. The tab will show:

  "No outstanding activities."

That is correct, not a fault. Pipedrive's own counters on the deals export say there are
1,023 activities to do across 357 open deals - they were filtered out of the file.

Re-export the activities with the outstanding ones included (check the Done column has a
mix, not 39,401 rows of "Done"), re-import, and they will populate this table. Remember the
import is wipe-and-replace, so export everything in one go rather than the to-dos alone.
