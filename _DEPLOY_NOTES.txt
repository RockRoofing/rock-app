THE COLUMNS
-----------
    App No. | Month | App date | Status | Gross to date | MCD | Retention |
    Ret. released | Prev. cert | This cert (net) | Payment due

The certificate, left to right, ending at what the customer owes for that application -
which is the question people actually ask of this table, and it meant opening each one to
answer it.

Deductions are shown in brackets and red, the release in green, the previous certificate
in grey. This cert stays bold: it is the answer.

Payment due comes from the application's own date, so you can see what is owed and when
without opening anything.

AND IT SCROLLS
--------------
Twelve columns needed somewhere to go. A table set to width:100% SHRINKS to fit rather
than overflowing, so the figures would have squeezed until they wrapped - the fault the
retention register had. Minimum width 1,100px inside a scrolling container, so it
overflows properly and gets a scrollbar when the window is narrow.
