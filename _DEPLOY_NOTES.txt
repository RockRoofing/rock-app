THE ANSWER TO YOUR QUESTION
---------------------------
    project forecast, NET of cost, over 12 months   1,007,553

At a 20-25% margin that implies turnover of FOUR TO FIVE MILLION:

    at 20%   5,037,765
    at 25%   4,030,212

You do not turn over that. So the netted figure is not a margin - it is sales with
most of the cost missing. That is why closing cash climbs to 736k and never comes
back down, and why it cannot be reconciled to a 100k profit.

pkg690 splits the column into Project sales / Materials out / Labour out. This
package adds a banner that says it outright when cost falls below 70% of sales:

    Cost is only 34% of project sales over the year. Roofing runs 75-85%. At 80%
    the cost on X would be Y, against Z forecast - roughly N missing. That is why
    the closing balance climbs and never comes back down.


YOUR OTHER THREE POINTS - TWO WERE REAL
-----------------------------------------
NO CIS ESTIMATED - fixed in pkg688, not in your screenshot yet. Forecast labour
now generates CIS at gross x 20%, landing the month after the labour.

NO VAT ESTIMATED - real, and now fixed. vatByMonth only ever held filed returns
and the current estimate, so every future month showed nil - on a twelve-month
view that is most of the page. Now estimates the reclaim from forecast materials
and bills at spend x 20/120, exactly as the 13-week does.

NO COMMITMENTS - real, and now fixed. This page only ever read the vehicle
commitments; the Balance Sheet items - PAYE/CIS arrears, HP capital, corporation
tax - were never included. Your 5,000 a month of PAYE arrears was not in the
twelve-month view at all.

NO RETENTION IN - you are right, that is correct behaviour. Retention with no
release date cannot be scheduled. The 13-week's amber banner lists them.
