REPLY-TO ON NOTIFICATIONS
-------------------------
"Replies go to" in the notification builder. Set it and replies reach a person instead of
the sending subdomain, which is not a mailbox.

Every notification now carries a footer saying where replies go - or, if the field is
blank, that they are not monitored. That matters more than the field itself: somebody
replying "yes, done that" currently believes they have responded.


1. LOCK THE WIP AND TELL ACCOUNTS
-----------------------------------
A banner at the top of the WIP page, in the two colours you asked for:

    WIP NOT COMPLETE   red    "Accounts have not been told these figures are final."
    WIP COMPLETE       green  "Signed off by James on 19/08/2026 at £412,880.14."

The button emails everyone with Bookkeeping access - accounts, management and admin, read
from the actual role map rather than a guessed field - with the month, the total and who
signed it off. It reports how many people were notified.

A LOCK IS A RECORD, NOT A RESTRICTION. It does not stop the commercial team editing
afterwards; a genuine correction found on the 3rd should not need an unlock ceremony. But
reopening is explicit and confirms, so a signed-off month cannot be quietly changed.


2. WIP IN BOOKKEEPING, VIEW ONLY
----------------------------------
New WIP tab, same pattern as Retention. Accounts see the figures and the sign-off status;
the margin editors, the Zero buttons and the manual adjustments are not rendered.

The accounts role can reach the embed and NOT the full page - checked both ways.


3. ZERO A PROJECT
-----------------
A "Zero" button beside the margin on each project. One click writes it down to £0 for
unclaimable costs, instead of working out the offsetting figure and fiddling the margin.

It posts ONE adjustment at margin 0, which is the important detail: the calculation grosses
every adjustment up at its own margin, and 0 passes the amount through at face value - so
-wipValue cancels exactly. A normal cost adjustment at the project margin would overshoot.

Proven: £53,846.15 WIP, zeroed, comes out at £0.00.

It shows in the adjustments list rather than being a hidden flag, so a written-down project
is visibly written down, and "Un-zero" removes it.


4. NO MORE JUMPING TO THE TOP
-------------------------------
Every edit called load(), which set a loading flag, unmounted the whole table and rebuilt
it - so you lost your place on every single change.

Edits now use a quiet refresh: same request, same data, no loading flag. React updates the
numbers that changed and leaves the scroll position alone. The month selector keeps its
spinner, because that genuinely is a new page of data.
