WHAT'S NEW (this was NOT previously built)
  - When a CUSTOMER ticks multiple drawings, an "Approve selected (N)" button now appears at

    skips any that are superseded, already approved, or assigned to a different approver.
  - Each approval still records the full digital record (name / company / role / email /
    phone / date+time) and notifies the Rock Roofing team.
  - The button only shows to customers (Rock Roofing staff never approve).
