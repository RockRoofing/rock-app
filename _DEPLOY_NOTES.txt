pkg313 - Activities tab: show WHY nothing matches, instead of guessing
=======================================================================

REPLACES pkg302 through pkg312. Deploy this one only.

CHANGED FILES
-------------
  pages/crm.js            diagnostic empty state

FIRST - THE GOOD NEWS
----------------------
"1023 outstanding activities found" means your re-export worked. The activities are in,
with the outstanding ones present, and the count matches Pipedrive's own figure exactly.
That part is done.


YOU WERE RIGHT TO PUSH BACK
----------------------------
"Re-import your Deals export" was a guess dressed up as an instruction. I did not know why
they were not matching, and telling you to redo a large import on a hunch was not good
enough. Sorry.

The join itself is sound - it matches an activity's Deal ID against the deal's id, both
compared as text, so a number/text difference cannot be the cause.

The most likely explanation is one I can now detect. pages/api/crm.js falls back to a
built-in set of 378 SAMPLE deals whenever crm:deals is missing or unreadable:

    const saved = await get(DEALS_KEY)
    if (Array.isArray(saved)) return saved
    return (SEED_DEALS || []).map(...)          // <- preview data

If that fallback is in play, the board looks populated but those are demo projects with
different ids, so nothing your activities point at can possibly match. The API now reports
which of the two you are looking at.


WHAT THE TAB NOW SHOWS
-----------------------
Instead of a guess, it shows the evidence:

  - how many deals are currently loaded
  - the first few of THEIR ids
  - the first few deal ids YOUR ACTIVITIES are attached to
  - and, if the sample data is in play, a plain warning saying so

Comparing those two lists of numbers should make the cause obvious in seconds. If the ids
look like different things, the deals and activities came from different exports. If they
look identical, it is something I have not thought of - send me that screen and I will
work from the real numbers rather than guessing again.


WORTH CHECKING WHILE YOU ARE THERE
-----------------------------------
Open the Pipeline tab and see whether the projects shown are actually yours. If you see
unfamiliar deals, the sample-data fallback is what you are looking at, and importing your
Deals export will fix both that and this.
