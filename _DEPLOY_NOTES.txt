WHAT WAS WRONG
--------------
The tracker showed each half as totalRetention / 2 - half of retention ACCRUED
on what had been applied for so far.

An application certificate releases half of retention on the FINAL ACCOUNT. The
comment in lib/applications.js has said so all along: "A release claimed at 90%
complete is still half of the whole retention pot, which is what the contract
says."

So the two disagreed on every part-complete job, and the tracker was always the
lower of the two.

  Final account 580,000 gross, 5% MCD, 3% retention, 437,000 applied for

    retention on the final account   16,530  -> each half  8,265   application
    retention on applied for         12,455  -> each half  6,227   tracker

  A 2,038 gap per half, closing only when the job is fully applied for.

NOW
---
New retentionOnFinalAccount and retentionHalf on the dashboard record, computed
as Final Account (Gross AFA less MCD) x retention %. The tracker's 1st and 2nd
Value default to retentionHalf.

Both column tooltips now say so: "half of the retention on the FINAL ACCOUNT
(Gross AFA less MCD x retention %), which is what the contract holds and what an
application certificate releases."

WHAT HAS *NOT* CHANGED
-----------------------
Retention Owed is still retention on APPLIED FOR, less anything claimed back.
That is correct and it is a different question: what the customer is holding
today, against what the contract will eventually release in two halves. The
register needs both figures and they are not the same until a job is finished.

CACHE MARKER BUMPED AGAIN
--------------------------
retentionHalfOnFinal. Without it the four-hour cache would keep serving records
with no retentionHalf field and the halves would fall back to the old figure.

EXPECT EVERY RELEASE VALUE ON A PART-COMPLETE JOB TO RISE. That is the
correction - they were understated, and by more the earlier the job is.
