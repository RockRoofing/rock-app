YOU WERE RIGHT AND IT IS ONE FAULT IN TWO PLACES
-------------------------------------------------
    appCalendarUsable = paymentDay && (valuationDay || applicationDay)

It only ever looked at the three DEFAULT DAY fields. J190 has those blank and the
monthly override table completely filled in - fifteen months of application,
valuation and payment dates - and still read as having NO CALENDAR.

Which caused both things you saw:

  the FORECAST fell back to period end plus terms, giving ONE date at the end
  instead of one per month;
  the APPLICATIONS screen told you to set days you had deliberately overridden,
  while displaying the correct dates immediately above the warning.

resolveAppDates has always preferred an override over the default day. Only the
test for whether a calendar EXISTS was wrong.

Now a calendar counts as usable when every month of the period has a payment date
and a valuation or application date in the override table:

    J190, no default days, full override table
      before  usable false -> one date at period end
      after   usable true  -> Sep val 30/09 cash 23/10
                              Oct val 31/10 cash 27/11
                              Nov val 30/11 cash 25/12

So your one-application-spread-over-several-months case now produces one
application date per month at that month's percentage, which is what you were
expecting.


AND THE WARNINGS NOW MEAN SOMETHING
-----------------------------------
Applications screen: only warns when the dates for THAT month are genuinely
unknown, rather than whenever the default days are blank.

Forecast: names the months actually missing - "No dates for 2026-11 - fill those
months in" - instead of claiming there is no calendar on a project where fourteen
of fifteen rows are complete. And it says it is using period end plus terms
meanwhile, rather than leaving you to work that out from the dates.
