pkg459 - date range filter on the Activities tab
================================================

Deploy after pkg458. One changed file: pages/crm.js. No new files.


WHAT YOU GET
------------
Two date boxes beside the search:

    Due [from] to [to]   clear

Both blank by default. Fill in either or both - from on its own is "everything due from
here", to on its own is "everything due up to here". A "clear" link appears once one is
set.


NOT REMEMBERED, AS YOU ASKED
------------------------------
The range resets on every visit and is not saved anywhere. It is session state only.

Your reasoning was right and worth writing down: a remembered date range would hide
activities on a later visit with nothing on screen to explain why, and an empty list reads
as "nothing to do" rather than "you filtered this out in June".


ONE DECISION I MADE - TELL ME IF IT IS WRONG
----------------------------------------------
An activity with NO DUE DATE is kept whatever the range.

It is not outside the range - it has no date to be outside of - and hiding it would bury
exactly the ones that need a date setting. Those are the activities most likely to be
forgotten, so filtering by date should not be what makes them disappear.

If you would rather a date range excluded undated activities, it is one line.


AND THE EMPTY MESSAGE NOW NAMES THE RIGHT FILTER
--------------------------------------------------
It used to say "Clear the person or customer filter to see the rest" whatever was actually
applied. With a date range on, that sends you looking in the wrong place.

It now mentions the date range when one is set, and also counts the search boxes - both of
which could empty the table while that message pointed at the dropdowns.
