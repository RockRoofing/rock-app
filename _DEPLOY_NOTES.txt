THE TWO GUARDS AND WHERE THEY LEAKED
------------------------------------
SALES were dropped once a period had been applied for - but that test reads
latestAppEnd from the DASHBOARD CACHE, which expires on a 4-hour TTL and whenever
a new marker ships. Stale cache meant latestAppEnd was empty, the forecast was NOT
dropped, and the real invoice sat in receivables beside it. Both counted.

COSTS were netted against real bills only within the SAME WEEK, matched on project
name. A bill paid in week 3 left a week 5 forecast untouched, so both counted.


FIX 1 - A PERIOD THAT HAS ENDED IS NOT FUTURE CASH
--------------------------------------------------
    isSpent(fc) = applied for  OR  fc.to < today

This depends on nothing external. A forecast period ending before today has either
been applied for - in which case its money is a real invoice - or it did not
happen. Either way it is not cash still to come.

  period ended 31/07, no application recorded   dropped   (was counted)
  period ended 31/07, application to 31/08      dropped
  period ends 30/09                             counted

It is the backstop that catches everything the supersede rule misses as time rolls
forward, cache or no cache. Applied to sales, to labour and materials, and to the
new CIS prediction.


FIX 2 - BILLS NET OVER THE MONTH, AND ONLY ONCE
-----------------------------------------------
A real bill and the forecast line it replaces almost never fall in the same week -
the bill pays on the supplier's terms, the forecast on its own schedule. Netting
now covers the MONTH, the smallest window that reliably contains both.

And a running tally stops one bill netting twice:

  one 10,000 bill, forecast 6,000 in wk1 and 6,000 in wk3
    wk1  allowance 10,000  netted 6,000  counted 0
    wk3  allowance  4,000  netted 4,000  counted 2,000
    bill used once in total: 10,000

Excluded bills no longer net anything either - they are not being paid, so they
cannot displace forecast spend.


WHAT IS STILL NOT COVERED
-------------------------
Bills are matched to forecasts by PROJECT NAME. Where Xero's tracking name and the
forecast's project name differ, no netting happens and both count. The names come
from the same dashboard record so they usually agree, but it is worth knowing that
is the remaining weak link rather than assuming it is airtight.
