1. "J190 - RUSSELL HILL", NOT "L:190"
-------------------------------------
projectName is blank whenever the dashboard cache has not named that job, and the
fallback was projectKey - the raw Redis key, which means nothing to read.

    J190 + Russell Hill        ->  J190 - Russell Hill
    number only, no name       ->  190
    neither                    ->  190          (key, stripped of "L:")
    name already has the no.   ->  J240 Market Drayton   (not printed twice)
    negotiated N:8335          ->  8335


2. CLICK A PROJECT FOR ITS MONTHS
---------------------------------
Every row opens to show sales in, materials out, labour out, net and cost % BY
MONTH:

    J190 - Russell Hill
       Sept 26   sales 184,148   mat      -   lab  9,327   cost  5%
       Oct 26    sales  87,500   mat 65,630   lab 11,069   cost 88%

Which is more useful than the project total, because the total averages the two.
A month at 5% has its materials missing; a month at 88% is behaving normally. On
the total those cancel out to something unremarkable.

Collected in the same pass that builds the table, so it costs nothing extra and
cannot disagree with the row above it.
