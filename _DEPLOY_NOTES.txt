Everything you asked for - supplier, project, reference, date used - plus two
things that came out of building it.

BILL VALUE vs STILL DUE. Where they differ the row turns amber and says how much
has already been paid. The column only ever showed the outstanding balance, so a
part-paid bill looked like a small one.

CIS MARKER on any bill carrying a deduction, because that bill has a second
payment behind it - the 20% leaves separately in the CIS to HMRC column on the
22nd of the following month. Seeing 4,000 without knowing another 1,000 follows
it is the sort of thing that makes a week look cheaper than it is.


"(PLANNED)" vs "(DUE DATE)"
---------------------------
Same marker as the invoices drop-down. "(due date)" means Xero's date is being
used because no planned payment date has been set - it assumes you pay exactly on
terms. "(planned)" means somebody decided. A week full of due dates is a week
built on assumptions, and you could not tell before.

The arrears row keeps its own existing breakdown; every other week gets this one.
