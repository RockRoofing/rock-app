THE VAT FAULT
-------------
13-week. The settlement date was:

    const monthEnd = isoDay(new Date(yy, mm, 0))

JS months are zero-based, so for "2026-08" that is 31 AUGUST - the end of the
VAT month itself. Every month's VAT was landing in the month it was earned, with
no lag at all. Now `new Date(yy, mm + 1, 0)`: August settles 30 September.

12-month. Same fault, different shape - it put month mk's VAT in month mk. The
row now reads the PREVIOUS month's VAT, on both the filed figure and the
estimate. What moves in September is August's VAT.

Both checked across the December/January boundary.

EFFECT: every VAT receipt and payment moves one month later on both pages. On
the 13-week the 18,193 currently sitting in w/c 31 Aug should move out to the
end of September, and the weeks after shift with it.

I have NOT added the extra 7 days. HMRC's deadline is a month and 7 days after
the period, so a PAYMENT is really due 7 October for August. You said end of
September, so that is what this does. Say the word and I will add the 7 days -
it is a one-line change, and it matters more for payments than refunds.
