WHAT'S NEW
  Selecting a period on a project row and clicking "Build application ->" now opens a
  HYPOTHETICAL APPLICATION for that project/period:
    - Full mirror of the real application: contract works seeded from the project's
      CONTRACTED RATES, with a cumulative "% complete" per line, and the certificate
      maths (MCD, retention, this-cert) via the same engine as real applications.
    - REVENUE (this-cert total) and LABOUR (from the rate labour column x % complete)
      are shown per period.
    - MATERIALS are added separately: a % of the materials budget OR a £ figure, with
      a DELIVERY DAY (materials hit cash on that single day, not spread).
    - CUMULATIVE: each new period starts from the most recent saved hypothetical
      application (carries % complete forward), so this-cert reflects only new work.
    - VIEW: the "View forecasts" button lists all saved hypothetical applications for
      the project (revenue / labour / materials / delivery day) and lets you delete.

  IMPORTANT: these are FORECAST ONLY. They are stored under a separate key
  (cashflow:hyp-apps:<projectKey>) and are NEVER written to the real applications.

RATES SOURCE
  - Negotiated projects: uses the negotiated contracted rates (Phase 2b).
  - Live/draft projects: uses the project's contracted rates from Xero. If a project
    has no rates yet, the modal says so - upload & lock them on Contracted Rates first
    (add the project in Xero so it appears there).
