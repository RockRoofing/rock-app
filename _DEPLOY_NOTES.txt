pkg420 - the month you click is the month you get
=================================================

Deploy after pkg419. One changed file: pages/scorecard-crm.js. No new files.


WHY IT LOOKED BROKEN
--------------------
The chart click was working. It was losing a race by one event.


THE FOUR REASONS THEY DISAGREED
-------------------------------
1. HIDDEN PROJECTS. The EOM report drops anything on the shared hide list. The scorecard
   counted them. Now excluded, from the same list.

2. THE CUT-OFF DATE - the big one. EOM measures to each project's own VALUATION DATE,
   with per-month manual overrides, matching the Application Calendar. The scorecard used
   everything to TODAY. Mid-month those are different amounts of work. The scorecard now
   uses the valuation date.
looked like it had not been built.

The chart container now stops the click there. Clicking a point gives that point's month;
clicking anywhere else on the card still gives the card's month, as before.


WHAT TO EXPECT
--------------
Click May on any trend line and the modal title reads "... - May 26" with May's projects
in it. Click the big number or the card background and you get the month named at the top
of the column - "Last full month: Jul 26" or the current month.

Both behaviours are wanted, they just needed separating.
