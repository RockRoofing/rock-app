NEW HERE: THE ARREARS ROW IS GONE
----------------------------------
"Overdue - brought forward" swept everything past its date into a row before
week 1 - overdue invoices, overdue bills, retention, and the inferred awaiting
figure. It is removed from the arithmetic entirely.

Anything overdue is now simply NOT in the forecast until a date is set for it. A
date nobody has committed to is not a forecast.

IT IS REPORTED, NOT VANISHED
-----------------------------
An amber bar above the table lists what has been left out: invoices, bills,
retention, awaiting - with the totals.

This matters most on the BILLS side. Dropping overdue money OUT makes the cash
line look BETTER than reality, and late money out is the most certain spend
there is. The bar says so explicitly rather than letting it quietly improve the
figures.

To bring something back in: set an expected date on the invoice, or a planned
payment date on the bill, and it lands in the week you actually expect.
