WHAT WAS MISSING
----------------
The pop-out only ever showed one pooled "Card headroom" figure. Even with that
rising correctly after a payment, it could not show that a particular card had
been paid down or cleared - there was no per-card balance anywhere on the chart.

NOW
---
The Cards block in the pop-out lists every card and what it still owes AT THAT
WEEK, after any scheduled payments:

  AMEX                    £0
  Capital on Tap    £-77,156
  paid this week    £-40,000
  Card headroom      £82,844
  Overdraft headroom      £0

Green where a card is clear, red where it still owes. The card name travels with
the payment event, so the balance and the Financing & tax line are the same
figure - they cannot disagree.

Checked against your cards:
  week 0            CoT owes 77,156   headroom  82,844
  after 40,000      CoT owes 37,156   headroom 122,844
  cleared           CoT owes      0   headroom 160,000

Balances fall and headroom rises by the same amount, which is the relationship
that was impossible to see before.

ALSO: the "Now" point at the left of the chart carries the opening balances, so
hovering it shows today's real card position rather than blanks.
