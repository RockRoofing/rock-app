2. ONE CAUSE OF DOUBLING FIXED
------------------------------
A project can exist TWICE in Redis - once keyed by tracking option id, once by job
number. Your handover flags exactly this, and names project:39002840 as a likely
duplicate of project:c59294a0. Both copies carry the same applications, so every
certificate on that project was counted twice.

Whole records are now matched on their FULL application list and the duplicate is
skipped. Deliberately not matched on a single application: two genuinely different
projects could agree on one, and merging them would be a worse error than the one
being fixed. One project stored twice always has an identical list.

I do NOT know that this accounts for your figures. See below.


3. HOW TO FIND THE REST - HOVER THE BAND
----------------------------------------
Each month band now lists every contributor on hover:

  Aug 26: 1,075,400.00 from 9 items
    2026-08-14 J240 App 4 92,766.50
    2026-08-21 J251 forecast 40,000.00
    ...

That will settle it in seconds, and it is cheaper than me guessing a third time.
What I would look for:

  THE SAME PROJECT TWICE, once as "App N" and once as "forecast". A forecast is
  only superseded when an application's VALUATION date is at or past the
  forecast's END date. A forecast that runs past the application it overlaps
  stays in - by design, since part of its period is still uncertified - but if
  your forecast periods do not line up with application months, that will double
  a lot of money.

  A DRAFT APPLICATION CLAIMING THE FULL CUMULATIVE VALUE. Marked "(draft)" in the
  breakdown. J240 Application 4 has "Previously certified (gross)" BLANK, so its
  certificate computes as the whole 92,766.50 instead of the increment - and that
  is what goes into this row. Any draft in that state inflates its month. This is
  the third time I have flagged it and it is now visible in the numbers.

  HISTORIC APPLICATIONS. Before pkg550 the chart held forecasts only, with no
  history at all. Every application ever raised now appears on its payment date,
  so Jun/Jul/Aug legitimately carry real money they never used to. Some of the
  increase is correct.

Tell me what the August tooltip shows and I will fix the actual cause.
