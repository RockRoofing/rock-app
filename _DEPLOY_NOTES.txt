WHAT WAS WRONG
--------------
I used the FORECAST RECORD as the period. J244 Waungron is one forecast running
01/09/2026 to 18/12/2026 containing FOUR applications. So the window came out as
1 Sept to 30 Nov:

  materials  71,950   three months of deliveries
  labour     48,675   pro-rated 65/79 working days across the whole record
  revenue   182,724

The window should have been 28-30 November - the days after the last
application, which is 27/11.

J240 Market Drayton applies on the 30th of each month, so there is NO unclaimed
window at all. It was showing 64,805.

WHAT CHANGED
------------
1. pages/api/business-financials.js now returns appDates - EVERY application
   boundary on a forecast, not just the last one as valDate. The intermediate
   boundaries were never exposed, which is why the code fell back to fc.from
   and fc.to.

2. The window is now: the day after the last application dated ON OR BEFORE
   30 November, through to 30 November.
   - An application ON the 30th leaves nothing unclaimed - that project is
     skipped entirely.
   - Nothing past 30 November is taken. December's cost sits in December on
     its own, as you said.
   - Labour is the application period spanning month end, pro-rated on working
     days for the part falling on or before the 30th.
