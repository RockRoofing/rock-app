THE TOGGLE - "USE PAYMENT PERFORMANCE"
--------------------------------------
In Facilities, beside the risk setting. OFF BY DEFAULT and deliberately so -
shifting every receipt by a measured average changes the entire forecast, and that
should be a decision rather than something that happens the moment data is
imported.

    off  "due dates"  every invoice assumed paid exactly on terms
    on   "measured"   each customer shifted by what they actually do

The label says which is live, so a forecast can never quietly be one or the other.


THE TABLE - EVERYONE WHO OWES YOU MONEY
---------------------------------------
  Customer   Invoices  Retention  Forecast   Total   Measured  Spread  Days used
  Wates       216,997     24,000   120,000  360,997      31d     19d   45d (typed)
  Torsion      38,125          -    87,500  125,625      12d     60d          12d
  Selsdon      11,998      5,000         -   16,998  no data       -           0d

Invoices, retention AND forecast sales in one place, so you see a customer's whole
exposure rather than three separate views of it.

BLANK USES THE MEASURED FIGURE, typed overrides it. Shared with the Payment Perf
tab - change it in either place, it is one store.

SPREAD IS THERE FOR A REASON. Torsion medians 12 days and swings 60. Applying that
12 will be wrong most months; the column tells you when the average is not worth
trusting.


TWO THINGS THAT HAD TO BE FIXED TO MAKE THE TABLE WORK
------------------------------------------------------
RETENTION carried the PROJECT name, not the customer, so those rows would never
have grouped with that customer's invoices. It now carries both.

FORECAST SALES carried no customer at all. Now taken from the dashboard record, so
a project's future billing sits with the rest of that customer's money.


AN EXPECTED DATE SET BY HAND ALWAYS WINS over both the measured figure and a typed
override. That is a judgement somebody has made about a specific invoice, and a
customer average should not overrule it.
