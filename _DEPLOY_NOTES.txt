NO, THEY DO NOT MATCH - AND NOT FOR A REASON WORTH CHASING
-----------------------------------------------------------
The app export was not filtered. exportCsv iterated `billsOnly`, the UNFILTERED
set, so every filter on the page was ignored - cost accounts, suppliers, dates,
the month click. Your export has all 117 bills, 4 overpayments and 1 credit,
totalling 302,771.49, which is the whole ledger.

Straight against your standing rule that an export reflects what is filtered on
screen. It has presumably always done this; the account filter is just the first
one where it was obvious.


FIXED
-----
  - exports `filtered`, not `billsOnly`
  - the TOTAL footer sums the SAME set. It summed billsOnly while listing
    filtered, so a filtered export would have carried a footer contradicting its
    own rows
  - new "Account codes" column, so a filtered export can be checked against Xero
    without taking the filter on trust

ONE THING THAT IS NOT A FAULT
-----------------------------
Your Xero export also contains codes 401, 412, 425, 433, 449, 463, 464, 800, 821.
That is not Xero ignoring your filter - it is the same whole-bill behaviour the
app uses. A bill with ANY line on a picked account comes through IN FULL,
including its lines on other accounts. Splitting bills would make the totals
wrong.

So a filtered list is "bills that touch these accounts", not "only spend on these
accounts". If you need the latter - spend BY account rather than bills - that is
a different view and worth building separately rather than bending this one.
