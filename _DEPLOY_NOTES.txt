pkg444 - change an application's dates and period without starting again
========================================================================

Deploy after pkg443. One changed file: pages/applications.js. No new files.


THE PROBLEM
-----------
The month was fixed when the application was created, and the four dates were read-only
cards. Get to the end of a full application, notice it is on the wrong month, and the only
way out was to delete it and do the whole thing again.


1. THE DATES ARE EDITABLE
-------------------------
"Edit dates" beside the Dates heading turns the four cards into date fields:

    Application date · Valuation date · Payment due · Final date for payment

Change what is wrong, press Done, then Save as usual. Nothing else about the application
is touched.


2. THE PERIOD IS CHANGEABLE
---------------------------
The month beside the application number is now a dropdown. Pick the right one and the
application moves - keeping every line, every variation, every value you have entered.

Choosing a period pulls that period's dates from Project Details where they are set. Where
they are not, whatever is already on the application is KEPT rather than blanked - so a
date you typed by hand is not wiped by correcting the month.

The list covers 6 months back and 12 ahead, plus any period Project Details holds dates
for - including the "#2" rows a fortnightly project uses from pkg443 - plus whatever the
application is currently on, so its own value can never be missing from its own dropdown.


BOTH RESPECT THE SENT LOCK
--------------------------
A sent application is locked, as before. Double-click to unlock and the dates and period
become editable with everything else. Editing a sent application still returns it to
draft, keeping its application number - unchanged behaviour.


ONE THING TO KNOW
-----------------
Changing the period does NOT renumber the application. If you move App 14 from August to
September and App 15 already exists in September, you will have two applications numbered
out of order.

That is deliberate - the number is permanent once assigned, and renumbering silently would
be worse. The number is editable beside the title if it needs correcting.
