pkg525 - find out where V01 and V02 actually are
================================================

Deploy after pkg524. One changed file: pages/api/variation-debug.js. No new files.

This does not fix anything. It tells us what is stored, because I have now been wrong
twice about where these live and a third guess is not worth your time.


RUN THIS
--------
Console on any portal page:

    fetch('/api/variation-debug?all=1').then(r=>r.json()).then(d=>console.log(JSON.stringify(d,null,2)))

It scans EVERY project:* record in storage and lists each one that holds variations, with
its raw key:

    {
      "redisKey": "project:12345-abcd-...",
      "count": 1,
      "numbers": ["V03"],
      "descriptions": ["Additional VCL to plant deck"]
    },
    {
      "redisKey": "project:J205",
      "count": 2,
      "numbers": ["V01", "V02"],
      ...
    }

It writes nothing.


WHAT THE ANSWER TELLS US
------------------------
V01 AND V02 APPEAR UNDER A DIFFERENT KEY
    They exist. The dashboard is reading the wrong record and I can point it at the right
    one - a small change once I can see the actual key.

THEY APPEAR IN THE SAME RECORD AS V03
    They exist and something downstream is filtering them out - the tracker only shows
    live in-progress projects, so a variation on a project that is not INPROGRESS in Xero
    would be hidden. Different fix entirely.

THEY DO NOT APPEAR AT ALL
    They were overwritten before pkg522's guard went in, and they need re-entering. I
    would rather tell you that plainly than keep shipping fixes for a problem that is not
    the one you have.

Paste me whatever it prints and the next package will be the right one.
