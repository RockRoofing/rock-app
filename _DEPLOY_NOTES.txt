THE PHOTOS
===========
A Photos panel in the Variation Builder, under Clarifications. Add photos, see them
as thumbnails, click one to open it full size, remove any before saving.

On a phone the picker opens the camera directly, and it takes several at once.

They appear on the variation PDF after the priced work, each with its filename as a
caption - and because Download, Send to customer and the instruction email all build
that same PDF through buildVariationPDF, they carry the photos with no change to any
of those paths.


WHERE THEY ARE STORED, AND WHY IT MATTERS
-------------------------------------------
On the builder block, as variation.builder.photos.

NOT on variation.attachments, which already exists and looked like the obvious home.
applicationPdf.js uses that array for something else:

    const has = Array.isArray(v.attachments) && v.attachments.length > 0
    if (has || !isInstructed(v) || !v.builder) return v     // ... else generate the
                                                            // variation PDF into it

A variation that already has attachments does NOT get its variation PDF generated
for the application. Putting photos there would have quietly dropped the variation
document from every application it appeared on, and the photos would have replaced
it. They live on the builder block instead, and reach the application anyway inside
the variation PDF.


UPLOADS
--------
Straight from the browser to Blob storage via @vercel/blob/client and
/api/blob-upload, which is the route every other upload in the portal takes. Not
through our own API: serverless request bodies cap at about 4.5MB and two phone
photos clear that on their own.

Compressed first, with the existing lib/compressImage - a phone photo is 3-6MB and
nothing in a variation PDF needs that. It returns the original untouched for
anything that is not a raster image, or if compression fails, so an upload never
breaks because of it.

A photo that fails to upload is named in the error rather than the batch reporting
"upload failed", which tells you nothing about which one to try again.

An instructed variation is locked, so photos cannot be added or removed once the
customer has instructed it - same rule as the rest of the builder.


VERIFIED
---------
Built the PDF end to end with two real photos - one landscape, one portrait - and
one deliberately broken URL, then rendered it to images and looked at it.

  landscape photo   scaled to the page width, caption above it
  portrait photo    too tall for the space left, moved to a fresh page rather than
                    being squashed or running off the bottom
  broken URL        prints "[broken.jpg could not be loaded]" and carries on

That last one is deliberate. A photo that will not load must not take the document
down with it, and it must not vanish silently either - a variation quietly missing
its evidence, with nothing to say any was meant to be there, is worse than a line
admitting it.
