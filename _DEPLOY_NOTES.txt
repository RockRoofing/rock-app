1. NUMBER FIX (revenue double-count)
   Materials were being ADDED to revenue as well as the contract works that already
   contain them - so revenue/gross were overstated (e.g. showing ~465k). Materials are
   now CASH-TIMING ONLY and do NOT add to revenue. Revenue = contract works this-cert.
   Re-open existing forecasts and Update to recalculate them.

2. MONTHLY SPREAD (sales + labour)
   When a period spans more than one CALENDAR month, a "Spread across the period's N
   calendar months" table appears. Enter what % of SALES and LABOUR falls in each
   month (defaults to an even split; each row should total 100%). The number of months
   is taken from the calendar months the period actually touches - so a period that
   runs 1 Feb -> 10 May offers Feb, Mar, Apr, May (4 months) even if it's ~3 months
   long. The payment term then sets the cash date from each month end (e.g. 40% in
   April, sales term EOM+35 -> that 40% lands end of April + 35 days).

3. TOTALS ROWS - now split by stream, each per column (day OR week view):
   Sales in, Retention in, Labour out, Materials out. They stick to the top when you
   scroll, and the Project + date columns stay frozen on the left.
