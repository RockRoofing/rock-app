1. "activity is not defined"
-----------------------------
My mistake in pkg344, and the error screen named it exactly.

I added the "ACTIVITY - James McVeigh" heading so you could see who owns an activity
without opening it. It was meant to go on an existing activity row. It landed in the
NEW-activity box instead - a different component, where there is no activity to read a name
from. So the page threw the moment a deal was opened.

Both places have an identical "Activity" heading, which is how I put it in the wrong one.

FIXED: the heading with the person on it is now on the activity ROW, where it belongs, and
the new-activity box has its plain heading back. Checked that every reference to that
activity now sits inside the component that has one.

Also corrected while there: an existing row said "Assigned to current user" when nothing
was assigned. It now says "nobody", which is what it means.


2. ASSIGNEE LIST LIMITED TO PRE-CONTRACT AND ADMIN
----------------------------------------------------
Only portal users with a pre-contract or admin role can be given a CRM activity. Estimators,
sales and admin - the people who actually work in here.

Post-contract, management and legacy "standard" users are excluded. Verified against a
sample of all five roles.

This applies everywhere you pick a person: adding an activity on a deal, editing one there,
and the Person Responsible cell in the Activities table.

WORTH KNOWING: this filters who you can CHOOSE. Historic activities already assigned to
somebody outside that group still show their name, so nothing is rewritten or hidden - they
simply cannot be picked for anything new. That is deliberate; silently reassigning
somebody's past work would be worse.
