YOUR QUESTION: YES, BOTH ARE INCLUDED
=======================================
assembleWeek folds overheads and water ingress into each person's day cells, and
the PDF prints every entry in a cell, so they come out alongside the project work.
Built one to look at rather than reading the code and assuming:

  Alan Clark   Farmstead Drive | Holidays | WI: Leak - Royal Tea | Ops Support |
               Winnersh (AM)

  Overheads       by category - Holidays, Sick, Internal Time, Ops Support,
                  Paid Leave, Unpaid Leave
  Water ingress   by job name
  Half days       marked (AM) / (PM)
  Provisional     in blue, confirmed in green
  Unnamed slots   a "TBC - <project>" row reading "2 unnamed"

ONE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN THE TWO VERSIONS, which is deliberate and worth knowing: the
ACCOUNTS version drops the "TBC - unnamed" rows and adds the O/A markers and an
overnight allowance total. Nobody is paid for an unnamed slot, so it has no place on
a sheet going to accounts. The general Weekly Labour Allocation keeps them, because
a gap in next week's labour is the thing you want to see.


AND A DEFECT THE CHECK TURNED UP
==================================
Water ingress was there, but you could not tell it apart from a project.

assembleWeek labels a call-out with a droplet - "<droplet> Leak - Royal Tea" - and
the PDF sanitiser strips anything above Latin-1 so pdf-lib cannot throw. The droplet
went with it, and a leak visit printed as a bare job name, identical in appearance
to a planned project. On screen the planner marks it clearly; on the sheet that goes
to accounts and out to operatives, it did not.

It now reads "WI: Leak - Royal Tea". Mapped before the catch-all strip, the same way
the sanitiser already handles smart quotes and bullets.

This affects both labour PDFs. The timesheet CSV from pkg824 was never affected - it
reads the water-ingress store directly and puts those rows under their own job
number.
