AND MY GUARD WOULD NOT HAVE STOPPED IT
----------------------------------------
pkg522 refused a POST that cut the count by MORE than one. Your case was two variations
down to one - a reduction of exactly one, which it allowed.

    2 -> 1    allowed        <- the case that lost your work
    3 -> 1    refused

The guard now checks WHICH ROWS SURVIVE, not how many:

    builder posts only V03, tracker holds V01+V02    REFUSED, names V01 and V02
    adds V03, keeps V01+V02                          allowed
    deletes V02 deliberately                         allowed
    drops two at once                                REFUSED

A genuine delete removes one row and keeps the rest. Anything dropping a variation the
caller was not deleting is a caller reading the wrong list, and now fails loudly.
