DO NOT BACKFILL YET. THE DRY RUN FOUND A REAL BUG.
==================================================

Your detail run did exactly the job it was meant to. Look at what it filed:

  "[SPAM] One fastener item to fit-check?"                 -> FILED to West One, 74%
  "TMHCC Portal - One-time verification code"              -> FILED to West One, 74%
  "[SPAM] The NHS no longer has one front door"            -> FILED to West One, 74%
  "Arbosil ONE: The ONE Silicone for Professional Sealing" -> FILED to West One, 74%
  "[SPAM] FW: One less thing to chase"                     -> FILED to West One, 74%
  "[SPAM] Quick follow-up - this one's worth GBP1,200,000" -> FILED to West One, 74%

Around a dozen of those in six weeks, all to the same project, all pure junk.


WHY
---
Rarity was measured across YOUR PROJECT TITLES ONLY. The word "one" appears in exactly one
title - West One - so the code treated it as a highly distinctive word, on a par with
"Ellington" or "Knowsley". It is not. It is one of the commonest words in English.

The same fault, visible in your data:
    "job in York"      claimed  "Where does a job go?", "The #1 mistake most job ads make"
    "Vision Labs"      claimed  "...Your Vision, Perfectly Crafted"
    "Temple Reach"     claimed  "Brand Reach"
    "5 Bridge Street"  claimed  "5-6 new leads!", "58.5% of your Google visitors"
    "Health Building"  claimed  every email with the word "building" in it

A word being rare in 6,000 project titles says nothing about whether it is rare in English.
That was the flaw, and only real mail would have exposed it.


WHAT IS FIXED
-------------
1. Common English and trade words carry almost no weight now, however rare they are in your
   titles: one, new, job, work, quote, tender, building, roof, cladding, project, vision,
   reach, and about 130 more.

2. A match must rest on at least TWO distinctive words - or one long word belonging to
   exactly one project ("ellington", "knowsley", "brookhill"). "one" can never carry a
   match on its own. Neither can "5".

3. Junk is filtered before matching: [SPAM], Undeliverable:, Automatic reply:, Accepted:,
   Declined:, out of office, postmaster bounces. Reported separately as "junk".
