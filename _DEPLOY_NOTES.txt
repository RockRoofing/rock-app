1. INVOICES IN 100,885 AGAINST 555k OUTSTANDING
-----------------------------------------------
    const invoicesIn = receivables.filter(i => inWk(i.expectedDate || i.dueDate))

inWk() requires the date to fall INSIDE the 13 weeks. Anything ALREADY PAST DUE
sits before week 1 and was dropped ENTIRELY - so the money you are most likely to
collect was the money the forecast ignored.

Overdue does not mean never paid, it means late. It now lands in WEEK 1, which is
also the honest place for it: if it does not arrive, you see the hole immediately.

Same rule applied to retention releases.


2. RETENTION IN WAS BLANK - THE SAME DEAD FIELD, THIRD TIME
-----------------------------------------------------------
    if (r1 && !e.release1Received && e.release1Date) out.push(...)

release1Received is NEVER SET by anything. Left over from the tracker's old design
where the released state was inferred at render time and never stored. And it
required a release DATE, silently dropping every half without one - which is most
of the register.

Now uses the same rule as the Retention Tracker and Retentions Due: manual mark
wins either way, otherwise a half ticked on an application. Complete projects are
excluded.


3. NOTHING IS SILENTLY MISSING ANY MORE
---------------------------------------
An amber banner above the table lists what could NOT be scheduled:

    Not in the forecast - no date to schedule it against.
    GBP x of retention across N releases - set the release dates on the Retention
    Tracker. GBP y of invoices across M - no due date in Xero.

Money with no date cannot go in a week. Being absent SILENTLY is how 555k showed
as 100,885.


4. FORWARD VAT RECLAIM
----------------------
vatByMonth only held FILED returns and the current estimate - nothing for months
still to come, so one reclaim showed and then nothing.

As a subcontractor most sales fall under the domestic reverse charge and carry no
output VAT, while materials and overheads carry input VAT - which is why the
position is a persistent RECLAIM. Now estimated for any month with no filed
return, from forecast bills and materials:

    reclaim = spend x 20/120        (spend is VAT-inclusive)
    50,000 -> 8,333.33      120,000 -> 20,000.00

A filed return or an existing estimate ALWAYS wins - this only fills the gaps. The
rate is editable beside the other facility settings; set it to 0 to switch the
estimate off.

WORTH CONFIRMING: this assumes reverse charge on sales, so no output VAT is netted
off. If some of your sales do carry VAT, the reclaim will be overstated.
