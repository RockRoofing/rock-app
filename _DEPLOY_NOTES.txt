WHAT IT DOES
=============
A Comments column on the main cost table in the Costs tab, on the grouped invoice
rows. Each row carries a pill: a count where comments exist, "Add" where they do
not. Clicking opens the thread for that invoice.

In the composer, typing @ brings up a list of people and completes the name. Anyone
tagged gets an email with the comment and a link straight back to the Costs tab of
that project.

The point being to say "this is on the wrong job" against the specific invoice, and
put it in front of whoever can move it.


DECISIONS WORTH KNOWING
------------------------
WHO CAN BE TAGGED. Post-contract, management and admin. Pre-contract has no access
to the Costs tab at all, so offering those names would only produce emails about a
page the recipient cannot open.

THE AUTHOR IS THE SESSION, NEVER THE CLIENT. A comment saying who flagged a
mis-coded cost is worth nothing if the name can be typed by somebody else.

EMAIL ADDRESSES STAY SERVER-SIDE. The tagging dropdown gets names only. It has no
need of addresses, and a dropdown is not a reason to publish a staff email list to
the browser.

ONE SEND PATH. lib/crmMentions.js already resolved @names against real portal users
and sent through Resend for the CRM, including the rule that two people sharing a
first name makes "@James" ambiguous rather than a guess. Rather than write a second
copy, I generalised it: getMentionableUsersForRoles(roles) and notifyMentions(...).
sendMentionEmails() now calls notifyMentions() too, so CRM behaviour is unchanged
and there is one place a mention email is sent.

FAILED SENDS ARE REPORTED, NOT SWALLOWED. If the comment saves but the email does
not, the composer says so and gives Resend's reason. "I tagged them and nothing
happened" should be answerable without reading a log. The comment is never lost to
a failed notification - it is saved first.

STORAGE. One Redis key per project, ops:cost-comments:{projectId}, holding a map of
invoice key -> comments. The Costs tab reads them all in one request; a key per line
would be dozens of round trips for one page.

Each comment also stores the supplier, reference and amount as they were at the
time, so a thread still reads sensibly if the invoice later falls outside the date
filter or is recoded and the row moves.

DELETING. Your own comments, or anything if you are admin.

THE COMMENT BUTTON DOES NOT EXPAND THE ROW. Clicking a grouped row toggles its line
breakdown; the comment cell stops the event so opening a thread does not also expand
the invoice.

The modal closes on the x and Escape only, never a backdrop click - a half-typed
comment should not vanish because you clicked past the edge.


WHAT I HAVE NOT DONE
---------------------
No notification anywhere except email - no badge in the portal, no digest. If you
want an unread marker or a daily summary of open cost queries, that is a separate
piece and worth scoping properly.

No status on a comment. If these are going to be worked through as a list -
"flagged / moved / not a problem" - they want a resolved flag and somewhere central
to see all open ones across projects. Say the word and I will scope it.


ALSO IN THIS PACKAGE (pkg806)
------------------------------
