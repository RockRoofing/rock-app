pkg748 - Credit card clearance on the 13-week
==============================================

DEPLOY OVER pkg747 (contains 742-747). Four files.

REPLACE:
  pages/api/business-financials.js
  pages/business-financials/cashflow.js
  pages/business-financials/monthly.js
  pages/business-financials/invoice-finance.js

WHAT
----
A "Credit card clearance" section directly below Balances on the 13-week. Each
card shows what it owes, what is scheduled, and lets you add payments - but with
a DATE rather than a month, because a week-by-week forecast needs the day.

ONE STORE, BOTH PAGES
---------------------
It shares config:card-payments with the 12-month. Schedule in either and both
follow. A row carries an optional exact date; the month is derived from it.

  entered on the 13-week   date 2026-09-18  -> 12-month puts it in 2026-09
  entered on the 12-month  month 2026-10    -> 13-week puts it on 2026-10-31
                                               (month end: no day given, and
                                                that is when a card statement is
                                                normally settled)

Two separate schedules for the same payments would drift the first time either
was edited - which is the fault that has cost the most time on this system.

WHERE IT SHOWS
--------------
In FINANCING & TAX, alongside vehicle finance and the balance sheet items. Card
repayment is financing, and a new column would have meant touching the header,
body, totals and drill-down colSpan again for no gain. The drill-down names it
"<card> - card payment".

THE RULE, same as the 12-month
-------------------------------
Only the OPENING balance is scheduled. Your ongoing card spend is already in
Overheads and Bills; treating a payment as a general outflow would take the same
cost out of the bank twice.

Payments are capped at what is left on that card, so a balance cannot go through
zero and invent cash. Enter more than is owed and the panel says so.

Cards are excluded from opening cash, so a payment is a real outflow - bank
down, card debt down, net position unchanged.

WHAT TO CHECK
-------------
  - Schedule a payment on AMEX or Capital on Tap and it appears in Financing &
    tax in that week, with the drill-down naming the card.
  - The same payment shows in the Cards column on the 12-month.
  - Enter more than the balance: the panel flags it and the forecast uses the
    capped figure.
