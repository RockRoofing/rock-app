WHAT WENT WRONG - pkg599, MY CHANGE
-----------------------------------
I moved the debt basis from unpaid INVOICES back to unpaid APPLICATIONS, because
Bibby's disapproval export says "Document Type: Application" on every row.

That was the wrong conclusion from a correct observation, and this in the API is
why:

    autoPaid = matchInv ? (amountDue <= 0.005) : null   // null = unmatched
    paid     = override != null ? !!override : (autoPaid === true)

`null === true` is FALSE. So every application that cannot be matched to an
invoice by "App N" in the reference counts as UNPAID. On any project whose
invoice references do not carry that, the WHOLE APPLICATION HISTORY is summed as
outstanding debt.

A project with twelve applications and one final invoice:

    application basis    360,000    the entire history
    invoice basis         34,000    what is actually owed

Across your ledger that is 1,375,463 against a real figure near 421,033.

An invoice cannot fail this way - it carries a real amountDue.


THE BASIS IS THE INVOICE AGAIN
------------------------------
Which is what you said in the first place: they fund applications, and need the
application to demonstrate what the invoice consists of. So:

    DEBT        the unpaid sales invoice
    EVIDENCE    the application it points at - materials on site, variations,
                gross to date, for the eligibility caps

The invoice basis reconciled to within 2,249 of Bibby's own Sales Ledger. The
application basis was out by a factor of three. I should have weighed that
against one line of their export and did not.

AGE DISAPPROVAL NOW MEASURES THE INVOICE too, not the application - the invoice
carries the real due date and the real outstanding amount, and it is the invoice
that is or is not paid.
