WHAT CHANGED
When adding staff to Overheads, you can now apply the same person + category to MULTIPLE days at
once. In the Overheads panel's "Add staff to overheads" box there is now a row of day chips for
the visible week:
  - The clicked day is selected by default.
  - Tap day chips to toggle which days to apply to.
  - "All days" / "Just this day" quick buttons.
  - The Add button reads "Add to N days" and saves the person+category to every selected day.
If the person is already on overheads on some of those days, those days are skipped and noted;
the rest still save.

Editing category / removing still works per day on the day you're viewing (as before).
