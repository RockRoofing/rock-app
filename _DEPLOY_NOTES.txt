pkg563 - Business Financials / Budgets: Switch to Actual button
================================================================

Independent of the cash flow packages. Deploy alongside or after pkg562.


NEW FILES
---------
  None.


CHANGED FILES (replace)
-----------------------
  pages/business-financials/budgets.js
  pages/api/business-financials.js


WHAT WAS WRONG
--------------
  const isComplete = (mo) => mo < thisMonth && availableSet.has(mo)

The moment a month rolled over, the considered forecast was replaced by whatever
had been posted to Xero so far. On the 1st that is usually almost nothing, so the
figure collapsed and then crept back up over the following weeks as bills came in.
No warning, and nothing on screen to say the month was only half posted.


WHAT IT DOES NOW
----------------
A month uses actuals only when you say so.

Once a month has ENDED, a "Switch to Actual" button appears under its column
heading and STAYS there until pressed - so an un-switched month is visible rather
than quietly still on forecast.

Press it and the header turns green and reads "OK Actual" with a tick. Press it
again to go back to the forecast. Nothing here is one-way.

The button is disabled with an explanation if there are no Xero figures for that
month yet, rather than letting you switch to nothing.

Saved to config:overhead-actual-months, so it holds across sessions and users.


THIS ALSO FIXES THE FORECASTS
-----------------------------
The forecast methods - Avg this FY, Last 3 mo, Last full month, Avg last
financial year - all derive from "completed" months. They were therefore
averaging in a month that was one day old and 5% posted. They now only use months
you have switched, so a forecast is never built on a half-posted ledger.


ON FIRST LOAD NOTHING MOVES
---------------------------
The config key has never been written, so on the first load every past month with
data is treated as already switched - exactly the old behaviour. You then
un-switch anything you want back on forecast.

Without that, every method that averages history would have found no completed
months and the whole grid would have read zero on deploy.

`null` (never set) is deliberately distinguished from `[]` (everything switched
off on purpose). An `|| []` would have collapsed the two and re-seeded the
defaults every time you switched the last month off.


NOT CHANGED - WORTH KNOWING
---------------------------
The two summary cards at the top - "last 3 completed months" spend and average -
still read straight off Xero for any past month, switched or not. They report
actual spend rather than replacing a forecast, so I have left them. But the same
half-posted-month problem applies to that average. Say the word and they will
follow the switch too.
