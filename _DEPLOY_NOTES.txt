WHAT'S NEW

  picks a PRIORITY: Low / Medium / High (defaults to Medium). Buttons are colour-coded
  green / amber / red.

  On the Site App Improvement log (Admin -> Site App Improvements), each entry now shows
  a coloured priority flag next to its status:
    green  = Low
    amber  = Medium
    red    = High

  Existing reports with no priority saved show as Medium (amber).
