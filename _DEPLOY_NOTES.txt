THE 841,401
-----------
    12-month, first three months
      Sept 26   invoices in  451,368
      Oct 26                  95,794
      Nov 26                       0

547,162 of a 555,310 ledger collected in three months - 81% of everything you are
owed, in ONE month.

WHY: 15 invoices worth 355,547 have no expected date, so they sit on Xero's due
date. For an overdue invoice that date is in the PAST, and everything in the past
falls into month one.

The balance sheet never does this - it spreads collection over DEBTOR DAYS. Two
pages guessing differently about the same invoices.

AND YOU WERE RIGHT THAT IT SHOULD CORRECT ITSELF LATER - it would, if this were a
timing difference. It is not. The money is collected EARLY, not twice, so the gap
opens in month one and persists to the end. That is exactly why it never came back
down, and why your instinct that something was structurally wrong was correct.


THE FIX
-------
Where you have SET a date, it is used as-is - nothing changes. Where you have not,
an overdue invoice is spread across the debtor-day window rather than assumed to
arrive at once:

    355,547 over 2 months -> Sept 177,774, Oct 177,774
    September invoices in falls from 451,368 to about 273,595

It now reads the debtor-day setting from the SAME config the Forecast Balance
Sheet uses, so the two pages cannot drift apart on this again.
