WHAT THIS DOES
- The existing "Import" button now accepts THREE Pipedrive exports and detects which one you
  uploaded automatically:
    * Deals export        -> replaces the deals (as before)
    * Organizations export -> fills the COMPANIES tab (address, phone, website, email, reg no,
                              company type, deal counts)
    * People export        -> fills the CONTACTS tab (company, work email, phone, job role,
                              deal counts)
- Each import is wipe & replace for THAT set only, matched by Pipedrive ID (no duplicates on
  re-import). Upload .xlsx OR .csv - either works.
- The Companies and Contacts tabs now read from the IMPORTED companies/contacts (rich detail),
  with live deal counts computed from the CRM's own deals layered on top. Before you import
  Organizations/People, those tabs fall back to being derived from the deals (as before), so
  nothing looks empty.

TESTED against the real exports: 3,542 companies and 4,715 contacts mapped, 0 skipped.
