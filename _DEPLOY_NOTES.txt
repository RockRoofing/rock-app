THIS IS THE ONE. NOT MINE, AND IT HAS BEEN THERE ALL ALONG
------------------------------------------------------------
An activity's due date is stored under one of TWO field names, depending on where it came
from:

    imported from Pipedrive   ->  dueDate
    created in the CRM        ->  due

The deal sends its own objects straight through when it saves, and those use `due`.

The Activities list reads  dueDate || due  - finds it either way.
The deal view read         dueDate         - and nothing else.

So a CRM-created activity lost its date the instant the deal was reopened. The list showed
19 Aug; the deal said "No due date". Same record, two screens, two answers - exactly your
two screenshots.

    CRM-created   deal shows ""            list shows "2026-08-20"
    imported      deal shows "2026-08-19"  list shows "2026-08-19"

That is the whole thing. Every symptom this evening - dates not sticking, the deal
disagreeing with the list, editing twice to make it hold - comes back to this line.


FIXED IN BOTH DIRECTIONS
------------------------
The deal now reads  dueDate || due, like every other reader.
The save now WRITES BOTH names, so no reader can miss it and none can pick up a stale one.


REPAIR THE EXISTING ONES - ONE CLICK
--------------------------------------
Activities already saved carry only one name. Go to /crm-scores and press
"Repair activity due dates".

It gives every activity the same date under both names, touches nothing already correct,
and is safe to run twice. It reports how many it fixed and across how many projects.

Do this straight after deploying - until you do, CRM-created activities will still show
blank on the deal.
