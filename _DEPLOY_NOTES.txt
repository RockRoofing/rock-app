THE FAULT, ON YOUR OWN NUMBERS
------------------------------
The prior certificate's gross was computed from measured work and variations only.
Materials on site was left out. J240, Application 4:

  Forecast "Gross to date"                27,076.22
  less V07 at 12.60%                         674.41
  = measured work to date                 26,401.81   matches the application
  plus materials on site                  65,690.28   MISSING
  = Application 4 gross                   92,766.50

Revenue read 0.00 and that part was right - nothing had moved. The error was
hidden and would have surfaced as the job progressed: the forecast would predict
274,138.21 - 27,076.22 = 247,061.99 still to come in, when the customer actually
owes 274,138.21 - 92,766.50 = 181,371.71. Over-forecast by 65,690.28 - exactly
the materials on site.

The reason is real. On an application, materials on site is an ADVANCE claim that
unwinds as the work gets measured. The forecast had no such figure to unwind, so
that money got forecast a second time as measured work grew.


THE FIX
-------
A new "Materials on site (claimed)" box alongside Revenue and Labour. Cumulative,
carried in from the last certificate, and part of gross on BOTH sides - so it
changes nothing today and stops the double count later.

AUTO WIND-DOWN. It falls by exactly the materials the measured work consumed this
period (matRate x qty on the works, plus the materials element of any included
variation). Stock bought for a roof stops being "on site" the moment that roof is
valued as measured work.

MANUAL OVERRIDE, same pattern as the labour box: click Override, type, Reset to
go back. The calculated figure stays visible underneath and the box takes a
purple border. Use it when a delivery lands that you want to claim before it is
fixed - that pushes the figure UP, which no rule can predict.

Worked through on J240, measuring 30,000 more work of which 12,000 came out of
stock:

  Materials on site   65,690.28 -> 53,690.28
  Gross               92,766.50 -> 110,766.50
  Revenue this period    17,100.00   (18,000 increment less 5% retention)

Note the increment is 18,000, not 30,000. The 12,000 of stock was already paid
for by the customer. That is the whole point.

It must reach zero by final account, because by then everything is measured.


KNOCK-ON: "FILL FROM LINE ITEMS" NOW BUYS ONLY WHAT IS NOT ALREADY THERE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Materials drawn out of stock already on site were paid for in an earlier period.
Charging them to suppliers again would double the cash out. The button now offers

  materials consumed  -  drawn out of stock

so in the example above it offers 0.00 and says why. Add stock manually and the
figure goes the other way - the new stock gets bought on top.


RENAME
------
The old "Materials on site" section - supplier payments going OUT, each with a
delivery day and payment term - is now "Materials forecasted on site". "Materials
on site" now means the claim, as it does on the application. Two different things
were sharing one name on the same screen.


REMAINING FIGURES NOW INCLUDE INSTRUCTED VARIATIONS
---------------------------------------------------
Revenue, Labour and Materials "remaining" measured against rates-only budgets
while gross to date counted variations, so they read low by the whole variation
account. They now add the INSTRUCTED variations' value, labour and materials
elements.

Uninstructed ones are excluded even when ticked into a forecast - not agreed
money, must not inflate a budget. Consequence: tick one in and "remaining"
shrinks by its value without the budget rising. Tell me if you would rather it
followed the ticks.

The materials % -of-budget lines still use the rates-only budget deliberately -
moving that would silently revalue every saved forecast.


ALSO IN THIS PACKAGE (pkg542-545)
---------------------------------
- Seeds from the latest real application, else contracted rates at 0%.
- Already-claimed value deducted, so the job to date is not re-spread.
- New forecasts deduct the latest certificate whatever period you pick; editing
  keeps the chain rule.
- Variations table: instructed in by default, uninstructed listed and badged.
  Value drives revenue, labour element drives labour, materials element drives
  materials - same as the contracted scope.
- "Revert to Application N" - resets works, variations, MCD, retention, and both
  overrides.
- Labour override.
- Backdrop click no longer closes the modal; Escape does.


TWO THINGS FOR YOU
------------------
1. Application 4 on J240 has "Previously certified (gross)" BLANK. Until you put
   92,766.50 in, that draft claims the whole 92,766.50 again - and the forecast
   reads that application.

2. variationValueToDate() in lib/applications.js tests `v.instructed === false`,
   but instructed is the STRING 'yes'/'no' - so an uninstructed variation is
   counted IN FULL in the applications maths. Confirmed. The cash flow cannot hit
   it. Still needs its own package.
