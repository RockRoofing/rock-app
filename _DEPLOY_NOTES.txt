WHERE THE BUSINESS FINANCIALS CASH FLOW PULLS FROM
--------------------------------------------------
  Invoices in        unpaid Xero sales invoices, by expected date or due date
  Retention in       the Retention Tracker's 1st/2nd release dates and values
  VAT in / out       filed returns, else the estimate, else a forward reclaim
  Bills out          unpaid Xero bills, by planned payment date or due date
  Overheads out      Budgets page x the Cash Schedule
  Financing & tax    commitments + the new Balance Sheet tab
  Project sales /
  Materials / Labour  the saved project forecasts - salesSchedule, matItems,
                      labourSchedule - excluding any period already applied for

The per-week drill-down added in pkg616 already shows which projects make up the
Project sales figure, with cost % against each.


CLICK A BAR ON THE PROJECT CASH FLOW
------------------------------------
That was the missing half. The forecast bars were pointerEvents:'none', so the
only way in was the View button - and the View modal is already busy, as you say.

Clicking a bar now limits the HORIZONTAL CASH ROWS above to that project alone:
sales in, retention in, labour out, materials out. Click again to clear, or press
"Show all" on the banner.

  no focus       sales in 121,560
  focus J190     sales in 83,435   labour 25,585   materials 42,441

The focused bar carries a dark outline so you can see which one you are on.

ACTUALS AND RETENTION FOLLOW THE FILTER TOO. Applications are keyed by Xero id and
retention by tracker entry, while forecasts are keyed "L:<jobNo>" - all three are
mapped across, otherwise focusing a project would have shown its forecast cash but
everyone else's certified cash.

A BANNER SAYS IT IS FILTERED. Quietly showing one project's totals in the same
place as the combined figure would be worse than not filtering at all.
