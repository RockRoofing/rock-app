1. NEW COLUMN: FORECASTED ACTUAL
--------------------------------
Sits between Full-yr budget and Tracking (yr), on every account row and on the
TOTAL row.

Actual for the months switched to Actual, plus the forecast (or the budget where
no forecast) for every month still to come.

The figure was ALREADY being computed - `projectedYear` - purely to produce
Tracking, but only the VARIANCE was ever shown. So you could see you were 800
over without any sight of the 48,800 it came from:

    Budget to date     20,000
    Actual to date     20,500
    Full-yr budget     48,000
    Forecasted actual  48,800    <- new
    Tracking (yr)        +800

The TOTAL row uses totals.projectedYear, which already existed and is the same
figure the Lock forecast confirmation quotes - so the column, the total and the
lock all agree by construction.

Header, body and totals cell counts verified at 5 each, and the empty-state
colSpan bumped to match.


2. RECONCILE: ALL ACCOUNTS VS SHOWN - NOW TICKED BY DEFAULT
-----------------------------------------------------------
It is the only thing policing the hide-accounts feature: hide a row that still
carries money and the year-end total silently understates. Costs nothing to leave
on - computes from data already loaded, saves nothing.


3. SWITCH TO ACTUAL (was pkg563)
--------------------------------
A month used to swap from forecast to Xero actuals the moment it rolled over,
when almost nothing was posted - so the figure collapsed and crept back up over
the following weeks. Now a "Switch to Actual" button appears once a month has
ENDED and stays until pressed. Green with a tick once switched; press again to go
back.

This also fixes the forecast methods, which all derive from "completed" months
and were averaging in a month that was one day old.

On first load nothing moves: the config key has never been written, so every past
month with data is treated as already switched.
