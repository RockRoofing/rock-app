THE BOOKKEEPING FRAMES SHOW THE PAGE AND NOTHING ELSE
-------------------------------------------------------
WIP: the Commercial nav is gone - it was still rendering inside the frame, which is both a
second navigation bar and a way out of the read-only view. The title and the Total WIP
card are gone too.

RETENTION: the four summary cards are gone. Its nav was already hidden.

Two things deliberately KEPT in both:

  THE MONTH SELECTOR on WIP. A view of one month with no way to change it would be worse
  than useless.

  THE SIGN-OFF BANNER. WIP COMPLETE / WIP NOT COMPLETE is the single thing Accounts most
  need from that tab. The total has moved onto that banner, so removing the card loses
  nothing.

Both remain view-only: no margin editors, no Zero buttons, no manual adjustments, no
project links.


DOWNLOAD PDF
------------
A button beside the month selector, on the full page AND in the Bookkeeping tab. Accounts
can see the figures there, so refusing them the download would be a distinction with no
purpose.

Landscape A4, one row per project: job, name, valuation date, post-valuation costs,
adjustments, margin, profit in WIP, WIP. Totals at the foot, and the sign-off state across
the top in red or green.

A SUMMARY, not the whole screen. The page lists every post-valuation cost line for every
project - hundreds of rows on a busy month, and a document nobody would print. What a WIP
PDF is for is the figure per project and the total.

Built from the same /api/wip the page reads, so the PDF and the screen cannot disagree.
Paginates with the headings repeated.
