WHAT YOU ASKED FOR, AND WHAT YOU NOW HAVE
-------------------------------------------
Pipedrive activities and activities you create on a deal are in the SAME PLACE:

    crm:activities:<projectId>

There is no second copy anywhere. An activity added on a deal appears in the Activities
tab; an activity completed from the tab updates the deal. Both read the same records.


WHY MY LAST TWO ATTEMPTS FAILED
--------------------------------
pkg331 put everything in one store, but wrote the activities in a SEPARATE request from
the deal. If that request did not land, the deal was still saved without them - so your
activities disappeared while the imported ones, already in the store, carried on showing.
That is exactly what you saw.

pkg337 then went back to keeping yours on the deal, which fixed the symptom but reinstated
the two-places arrangement you had told me to get rid of. Wrong answer to the right
complaint.

NOW: the activities travel WITH the deal in the SAME request. The server receives the deal
and its activities together and files both. There is no window where one can be saved
without the other, because there is only one call.


WHAT HAPPENS WHERE
------------------
  You add an activity on a deal
      -> saved with that deal, into the shared store
      -> appears in the Activities tab and on the kanban dot immediately

  You complete one from the Activities tab
      -> written to the same store
      -> the deal shows it completed

  Imported Pipedrive activities
      -> already in that store; shown on the deal alongside yours

Verified end to end: after saving, the store holds the imported one AND yours, the deal
record holds neither (by design), the tab lists yours, and opening the deal shows both.


ONE THING TO EXPECT ON FIRST USE
---------------------------------
The first time you open a deal after deploying, it may save itself once - that is the deal
moving its records into the shared store. Harmless, and only happens once per deal.
