pkg465 - import retention entries from a spreadsheet
====================================================

Deploy after pkg464.

CHANGED FILES (replace)
-----------------------
  pages/retention.js
  pages/api/retention.js

NEW FILES: none.


HOW TO USE IT
-------------
Retention page, "Import spreadsheet" beside + Add Manual Entry. Pick your file. It reports
how many rows were added and how many updated.

I built the button rather than seeding your nine rows directly, because you will do this
again and a one-off seed would have meant coming back to me next time.


IT READS YOUR FILE AS IT IS
---------------------------
Tested against retentions_upload_for_app_-_Formatted.xlsx - all nine rows parse.

    Ref, Customer, Project, Gross AFA, Final Account, Applied for, Ret %, PC Type, QS,
    1st Value, 1st Date, 2nd Value, 2nd Date, VAT Type, Total Paid, Comments

Columns are matched by NAME, so order does not matter and extra columns are ignored.
Ref is the only one that must be there.

Three things it handles that would otherwise have gone wrong:

  RET %      arrives as 0.05 in your file but as 5 in others. Anything above 1 is read as
             a percentage - a 300% retention is not a thing.

  DATES      Excel stores these as serial numbers - 45356, not a date. Read straight,
             new Date(45356) gives the year 45356, and the first version of this produced
             "+045356-01-01". Converted properly now, and checked against your file:
             J124 comes out as 05/03/2024 and 05/03/2025.

  "TBC"      a real answer in your sheet, in the date columns. Kept as blank rather than
             being turned into a date. Four of your nine rows use it.


RE-UPLOADING IS SAFE
--------------------
Rows are matched on REF. Upload a corrected file and those rows are UPDATED, not
duplicated - which matters, because the usual reason to upload again is that a figure was
wrong.

One request and one write for the whole file, rather than one per row. Nine round trips
with a failure half way would have left you not knowing which had landed.


WHAT THESE ROWS ARE
-------------------
Imported as tracker-only entries - manual rows that live in the register and are not tied
to a Xero project. That is right for these nine: they are older jobs still owed retention
but no longer in progress in Xero. They behave exactly like a row added by hand.
