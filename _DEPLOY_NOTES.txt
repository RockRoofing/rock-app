pkg375 - repeated word in a project title
=========================================

Deploy after pkg374. One changed file: lib/crmEmailSync.js. No new files.


THE BUG
-------
From your 2024 run:

    "Wembley Park Masterplan - Plot NE01"  ->  FILED to "Wembley Hill Road, Wembley", 81%

Two different Wembley projects. It should not have filed.

Cause: the title "Wembley Hill Road, Wembley" contains the word Wembley TWICE. The word
list was not de-duplicated, so matching that one word counted as TWO distinct words -
enough to clear the two-word bar that is supposed to stop exactly this - and its weight
was counted twice, pushing the score from 46% to 81%.

Fixed by de-duplicating each title's word list. Same subject now scores 46% on one word,
so it suggests rather than files. All 16 regression matches and the threading tests still
pass unchanged.

Any project whose title repeats a word was exposed to this. Worth a look at the Wembley
project in the CRM - one email will be on it that should not be.
