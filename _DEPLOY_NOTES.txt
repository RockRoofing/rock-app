WHAT IS NEW
-----------
Two panels under the "this period" boxes. Those boxes are all about the period in
front of you; these are the cumulative position for the whole job.

  LABOUR
    Total budget      labRate x qty across the rates, plus instructed variations'
                      labour element
    Claimed to date    the labour element of everything measured to date, works
                      and variations - cumulative, not this period
    Spend to date      actual cost out of Project Financials
                      + "x% of budget spent", red once over 100%

  MATERIALS
    Total budget       matRate x qty, plus instructed variations' materials
    Claimed to date    line items + materials on site, broken out underneath so
                       you can see which is which
    Spend to date      actual cost out of Project Financials

Three different questions, deliberately kept apart: what the rates ALLOW, what
has been billed to the CUSTOMER, and what has actually LEFT the business.


WHERE THE SPEND COMES FROM
--------------------------
costs:latest:<xeroId> - the same cache Project Financials and the CM view read,
written by the wip-sync cron from Xero bills plus the labour journals. The date
it was last synced is shown under the figure, because a stale sync silently
flatters the comparison.

Negotiated projects (N:<dealId>) are not in Xero, so there is nothing to read.
Those show "n/a (not in Xero)" rather than a zero, which would read as "spent
nothing".

ONE BUG CAUGHT IN BUILD
-----------------------
The panel first used fmtD() for the sync date. fmtD is declared INSIDE two other
components, not at module scope, so it compiled perfectly and would have thrown
ReferenceError on every render of the modal. Date is now formatted inline.
