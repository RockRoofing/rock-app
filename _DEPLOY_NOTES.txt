=========================================================================

THREE files, all EDITS - just replace them. Changes apply to BOTH the Commercial
"Outstanding Invoices" page and the Business Financials "Invoices Owed" page, and
they share the same stored data.

  pages/outstanding-invoices.js             (Commercial page)
  pages/business-financials/invoices.js     (Business Financials page)
  pages/api/outstanding-invoices.js         (stores the new "confirmed" flag)

EXPECTED DATE COLUMN - new behaviour
------------------------------------
- Auto-set to the DUE DATE and shown LIGHT GREY until you change it.
- If you adjust it: ORANGE, not bold.
- If you adjust it AND the invoice is overdue (by due date): ORANGE and BOLD.
- Once CONFIRMED (new column): the date turns GREEN (bold if overdue, else normal).

OVERDUE still shows even with an expected date
----------------------------------------------
Overdue is judged on the DUE DATE, so entering an expected date does NOT hide an
overdue invoice - the Due date and Overdue columns stay RED and BOLD as normal.

"EXPECTED DATE PAST" warning
----------------------------
If the (effective) expected date is in the past, a red warning appears under the
date: "! Expected Date Past" with a warning symbol.

NEW "CONFIRMED" COLUMN
----------------------
A toggle button per invoice:
  - Not confirmed = ORANGE ("Not confirmed")
  - Confirmed = GREEN ("Confirmed")
Confirming turns the expected-date field green. Changing the expected date
automatically resets it back to "Not confirmed" (so a new date must be re-confirmed).

The confirmed state is saved and shared between both pages.
