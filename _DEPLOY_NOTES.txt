pkg513 - the WIP PDF now shows the build-up behind each project
===============================================================

Deploy after pkg512. One changed file: lib/wipPdf.js. No new files.


THE SHAPE
---------
    PAGE 1        the summary, exactly as it was - one row per project, totals, sign-off
    PAGE 2 on     one page per project, showing where its figure came from

The summary answers "what is the WIP". The pages behind it answer "why", which is the
question anybody reviewing it asks next - and the reason a summary on its own tends to
come straight back with "where has that come from".

A page per project, so a single job can be printed or sent on its own.

Manual adjustments include any "Zeroed" write-down, which is precisely what somebody
reviewing the WIP needs to see rather than a project quietly reading nil.


TWO THINGS THE TEST DOCUMENT CAUGHT
-------------------------------------
THE POUND SIGN WAS BEING STRIPPED. Sanitising to printable ASCII turned every "£" into a
hyphen, so £27,400.75 printed as "-27,400.75" - which on a page of costs reads as a
negative number rather than a currency symbol. The application PDF already keeps £ for the
same reason; this one now does too.

NEGATIVES read -£5,000.00 rather than £-5,000.00.

Long values are trimmed to their column rather than running into the next one, and both
the cost list and the adjustments repeat their headings across a page break.
