ONE DESCRIPTION, FOUR PLACES
------------------------------
describeApplication() in lib/applications.js decides what an application IS, and the email
subject, the email body, the PDF title, the PDF banner, the filename and the list badges
all read from it.

Written separately they would have said four slightly different things about the same
document - and the one that reaches the customer is the one that matters.


THE RULES, AS SPECIFIED
-----------------------
FINAL ACCOUNT ONLY WHEN THE FLAG IS TICKED. Releasing retention does not make an
application a final account. That is a statement about the contract and only you get to
make it.

    releasing 1st retention, not ticked final
        subject   1st Retention Release 14 - August 2026
        PDF       "1st Retention Release", banner "Includes the 1st retention release"
        file      1st Retention Release 14 - J178 Gas Lane.pdf
        list      1ST RETENTION

    final account that also releases the 2nd half
        subject   Final Account (incl. 2nd Retention Release) 14 - August 2026
        PDF       "Final Account", banner names both
        file      Final Account 14 - J178 Gas Lane.pdf
        list      FINAL ACCOUNT   2ND RETENTION

    ordinary application
        unchanged in every respect


THE EMAIL BODY SAYS IT TOO
--------------------------
    This is our final account for this project.
    This application includes the 1st retention release.

Not just the subject. A retention release is a claim the customer has to recognise and
approve, and a subject line is easy to skim past.


ONLY THE HALF BEING CLAIMED NOW
---------------------------------
A half claimed last month is not mentioned again. It stays ticked on the application - it
has to, or the certificate would double-count it - but it is not news to the customer and
does not belong in this month's subject line.

That distinction runs through all four outputs, which is the main reason they share one
function rather than each working it out.


IN THE LIST
-----------
Up to three markers beside the period: FINAL ACCOUNT in teal, 1ST RETENTION and 2ND
RETENTION in indigo. Same wording as the documents.
