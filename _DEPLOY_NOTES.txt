WHY THIS RATHER THAN ANOTHER FIX
---------------------------------
I have been unable to find the Add Project crash by reading the code, and I have checked
everything I introduced. Guessing again would just cost you another deploy.

The blocker is the message itself. "Application error: a client-side exception has
occurred" is Next.js's stand-in for a crashed page - the real error only exists in the
browser console, so every attempt has been me working blind.

This puts the actual error ON SCREEN. Instead of that generic line you will get:

  - the error message
  - the component stack showing exactly which part failed
  - a Reload button
  - a "Reload and clear saved view" button, in case a remembered filter or column is
    involved (that would also confirm it in one click)

Screenshot that and I can fix it properly. It is one round trip instead of the several
we have just had.

Your data is not at risk either way - a crash on this page is a rendering fault, nothing
is saved or lost by it.


ALSO WORTH TRYING WHEN YOU DEPLOY
----------------------------------
Press "Reload and clear saved view" once even if the CRM looks fine. The remembered-view
feature was behind the Activities bug in pkg315, and it is the most likely culprit here
too - a saved column or filter referring to something that no longer exists would crash
on render. If clearing it fixes Add Project, that tells us both the cause and the fix
immediately.
