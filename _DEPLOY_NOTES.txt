AND A SECOND REASON THE PERCENTAGE CANNOT BE READ AS A MARGIN
---------------------------------------------------------------
A twelve-month window collects revenue for work ALREADY DONE, whose cost was paid
before the window opened. Cost is truncated at the start; revenue is not. The
percentage will always sit below your true margin and it is not a fault.

Look at it month by month and the artefact is obvious:

    Sep 26   sales 166,381   cost 263,658   158%
    Oct 26   sales 285,838   cost  52,522    18%

Same work. September pays for what October bills. In a CASH flow that is correct.
