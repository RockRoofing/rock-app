pkg373 - one word can ask, but it cannot decide
===============================================

Deploy after pkg372. One changed file: lib/crmEmailSync.js. No new files.


THE SECOND RUN WAS A BIG IMPROVEMENT
------------------------------------
West One is clean. Every one of the dozen junk emails it was claiming is now NO MATCH:

    "TMHCC Portal - One-time verification code"     NO MATCH
    "Arbosil ONE: The ONE Silicone..."              NO MATCH
    "Building Safety Regulator Updates"             NO MATCH
    "The #1 mistake most job ads make"              NO MATCH

West One now holds only its real tender thread. Junk filtering took 585 of 1,462 messages
out before matching, and the review queue dropped from 1,266 to 713.


BUT IT LEFT ONE FALSE POSITIVE, AND COST THREE REAL ONES
--------------------------------------------------------
FALSE POSITIVE
    "Bellway Barking: Gran Paradiso and Mont Blanc"  -> FILED to
    "Barking sites flat roofing works" at 83%, and it dragged two more in on the thread.

    Cause: damping the common words worked almost too well. In that title, "sites",
    "flat", "roofing" and "works" now carry near-zero weight - so the title is effectively
    just the word "barking", and any email mentioning Barking scores 83% against it.

REAL MATCHES LOST
    "Fw: Maelfa Roofing Package tender"     was suggested, became NO MATCH
    "RE: Ty Menyn - Waterproofing Options"  was suggested, became NO MATCH
    "1371 - DWP Hoxton jobcentre roofing"   was suggested, became NO MATCH

    Cause: the rule allowed a single word to carry a match only if it was 7+ letters.
    "maelfa" is 6, "menyn" is 5, "hoxton" is 6. Good project names, wrongly excluded.


THE FIX - ONE RULE COVERS BOTH
------------------------------
A single distinctive word is now enough to SUGGEST, but never enough to FILE. Two or more
distinctive words are required before anything files itself.

That is the honest line. One word is a good enough reason to ask you. It is not a good
enough reason to decide on your behalf. The length limit came down from 7 to 5 at the
same time, since it is no longer doing the safety work.

Result:
    Bellway Barking                -> now a SUGGESTION, not filed
    Maelfa / Ty Menyn / DWP Hoxton -> back as suggestions
    All 16 genuine auto-files      -> unchanged, same scores
