VAT IN AND VAT OUT NOW LABEL THEIR ESTIMATES
--------------------------------------------
Three different things were landing in that column and looking identical:

  a FILED return          real, Box 5 as submitted
  the VAT ESTIMATE        the current period's working figure
  a RECLAIM ESTIMATE      my forward calculation from forecast materials and
                          bills at spend x 20/120

Only the first is fact. A figure marked "est" in amber is not a filed return.

Hovering gives the detail, month by month:

    2026-09: VAT estimate; 2026-10: estimated reclaim on forecast materials and bills

A week that mixes a filed return with an estimate IS marked - part of it is a
forecast, so the total is.

VAT OUT gets the same treatment. It comes from the same months, so a PAYMENT can
be just as much an estimate as a refund, and it was equally unlabelled.
