pkg475 - automatic replies logged against the project
=====================================================

Deploy after pkg474. One changed file: lib/crmEmailSync.js. No new files.


WHY YOURS VANISHED
------------------
"Automatic reply:" is in the junk filter, alongside out-of-office, bounce-backs, spam,
read receipts and calendar accepted/declined. Junk is dropped BEFORE matching, which is
why it appeared neither on the deal nor in the review queue.

Not a fault - that filter is there because on a real month junk was the bulk of everything
landing in the queue.


WHAT CHANGES
------------
An automatic reply on a conversation WE HAVE ALREADY FILED is now kept and logged against
that project, marked "automatic reply on a filed thread".

The junk check now runs AFTER the thread lookup rather than before it, which is the whole
change: whether we know the conversation now decides what happens.

    Automatic reply, thread we know     -> filed on the project
    Automatic reply, thread we do not   -> dropped, as before
    RE: on a known thread               -> filed, unchanged

So "Danny is on annual leave until 01.09.26" lands on the tender you are chasing, and the
review queue stays as clean as it was - none of this ever reaches the queue.


TWO THAT STAY OUT WHATEVER THE THREAD
---------------------------------------
    [SPAM]      spam can land in a real conversation, and filing it because we recognise
                the thread would be worse than dropping it
    Read:       a read receipt tells you nothing you cannot see from the email itself
    [postmaster]

UNDELIVERABLE and out-of-office are kept on a known thread, deliberately. A bounce-back on
a live tender is exactly the sort of thing you want to see - it means your email never
arrived.


THE SYNC REPORTS IT
-------------------
Each run now returns an "autoReplies" count per mailbox and overall, so you can see how
many are being kept rather than having to guess.

Only applies going forward. The one from Danny was discarded at the time and is not
recoverable - if you want it on the project, forward it to crm@rockroofing.co.uk with
[CRM-nnnn] in the subject.
