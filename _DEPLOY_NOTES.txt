WHY THE H&S FORMS AREN'T SHOWING
  Built-in "standard template" forms only auto-load when the forms store is EMPTY.
  Your app already had forms saved before the two H&S forms were added to the code, so
  they never got inserted into your live data - that's why they're missing from both
  the Form Builder and the Site App.

FIX - ONE CLICK
  Form Builder now has an "Add missing default forms" button (top right, next to
  "Reset to latest defaults"). Click it once after deploying. It ADDS any built-in
  standard forms that aren't already in your list - including:
     - ACCIDENT BOOK: H&S Incident Record
     - Near Miss Report Form
     - H&S Accident and Incident Report and Lessons Learnt
  It does NOT overwrite or remove any form you've created or edited (unlike "Reset to
  latest defaults", which replaces everything). It tells you which forms it added.

  After clicking, the H&S forms appear in the Form Builder AND in the Site App under
  the "H&S Incidents & Accidents" section for both Operatives and Contracts Managers.
