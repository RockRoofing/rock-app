A CORRECTION FIRST
------------------
I flagged your J248 figure as retention possibly missing. It is not. My test
listed "MCD 3%, no retention" as the match, but 3% RETENTION with 0% MCD gives the
identical number - multiplication does not care about the order, and I read the
label rather than the arithmetic.

    39,304.99 x 0.97 = 38,125.84

Exactly right, with your 3% retention and 0% MCD. Nothing to fix there.


WHAT WAS ACTUALLY WRONG - THE LABELS
------------------------------------
    Revenue this period    38,125.84
      "Increment less MCD and retention"
      "39,304.99 left after the last forecast"

Two figures that look like they should agree and cannot: the first is AFTER
deductions, the second is GROSS, and neither said which. That ambiguity is what
sent us both chasing a phantom.

Now the box states the arithmetic with the real rates:

    "£39,304.99 gross, less retention 3%"
    "£39,304.99 of contract left after Application 1 (gross, before deductions)"

So a missing retention would read "no retention set" instead of having to be
reverse-engineered from a percentage.

It also handles the fraction/percentage trap in your notes: retentionPct is stored
as a FRACTION (0.05) while mcdPct is a PERCENTAGE (2.5). A value under 1 is scaled
for display, so 0.05 shows as 5% rather than 0.05%.
