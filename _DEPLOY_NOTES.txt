1. CARD HEADROOM NOW RISES AS THE BALANCE IS CLEARED
-----------------------------------------------------
It was a constant taken from the opening balances. So scheduling a Capital on
Tap payment pulled the CASH line down and left the AVAILABLE line flat - which
reads as though paying a card costs you borrowing power. It restores it.

The weekly figure is read off the SAME event list that feeds the Financing & tax
column, so the two cannot report different payments.

  week 0                    82,844   (AMEX 60,000 nil owed + CoT 100,000 less 77,156)
  after a 20,000 payment   102,844
  after a further 30,000   132,844

2. THE GRAPH NOW STARTS AT TODAY'S CASH
----------------------------------------
Every point plotted the CLOSING cash for its week, so the leftmost point was
already a week into the forecast - the line began at a figure you had never
held. There is now a "Now" point at the current balance, and the second point is
the end of that same week, so the first segment shows that week's movement
instead of hiding it.

3. A KEY UNDER THE CHART
-------------------------
Three entries with the actual line styles:

  Projected cash balance (solid gold)  - bank only. Facilities NOT folded in, so
                                         it can and should go negative.
  Total available (dashed teal)        - cash PLUS card headroom, overdraft
                                         headroom and still-drawable invoice
                                         finance. Borrowable, not owned.
  Zero (dashed red)                    - crossing it means you are into the
                                         overdraft.

The old prose footnote is replaced, and it now says the card figure RISES as
payments clear rather than describing it as flat.

WHAT TO CHECK
-------------
  - The line starts at your current balance, not week 1's close.
  - Schedule a card payment: cash drops that week and the dashed line does NOT.
  - Card headroom in the tooltip rises after a payment week.
