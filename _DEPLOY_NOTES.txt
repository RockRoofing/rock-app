YOUR QUESTION - YES, 100k COMES THROUGH, AND THAT IS RIGHT
-----------------------------------------------------------
Gross claimed includes materials on site. You claimed 100k and you get paid on
100k, so for cash it is correct and unchanged.

THE PROBLEM WAS EVERYTHING MEASURED AGAINST IT. salesBudgetTotal is contract works
plus variations - it does NOT contain MOS, because MOS is an advance on work not
yet done. Comparing the two understated remaining works by the whole MOS balance:

    contract + variations   135,000
    claimed                 100,000  (20,000 works + 80,000 materials on site)

    BEFORE  "35,000 of contract left"     wrong - works+MOS against works
    AFTER   "115,000 of works left (plus 80,000 materials on site claimed)"

At the exact moment you build the next forecast - your Step 2 - it was telling you
35,000 of work remained when 115,000 did.

ON YOUR STEP 1 AND 2 - one nuance worth holding onto. Materials on site is what
you CLAIM; delivery is what you PAY. Same materials, different dates, and the
forecast keeps both - which is why a materials figure can sit in a valuation
period and leave the bank two months later. That gap is exactly what WIP holds.
