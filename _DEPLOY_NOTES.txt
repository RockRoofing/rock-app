WHAT IT DOES
------------
Every note in the deal timeline now has a "Pin" link beside Edit and Delete.
Pinned notes sort to the top of the feed and stay there.

  Pin      the note lifts to the top
  Unpin    it drops back into date order

ORDERING
--------
Pinned first, then newest. Two pinned notes keep their own date order between
them, so pinning lifts a block to the top rather than scrambling the sequence:

  n4  pinned  22 Jul
  n2  pinned  10 Jun
  n3          07 Sep
  n1          01 Sep

A PINNED NOTE LOOKS PINNED
---------------------------
Sitting at the top is not enough on its own - the next note anybody adds is also
at the top, and the two would be indistinguishable. So a pinned note gets a blue
border and a small PINNED label showing who pinned it and when.

That last part matters more than it sounds. A pin from four months ago that
nobody has revisited looks exactly like one from this morning otherwise, and the
whole point of pinning is that the note still matters.

WHERE IT IS STORED
------------------
On the history entry itself - pinned, pinnedAt, pinnedBy - saved through the same
patch path as editing or deleting a note. No new store, no migration, and
existing notes are simply unpinned.

NOTE: pinning applies to NOTES only. Activities and emails share the timeline but
sort by date as before - an activity is pinned by its due date, which is what a
due date is for.
