YES - I UNDERSTAND, AND YOUR IDENTITY ARGUMENT IS THE RIGHT TEST
------------------------------------------------------------------
Closing cash = opening + profit - the increase in working capital. You cannot show
50k of net assets and 700k of cash unless 650k of working capital has been
RELEASED. The Forecast Balance Sheet shows debtors RISING. Both cannot be true.

So I used your test - the same month on two pages:

    Forecast balance sheet, bank at Nov 26     220,652
    12-month cash flow, closing at Nov 26      651,597
    difference                                 430,945

The balance sheet is constrained: it ties to reserves and "Out by" reads ties. The
12-month just adds up receipts and payments. So the 12-month is the one that is
wrong, and by 430,945.


AND MOST OF IT IS ON YOUR SCREENSHOT: "Opening cash 0"
--------------------------------------------------------
    openBank = startCash !== '' ? Number(startCash) : (data.cashAtBank || 0)

data.cashAtBank is the Xero bank summary's CLOSING BOOK BALANCE, which can be
months stale - and where it is missing, the page starts at ZERO. Your actual bank
at Jul 26 is -286,808.

ON A RUNNING BALANCE THE OPENING FIGURE NEVER WASHES OUT. Every one of the twelve
months is wrong by the same amount and the closing position inherits all of it.

    opening 0        -> Nov 26 closing 651,597
    opening -286,808 -> Nov 26 closing 364,789
    balance sheet says                 220,652
    remaining gap                      144,137

So the opening balance is about two thirds of it. The 12-month now opens from the
MANUAL BALANCES - the same source the 13-week has used since pkg609, because the
book balance is not what is in the account.
