Two things came in wrong.


1. RETENTION SHOWS 0% ON ALL NINE
-----------------------------------
The register stores retention as a WHOLE percentage - 5 means 5%. Xero rows are converted
on the way in with retentionPct * 100, and the cell renders with toFixed(0).

Your sheet holds fractions - 0.05 - and I stored them as they were. 0.05 rendered with
toFixed(0) is "0%".

Worse than cosmetic: 0 is the value that means NO RETENTION, which is what switches the
release columns to N/A. So the register currently believes there is nothing to collect on
any of the nine.

Both formats are now accepted - a value at or below 1 is treated as a fraction and
multiplied up, anything above is already a percentage.


2. DATES ARE A DAY EARLY
------------------------
J195 shows 29/04/2026; your sheet says 30/04.

SheetJS builds dates in LOCAL time, so 30 April becomes midnight local. In BST that is
23:00 UTC on the 29th, and toISOString() then returns the 29th. Every date landed a day
early.

It passed my testing because this machine runs on UTC, where the shift is zero. A date in
a spreadsheet has no timezone - it is just a day - so it now reads the calendar fields
directly instead of converting through UTC.
