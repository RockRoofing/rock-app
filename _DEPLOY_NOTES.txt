DEPLOY NOTES - Invoice Finance rebuilt around APPLICATIONS (not invoices)
========================================================================

TWO files, both EDITS - just replace them.
  pages/api/business-financials.js              (new project/application funding model)
  pages/business-financials/invoice-finance.js  (rebuilt page)

WHY
  Bibby fund against your APPLICATIONS, not invoices. The page now works from the
  application "This cert (net)" figure - which is already NET OF RETENTION and
  inherently INCLUDES materials on site. So the separate materials and retention
  inputs are gone (they were double-handling what's already in the application).

HOW IT WORKS NOW
  - Grouped by CUSTOMER (insured limit is per customer), with PROJECTS listed under
    each, and APPLICATIONS expandable under each project (click a project row).
  - Funding basis per project = sum of "This cert (net)" for UNPAID applications only
    (usually the latest, but earlier unpaid ones too if not yet paid).
  - Advance = advance-rate% (60) of the customer's total fundable, capped at their
    insured limit; total capped at the facility maximum; minus drawn = availability.

PAID STATUS (auto + manual)
  Applications have no paid flag, so each is auto-matched to its invoice by the
  "App N" number in the invoice reference, and that invoice's paid/outstanding status
  is used. Where it CAN'T be matched, the app is flagged "! unmatched" and treated as
  UNPAID (included in funding) so nothing is silently dropped. Each app has a
  Paid/Unpaid toggle that overrides the auto value and is saved.

SETTINGS
  Advance rate %, Facility cap, Currently drawn. Insured limit per customer (type or
  import the Bibby CSV). The Cash Flow "invoice finance available" figure still uses
  the older invoice-based estimate - tell me if you want it switched to this
  application-based number too (recommended, for consistency).

NOTE: needs projects to have applications and a recent dashboard sync. Set the
Facility cap to your real figure.
