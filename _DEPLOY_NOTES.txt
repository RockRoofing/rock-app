NEW - "Completed projects" filter (multi-select)
A new filter at the top of Planning lets you pick one OR MORE Complete/Archived projects. The
selected ones appear in the planner in a read-only section ("COMPLETED / ARCHIVED - VIEWING")
showing exactly which days were worked - useful for recalling when you were on a job. Archived
projects are labelled "(archived)". They are view-only here (no editing/allocating) and are not
shown unless you pick them, so the live planner stays clean.

CHANGED - Installer filter is now MULTI-select
You can now filter by several installers at once (previously one). Projects that have ANY of the
selected installers on them are shown. This also fixes the old single-installer selector.

Both new filters use a compact "N selected" dropdown with checkboxes and a Clear option. The
top-level Clear button resets everything including these.
