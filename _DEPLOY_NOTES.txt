WHAT CHANGED
------------
The two year-end WIP rows in the table now carry the same arrow as the month
headings. Click either one and the project-by-project working opens under the
table:

  Project | Period | Materials | Labour (working days) | Accrued cost |
  Already in Nov | Cost added | Revenue

Both rows open the same panel - they are two halves of one calculation, so
opening either shows the whole thing.

The working used to sit permanently expanded in the panel above the table. That
table has MOVED into the drill-down rather than being duplicated; the panel
keeps the include tick, the typed override and the explanation, and now points
at the arrow.

Nothing about the numbers has changed.
