WHAT CHANGED
------------
Both emails - your own due/overdue activities, and the projects-with-no-activity list -
now go at 07:00 UK instead of 01:00.

The cron fires at minute 0 of UTC hours 6 and 7, and sends on whichever is 07:00 in London:

    August (BST):   06:00 UTC = 07:00 UK  <- sends     07:00 UTC = 08:00 UK  <- skips
    January (GMT):  06:00 UTC = 06:00 UK  <- skips     07:00 UTC = 07:00 UK  <- sends

So it holds at 07:00 through the clock change rather than drifting an hour twice a year.
Verified for both seasons.

Expect 07:00 give or take a few minutes - Vercel does not guarantee to-the-minute
execution.


STILL AVAILABLE FOR TESTING
----------------------------
  ?dryRun=1   reports what both emails WOULD send, sends nothing
  ?force=1    sends both immediately, whatever the time

Both bypass the hour gate, so you can still check either one at any point in the day
without waiting for tomorrow morning.
