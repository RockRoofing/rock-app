CHANGES
1. DAILY DIGEST EMAILS now name comments vs documents correctly. Instead of always saying
   "new comments", they now say:
     - "new comments"        (only comments that day)
     - "a new document" / "new documents"  (only uploads that day)
     - "a new comment and new documents"   (both)
   ...and end with "Please log in to review." This applies to Tech Subs, Rock Drawings and
   Calculations (which can have document uploads). RFIs stay comment-based (no docs there).
   New document uploads (add / new revision) are now counted for the digest, and the digest
   fires if there are new comments OR new documents that day.

2. O&M PAGE - COMMENTS: a comments section now sits at the top of the O&M page. Customers
   (and Rock Roofing) can comment - e.g. the customer requesting changes. @name mentions
   notify that person by email. Works like the comments on the other pages.

3. O&M PAGE - DOWNLOAD FLAG: a flag at the top shows "CUSTOMER NOT DOWNLOADED" (red) until a
   customer downloads the current manual, then "CUSTOMER DOWNLOADED" (green). Rebuilding a
   new revision resets it to red until the customer downloads the new revision.
