1. APP IMPROVEMENT - ATTACHMENTS AND PASTED SCREENSHOTS
--------------------------------------------------------
The "Report app improvement" window now has a "Screenshots / attachments" box.

  - Click "+ Attach a file, or paste a screenshot" to pick files (multiple at once).
  - OR just paste. Win+Shift+S to snip, then Ctrl+V anywhere in the window, and the image
    is uploaded and attached automatically. Pasting ordinary text still behaves normally -
    only image data is intercepted.
  - Each attachment shows a thumbnail with a Remove link before sending.
  - Send is blocked while an upload is still running, so nothing gets sent half-finished.

Attachments are stored on the report and appear in two places:
  - The notification email to the office - images are embedded inline, so a pasted
    screenshot can be read straight from the email without clicking anything.
  - Admin > App Improvements - thumbnail grid on each report, click to view, or Download.

Limit of 10 attachments per report.

NOT COVERED: the Site App has its own separate report button (pages/forms/index.js) and
still has no attachments. Say if you want it there too - it is a similar change but the
Site App needs /api/blob-upload adding to the middleware passthrough first.
