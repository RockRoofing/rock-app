WHAT TRIGGERS - AND WHAT DOES NOT
----------------------------------
Only CSCS and Working at Height. Matched on the COLUMN LABEL, so it keeps working if a
column is renamed, as long as it still contains "CSCS" or "Working at Height". Confirmed
matching: "CSCS", "Working at Height", "Working at Height / H&S (office)". Not matched:
SSSTS, First aid, IPAF and the rest.

Cells marked "No expiry" and cells with no date are ignored.


1. THE OPERATIVE'S OWN REMINDER
--------------------------------
From 6 weeks (42 days) before expiry the person is emailed directly, then again every 7
days, stopping once the ticket is 2 weeks overdue. It stays on the management list after
that - it just stops chasing the individual.

The email says their CSCS card / Working at Height training expires (or expired) on the
date, and asks them to send the renewal to Rock Roofing to keep their training up to date
and maintain their ability to work for Rock Roofing.

Sent to the email on their Site App user record. Anyone without an email is skipped and
counted in the cron response - worth checking, since a missing email means silent
non-delivery.

Throttling is per person PER TICKET, so somebody whose CSCS and Working at Height both
lapse gets one email about each, not one merged one.


2. THE WEEKLY LIST - RECIPIENTS ARE EDITABLE, AS ASKED
-------------------------------------------------------
Nobody is hard-coded. On the H&S Training Matrix page there is a new
"Renewal notifications" button next to "+ Add training column". It opens a panel where you
can:

  - add and remove recipients (any email, internal or external)
  - set the day and hour (defaults to FRIDAY 17:00 UK, as requested)
  - see how many tickets are currently due/overdue
  - "Send now" to fire it immediately for testing

So add Dori, Carl, Mike and Will there yourself, and change it later without me.

*** THE WEEKLY EMAIL WILL NOT SEND UNTIL YOU ADD RECIPIENTS. *** That is deliberate - it
will not guess. The panel warns you in orange when the list is empty.

The email lists everything overdue and everything due within 6 weeks, split into two
tables, with person, company, training and expiry date. In-date tickets beyond 6 weeks are
never included.


HOW IT RUNS
-----------
One hourly cron, /api/cron/hs-renewals, with both jobs self-gating - the same approach the
Outstanding Invoices weekly report already uses. Hourly is needed because a 17:00 send
cannot be guaranteed by a once-daily cron, and the gate is evaluated in UK time so it does
not drift an hour when the clocks change.

  Operative reminders: run once a day, in the 08:00 UK hour.
  Weekly list:         runs on the configured day/hour, once per day.

TESTING: append ?force=1 to run both immediately ignoring every gate, or ?dryRun=1 to see
what the weekly list would do without sending. Both return a JSON summary of what happened.
