WHAT THIS DOES
1. PERSISTENCE: the CRM now loads its deals + field schema from the database on open, and
   saves automatically whenever you change something (there's a "Saved / Saving..." note in
   the CRM's top bar). Data is SHARED across all pre-contract staff. Until the first import,
   it shows the built-in sample deals.

2. PRE-CONTRACT PORTAL: a new "CRM" tab in the Pre-Contract nav (and a "<- Portal" link in
   the CRM top bar). Access is limited to pre-contract / management / admin.

3. IMPORT FROM PIPEDRIVE: an "Import" button in the CRM top bar. Upload the Pipedrive DEALS
   export (.xlsx OR .csv). The file is read in your browser and its deals REPLACE everything
   in the CRM (wipe & replace). Matching is by Pipedrive Deal ID, so re-importing never makes
   duplicates. Tested against the real 6,850-deal export: all 6,850 mapped, 0 skipped.
   (The file is parsed in the browser, so the 3-4 MB export size is not a problem.)

NOTE ON EMAILS: the Deals export includes email COUNTS and last-sent/received dates (these
   import into each deal) but NOT the email bodies. Pulling the actual emails is a separate
   piece we'll look at next, as agreed.
