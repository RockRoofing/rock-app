pkg450 - URGENT. pkg447/449 could delete activities.
====================================================

Deploy after pkg449, as soon as you can. One changed file: pages/crm.js.


I INTRODUCED A DATA-LOSS BUG. THIS FIXES IT.
----------------------------------------------
Activities are all in ONE store per deal, crm:activities:<dealId>. Each record is flagged
either crm:true (created in the CRM) or not (imported from Pipedrive).

The write endpoint REPLACES that whole key. The deal save knows this and merges - it keeps
the imported ones and swaps in the CRM ones.

The helper I added in pkg447 did not. It wrote back only the imported records, so
completing or editing an imported activity DELETED every CRM-created activity on that
deal.

Proven on the bench: a store holding one imported and one CRM activity comes back holding
only the imported one.

Now it touches only imported records and passes CRM-created ones straight through
untouched. There is also a guard so a deal whose activities have not finished loading can
never write an empty list over the store.


TO YOUR QUESTION - WHY TWO PLACES?
------------------------------------
They are not in two places. They are in ONE store with two writers, which turns out to be
just as bad:

    the DEAL SAVE      owns the crm:true records, merges, keeps imported ones
    my helper          owned the imported records, and did not merge

Two writers to one key, with different ideas about what they were allowed to touch. Every
problem you have reported this evening comes from that - the dates disagreeing, the
completions not sticking, and now this.

They now agree: each writer touches only its own kind and leaves the other alone.


ABOUT THE DUPLICATE ON POOL MOTORS
------------------------------------
The two rows - 19 Aug to Edita, 20 Aug to James - are two genuinely different activity
records, not one shown twice. Different people, different dates.

What I cannot tell from here is whether the 19 Aug one was created deliberately or is the
wreckage of an edit that half-wrote. Once this is deployed, delete whichever is wrong and
it will stay deleted.

If activities have gone MISSING from other deals over the last hour, that is the bug above
and I am sorry - they were removed from the store and cannot be recovered from the app.
Worth a quick look at a few busy deals to see whether anything you expected is absent.
