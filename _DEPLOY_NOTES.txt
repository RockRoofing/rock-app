pkg499 - email bodies tidied up
===============================

Deploy after pkg498. One changed file: pages/crm.js. No new files.


WHAT IS BEING STRIPPED
----------------------
Outlook's plain-text version of an HTML email carries a lot nobody wants to read:

    BRACKETED LINK REPEATS   every hyperlink has its target repeated after it -
                             "01282 442 300 <tel:01282%20442%20300>"
    INLINE IMAGE PLACEHOLDERS  [cid:image767571.jpg@FE6AC60D.0CC23966]
    SIGNATURE TRACKING URLS  the Exclaimer link in your purple box, which runs to about
                             600 characters across five lines
    HUGE BLANK GAPS          collapsed to a single blank line

Your example now reads exactly as the red box: the header, the message, the sign-off and
the phone number. Nothing else.


WHAT IS DELIBERATELY KEPT
-------------------------
A NORMAL LINK SOMEBODY TYPED. "See our brochure: https://rockroofing.co.uk/..." stays -
that is part of what they said. The length test is what separates them: a line that is
nothing but a URL over 120 characters is a tracker, a short one is a person sharing
something.

A known tracker is dropped at any length, but only when the line is JUST that link -
never when it appears inside a sentence.

THE SENDER'S ADDRESS. "From: Jack Belshaw <Jack.Belshaw@barnfield...>" is untouched: the
rule only matches brackets that START with http, mailto: or tel:, so a plain address in
angle brackets is safe.


CLEANED ON DISPLAY, NOT ON SYNC
---------------------------------
The stored copy is untouched. If a rule ever strips something it should not, the original
is still there, and "Open in Outlook" always shows the real email.

That was the deciding factor - a cleaning rule applied at sync time would have quietly
destroyed the only copy we hold.
