1. IT NOW SAYS WHAT TO DO
-------------------------
An unmarked cell said "due", which describes the state and gives no clue the cell
is the control. It now says the action, is underlined, and carries a dashed
outline so the column reads as a row of buttons rather than another money column.

  unmarked, nothing known      orange, dashed   "click to confirm released"
  unmarked, Xero looks paid    blue,   dashed   "looks paid - click to confirm"
  marked by hand               green,  solid    "OK released"
  marked by application        green,  solid    "OK released (app)"
  app says yes, overridden     amber,  dashed   "not released - click to accept"

The column headings now read "1st Value - click to release" and "2nd Value -
click to release", and their tooltips spell out the click, the undo, and what
each colour means.

The note inside the edit form used to say "Auto: turns green once...", which was
describing behaviour that no longer exists. It now points at the row.


2. RELEASE MARKS - MANUAL AND AUTOMATIC
---------------------------------------
Click a release value to confirm that half; click again to undo. The date marked
is stored.

A half ticked in the retention section of the project's latest application marks
the register by itself.

Manual beats automatic BOTH WAYS - the flag is an explicit true/false, so
clicking a green "released (app)" cell records false and it stays false, going
amber so a deliberate override never looks like an oversight.


3. 612 COLUMNS AND THE RECONCILIATION
-------------------------------------
  ... Retention Owed | 612 Deducted | 612 Released | tick | Ret % ...

Called "612 Deducted", not "Allocated", because the old Allocated column was
Math.abs() of a SIGNED sum - i.e. the net, the same thing as Held. Reusing the
name for the gross figure would have shown a different number under the same
heading with nothing to say so.

  Retention Owed - 612 Deducted + 612 Released, green at zero.

  nothing released           10,000 - 10,000 +      0  ->  tick
  half claimed + released     5,000 - 10,000 +  5,000  ->  tick
  claimed on app, not in 612  5,000 - 10,000 +      0  ->  FLAG, out by -5,000
  deduction never coded      10,000 -  4,000 +      0  ->  FLAG, out by  6,000
  no 612 lines at all                                  ->  dash

Imported rows keep their spreadsheet "612 Allocated" figure on the record but it
is NOT fed into 612 Deducted - that column was a net, and a net in a gross field
would flag every imported row.
