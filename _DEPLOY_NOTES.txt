1. MOVE AN EMAIL TO ANOTHER PROJECT
-----------------------------------
Every email in a project's Email section now has TWO actions:

    Move     file it against a different project
    Remove   take it off projects altogether

Move opens the same project search you get in the review queue. Pick the right job and it
goes, immediately, with the row put back if the save fails.

Why this needed its own action rather than reusing Remove: removing does NOT put the email
back in the review queue. It marks it do-not-assign and it is gone. So "remove it and file
it properly" was never actually a route - you would have lost it. Filed against the wrong
job is also far commoner than filed wrongly altogether.

THE THREAD FOLLOWS THE CORRECTION. Move an email and the rest of that conversation files
against the new project from now on, instead of continuing to land on the one you just
corrected. And unlike Remove, a moved email is NOT marked do-not-assign - it belongs on a
project, just a different one.

The Remove confirmation now points at Move, so nobody reaches for the destructive one by
mistake.
