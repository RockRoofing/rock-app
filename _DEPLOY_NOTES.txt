What IS serious is a different thing, which that grep missed.


THE ACTUAL FAULT IN THIS PACKAGE
--------------------------------
Six sites took "today" from the browser's clock, and nine more anchored a WEEK
from it with mondayOf(new Date()). Those give a different answer depending on
where the person is sitting.

The one that matters most: cashflow.js line 555.

    const start = mondayOf(new Date())      // the 13-week window START

pkg851 fixed the two todays in that file and MISSED this. Viewed from a
timezone ahead of London, on a Sunday evening UK time, that is already Monday
locally - so the whole 13-week window starts a week early and every figure in
it is for the wrong thirteen weeks. My omission; this closes it.

Also fixed: the Planner's current-day column and week windows, the project
cash flow's week anchor and Today button, and the Missing Forms week on both
the Operations page and the CM Site App.


THE FIX
-------
lib/businessDate.js gains businessNow(): a Date sitting at MIDDAY on the
business's today. Midday rather than midnight so a daylight-saving shift of an
hour cannot move the anchor onto the previous or next day - a week anchor that
jumps once a year is the kind of fault nobody ever traces.

Every mondayOf(new Date()) becomes mondayOf(businessNow()).
Every iso(new Date()) becomes businessToday().

After this there is no viewer-local "today" left anywhere outside pages/api.
Verified by grep.
