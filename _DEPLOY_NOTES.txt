THE DATA DID NOT EXIST - NOW IT DOES
------------------------------------
Nothing in the app captured FullyPaidOnDate. The existing pulls take PAID invoices
for VAT totals and throw the date away, and receivables only ever holds what is
OUTSTANDING - which tells you nothing about how long anything takes.

New fetch: paid sales invoices from the last 24 months with their due date and the
date they were actually settled.


THE PAGE
--------
  Customer      Invoices  Value     Typically   Issue to cash  On time  Spread
  Wates              42   612,400   31d late         74d          0%      19d
  Torsion            18   204,900   12d late         55d         20%      60d
  Alheembouw          9    88,100    3d late         33d          0%       4d

TYPICALLY PAID IS THE MEDIAN, not the average. One disputed invoice sitting at 300
days would wreck a mean and make a reliable customer look terrible.

SPREAD IS THE MORE USEFUL COLUMN for forecasting, and it is why the median alone
is not enough. Torsion medians 12 days but swings between 0 and 60 - that is a
customer you cannot plan around whatever the middle figure says. Wates is 31 days
late every time, which is worse on paper and far easier to forecast.

Click a customer for their last 20 invoices - due date, paid date, days late.


IT FEEDS THE FORECAST
---------------------
A "Forecast offset" box against each customer, pre-filled with their median as a
placeholder. Set it and every one of their invoices shifts that many days in the
13-week cash flow, instead of sitting on a due date they have never once hit.

Shown in the Invoices in drop-down as "+31d - this customer pays late", so a week
never moves for a reason you cannot see.

IT DOES NOT OVERRIDE A DATE YOU HAVE SET BY HAND. An expected date is a judgement
somebody has already made; shifting it would overrule them.


PRESS "READ PAID INVOICES FROM XERO" FIRST - the page is empty until you do.
