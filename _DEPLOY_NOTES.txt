FOUND IT - AND YOUR INSTINCT ABOUT OLD FORECASTS WAS RIGHT
-----------------------------------------------------------
The PROJECT cash flow page has fallbacks for older forecast records. The API did
not - except for sales.

  project-cashflow.js:211  sales     falls back to fc.salesDate + revenueThisPeriod
  project-cashflow.js:213  labour    falls back to fc.labourDate + labourThisPeriod
  project-cashflow.js:214  materials reads fc.matItems

  business-financials.js   sales     HAD the fallback
                           labour    had NONE
                           materials had NONE

So on any forecast saved before the schedule fields existed, THE SALES CAME
THROUGH AND THE COSTS DID NOT. Income with no spend against it - exactly the
668k shape, and exactly what you were seeing.

NOW:
  labour    falls back to labourDate + labourThisPeriod, same as the project page
  materials rebuilt from matDeliverDay + materialsThisPeriod where matItems is
            absent, paid at end of month + 30 to match the save

  old forecast, matDeliverDay 25/09, materialsThisPeriod 70,000
    before  nothing at all
    after   70,000 on 30/10
