BEFORE YOU READ THE 12-MONTH, IT HAD DRIFTED BADLY
----------------------------------------------------
It has its own copy of the forecast logic and none of this session's work was in
it. It was still on the pre-pkg618 rules:

    one invoice killed a project's ENTIRE month of sales
    one bill killed a project's ENTIRE month of costs
    no CIS on forecast labour at all
    no valuation-date boundary
    labour treated as net when it is gross

So it could not have agreed with the 13-week even on the weeks they share, and it
would have answered your December/January question wrongly.

Now netted rather than discarded on both sides, labour paid at gross x 80%, the
valuation date as the boundary, and CIS at gross x 20% landing the month AFTER the
labour:

    month     labour gross   paid to subbie   CIS to HMRC
    Sept 26         60,000           48,000             0
    Oct 26          45,000           36,000        12,000
    Nov 26          30,000           24,000         9,000
    Dec 26               0                0         6,000

That lag is worth seeing over twelve months - the 13-week already did it, the
monthly page did not do it at all.
