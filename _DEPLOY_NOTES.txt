pkg778 - Add companies and contacts directly
=============================================

DEPLOY OVER pkg777 (contains pkg777). Thirteen files.

FOLDER NOTE: pages/project/[id].js uses LITERAL square brackets.

REPLACE:
  pages/crm.js                      (changed here)
  everything else as pkg777

WHAT IT DOES
------------
"+ Add company" and "+ Add contact" in the header of their respective pages,
next to Choose Columns.

Both lists were read-only. A company only existed once a project referenced it,
so a customer you had met but not yet tendered for could not be recorded at all.

WHAT YOU GET IN THE FORM
-------------------------
  Company   Company name*, Address, Phone, Website, Email,
            Registration Number, Supply Chain Approved?
  Contact   Name*, First name, Last name, Company, Phone,
            Email, Job Role

Driven by the SAME COMPANY_FIELDS / CONTACT_FIELDS lists the tables use, so a
field added there appears here without anybody having to remember. The derived
columns - Deals, Open value, Won, Lost - are counted from projects and are not
editable, so they are left out.

DUPLICATES ARE BLOCKED
----------------------
Case-insensitive and trimmed, so "wates construction limited" is caught against
"Wates Construction Limited". Save is disabled and the reason is shown.

It will NOT catch "Wates Construction Ltd" against "...Limited" - those are
different strings and no rule can tell a typo from two genuinely different
companies. Same limitation the rest of the CRM has.

NOTHING NEW UNDERNEATH
----------------------
It calls the existing upsertOrg and upsertContact, which already handle the
merge, the API write and the optimistic update. No new endpoint, no new store,
and records added here behave exactly like ones created from a project.

Escape or the X to close, like every other modal.
