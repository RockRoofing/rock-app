pkg322 - Activities tab: faster, and it now says what it is hiding
===================================================================

REPLACES pkg302 through pkg321. Deploy this one only.

CHANGED FILES
-------------
  pages/crm.js
  pages/api/crm.js        caches the rebuilt outstanding list
  lib/crmImportMap.js     unchanged since pkg305


1. WHY IT WAS SLOW
-------------------
Your activities were imported before the flat outstanding-list existed, so every time you
opened the tab it rebuilt that list from scratch - reading the per-deal records for every
project with something outstanding, a few hundred keys, on EVERY page load.

It now saves the list once it has built it, so that happens a single time and every load
afterwards reads it directly. I also halved the batch size (150 keys per request rather
than 300), which is gentler on the database and less likely to be throttled.

First open after deploying will still take a moment while it builds and stores the list.
After that it should be quick.


2. YOUR ACTIVITY NOT SHOWING
-----------------------------
I could not tell you which of two rules hid it, so the tab now accounts for every row it
holds back rather than dropping them silently:

  A REMEMBERED FILTER. The CRM remembers your Person responsible and Customer filters
  between visits. An activity you add with no person assigned will not match a saved
  "person" filter - and you would have no way of knowing a filter was even set.
  There is now an amber line: "Filtered by person: Edita  clear" with a one-click clear.

  THE PROJECT IS NOT OPEN. The tab only lists activities on open projects (you asked for
  that in pkg317). If you added it to a project marked won or lost, it will not appear.
  There is now a line saying "N more on won or lost projects (not shown)".

Between them, one of these should account for your missing activity the moment you open
the tab. If neither line appears and it is still missing, that is a genuine bug and I will
chase it - but the screen should now tell you rather than leaving you guessing.

Both counts sit under the activity total on the right of the filter bar.
