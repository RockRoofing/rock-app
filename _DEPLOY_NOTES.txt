--------------------------------------------------------

      468,326.86 - 145,297.40    = 323,029.46          exact

  (Approved Debt - High Involvement) x 60% = Approved Funding
      (323,029.46 - 35,069.31) x 0.6       = 172,776.09   exact

2. RECONCILIATION EXPORT
------------------------
New "Export reconciliation" button. CSV in BIBBY'S OWN ORDER so the two read side
by side:

  Sales Ledger (all unpaid sales invoices incl retention)
    less retention invoices
    less eligibility caps (materials / variations / ceiling)
    less over insured limit
    less no insured limit set
  = Approved Debt
  High involvement
  Advance rate
  = Approved Funding
  Funds in use / drawn
  = Availability

Then every invoice underneath - customer, project, invoice number, reference,
date, amount due, retention flag, which application it matched, contract value,
what the caps excluded on that project and why.
