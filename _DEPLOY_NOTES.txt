1. THE GRAPH STARTED AT ZERO
-----------------------------
The "Now" point I added in pkg751 read `startCash` - the OVERRIDE BOX - rather
than the resolved opening balance. With nothing typed in that box the value is
an empty string, and Math.round('') is 0.

So whenever you were NOT overriding, the line began at zero instead of your
actual cash, and the key underneath read "Starts at today's balance, £0" - which
is exactly what your latest export shows with -172,611 in the bank.

Now reads the resolved figure: the override when one is typed, otherwise the
balances.

2. "0 NOT PLACED" WHILE 34,315 WAS MISSING
-------------------------------------------
The placement check said everything landed while the amber bar said 34,315 of
invoices were overdue and excluded. Both could not be true.

When the arrears row came out in pkg743, the arrears BRANCH stayed - it still
counts the total for the amber bar, but it feeds nothing into any week. It was
still marking those invoices as placed, so the check reported them as landed
when they had been dropped.

Overdue invoices now show as NOT PLACED with the reason "expected date has
passed - set a new one and it comes back in", and the summary counts them.

On your figures the check should now read something like 42 placed, 1 not placed
(£34,315) - that being INV-1732, Wates, Waungron App 1, still dated 04/09.
