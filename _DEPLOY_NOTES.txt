WHAT WAS HAPPENING
------------------
Actuals tie perfectly now. The forecast did not, and the table showed why:

    Jul 26  net assets  56,460   reserves  56,460   ties
    Aug 26  net assets 853,192   reserves  19,686   out by 833,506

RESERVES was re-based onto Xero. THE COMPONENTS WERE NOT - bank, debtors and
creditors carried on rolling forward from the same wrong opening. So the bottom
line was right and everything above it was not.

The parser was reading every monthly cell and keeping only the last column. It now
keeps them all, so each closed month sets bank, debtors, retention, cards and
creditors to Xero's real figures, and the forecast rolls on from THOSE.

FINANCING BALANCES THE MONTH. Fixed assets, WIP, inventory, prepayments and
deferred tax are not modelled as their own lines, so that line absorbs them and
the total equals Xero exactly:

    Jul 26  bank 0 + debtors 415,457 + retention 115,975
            - cards 118,755 - creditors 381,187 + financing 24,969
            = 56,460, which is Xero's figure to the penny


YOUR SECOND QUESTION - WHY THE FOUR SETTINGS
--------------------------------------------
They ONLY affect forecast months now. Actual months come from Xero and need no
assumptions at all, which was not true before this package.

They exist because a P&L does not tell you when cash moves. On 100,000 of billing:

    debtor days  0 -> collected in the month, debtors nil
    debtor days 45 -> nothing collected, 100,000 sitting as a debtor

Identical profit. Completely different cash, and a completely different balance
sheet. Without them profit and cash would be the same thing, which is the one
assumption a construction business cannot make.

  debtor days      how much of a month's billing is still owed at month end
  creditor days    the same for what you owe
  retention %      the slice of a bill you will not see for a year
  retention held   how long before it is released

WORTH KNOWING: the Payment Performance tab now measures your REAL debtor days per
customer. Once you are happy with those figures, the 45 here should be replaced by
what the data actually says rather than a round number. Say the word and I will
wire it through.
