THE CAUSE
---------
The project NAME from Xero is the whole tracking option - "J1 - Test" - and jobNo is
extracted from the front of it:

    name:  "J1 - Test"
    jobNo: "J1"        <- taken from the name

Nine places joined the two with " - ", which repeats the number every time.

It is not just your test project. Every project doubles:

    "J1 - Test"          ->  J1 - J1 - Test
    "J178 - Gas Lane"    ->  J178 - J178 - Gas Lane
    "J205. Pets Choice"  ->  J205 - J205. Pets Choice


FIXED IN ONE PLACE
------------------
A projectLabel() helper strips a leading job number from the name before joining, and all
nine call sites use it:

    J1    "J1 - Test"           ->  J1 - Test
    J205  "J205. Pets Choice"   ->  J205 - Pets Choice     (handles a full stop too)
    J205  "Pets Choice"         ->  J205 - Pets Choice     (name without the number)
    none  "Bensham Lane"        ->  Bensham Lane

It covers the variation emails, the variation PDF, the instruction page and its
confirmation email, the reminders, the PDF filenames, and the WIP page and PDF - all of
which had the same fault.
