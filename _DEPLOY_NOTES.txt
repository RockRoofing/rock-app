WHAT I BROKE IN pkg355
-----------------------
To get Tender Return date out of the deal summary I deleted its entry from the field
schema. But that schema is also what tells the forms WHAT KIND of field it is:

    schemaFor = (k) => SCHEMA.find(x => x.key === k) || { key: k, type: 'text' }

With no entry, it fell back to type 'text' - so the Add Project form gave you a plain
typing box instead of a date picker.


THE FIX
-------
The field is declared again, as a date, but in a group of its own ('none') rather than
'summary'. So:

  - the Add Project form shows a proper date picker
  - it still does NOT appear in the deal summary
  - it does not turn up in Details, or in the field manager lists either

Checked all three: date picker restored, and the field appears in none of the group
listings.
