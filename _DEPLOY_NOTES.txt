THAT IS EXACTLY THE CONFUSION, AND IT IS A LABELLING FAULT
------------------------------------------------------------
The grid is keyed by CALENDAR MONTH, but your applications are not calendar
months. On a 9th-of-the-month cycle the column headed "Aug 26" is the application
VALUED 09/08 - covering work from 10 July. Calling it "August" invites precisely
the reading you had, and you were right to question it.

Nothing about the maths was wrong. The percentage has always belonged to the
application; the header just named the wrong thing.

    BEFORE   Aug 26          Sep 26
    AFTER    App 09/08/26    App 09/09/26
             Aug 26          Sep 26        <- small, for orientation only

The heading and caption follow: "Spread across the period's 2 applications - set
what % of sales and labour falls in each application."

WHERE THERE IS NO APPLICATION CALENDAR it still says the month, because that IS
what the spread means on an end-of-month cycle. The label now tells you which
convention is in force rather than assuming you know.

THE STORED KEYS ARE UNCHANGED - still "2026-08" and so on. Display only, no
migration, nothing to re-save.


AND ON THE BUSINESS FINANCIALS SIDE
-----------------------------------
The project drill-down there groups by the month the CASH lands, which is a
different thing again - Winnersh's August application appears under September
because that is when it pays. The caption now says so, so the two pages cannot be
read as if they meant the same thing.
