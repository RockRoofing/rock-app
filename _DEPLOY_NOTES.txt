pkg357 - Delete a project from inside the deal
===============================================

REPLACES pkg302 through pkg356. Deploy this one only.

CHANGED FILES
-------------
  pages/crm.js
  pages/api/crm.js        server-side delete
  lib/crmFieldSchema.js   unchanged since pkg356
  lib/crmImportMap.js     unchanged since pkg305


WHAT YOU GET
------------
A small red "Delete" in the deal header, immediately after Won / Lost (and Reopen when it
is there). Plain red text, no border, noticeably smaller than the others - deliberately not
something you hit by accident next to two buttons you press often.


TWO CONFIRMATIONS
-----------------
    Delete "Bensham Lane"?
    This removes the project and all of its activities, notes and history. It cannot be
    undone.

then

    Last check - permanently delete "Bensham Lane"?

Two prompts because there is no undo and no recycle bin. A single confirm on a destructive,
irreversible action sitting beside Won and Lost is not enough.


WHAT IT ACTUALLY REMOVES
-------------------------
The project, and everything filed against it:

    the deal record
    its activities        (crm:activities:<id>)
    its notes             (crm:notes:<id>)
    its entry in the outstanding activity list and the kanban counts
    its index entries

That matters. Activities and notes live in their own keys per project - deleting only the
deal would leave those behind permanently, invisible and unreachable, quietly filling up
the database. They are cleared properly.

The screen updates immediately and you are returned to the board. If the server delete
fails you are told to reload before carrying on, rather than being left with a board that
disagrees with what is stored.


WHAT IT DOES NOT TOUCH
-----------------------
The company and the contact stay on the Companies and Contacts pages. They are shared
records - other projects may use them - so deleting one project must not take a customer
with it. Remove those separately if you want them gone.
