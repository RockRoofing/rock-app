WHAT THE CONSTANT 952,337 TOLD US
---------------------------------
"Out by" was IDENTICAL in all twelve months. That is not a modelling drift - a drift
grows. A constant means the error is entirely in the OPENING figures, and the
roll-forward itself is fine, which matched the test I ran when building it.

The cause: I summed every row of Xero's balance sheet and called it equity. XERO
RETURNS LIABILITIES AS POSITIVE FIGURES under their own headings, so that sum is
assets PLUS liabilities instead of assets MINUS liabilities. Your creditors at
31 Jul are 533,399 - double-counted, that is the order of the error.

    31 Jul 2026   Xero net assets    56,459.73
                  my model          991,136.00


THE FIX - AND IT IS THE ONE YOU SUGGESTED
------------------------------------------
Xero's Balance Sheet report takes periods=11&timeframe=MONTH and returns twelve
real month-end columns in ONE call - the same shape as the PDF you sent. So there
is no need to reconstruct anything.

  ACTUAL MONTHS now show Xero's OWN "Net Assets" total. Not summed by me, taken
  from the report. They are fact, they tie by definition, and "Out by" reads 0.

      Dec 25  249,639.83     Apr 26  251,938.21
      Jan 26  204,640.21     May 26  133,000.18
      Feb 26  181,949.54     Jun 26  119,572.72
      Mar 26  180,394.83     Jul 26   56,459.73

  FORECAST MONTHS re-base onto the LAST ACTUAL, so they start from the truth
  rather than from twelve months of accumulated error:

      Jul 26 actual   56,460
      Aug           -36,773  ->   19,687
      Sept          174,274  ->  193,961
      Oct            14,462  ->  208,423
      Nov            81,880  ->  290,303

  Which lands where you said it should - 250-275k - instead of 1.2m.

"OUT BY" NOW ONLY APPLIES TO FORECAST MONTHS. On an actual month both sides are
Xero's own figure, so a tie there would prove nothing and would hide a real
forecasting error behind a row of green ticks.


PRESS "READ BALANCE SHEET FROM XERO" on the Balance Sheet tab after deploying -
it now pulls twelve months instead of one.
