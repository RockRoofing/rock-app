WHAT WAS STILL WRONG
--------------------
pkg760 fixed the CASH FLOW reading the drawn figure. It did not fix the Invoice
Finance page, which keeps its own statement log - and that log still held
122,711 as at 30/08.

So the same number lived in two places and they had drifted:

  statement log, this page    122,711  as at 30/08  -> availability 38,968
  balances panel, 13-week     138,581  as at 07/09  -> availability 23,098
  Bibby portal                138,581               -> availability 26,989

THE FIX
-------
Whichever reading is MORE RECENT is used, and the "Currently drawn" card says
which - "from the cash flow balances, as at 07/09/2026" or "statement log, as at
30/08/2026".

Nothing is deleted. The statement log is still there and still editable; it is
simply no longer able to override a newer figure typed elsewhere.

An amber bar appears while the two disagree, naming both and saying which is in
use, so a stale log cannot quietly overstate your headroom again. It is tagged
rr-warn, so it does not print.

WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE
-------------------
  Currently drawn    £138,581  (from the cash flow balances, as at 07/09/2026)
  Availability now    £23,098

Against Bibby's own 26,989. The remaining 3,891 is this page calculating the
advance from your ledger (161,679) versus Bibby's approved figure (165,570) -
two different calculations of the same thing, and the smaller discrepancy by far.

THE PATTERN, SINCE IT KEEPS HAPPENING
--------------------------------------
Drawn was held in two places. Receivables were read from two sources. Card
headroom was computed twice. The project labels came from a cache that expired.
Every one produced a wrong number that looked plausible. Where the same fact can
be entered in two places, it will diverge - so the fix is always to make one of
them read the other rather than to correct both.
