WHY THE LINE TRACKED THE CASH - TWO REASONS, BOTH MINE
--------------------------------------------------------
    .filter(x => (x.appDate || x.date) && inWk(x.appDate || x.date))

1. IT FELL BACK TO x.date - the PAYMENT date - wherever appDate was missing. So
   availability moved with the cash instead of the billing.

2. It sat INSIDE the block guarded by salesSpent, so a period whose forecast sales
   had been superseded raised nothing at all. But the application still happened
   and still created fundable debt.

Now: appDate only, over every forecast, regardless of supersession.

    Waungron App2, applied 27/09, cash 27/10
      BEFORE  availability rose in the week of 27/10, with the cash
      AFTER   rises 27/09, falls 27/10 as the advance is repaid

The dashed line now LEADS the cash line by the application-to-payment gap, which
is the whole point of the facility.


AND THE PROJECTION IS ON THE INVOICE FINANCE PAGE, AS YOU ASKED
-----------------------------------------------------------------
"Projected availability, by application date":

    Application month   Applied for   Over insured limit   Advance created

Built from the project forecasts, grouped by the month each application is RAISED,
with the advance calculated per customer using the SAME rules as the live table -
your advance rate, and the insured limit applied to the DEBT with only the excess
disapproved.

THE "OVER INSURED LIMIT" COLUMN IS THE USEFUL ONE. Where no limit is recorded for
a customer, none of that customer's debt is fundable - so a large figure there is
either a limit you have not entered or an application Bibby will not fund. Either
way it is worth knowing before you count on the advance.
