THE DETAIL IN EACH GROUP IS DIFFERENT ON PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------
    Won and Lost      the date it closed
    Still tendered    the stage it is at, because a close date would be a guess
    Lost              the lost reason instead of the estimator, which is the thing you
                      actually want to know about a job you did not get

Strike rate is by VALUE, matching how it is measured everywhere else on the portal.


EVERY PROJECT IS A LINK
-----------------------
Real links, so right-click and ctrl-click open them in a new tab exactly as they do on the
board and the activities list. A normal click closes the panel and opens the project.


CLOSES ON THE X OR ESCAPE ONLY
--------------------------------
Never on a click outside, as you asked. You open this to read it, often while part-way
down a long list, and a stray click should not shut it.


ONE THING WORTH KNOWING
-----------------------
Projects are matched to a company by NAME. If a contractor is recorded as both "Barnfield
Construction" and "Barnfield Construction Ltd" they will appear as two companies with
their history split between them.

That is the name-as-identity problem I have flagged a few times. It shows up plainly here
for the first time - if a company's history looks short, check the list for a near-
duplicate above or below it.
