WHAT WAS WRONG
--------------
Cards were parked everywhere. On the Forecast BS the balance is set from the
opening position and only ever reassigned on ACTUAL months, so the row was flat
across every forecast month - never paid, never grown. On both cash flows cards
are filtered out of opening cash entirely and appear only as headroom in the
dashed availability line. So no card payment ever left the bank in any forecast.

12-MONTH CASH FLOW - per-card clearance
---------------------------------------
New "Credit card clearance" panel below the table. Each card shows what it owes
today, what you have scheduled, and what is still owing at the end of the
window. Add as many payments per card as you like, each with a month and an
amount.

  - New "Cards" column in the table and in the totals row
  - Payments are capped at what is left on that card, so a balance cannot go
    through zero and invent cash
  - Cards come from your manual balances of kind "card" where they exist,
    otherwise from Xero's card accounts - the same precedence as everything
    else on the page

ONLY THE OPENING BALANCE IS SCHEDULED, and this matters. Your ongoing card
spend is already in the forecast, inside Overheads and Bills. If a payment were
modelled as a general monthly outflow, that same cost would leave the bank
twice. What is cleared here is what is already owed. Same reasoning as the
Bibby drawn balance.

HOVER
-----
The month tooltip on "Projected cash balance" now gives closing cash, money in,
money out, then each card's balance owed at that month end, a total where there
is more than one card, and what was paid that month.

FORECAST BALANCE SHEET - diagnosis only, no payments wired in
--------------------------------------------------------------
As you asked, payments do not touch the balance sheet yet.

You said the Credit cards row is all zeros while the sheet still matches Xero.
I have NOT guessed at why. Every Xero account is now classified by the same
tests the roll-forward uses, and shown:

  - a red banner naming any account that looks like a card but is being counted
    somewhere else, and where it is going instead
  - "How Xero's accounts are being classified" - expandable, every account with
    its section, opening balance, the bucket it fell into, and whether it has
    monthly values

The likely cause is the name pattern: it matches "credit card", "visa",
"mastercard", "amex", "american express", "capital on tap". An account called
"Barclaycard" or "Company Card" matches none of them and falls into Financing,
which is the catch-all that balances the sheet - which is exactly why net
assets still tie while the row reads nil.

Open that panel and send me what it says. I will widen the rule to the real
names rather than adding more guesses to the pattern.

Note: paying a card does NOT change net assets - bank down, liability down. So
nothing about this should move the balance sheet bottom line when it is wired
in later.
