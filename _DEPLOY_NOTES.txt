pkg519 - the Gantt scrollbar stays on screen
============================================

Deploy after pkg518. One changed file:
  pages/operations/project-management/planning.js

No new files.


THE CAUSE
---------
The Gantt was capped at a flat "100vh - 220px" - 220 being somebody's estimate of how far
down the page the box starts.

It is not a fixed number. The controls above it wrap onto a second row on a narrower
window, and the warning banners appear and disappear, so the box starts anywhere from
about 300px to about 410px down. Whenever 220 was too small the box ran past the bottom of
the window, taking its horizontal scrollbar with it - so you had to scroll the page down
to find the bar, then scroll back up to see what you had moved.

Same fault the retention register had, and the same fix.


MEASURED, NOT GUESSED
---------------------
The box now measures its own distance from the top of the window and sizes itself to fill
what is left, less 16px so the bar is not flush with the edge.

Remeasured on load, on resize, and whenever anything above it changes height - so wrapping
the controls or showing a banner adjusts it rather than breaking it.


AND WHEN THE PAGE SCROLLS
-------------------------
It remeasures on scroll too, throttled through requestAnimationFrame.

That was the part a height alone would not have fixed: this page sits inside the Operations
shell and the page itself can still scroll, which moves the box up the window and would
have put the bar out of sight again.

A floor of 120px stops the box collapsing to nothing if it scrolls right up.


UNCHANGED
---------
The sticky date header at the top of the Gantt. It was already correct and is untouched.
