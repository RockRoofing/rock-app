YOUR QUESTION: YES, THERE WAS A DOUBLE COUNT
----------------------------------------------
Bills out pays EVERY outstanding Xero bill - including overhead ones: insurance,
rent, accountancy, hire. The Overheads column then pays the PREDICTED figure for
the same month. The same cost left the bank twice.

    insurance predicted 4,000, real bill 4,000 due 15 Sept
      BEFORE  bills 4,000 + overheads 4,000 = 8,000
      AFTER   bills 4,000 + overheads     0 = 4,000

Every bill has carried lineCodes from the sync all along - the account codes on
its lines - and nothing was reading them. Now netted per overhead code within the
week or month, using the same code list the API builds the predicted grid from.

NETTED, NOT DROPPED. A month with no bill yet still pays the full budget:

    predicted 4,000, bill 1,500 -> bills 1,500 + overheads 2,500 = 4,000

Applied to the 13-week and the 12-month.


YOUR SECOND QUESTION - NO, DO NOT HOLD OFF
--------------------------------------------
Switching a month to Actual is the RIGHT thing to do as soon as the month is
closed, and it is now safe:

  - the Forecast P&L takes revenue AND cost from Xero together for that month,
    which is the only way the two sides tie
  - the cash flow nets real bills against predicted overheads either way

Holding off does not protect you from anything - it just leaves the P&L reading a
budget when Xero has the real figure. What was unsafe was the double count, and
that is what this fixes.

THE ONE THING TO AVOID is switching a month to Actual BEFORE the purchase ledger
is complete. Then Xero's cost of sales is understated, and the P&L will show a
margin that is too good. Wait until the month end accounts are closed - which is
what you were already proposing - and switch it then.
