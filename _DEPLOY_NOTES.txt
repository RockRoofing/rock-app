ANSWERING THE QUESTION FIRST
----------------------------
NO - it works on forecasts already saved. Nothing needs re-entering.

The current save has always written `amount` onto every material line, so existing
forecasts carry their figures. The pkg617 fallback was for edge cases only.

The reason materials were not coming through is different, and worse.


ONE BILL KILLED EVERYTHING
--------------------------
    const hasBill = projNamesWithBillThisWk.has(normName(fc.projectName))
    const mOut = hasBill ? 0 : (fc.matItems || [])...

All or nothing. ONE real bill on a project - any bill, any amount - discarded
EVERY forecast cost for that project that week. Labour and materials both.

    a 600 bill on the project  ->  materials counted 0, labour counted 0
                                   a 65,630 materials payment, gone

That is why the cost columns were thin while sales ran high, and why the closing
balance climbed to 668k. The suppression was meant to stop double counting, but it
was throwing away the whole forecast to avoid counting a fraction of it twice.

NOW THE BILL IS NETTED OFF instead:

    labour 8,727   materials 65,630   (bill applied to labour first)

The 600 bill is already counted in Bills out, so nothing is double counted - only
the part of the forecast the bill actually covers is removed. Applied to labour
first because a project bill is far more often subcontract labour than materials.


ALSO REMOVED A DUPLICATE CALCULATION
------------------------------------
The same figures were being worked out twice in that loop - once for the
breakdown and once for the totals - so the drill-down and the column could
disagree. Now computed once and used for both.
