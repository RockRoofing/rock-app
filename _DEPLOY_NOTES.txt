pkg342 - "Assign to (current user)" was saving nobody
======================================================

REPLACES pkg302 through pkg341. Deploy this one only.

CHANGED FILES
-------------
  pages/crm.js
  pages/api/crm.js        returns who is logged in
  lib/crmImportMap.js     unchanged since pkg305


GOOD SPOT - AND IT WAS EXACTLY THAT
-------------------------------------
The Assign to dropdown on the add-activity form read:

    <option value="">(current user)</option>

The LABEL said current user. The VALUE was an empty string. So leaving it alone - the
obvious thing to do - saved the activity with nobody responsible, and Person Responsible
came through blank.

Worse, the page had no idea who was logged in. It never asked. So "(current user)" could
not have worked even in principle; there was nothing behind it.


THE FIX
-------
  - the CRM now knows who is logged in, from the portal session
  - the dropdown's first entry is your actual name: "James McVeigh (you)"
  - it is PRESELECTED, so an activity is assigned to you unless you change it
  - "Nobody" is still there as a deliberate choice rather than an accident


THIS ALSO EXPLAINS THE PERSON FILTER TROUBLE
----------------------------------------------
Several rounds back, your activity vanished behind a saved "person: James McVeigh" filter.
That is why: every activity you created had NO person on it, so a person filter could never
match one. Both symptoms, one cause.


ONE THING WORTH KNOWING
------------------------
The rest of the dropdown is a hard-coded list of five first names - Roman, Niall, James,
Simon, Edita - written when the CRM was a prototype. It is not your portal users.

So you can assign to yourself correctly now, but assigning to a colleague is limited to
those five, by first name only. Worth replacing with the real portal user list, which would
also let the Activities tab's person filter line up with actual people. Say the word - it is
a small job and I did not want to bundle it into a fix you are waiting on.
