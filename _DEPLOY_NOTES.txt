THE LIMITS NOW AUTO-SAVE
------------------------
They saved on BLUR only. So a figure typed and then navigated away from - or
committed with Enter, or left while you went to look at something else - never
reached the server. And nothing on screen said either way, which is why it did
not feel like it was saving.

Now: saved automatically 0.7 seconds after you stop typing, with the blur handler
kept as an immediate flush for when you tab straight out.

ONE SAVE PER CUSTOMER, not one per keystroke. Typing "300000" fires a single
write once you stop, carrying 300000 - checked.

THE WHOLE MAP IS WRITTEN each time, not just the one key. It is small, and a
partial write would race with anyone editing a different customer.

The value is passed explicitly into the debounced call rather than read back from
state or the ref - both would still hold the PREVIOUS value when the timer fires,
so it would save the figure you had typed over.


IT NOW TELLS YOU
----------------
Next to each limit box:

    saving      amber, while the write is in flight
    saved       green, for about two seconds
    NOT SAVED   red, and it stays

Without that there is no way to tell an auto-save from a lost keystroke - which
is exactly the position you were in.


ALSO IN THIS PACKAGE (pkg607)
-----------------------------
INVOICE FINANCE FIGURES COME FROM THE IF PAGE. The Cash Flow had its own older
model of the facility - flat 10% retention, the limit capping the advance instead
of the debt, no High Involvement, no caps, no age rule, and the old single drawn
setting. It could never have agreed. The IF page now publishes its position and
the Cash Flow reads it, with a panel saying where the numbers came from and when.

AS-AT DATES on every bank account, every card, the combined opening cash and the
drawn balance.

OPENING BALANCE ITEMISED - bank cash and card debt, then the facilities listed
separately under "available on top, not counted as opening cash". Facilities are
deliberately NOT added in: fold them into the opening figure and the 13-week line
can never go negative, which is the one thing it exists to show.

OVERDRAFT with no double counting, and one limit per card account.
