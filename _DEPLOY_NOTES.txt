1. THE OVERWRITE - this is the one that matters
-------------------------------------------------
The builder read the project's existing variations from settings.variations only.

The dashboard returns them on project.variations, and the tracker mirrors them onto
settings. So on any project where they came back the first way, the builder saw an EMPTY
list - and saving a new variation wrote a list of ONE over everything already there.

That is why previous variations were disappearing. It now reads both.

AND A BACKSTOP AT THE SERVER. The settings endpoint refuses a POST that would reduce the
variation count by more than one:

    5 -> 6   allowed
    5 -> 4   allowed (that is a deletion)
    5 -> 1   REFUSED

Refused rather than merged: merging would hide the caller's bug and leave two versions of
the truth. Any caller that gets it wrong now fails loudly instead of quietly destroying
the history.

Variations lost before this deploy are gone from the app and would need re-entering - the
sent PDFs are still whatever went to the customer.


2. THE INSTRUCTION PAGE FILLS ITSELF IN
-----------------------------------------
Name, role and company are prefilled from the handover for whoever the link was sent to,
and it says so - "Filled in from our records, please correct anything that is wrong."

Somebody confirming a variation should not have to type their own job title, and a
prefilled field comes back correct far more often than an empty one.


3. ONE PERSON IN "TO", THE REST IN "COPY IN"
----------------------------------------------
To is now a dropdown - one customer contact. The instruct link is issued to that address
and the instruction records who used it, so it has to be one person or the record names an
address rather than a person.

Everyone else - the customer's other contacts AND the Rock team - goes in Copy in, in two
labelled groups. They receive the variation and the confirmation, but cannot instruct it.


4. THE INSTRUCTION IS CONFIRMED BACK TO EVERYONE
--------------------------------------------------
When the customer instructs, everyone on the original - to, cc and the sender - receives:

    Subject: Instructed: Variation V01 - J205 Pets Choice
    Instructed by Jack Belshaw, Senior Quantity Surveyor, Barnfield Construction
    on 21/08/2026 at 09:14
    via the authenticated link sent to jack.belshaw@barnfieldconstruction.co.uk

with the PDF attached, showing the instruction ON the document. Both sides then hold the
same signed copy, which is the whole point of capturing it. Recipients are deduplicated.
