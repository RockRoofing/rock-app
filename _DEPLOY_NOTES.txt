1. STAMPS 30% SMALLER
---------------------
The APPROVED / CONSTRUCTION ISSUE stamps are now 30% smaller. They still scale with sheet
size, so they stay readable on a large drawing without dominating an A4. The distance from
the corner is unchanged, so they sit slightly further in from the edge than before.

Re-rendered and visually checked at A4, A4 rotated 90, and A1 landscape.

IMPORTANT: existing stamped copies are NOT regenerated. Anything already stamped keeps its
old, larger stamp until the status is toggled again. Use Select all -> Unmark Construction
Issue -> Mark Construction Issue to re-stamp a whole project at the new size.


2. CALCULATIONS - SELECT ALL (and bulk Construction Issue)
-----------------------------------------------------------
Calculations already had tickboxes and "Approve selected" but no way to tick everything.
It now has the same toolbar as Rock Drawings:

  "Select all (n)"                  everyone, including customers. Ticks every current
                                    calculation; flips to "Clear selection" once all are
                                    ticked. Superseded revisions are ignored.

  "Mark Construction Issue (n)"     internal only. Flips to "Unmark Construction Issue"
                                    when everything ticked is already marked.

For a customer this means: Select all -> Approve selected, in two clicks.

"Approve selected" only counts calculations where that customer is the assigned approver,
so a customer ticking everything will only approve the ones that are actually theirs.
