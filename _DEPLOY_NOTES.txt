WHAT WAS WRONG
--------------
The deduction was applied at the FIRST forecast month and then carried forward,
so it showed on August, September, October and November alike - and August's net
assets read -3,216 in a month that made 62,250 of profit.

NOW
---
Every month's balance sheet stands as it is. The WIP comes off ONCE, in the last
month of the financial year, which is the only date the adjustment is actually
made.

On your figures:

  Jul 26   reserves  56,460   after WIP  56,460
  Aug 26   reserves 118,710   after WIP 118,710
  Sept 26  reserves 289,113   after WIP 289,113
  Oct 26   reserves 363,511   after WIP 363,511
  Nov 26   reserves 401,391   after WIP 283,782   (-121,926 +4,317)

August returns to 118,710 and the -3,216 is gone. November still lands on the
283,782 you have now, so the year end is unchanged - only the months in between
stop carrying it.

The row is relabelled "less last completed WIP (Jul 26, at year end)" so it is
clear both which month's figure it is and when it is applied.
