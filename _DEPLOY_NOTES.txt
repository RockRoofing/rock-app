1. LOST REASON - the same class of fault as the close date
-----------------------------------------------------------
The Sales dashboard has a whole Lost Reasons panel built on this field. It was imported
(6,005 lost deals carry one) but was NOT in the CRM field schema, so there was no way to
set or edit it. Anything lost in the CRM had no reason, for ever, and that panel would
have decayed month by month exactly as the close date did.

Now:
  - Lost Reason is a field on the deal, in the Details group
  - Pressing LOST asks why before it closes the deal

The list is what you actually use, taken from your export - the 15 below cover 99% of
6,005 lost deals:

    Unable to make contact (2,667)      Project Not Suitable (1,220)
    Roofing package already let (1,183) Price (124)
    Alternative spec (117)              Project Shelved (116)
    Main Contractor not suitable (94)   No Time to Price (94)
    Duplicate (62)                      Gone with preferred, approved suppy chain (51)
    Package Contractor (49)             Main Contractor didn't secure (47)
    No Single Ply (29)                  No Feedback Given (25)
    No available labour resource, Lead Time, Design Liability

("suppy chain" is spelt that way in your data - matched deliberately so the existing 51
deals group with new ones instead of splitting into two near-identical categories.)

There is a "Something else..." option for anything not on the list, and a "Mark lost
without a reason" button. Blocking someone from closing a deal because a dropdown is
empty only teaches them to pick anything, so it is a prompt, not a gate.

ALSO FIXED WHILE IN THERE: a dropdown holding a value that is not one of its options used
to render BLANK. Your import has 110 distinct lost reasons, most of them one-off free
text. Without this, opening one of those deals would have shown an empty Lost Reason and
looked like the data had gone. Now the stored value is kept and shown. This applies to
every select field, not just this one.


2. A FILTER THAT MATCHED NOTHING
--------------------------------
On Sales (CRM), the Projects Priced panel has a "Project stage (multi-select)" filter with
chips: MC Unsecured, MC Secured, Negotiating, Variations, Review, MC Unsecured - Not
Priced.

Those are CRM pipeline stages. The filter was testing them against projectStage - which,
as you said, is a different thing entirely: Live Project / Contractor tendering, meaning
whether the customer is pricing or has secured.

So ticking any chip emptied the panel. Not an error, just nothing - which is the sort of
thing that gets read as "no projects priced this month".

Now filtered on stageName, the CRM stage, which is what the chips are. The same applies
to the zero-value warning list underneath it.

The Strike Rate panel was already correct - it uses stageName - which is why that page
matched Pipedrive to the pound when you tested it.

STILL OPEN: LINKING BY NAME
---------------------------
You asked the right question. The CRM links deals to companies and contacts BY NAME. The
export carries Organization ID and Contact person ID, which we do not import.

And yes - fixing it properly means the CRM needs its own stable ids for companies and
contacts, which it does not have today: those records are derived from the names on deals.
That is a real piece of work, not a patch, and it touches the Companies and Contacts tabs,
the delete/merge behaviour, and the import.

What actually breaks today: rename an organisation and its deals stop pointing at it. How
often that happens is your call. If it is rare, this can wait; if people rename companies
regularly, it should be scheduled properly rather than bolted on.
