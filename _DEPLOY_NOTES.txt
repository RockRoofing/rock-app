pkg510 - a busy cursor while the WIP saves
==========================================

Deploy after pkg509. One changed file: pages/wip.js. No new files.


WHY IT WAS NEEDED
-----------------
pkg504 took the loading screen away so the page stops jumping to the top. That was right,
but it left NOTHING to say a click had registered. On a slow save that reads as "nothing
happened", and the natural response is to click again.


THE CURSOR
----------
It turns to the busy pointer while a change is saving, and back when it lands.

The cursor is the honest place for this. It follows the pointer, so it is visible wherever
on a long page you were working - a spinner at the top of the screen would be somewhere
you are not looking, which is the problem pkg504 was fixing in the first place.


IT COVERS THE SAVE, NOT JUST THE REFRESH
------------------------------------------
The write is the slower half, and it is the half that happens while somebody is wondering
whether their click landed. Every save on the page goes through it:

    margin save and clear override
    Zero and Un-zero
    manual adjustments, added and removed

Manual adjustments already tracked their own busy state for the button; that is now
mirrored to the cursor rather than the two saying different things.


A COUNTER, NOT A FLAG
---------------------
Two quick edits in a row would otherwise have the first one finishing and clearing the
cursor while the second was still running. Counted up and down, so the cursor stays busy
until everything has settled.

Decremented in a finally, so a save that throws cannot leave the cursor stuck, and cleared
on unmount so it cannot follow you to another page.
