pkg458 - the toolbar search now narrows the Activities list too
===============================================================

Deploy after pkg457. One changed file: pages/crm.js. No new files.


TO YOUR QUESTION FIRST - THEY WERE NOT DUPLICATES
---------------------------------------------------
    TOOLBAR SEARCH     a jump-to. Matches project titles and company names, offers up to
                       eight suggestions, opens the one you pick. Filters nothing.

    ACTIVITIES SEARCH  a filter. Narrows the rows in front of you, matching across
                       project, activity text, assignee, company and customer name.

Different jobs, which is why both exist. But you are right that while you are ON the
Activities tab, typing a project name in the toolbar and having the list ignore you is
odd.


WHAT CHANGED
------------
The toolbar search now narrows the Activities list as well as offering its suggestions.
Both behaviours at once - the dropdown still appears, and you can still click through to
open a project.

The two boxes AND together, which is the useful way round:

    toolbar "pool"                 -> Pool Motors, Poole, Liverpool Road
    toolbar "pool" + activities "high" -> 131 High Street, Poole

The toolbar picks the project, the Activities box narrows within it.


AND IT SAYS SO
--------------
A chip appears beside Refresh: Search: "pool" with an x.

A filter you cannot see is how an empty table becomes a mystery - you would clear the
Activities box, still see nothing, and have no idea the toolbar was the cause. The x
clears the toolbar box from where you are looking.


NOT CHANGED
-----------
The Emails tab has its own search too, filtering on subject, sender and preview. Left
alone - say if you want the toolbar feeding that one as well.

And the toolbar search still only finds PROJECTS and COMPANIES, not contacts. Searching a
contact's name there finds nothing even though the CRM knows them. That is a real gap
rather than a duplication, and worth doing if people expect it to be a general search.
