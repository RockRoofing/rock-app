YOUR CIS QUESTION - YES
-----------------------
pkg653 wired it. Forecast labour is GROSS, so:

    J190 gross 25,585  ->  Labour out 20,468  +  CIS to HMRC 5,117

The 20% taken off the labour column IS the figure in the CIS column, paid on the
22nd of the month after. The two add back to the gross.


1 + 2. MATERIALS OUT AND LABOUR OUT
-----------------------------------
Both open the SAME per-project breakdown the Project sales figure opens - it

3. CIS TO HMRC
--------------
    Labour paid in Sept 26 -> HMRC 22/10/26
      AJ Roofing             bill, net                 4,000    1,000
      J190 - Russell Hill    forecast labour, gross   25,585    5,117
      J247 - Kilburn Square  forecast labour, gross   12,000    2,400
      Paid 22/10/26                                             8,517

THE BASIS IS NAMED ON EVERY LINE, because the two are not the same sum and that is
the whole reason this was wrong twice:

    bill, net              already net of CIS  ->  value x 20/80
    forecast labour, gross gross of CIS        ->  value x 20%

Grouped by the month the labour was paid, so you can see which month each HMRC
payment settles.


4. VAT IN

    VAT month   Where it came from                                  Amount
    Sept 26     Filed return - Box 5 as submitted                   18,193
    Oct 26      Estimated reclaim: forecast materials and bills
                that month x 20/120                                 14,513

Says outright which is fact and which is a forecast, and shows the actual
calculation for the estimate rather than just labelling it "est".
