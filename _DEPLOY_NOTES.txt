pkg428 - variations out of average value of projects priced
===========================================================

Deploy after pkg427. One changed file: pages/scorecard-crm.js. No new files.


YOUR QUESTION FIRST: NO, IT DOES NOT DOUBLE COUNT
---------------------------------------------------
A project priced at £180,000 in March and revised to £250,000 in June contributes
£250,000, ONCE. Not both, not the £70,000 uplift.

All of a project's pricing events in the window collapse to the LATEST one - the current
answer rather than the first guess. That is why the card says "from 14 projects" rather
than counting pricing events.

Proven in the same test as the change below: that project appears in the values list as
250000 and nothing else.


THE CHANGE
----------
Variations are now excluded, matching what you asked for on the secured card.

Worked example - two real projects and one variation that was priced then revised:

    with variations       3 projects, values [250000, 150000, 2253], average £134,084
    without variations    2 projects, values [250000, 150000], average £200,000

Judged on the DEAL's stage, not on anything held on the value-change record. That matters:
a record only carries a stage when it came from a stage move, so filtering on the record
would have caught variations that entered the stage and missed every variation that was
simply re-priced. Going via the deal catches both.


STILL INCLUDING VARIATIONS, DELIBERATELY
------------------------------------------
    Total value of work priced
    Total value of work secured

Both measure money, and a variation is money. Say the word if you want either changed, but
be aware the totals and the averages now count different things - dividing one by the
other will not give you the other.
