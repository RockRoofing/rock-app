THE INSTRUCTION NOW CARRIES WHO THEY ARE
------------------------------------------
The page asks for name, ROLE and COMPANY. Only the name is required, but a signature on a
variation is worth more when it says who signed it - "Jack Belshaw, Senior Quantity
Surveyor, Barnfield Construction" stands up in a way that "Jack" does not.

The email address is still taken from the LINK, not typed. That is the part that makes it
evidence.


ON THE PDF
----------
A bordered block above the footer:

    INSTRUCTED
    Instructed by Jack Belshaw, Senior Quantity Surveyor, Barnfield Construction
    on 21/08/2026 at 09:14
    via the authenticated link sent to jack.belshaw@barnfieldconstruction.co.uk
    Recorded from 82.14.220.7. This is a digital instruction and is retained as a
    record of authorisation.

Printed on the document itself rather than held only in the portal. The point of capturing
an instruction is that it can be produced later, and a record that exists only on a screen
we control is worth less than one on the document both sides hold.

Underneath it, as before: Raised 19/08/2026 at 14:32 by Roman Jarosz, with his number and
email.


ON BOTH SCREENS
---------------
VIEW on the tracker, and the variation opened in the builder, both show the same block -
and both show it plainly when there is NOT one:

    INSTRUCTED                      who, when, which address
    SENT - NOT YET INSTRUCTED       when it went, to whom, when it was chased
    DRAFT - NOT SENT

"No instruction" is the answer to "can we start", and it is what somebody opens the window
to check.

The builder also gets a Download PDF link on the open variation, so the document with its
instruction is one click from where you are working.


THE LEFT-HAND LIST
------------------
Already marks instructed - the blue INSTRUCTED badge went in with pkg517, alongside SENT,

ONE THING TO KNOW
-----------------
Variations instructed BEFORE this deploy have no role or company recorded - there was
nowhere to enter them. They will show the name, the date and the email address, which is
still the substance of it. Anything instructed from here carries all of it.
