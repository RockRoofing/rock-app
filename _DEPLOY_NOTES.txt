1. REVENUE OVERRIDE
-------------------
"Revenue this period" is now editable, same as Labour and Materials on site.
Override / type / Reset, calculated figure shown underneath, teal border when
overridden. All three overrides now share one component.

It drives the CASH only - month spread, sales payment schedule, sales-in stream,
and the revenueThisPeriod saved on the forecast, which is what the chart reads.

It does NOT rewrite the percentages. Gross to date and the labour and materials
positions still show the true measured position, and the next forecast still
carries on from the real numbers. If a typed revenue fed back into percentages,
one adjustment would move what the FOLLOWING forecast thinks has been claimed and
the error would compound. thisCertTotal is still saved as the calculated value.


2. "REMAINING" NOW MEASURES FROM THE PREVIOUS CERTIFICATE
---------------------------------------------------------
The grey line under each box was budget less EVERYTHING INCLUDING what you were
typing, so it shrank as you worked and duplicated what the figure above already
told you.

It now reads budget less the PREVIOUS certificate, and says which one:

  "GBP 225,511.46 left after App 4"

So it is headroom - how much is left to claim in this period - and it holds still
while you type.

  Revenue     salesBudgetTotal   - prevGross
  Labour      labourBudgetTotal  - prevLabourToDate
  Materials   materialsBudgetTotal - materialsUsedPrior

All three budgets include instructed variations, as before.


3. YOUR QUESTION - WHAT HAPPENS WHEN AN APPLICATION IS RAISED AFTER A FORECAST
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Right now: NOTHING, and it is worse than it sounds.

The chart is built only from saved forecasts, plus retention releases. Real
applications never enter it at all. So when a forecast period passes and you
raise the real application:

  - the forecast's GUESSED revenue stays in the chart, at its guessed date,
    for ever
  - the real application's actual value and its known payment due date never
    appear
  - the chart keeps showing a prediction that has already been overtaken

The percentages do self-correct - the NEXT forecast picks the application as its
prior, because prior is whichever certificate ends last. So the measured position
recovers. The stale CASH does not.

Not fixed in this package - see the message accompanying it. It needs a decision
from you first, because "auto change the forecast" can mean two quite different
things and one of them destroys what you predicted.
