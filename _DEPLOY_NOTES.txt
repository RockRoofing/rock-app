WHAT'S NEW (Ops -> Projects -> Pre-Start Minutes)

1. DESIGN APPROVALS - new question:
   "Who from the customer's organisation or stakeholders needs access to the Rock Roofing
    Design Portal?"
   Add each person as a row: First name, Last name, Email, Phone, Company. Repeatable.

2. NEW SECTION "Meeting Actions":
   Each action has: Task, Required Resolution Date, and Person Responsible.
   The Person Responsible dropdown lists BOTH:
     - Rock Roofing portal users, and
     - the customer attendees entered at the top of the meeting ("Customer's Team").
   When an action is assigned to a Rock Roofing portal user, a LIVE PROJECT TASK is created for
   that person (with the task text, due date = resolution date) at the moment you use
   "Save & send". Actions assigned to a customer attendee are recorded in the minutes only.

Both new items appear in the emailed/downloaded Pre-Start PDF.

NOTES
- Live tasks are created on SAVE & SEND only (not on draft auto-save), so nothing is created
  until the minutes are actually issued.
- The customer attendee options come from the "Customer's Team" list in Meeting Details, so fill
  that in first for those options to appear.
