pkg607 - Cash Flow: invoice finance comes FROM the IF page, plus overdraft,
          per-card limits and as-at dates
============================================================================

*** SUPERSEDES pkg591 - pkg606. Three files. ***


CHANGED FILES (replace)
-----------------------
  pages/business-financials/cashflow.js
  pages/business-financials/invoice-finance.js
  pages/api/business-financials.js


1. THERE WERE TWO DIFFERENT MODELS OF THE FACILITY
--------------------------------------------------
The Cash Flow was NOT reading the Invoice Finance page. It had its own, older
calculation at business-financials.js:748 - a flat 10% retention deduction, the
insured limit capping the ADVANCE instead of the debt, no High Involvement, no
eligibility caps, no age rule, and the old single `drawn` setting rather than the
dated history.

It could never have agreed with the IF page. Two availability figures on two
pages is worse than one that is slightly wrong.

Now: the IF page PUBLISHES its computed position (config:if-position) whenever
the numbers settle, and the Cash Flow reads it. One calculation, one answer.

The "Invoice finance limit" and "Invoice finance drawn" boxes are GONE from the
Cash Flow, replaced by a panel that says where the figures came from:

    Invoice finance - from the Invoice Finance page
    Funded GBP 172,776 - drawn GBP 122,711 as at 29/08/26
    Available GBP 50,065 - updated 30/08/26

Publishing happens on load, not on a button - the Cash Flow must not depend on
somebody remembering to press something on another page. If you have not opened
the IF page since deploying, the panel says so.


2. AS-AT DATES ON EVERY BALANCE
-------------------------------
Each bank account, each card, and the combined opening cash now carry the date
they were read from Xero. No date shows "no balance date - press Refresh
balances", so a stale figure is obvious rather than being taken as today's cash.
The drawn balance carries its own date from the IF page.


3. OPENING BALANCE - WHAT IT IS MADE OF
---------------------------------------
Under the opening cash box, the components are itemised:

    Bank cash            GBP x
    Card debt            GBP y   already owed, not cash
    Available on top, not counted as opening cash:
    Invoice finance      GBP a   (drawn as at dd/mm/yy)
    Card headroom        GBP b
    Overdraft            GBP c
    Max cash available   GBP z

FACILITIES ARE LISTED BUT NOT ADDED IN. Opening balance is CASH. Fold headroom
into it and the 13-week line can never go negative, which is the one thing the
forecast exists to show.


4. OVERDRAFT AND PER-CARD LIMITS (from pkg606)
----------------------------------------------
Overdraft limit field, with no double counting - drawn overdraft already shows as
a negative bank balance, so headroom is the limit LESS what is used:

  bank -20,000  limit 20,000  ->  headroom 0, max cash -20,000
  (adding the limit on top would have shown 45,000 of headroom on a fully drawn
   overdraft)

One limit per card account, because a pooled figure hides which card is full.


TWO WHITELIST BUGS FOUND
------------------------
The finance config is whitelisted on BOTH the load and the save. Anything not
named in each is silently dropped - the overdraft and card limits had to be added
to both or they would have been accepted on screen and never stored. Same shape
of fault as the invoice finance settings whitelist earlier.
