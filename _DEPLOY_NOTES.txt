MY MISTAKE - THE IMPORT WROTE SOMEWHERE NOTHING READS
-------------------------------------------------------
Your activities almost certainly DID upload. Nothing displayed them.

The CRM reads activities and notes from arrays ON the deal object - deal.activities and
deal.notes. I wrote the import to the separate per-deal keys agreed in the earlier scoping
(crm:activities:<dealId>) and never wired the read side, and I did not check how the board
gets its data before shipping it. That is on me.

WHY NOT JUST PUT THEM ON THE DEAL? I measured it: deals + activities + notes merged into
the single crm:deals key comes to 31.2MB, and that key is read in full every time the CRM
page opens. That is not viable. So the split storage was right - the read side was missing.

NOW:
  - the import also writes a small per-deal SUMMARY (open count + next due date).
    234KB for all 6,261 projects, loaded with the board.
  - the kanban dot and the "next activity" column use that summary.
  - opening a deal fetches THAT deal's activities and notes and merges them into the
    view, including into the History feed. Note HTML is converted to plain text.
