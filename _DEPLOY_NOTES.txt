pkg402 - remove the deal count and total from the CRM toolbar
=============================================================

Deploy after pkg401. One changed file: pages/crm.js. No new files.

"471 deals - £35,160,191 open" is gone from the right-hand end of the black toolbar.

The calculation behind it was removed too, not just the display - it summed the value of
every open deal on every render, and leaving that running to produce a number nobody sees
would be waste.

The per-stage counts and totals on the kanban column headers are untouched. Those are the
ones that tell you something.
