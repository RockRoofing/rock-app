THE SPREAD TOTALLED 90%
-----------------------
    saved      2026-08: 60,  2026-09: 40
    columns    2026-09,      2026-10
    result     2026-09: 40 (carried from the old AUGUST column)
               2026-10: 50 (evened)
               total 90%  ->  4,176.84 of the period simply gone

The re-even guard compared how MANY keys there were, not WHICH:

    Object.keys(spread).length === periodMonths.length

Two old keys, two new keys, so it decided nothing had changed - and part-carried
percentages into columns they were never meant for. Entirely my fault from
pkg684, and it looked plausible: 16,707 in October was a believable figure.

Now compares the KEYS themselves, and re-evens whenever they differ OR the
carried-over figures do not add to 100.

    keys changed     -> 50 / 50, total 100%
    keys unchanged   -> 60 / 40 kept, total 100%
    column added     -> 33 / 33 / 34, total 100%


AND IT NOW SAYS WHEN A SPREAD DOES NOT ADD UP
-----------------------------------------------
    Sales spread totals 90% - GBP 4,176.84 of this period is not in the forecast.

The per-row Total already went red, but a red 90% is easy to read past. Naming the
money makes it much harder to ignore - and that is what would have caught this
without you having to spot it.
