DEPLOY NOTES - Design Portal PHASE 1: Designer role + Design area
================================================================

Part 1 of a phased build of the Design Portal.

FILES
  EDIT: lib/roles.js            (new "designer" role + "design" area access)
  EDIT: pages/index.js          (new Design card on the portal home)
  EDIT: pages/admin.js          (helper text mentions Designer)
  NEW : pages/design/index.js   (Design portal landing page)  <-- ADD via GitHub

WHAT THIS DOES
  - Adds a new permission level "Designer" (selectable in Admin > Users; it appears in
    the role dropdown automatically).
  - Designer can access ONLY the Design area - nothing else. Standard Post-Contract
    (plus Management and Admin) also get access to Design, as requested.
  - New "Design" card on the portal home, shown only to those roles.
  - New /design landing page listing the portal's pages. Each page is a placeholder
    ("SOON") for now - the pages themselves are built in later phases. Internal-only
    tools (Tech Sub Builder, Leak Test Cert Builder) are hidden from non-internal users.

WHAT'S NOT IN THIS PHASE (coming next)
  Permissions matrix, external customer users, then each Design page in turn (RFIs,
  drawings + markup, tech sub builder/view, calculations, leak tests, warranties, O&Ms).
