THE BUG YOU JUST FOUND
----------------------
You reopened J243, moved it, marked it Won - and it did not appear. That is not the data.
That is a fault in the CRM.

Marking a deal Won or Lost set the status and wrote a history line. It never stamped
WHEN. The dashboard and scorecards build their close date from won_time / lost_time, so a
deal decided in the CRM had no close date at all, and every date-filtered view dropped it.

Imported deals were unaffected - they carry the date from Pipedrive. So ONLY deals decided
in the CRM went missing, which is the hardest kind of gap to spot: the numbers look
plausible, they are just quietly short.

Every deal you have marked won or lost in the CRM since it went live is affected.


FIXED
-----
Won now stamps won_time. Lost stamps lost_time. Reopening clears both, so a deal that goes
won -> reopened -> lost does not keep a stale won date hanging off it.


AND THE ONES ALREADY BROKEN
---------------------------
The history recorded the date at the time, so it is recoverable.

    Go to /crm-scores  ->  "Repair close dates"  ->  press the button

No file needed. It reports how many were recovered, how many already had a date, and how
many were decided with no history entry to date them from. Only fills blanks - it never
changes a date that is already there. Safe to run twice.

A deal won, reopened and then lost takes the LOST date, not the stale won one. Tested.

Do this before you compare against Pipedrive again, or the comparison will still be short.


ON J243 SPECIFICALLY
--------------------
Worth knowing separately: J243 - Christchurch School - V01 Therapy Building sits in the
Review stage with Variation = No, at 12,421. Every one of its nineteen siblings - the
J-numbered V01, V02, V03 variations - sits in Variations with Variation = Yes. That looks
like a mis-set stage rather than anything the system did.

Once the close date is repaired it will appear in whichever stage it is actually in.
