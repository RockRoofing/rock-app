WHAT WAS ACTUALLY WRONG
------------------------
Applied for read grossCurrent - the account BEFORE main contractor's discount.

Retention is not charged on that. On a certificate MCD comes off first and
retention is charged on the sub-total after it. So Applied for was overstated by
the discount, and every retention figure derived from it was overstated by the
same proportion.

On a job at 437,000 gross with 5% MCD and 3% retention:

  Applied for   was 437,000   now 415,150
  Retention     was  13,110   now  12,455

It now reads current.netBeforeRet - MCD taken off whatever it applies to,
retention still inside it. That is the exact line the certificate shows above
the retention deduction, so the tracker and the application agree.

YES, IT INCLUDES RETENTION
---------------------------
You asked me to check. netBeforeRet is the figure BEFORE retention is deducted,
so retention is inside it and is not applied twice. mkCol() in lib/applications.js
computes retention FROM netBeforeRet, which is the same basis.

MCD is also handled properly per project: some contracts charge it on the
contract sum only, valuing variations and materials separately. netBeforeRet
respects the mcdOnVariations and mcdOnMaterials flags stamped on each
application, so a split-MCD job is right too.

CACHE MARKER BUMPED
-------------------
The dashboard cache validity check now requires appliedForNetOfMcd. Without it a
cache written by the old code would keep serving the old figures for up to four
hours and the fix would look like it had not deployed.
