WHAT CHANGED
------------
Every money figure in the Commercial portal now shows two decimal places.

    £17,534    ->    £17,533.70
    £996       ->    £996.00
    £0         ->    £0.00

Fifteen formatters across seven files. Most pages shared one helper, so a single change
covered the whole page.


ALREADY CORRECT, LEFT ALONE
---------------------------
Applications for Payment, Outstanding Invoices, Variations and Contracted Rates were
already showing pence. Nothing to do there.


DELIBERATELY NOT CHANGED - THREE CHART AXES
---------------------------------------------
Three formatters on pages/project/[id].js use compact notation for chart axis labels and
tick marks - "£1.2M", "£450K". Pence on those would read "£1.20M" and make the axis
unreadable without telling you anything.

They are labels on a graph, not figures you would reconcile against Xero. If you want them
changed too, say so - but I would leave them.


ONE THING WORTH KNOWING
-----------------------
Some totals may now show a penny or two of rounding that was previously hidden. A column
of figures each rounded to the pound could add up to a total that looked wrong by a few
pounds; with pence showing, those will reconcile exactly. If a total looks like it has
changed, it has not - you are just seeing what was always there.
