WHY THEY WERE DIFFERENT
------------------------
The link's styling had been COPIED into all eight files rather than shared. They were
close but never identical, and they drifted further apart every time one got touched -
most recently when the Operations nav went to 17px, because that one link inherits the
nav's link style and the other seven do not.

Rather than paste the Operations styling into seven more files (which would drift again),
there is now ONE component:

  components/ReportImprovementLink.js

All eight places render that. Change it once and every location changes together. It
cannot go out of step again.


WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE
-------------------
Exactly the Operations portal version, everywhere:

  gold #ca8a04, 17px, warning icon, plain text - NOT a button
  padding 6px 12px, no underline

Locations updated: Portal home, Operations, Pre-Contract, Commercial, Business Financials,
Business nav, Design portal, Project page.

WORTH A LOOK: 17px is noticeably larger than the surrounding link text in the navs OUTSIDE
Operations - those rows are still 13px, because only the Operations nav was resized. So the
link will now be the biggest text in those bars. That is what "same as Operations" means,
but if it looks out of place, tell me and I will either size it to sit with each nav or
bring the other navs up to 17px.

The SITE APP link is deliberately NOT included - you asked for that one to be orange,
underlined and phone-sized, and it stays that way. It is noted in the component file so it
does not get folded in by mistake later.
