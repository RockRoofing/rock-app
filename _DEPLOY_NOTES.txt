ROOT CAUSE - "TODAY" WAS HARD-CODED
-------------------------------------
The CRM was not working out today's date at all:

    const today = PREVIEW_TODAY;          // '2026-07-09'

PREVIEW_TODAY lives in lib/crmSeedDeals.js, the demo seed file, and was baked in when that
preview data was generated back in July. It never moved. So for the last five weeks the
whole board has been comparing every due date against 9 July.

Anything due between 9 July and today looked like it was still in the future - grey -
when it was actually overdue. That is exactly what you are seeing.

    due 20 Jul   ->  was GREY,  should be RED
    due 10 Aug   ->  was GREY,  should be RED
    due 15 Aug   ->  was GREY,  should be GREEN (today)

FIXED: it now uses the real date, and re-checks every minute so a screen left open
overnight rolls over rather than sticking on yesterday.

Deliberately NOT toISOString() - that converts to UTC and would show the previous day for
anyone looking during a BST evening.


ABOUT THE COLOURS YOU SPECIFIED
--------------------------------
You wrote:

    Grey: < Today        Green: Today        Red: > Today

Taken literally that means an activity due BEFORE today is grey and one due AFTER today is
red - which is the opposite of your own point that overdue activities should be showing
red. So I have left the logic as it stands, which is:

    RED    - due before today   (overdue)
    GREEN  - due today
    GREY   - due after today    (upcoming)

That matches what you actually described wanting, and it is what the code already did.
Nothing was wrong with the colour rules - only the date they were being compared against.

If you did mean it the other way round, say so and it is a two-line change.
