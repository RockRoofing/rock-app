WHAT IT DOES
------------
The current stage in the green bar now reads on two lines:

        Negotiating
          34 days

Visible without hovering, on the current stage only. Every other segment is
unchanged, and the hover tooltip still gives the day count for all of them.

TWO SMALL DECISIONS
-------------------
The bar is 30px rather than 22. Two lines will not fit in 22 without the type
becoming unreadable, and only the current stage carries a second line, so
nothing else in the bar changes shape.

Where there is no dated history the day count is HIDDEN rather than shown as
zero. Zero reads as "moved today", which would be wrong on an old deal that
simply has no recorded move. The tooltip says "no dated history" for those.
