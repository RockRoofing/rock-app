WHAT CHANGED
------------
Every page-level warning, notice and diagnostic now carries a class the print
stylesheet hides. On screen they behave exactly as before; in the PDF they are
gone.

Twelve tagged in total:

  13-week (9)   Invoice Finance behind your balances
                Overdue items are NOT in the forecast
                Placement check
                The build marker (pkg761 ... store synced ...)
                Could not read balances from Xero
                and four further amber notices further down the page

  Forecast BS (2)  Press "Read balance sheet from Xero"
                   Card accounts are not being matched

  Invoice Finance (1)  Applications with no customer name

ALSO: COLLAPSED DRILL-DOWNS
----------------------------
A closed <details> prints as its one-line summary and nothing else - "Click for
every invoice and where it landed", with no list under it. Those are hidden too.
Anything you leave OPEN still prints in full, so the drill-downs you actually
want in the pack are unaffected.

WHY THIS MATTERS BEYOND TIDINESS
---------------------------------
These messages exist to prompt an action while you are looking at the page.
Printed into a document that goes to a bank, an accountant or a prospective
client they read as faults in the figures - and by the time anyone reads the PDF
they may already have been dealt with.

The underlying figures are unchanged. Only what is displayed on paper.
