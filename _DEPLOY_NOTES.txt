THE NEW ROW
-----------
"Income / month", directly under Total OUT / week and sticky with the others, so
it stays visible while you scroll the projects.

Income = sales in + retention in.

Each month is one continuous coloured band across the columns it covers, cycling
through six colours so no two neighbours match. The month name and total sit in
the band. Works in both day and week view.


TWO THINGS I DECIDED
--------------------
A WEEK COLUMN CAN STRADDLE TWO MONTHS. It is assigned to the month holding the
MAJORITY of its days - its middle day - so the band breaks where the month mostly
changes rather than on a technicality. The week of 28/09 to 04/10 lands in
October, which is where four of its seven days are.

THE FIGURE IS THE TRUE CALENDAR-MONTH TOTAL, summed from cashByDay, never the sum
of the columns in the band. Summing the band would silently mis-state a month
every time a week straddled a boundary - and it would look perfectly plausible.
Checked: bands total 52,000 against 52,000 of daily cash, with a straddling week
in play.

On a narrow band (under ~54px, so a single week column) the month name is dropped
and only the figure shows. A truncated month name is worse than none, and the
colour plus the tooltip still identify it.
