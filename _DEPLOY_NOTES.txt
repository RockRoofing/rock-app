TWO OTHER PLACES SAID THE OPPOSITE
------------------------------------
Worth flagging, because the wording only works if everything agrees.

The PDF banner read "FINAL ACCOUNT - this is the last application on this project", and
the email body read "This is our final account for this project." Both assert finality -
which is the one thing the new wording is careful not to do.

Both now say proposed, and that the payment is interim.


SHORT FORM WHERE THE LONG ONE WILL NOT FIT
--------------------------------------------
    filename    Proposed Final Account 14 - J178 Gas Lane.pdf
    list badge  FINAL ACCOUNT

58 characters is unusable in a filename and impossible in a badge. The documents the
customer reads carry the full phrase; the places only you see carry the short one.


THE HEADING SCALES TO FIT
-------------------------
At 20pt the full title runs off the page. It now shrinks until it fits the width available
- about 14pt - rather than being cut off. Floor of 11pt so it stays readable.
