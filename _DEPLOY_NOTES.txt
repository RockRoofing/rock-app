?queue=0  - THE ONE THAT MATTERS
--------------------------------
Files the matches. COUNTS the rest instead of queueing them.

Without it, a 24-month backfill across four mailboxes would push roughly 10,000 rows at a
queue that caps at 1,000 - so most would fall off the end anyway, and the ones that stayed
would bury the genuine suggestions from your day-to-day mail.

Nothing is lost silently. The counts come back in the response, and ?detail=1 still lists
every subject line with its outcome whether you queue or not.


?until=  - RUN IT IN CHUNKS
---------------------------
    ?since=2025-01-01&until=2025-04-01

Bounds the far end of the window as well as the near one, so you can work through two years
a quarter at a time without re-fetching everything before it on each run. Graph does the
filtering, so each chunk is quick.


THE SEQUENCE I WOULD USE
------------------------
Per mailbox, oldest quarter first. Dry run, glance at it, then run it for real:

    ...crm-email-sync?dryRun=1&detail=1&queue=0&since=2024-09-01&until=2024-12-01&mailbox=edita@rockroofing.co.uk
    ...crm-email-sync?detail=1&queue=0&since=2024-09-01&until=2024-12-01&mailbox=edita@rockroofing.co.uk

Then move the dates on a quarter and repeat. Eight windows per mailbox for two years, four
mailboxes - about thirty URLs. Tedious, but each one takes seconds and you can stop and
look at any point.

If a chunk comes back with fetched at or near your max, narrow the window and re-run it -
that is the one sign a chunk did not see everything.

Re-running any window is harmless. Writes de-duplicate on message id, the watermark is
never moved by a `since` run, and the 15-minute sync carries on throughout.
