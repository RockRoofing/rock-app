1. THE MESSAGE ITSELF IN GREEN
--------------------------------
The part somebody actually wrote is in the CRM green (#1c704f). Everything the email drags
along with it - the quoted thread below, the signature block, the legal footer - is grey
underneath, separated by a line.

    Hi Roman,                                            <- green
    Forgot to mention, we agreed the separate visit.
    Kind Regards
    Jack Belshaw
    Senior Quantity Surveyor
    ------------------------------------------------
    01282 442 300                                        <- grey
    From: Roman Jarosz <roman@rockroofing.co.uk>
    Sent: 16 August 2026

Split on the first marker that ends a message: "From:", "On ... wrote:", Outlook's
divider, "Sent from my iPhone". A sign-off keeps the two lines after it, so "Regards /
Jack Belshaw / Senior QS" stays with the message rather than being cut mid-signature.

Where it cannot tell, it errs towards MORE green. A sentence cut off half way is worse
than a line of signature carried into it.

One EmailCard serves both the deal timeline and the Emails tab, so both change together.


2. FILING TO WON AND LOST PROJECTS
------------------------------------
Two separate blocks, both fixed:

THE PROJECT PICKER listed open deals only. Won and lost projects now appear, marked with a
WON or LOST badge so an email is not filed against a job that finished two years ago in
mistake for the live one of the same name. Open deals still come first before you type.

THE SYNC validated every match against open deals only. So a deliberate [CRM-972] to a job
we had already won was rejected, and mail on a filed thread stopped landing the moment the
deal closed - which is exactly when the invoice chasing starts.


ONE THING I DELIBERATELY DID NOT CHANGE
-----------------------------------------
Matching by SUBJECT is still open-projects-only.

A reference or a known thread is somebody saying where an email goes. A title match is the
system guessing - and letting a closed project start capturing mail on a name it shares
with a live one would be a guess that gets worse over time, as the list of closed projects
grows and the live ones stay few.
