1. REAL INVOICES WERE IGNORED IN A FORECAST MONTH
--------------------------------------------------
A forecast month took revenue from the project forecasts ONLY. August showed NIL
against roughly 270,000 of invoices sitting in Xero - raised, real, and simply in
a month nobody had switched to actual yet.

An invoice is revenue on the date it is RAISED, whether or not the month has been
closed. Now included, on the invoice date rather than the due date.

NETTED, NOT ADDED. Where a month has both, the invoice has replaced the forecast:

    forecast 300,000, invoiced 270,000  ->  revenue 300,000, not 570,000

The forecast BALANCE SHEET does the same, or it would build debtors from forecasts
alone and diverge from the P&L - which is precisely what "Out by" exists to catch.


2. OVERHEADS WERE ACTUALS-TO-DATE, NOT THE BUDGET
--------------------------------------------------
    isCompleteMo = mo < nowKey && availableSet.has(mo)

August is before September and has Xero data, so it counted as COMPLETE and
returned what had posted so far - 12,198 against your 54,370 budget, because only
a fortnight of invoices had been entered.

Right for a cash flow, where it is what is left to pay. Wrong for a P&L.

A month is now complete only once it has been SWITCHED TO ACTUAL on Budgets - the
same switch that already decides everything else. Fixed at source, so the Forecast
P&L, the Forecast Balance Sheet and the 12-month all pick it up.


3. THE FLAT -876,101 FINANCING LINE IS CORRECT
-----------------------------------------------
It is the balancing figure - fixed assets, WIP, inventory, prepayments, deferred
tax, everything not modelled separately. On ACTUAL months it balances to Xero
exactly, which is why net assets tie. On forecast months it moves only by the
tracked repayments:

    Jul -876,101  ->  Nov -849,181  =  26,920 repaid over four months

That is your PAYE/CIS and HP schedule, and it is the only part being forecast.
