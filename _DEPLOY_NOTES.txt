WHAT'S NEW
  The 13-week cash flow now has a "Project forecast" column driven by the Commercial
  Project Cash Flow forecasts. Per week it shows the NET of forecast sales in minus
  forecast labour + materials out, and it's folded into Net and Closing cash and the
  13-week totals.

  GAP-FILL OVERLAP (no double-counting):
   - A project's forecast SALES are suppressed in any week where that project already
     has a REAL invoice (from Invoices Owed).
   - A project's forecast COSTS (labour + materials) are suppressed in any week where
     that project already has a REAL bill.
  So as real invoices/bills come in, the forecast for that project drops off those
  weeks automatically, leaving only the still-forecast periods.
