pkg498 - "Make Contact" panel on the Activities tab
===================================================

Deploy after pkg497. One changed file: pages/crm.js. No new files.


THE COLUMN
----------
"Mark Done" is now "Make Contact", which is what the button is actually for - you press it
because you are about to ring somebody, not because you have finished.


THE PANEL
---------
Wider (1,180px) and in two columns, with the timeline underneath:

    SUMMARY                    WHAT HAPPENED?
    Value, stage, organisation  (call notes, outcome)
    contact, phone, email
    job role, owner            SET THE NEXT ACTIVITY
                                what / when / who
    PROJECT DETAILS
    score, tender return,
    region, project type,
    estimator, lead source,
    site location, size m2,
    general information

    ---------------- TIMELINE ----------------
    email, notes, previous calls and changes, newest first

So everything you need while the phone is ringing is in front of you, instead of opening
the project in another tab to find the number and then losing your place.

Phone and email are links - tel: and mailto:. The panel is open because somebody is about
to make contact; the number should be one click, not a copy and paste.


READ-ONLY ON THE LEFT AND BELOW
---------------------------------
Deliberately. This is somewhere to LOOK while you talk. The only things you type are what
happened and what comes next, which is what the panel was always for - a second place to
edit the deal would be a way to change something by accident mid-call.


UNCHANGED WHERE IT SHOULD BE
------------------------------
Opened from inside a deal, the panel has no project to show you - you are already looking
at it. There it stays exactly as it was, at its original width.
