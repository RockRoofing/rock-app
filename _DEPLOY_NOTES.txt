THE CHANGE
===========
A Daily Site Diary is now matched to the day using its own "Site Diary Date"
answer, not the moment it was submitted.

It read:

    s.submittedAt && iso(new Date(s.submittedAt)) === dk

So a Monday diary written up on Tuesday morning counted as TUESDAY'S - Monday showed
missing, and Tuesday showed complete before Tuesday had happened. Anyone writing up
the week on a Friday produced four missing days and one day doing the work of five.

Same rule pages/forms/fill.js already uses to pre-select the crew from the Gantt for
the diary date, hoisted into lib/formDates.js. A form that pre-fills from a date is
now scored on the same date.


THE AWKWARD PART, AND HOW IT IS HANDLED
=========================================
The submission index deliberately holds NO answers - "so listing is cheap" - so the
diary date simply was not available to the page.

  GOING FORWARD: the date is resolved when the form is submitted and stored on the
  index entry as formDate. Resolved SERVER-SIDE from the form definition rather than
  sent by the browser, so it cannot drift if the client changes.

  ALREADY SUBMITTED: backfilled lazily. The first time Forms Missing runs, any diary
  in range without a formDate is read once, its date pulled out, and written back to
  the index. The cost is paid a single time per submission and never again.

  The backfill is keyed on the property being ABSENT, not falsy. A diary whose date
  question was left blank resolves to '' and must not be re-read on every load.

  A diary with no date answer at all still falls back to the submission date, so
  nothing gets worse than it is today.

The backfill is bounded to submissions within 30 days either side of the weeks being
viewed, so opening the page never walks the whole index.

Verified: a diary dated Monday and submitted Tuesday now marks MONDAY complete.
Before this change Monday read missing and Tuesday read complete.
