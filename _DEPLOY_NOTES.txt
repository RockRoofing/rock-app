NOT ANOTHER CYCLE - A SUPPLEMENT TO WHICHEVER ONE YOU PICK
------------------------------------------------------------
Materials paid on delivery while the works follow application terms is TWO payment
behaviours in one period. No single cycle can express that, so adding a fifth
option would have forced you to choose one and lose the other.

"Received on a specific day" sits under Sales received. Each line is a date, an
amount and a note. They come OFF THE TOP; the remainder spreads by the cycle you
have chosen.

    Moseley Hall, revenue 284,478.29
      14/02/27   80,000.00   materials on delivery
      remainder 204,478.29 spreads Feb/Mar/Apr/May at 40/30/25/5
        Feb 81,791   Mar 61,343   Apr 51,120   May 10,224

A dated receipt is VALUED on the day it is received, so the P&L and the balance
sheet see it in the right month too - not just the cash flow.


IT WILL NOT LET YOU OVER-ALLOCATE
---------------------------------
Put more on fixed dates than the period is worth and the remainder would go
negative and spread as a credit. The line under the editor turns red and says so:

    120,000 of 84,478 on fixed dates - MORE than this period, so the remainder
    would go negative. Reduce it.

Otherwise it tells you what is left to spread, so the two halves always add up in
front of you.
