1. OPENING CASH SHOWED 0 WITH A BALANCE ON SCREEN ABOVE IT
----------------------------------------------------------
The editor writes to local state and saves, but the CALCULATIONS read
data.manualBalances - the server copy - and `data` is not refetched after a save.
So the totals ignored everything you had just typed until a full page reload.
Now read from local state.

  opening cash   was 0.00   now 2,500.52


2. CARD BALANCES WERE READ AS MONEY IN THE CARD
-----------------------------------------------
You typed Capital on Tap 77,156.42 as a positive - which is how a statement reads
- but the page treats a negative card balance as debt. So card debt came out at
ZERO and the whole 160,000 limit showed as headroom.

Cards are now entered as the AMOUNT OWED, positive, and held negative internally.
The field is labelled "owed" so there is no ambiguity.

  card debt        was 0.00        now 77,156.42
  card headroom    was 160,000.00  now 82,843.58
  max cash         was 266,188     now 191,532


3. THE DOUBLE COUNT - YOU WERE RIGHT
------------------------------------
HOW MONEY IN IS BUILT:

    moneyIn = invoicesIn + retIn + vatInPos + fcSalesIn

    invoicesIn   unpaid sales invoices, by expected or due date
    retIn        retention releases from the tracker
    vatInPos     VAT refunds
    fcSalesIn    project cash flow forecasts from Commercial

There WAS a guard, but a weak one: a project's forecast sales were suppressed only
if a real invoice landed in the SAME WEEK. An application invoiced in week 2 whose
forecast scheduled cash in week 6 was counted TWICE - once as the invoice, once as
the forecast. That is most of why 1,407,928 looks high against a 468k ledger.

Now a forecast whose PERIOD HAS ALREADY BEEN APPLIED FOR is dropped entirely -
the same supersede rule the project cash flow uses. Drafts do not count as applied
for.

  J240 forecast to 31/07, applied for to 31/08   ->  dropped, it is an invoice now
  J240 forecast to 30/09                          ->  kept, still future
  J251 no applications                            ->  kept

On a three-forecast example that is 192,766 down to 100,000.

Expect Total money in to FALL substantially, and to be closer to reality.
