pkg449 - the deal and the Activities list disagreeing about a date
==================================================================

Deploy after pkg448. One changed file: pages/crm.js. No new files.


WHY THE TWO SCREENS DISAGREED
-----------------------------
An imported activity exists in two places at once: the store it lives in, and a cut-down
copy merged onto the deal when you open it.

The store holds  subject, dueDate, createdBy, the Pipedrive id, type...
The deal holds   id, text, due, done, assignee, type

Note DUEDATE against DUE. The deal reads dueDate into due when it loads, and drops the
original. So when pkg447 started writing the deal's version back, it saved `due` and threw
`dueDate` away - and every reader looks for `dueDate` FIRST.

Result: one screen showed the new date, the other showed the old one, and which was which
depended on where you were standing. Exactly what you described - the deal saying no due
date while the Activities list showed one.

It was also quietly discarding everything else the store held that the deal does not map:
the Pipedrive id, who created it, the activity type.


FIXED
-----
The deal now READS the store and MERGES into it, field by field, rather than overwriting
it with its own smaller version. Nothing the deal does not know about is lost, and the
date is written to BOTH names so no reader can pick up a stale one.

Verified on the bench: after an edit, dueDate and due agree, and the Pipedrive id, creator
and type all survive.


AND BOTH DIRECTIONS NOW UPDATE BOTH SCREENS
---------------------------------------------
Edit on the DEAL      -> the Activities list updates immediately
Edit in the ACTIVITIES list -> the deal in memory updates immediately

Before, each only told its own screen and the other caught up on the next refresh, if at
all. That is the second half of "it doesn't always reflect the change".

Completing one from the deal now also removes it from the Activities list straight away,
rather than leaving it sitting there until a refresh.


WHAT TO EXPECT
--------------
Any activity currently showing two different dates is holding two different values in the
two stores. Edit it once after deploying and both will be written together, which settles
it. There is no way to tell from here which of the two was the one you meant, so it needs
the edit rather than something I can repair automatically.
