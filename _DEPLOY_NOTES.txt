  pages/api/sync-invoices.js

THE CAUSE
---------
Your Xero export shows Gas Lane retention across TWELVE applications:

    Jul 24     43.39        Mar 25    326.94
    Nov 24  10,967.15       Apr 25  1,128.67
    Jan 25   2,048.22       May 25    246.11
    Jan 25     360.45       Jun 25    695.83
    Feb 25     721.31       Jul 25    297.36
                            Aug 25    268.24
                            Oct 25    213.87
                                    ----------
                            TOTAL  17,317.54

The invoice sync only RE-FETCHES invoices inside its window. Anything outside is kept
from what is already stored - it is not read from Xero again.

retention612 was added to this sync AFTER many of those lines were first stored. Lines
stored before it existed have no retention612 at all, and the total treats a missing
value as zero.

Gas Lane's most recent application is October 2025. On the default SIX MONTH window, not
one of its twelve applications is re-fetched:

    window   apps re-fetched   612 recovered
     6 mo         0 / 12          £0
    12 mo         2 / 12        £482
    18 mo         8 / 12      £3,898
    24 mo        11 / 12     £17,274
    30 mo        12 / 12     £17,317

The Retention page's own sync button was set to 12 months, which recovers £482 of £17,317.
That is the £996 you are seeing - a fragment, from whichever lines happened to be
re-fetched at some point.

This is NOT a Xero problem. Your figures there are right.


FIXED
-----
The sync cap was 24 months, which was not even enough to repair Gas Lane - it starts July
2024. Now 60. The dropdown on the sync bar offers 3, 6, 12, 18, 24, 36 and 48 months.


DO THIS AFTER DEPLOYING
-----------------------
On the Retention page, set the sync dropdown to 36 months and press Invoices.

It will take a while - it is re-reading three years of sales invoices from Xero and Xero
rate-limits. Leave it to finish.

Then check Gas Lane. 612 Allocated should read about £17,317.

EVERY project is affected, not only Gas Lane. Any project whose invoicing stopped more
than six months ago will be showing a partial 612 figure or none at all. One 36-month
sync repairs the lot.


WORTH KNOWING FOR NEXT TIME
---------------------------
This will happen again to any field added to the invoice sync later. Lines outside the
window keep whatever they were stored with, so a new field is missing on old lines
permanently until a wide enough sync is run.

If a field is ever added again, the accompanying instruction has to be "run a full-history
sync once" - otherwise it silently reads zero on everything historic, and it looks like
Xero is wrong rather than the sync.
