pkg461 - no missing-info flag on variations
===========================================

Deploy after pkg460. One changed file: pages/crm.js. No new files.

Deals in the Variations stage no longer carry the red missing-information flag, on the
kanban or in List view.


WHY IT WAS WRONG
----------------
A variation is extra work on a job already won. The credit checks, project score, lead
source, site location and the rest were all done on the parent project - asking for them
again on a £2,000 add-on is asking for something that already exists somewhere else.

It was worse than just noise. Variations sit LATE in the stage order, after MC Unsecured,
so they were picking up the full Received list AND, being past Negotiating in the
ordering, could pick up the Negotiating list on top. Every variation carried a flag it
could never clear - and a flag that can never be cleared is how people learn to ignore the
flag everywhere else.


NOTHING ELSE CHANGED
--------------------
Checked every stage:

    Project In          no flag, as before (earlier than Received)
    Received            flags, as before
    MC Unsecured        flags, as before
    Variations          NO FLAG - the change
    MC Secured          flags, as before
    Negotiating         flags, both lists, as before

MC Secured sits after Variations in the stage order and is unaffected - worth stating,
because a change based on position rather than the stage itself would have caught it.
