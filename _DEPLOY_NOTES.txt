TO YOUR QUESTION - YES, MCD APPLIES TO VARIATIONS
---------------------------------------------------
Confirmed from your own application code, not assumed:

    gross     = measured + variations + materials on site
    mcd       = gross x mcdPct
    subTotal  = gross - mcd
    retention = subTotal x retentionPct

MCD comes off the WHOLE gross, variations included. And retention is calculated on the
sub-total AFTER MCD - which is exactly why you were right that the Final Account needs to
be net of it.

On a £500,000 account at 2.5% MCD and 3% retention:

    Gross AFA     £500,000
    MCD 2.5%       £12,500
    Final Account £487,500   <- retention calculated on this
    Retention      £14,625   (was showing £15,000 - overstated by £375)


1. IHM - the source
-------------------
"Discount" was free text. Now "Main Contractor's Discount (MCD) %", a number field with a
% sign, MANDATORY, 0 to 100.

Required ON FINALISE, not on save - somebody starting a handover before they know the
discount is not doing anything wrong. Finalising without one is. If it is missing the form
jumps to the Commercial section and says so.

Zero is a valid answer. Blank is not: blank means nobody asked, and the retention register
then has no basis for its Final Account.

Old free-text values still work - "2.5", "2.5%" and "MCD 2.5" all read as 2.5.


2. COMMERCIAL - Edit Project Details
------------------------------------
New MCD % box under the contract value, marked required. Prefilled from the IHM, editable
here, and what is typed here wins. Same override -> IHM -> nothing chain retention already
uses.


3. APPLICATIONS - automatic
---------------------------
A new application takes the project's MCD without anyone entering it.

A BUG FOUND ON THE WAY: a new application with no previous one defaulted mcdPct to ZERO,
not to the project's figure. So the FIRST application on every project applied no discount
at all, and everything downstream of it was wrong by the value of the MCD. Retention now
falls back correctly too.


4. RETENTION REGISTER - two new columns
---------------------------------------
    Gross AFA   contract value + instructed variations, before MCD
    MCD         the deduction, with the percentage underneath
    Final Acct  gross less MCD - what retention is calculated on

A project with no MCD recorded shows "not set" in amber rather than a silent zero, so you
can see which projects still need one instead of assuming they are all discount-free.

Account Remaining, Total Due and the final-account balance all now work off the net Final
Account. They were all overstated by the MCD.


AFTER DEPLOYING
---------------
The dashboard cache is invalidated automatically - I added mcdPct to the validity check,
so it rebuilds once. The first load of Project Financials will be slower than usual.

EXPECT MOST PROJECTS TO SHOW "not set" AT FIRST. MCD only exists where an IHM recorded a
discount, and it was free text before, so many will be blank. The register makes that
visible rather than assuming zero - which is the honest position, but it does mean there
is some data entry to do.
