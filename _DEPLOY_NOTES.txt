WHAT CHANGED
------------
1) OPENING CASH NOW WORKS (properly)
   Adds a "Refresh balances from Xero" button. It reads the Balance Sheet and pulls
   EACH bank + credit-card account balance. Opening cash for the forecast now uses
   the combined BANK balance (not the old bank-summary closing figure, which was
   never being synced and lumped cards in with cash).

2) BALANCE BOXES AT THE TOP (shown separately, as you asked)
   - One box per bank account (by name)
   - One box per credit card (by name), flagged "credit card"
   - "Opening cash (all bank combined)" = sum of the bank accounts
   - "Credit card debt" = total owed on cards
   - "Max cash available" = bank cash + undrawn invoice finance + unused card
     headroom (see below)

3) INVOICE FINANCE = a facility you draw down when YOU choose
   Since you decide how/if cash is released, it is NOT modelled as automatic weekly
   cash. Instead you enter, under "Facilities":
     - Invoice finance limit
     - Invoice finance drawn
     - Credit card limit (total)
   Undrawn invoice finance (limit - drawn) + unused card headroom (limit - debt) is
   added to your bank cash to show "Max cash available" - the most you could raise
   right now. These settings are saved (Save facilities).

HOW CARDS ARE DETECTED
   Any Balance Sheet bank-section account whose name contains card words (credit
   card / visa / mastercard / amex / cc) OR has a negative balance is treated as a
   card. If a card isn't picking up, tell me its exact name in Xero and I'll add it.

IF BALANCES DON'T LOAD
   If the Balance Sheet call is blocked by Xero permissions, you'll see an amber
   note and opening cash falls back to the old figure. If that happens, the Xero
   connection may need reconnecting with report access - let me know and we'll sort
   the scope.

TRY IT
   Deploy -> open Cash Flow -> click "Refresh balances from Xero". Check the per-
   account boxes match Xero, then enter your facility limits and Save.
