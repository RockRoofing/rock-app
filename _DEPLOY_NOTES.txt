-----------------------------------------------------
INVOICE FINANCE PAGE (Bibby availability - v1)
-----------------------------------------------------
Answers "how much can Bibby make available right now", built from your Xero sales
ledger + the insured limits.

  For each debtor:  eligible advance = min( 60% x outstanding , insured limit )
  Debtors with NO insured limit are excluded (flagged amber so you can spot them).
  "Materials only" debtors can be ticked to exclude (Bibby don't advance materials).
  Availability now = total eligible advance  -  currently drawn.

Set-up:
  - Advance rate defaults to 60% (editable).
  - Enter "currently drawn" (from Bibby) so availability nets it off.
  - Insured limits: type per debtor, OR click "Import Bibby limit list (CSV)" and
    select your exported list. It reads Buyer Name + Approved Amount, sums duplicate
    buyers (e.g. EPC's two CLNs), and matches names to your Xero debtors (ignoring
    Ltd/Limited/punctuation differences). Unmatched buyers are kept under the Bibby
    name and reported so you can reconcile. Then click Save.

The debtor table shows outstanding, insured limit, raw vs capped advance, and a
status (Full advance / Capped at limit / No insured limit / Materials only).

-----------------------------------------------------
YOUR QUESTION: how does the forecast handle the changing IF balance + invoices
paid off against it?
-----------------------------------------------------
The Cash Flow "Max cash available" now pulls the CALCULATED availability from this
page (not a static typed number). So as invoices are raised/collected in Xero and
as you update "drawn", the availability - and therefore max cash available - moves
with it. The manual IF limit/drawn boxes on Cash Flow are now only a fallback if the
Invoice Finance page hasn't been set up.

NOTE (honest): this gives you an accurate CURRENT availability that updates every
time you refresh. It does not yet roll the drawdown forward week-by-week inside the
13-week grid (advancing new invoices / repaying as customers pay across future
weeks). That "rolling facility ledger" is the natural next step once you've
confirmed this current-availability figure reconciles to Bibby. Say the word and
I'll build it on top.

v1 simplifications (as agreed): disallowed/overdue handled manually (lower the
insured limit or tick materials-only); final applications not auto-excluded yet.
