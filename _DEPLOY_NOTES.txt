pkg481 - bulk delete on Contracted Rates
========================================

Deploy after pkg480. One changed file: pages/contracted-rates.js. No new files.


WHAT YOU GET
------------
A "Delete 11 lines" button in the selection bar, beside the format and move buttons that
were already there. Tick the rows, press it, gone.

Only shown when the document is editable - a locked set of rates cannot be bulk deleted
any more than it can be bulk edited.


THE CONFIRMATION NAMES THE VALUE
----------------------------------
    Delete 11 lines?

    Total rate on those lines: £40,000.00.

    Use strike-through instead if you want to keep them on the document.

Deleting eleven lines worth £40,000 is a different thing from deleting one, and the
selection is easy to lose track of once it spans both sides of the line - the bar is
sticky, so you can be scrolled a long way from what is ticked.

The strike-through reminder is the same one the single-line delete gives. Most of the time
somebody wants the line off the quote but still on the record, and that is what strike is
for.


THE SELECTION IS CLEARED AFTERWARDS
-------------------------------------
It points at lines that no longer exist. Left alone the bar would sit there saying
"11 selected" over nothing, and the next format or move button pressed would do nothing
with no explanation.
