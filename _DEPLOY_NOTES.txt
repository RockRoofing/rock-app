YES - THAT IS EXACTLY WHY
-------------------------
You overrode the revenue instead of filling in the percentages. The override was
built to drive the CASH only and never touch the percentages, which is right -
otherwise a typed figure would silently rewrite the measured position.

But "left after ..." is measured from prevGross, and prevGross was computed
ENTIRELY from the prior certificate's percentages. So an overridden forecast put
cash into the chart while recording no claimed position at all. The next forecast
read the percentages, saw nothing claimed, and offered the whole budget again.

Worse than a wrong grey figure: the next forecast would have RE-CLAIMED the same
money as revenue.


THE FIX
-------
A forecast now records what it actually claims, not just what its percentages
measure:

  grossClaimedToDate    cumulative gross claimed, including override uplift
  labourClaimedToDate   the same for labour

When a forecast is used as the prior, the RECORDED figure wins. Where there is no
override the two are identical, so nothing changes. A real application always
measures everything, so there it is the same number too.

Uplift carried in from an earlier overridden forecast is carried on down the
chain rather than being lost at the next link.

GROSSING UP. Revenue is net of MCD and retention; the claimed position is gross,
before both. Adding the net figure straight onto a gross total would understate
the claim, so the uplift is grossed up on the same deductions the certificate
used. Labour has neither, so its uplift goes on as-is.

Worked through - App 4 at 92,766.50, revenue overridden to 40,000 with no
percentage touched, 2.5% MCD and 5% retention:

  uplift as gross      40,000 / (0.975 x 0.95)  =  43,184.89
  grossClaimedToDate                              135,951.39

  "left after" was    181,371.71   (ignored the 40,000)
  "left after" now    138,186.82

  and the NEXT forecast, measuring up to 140,000 gross:
    revenue this period      3,750.03
    without this fix        43,750.03   <- the 40,000 claimed a second time


BACK-COMPATIBILITY
------------------
Forecasts saved before this package have no grossClaimedToDate, so they fall back
to their percentages - the old behaviour, unchanged. Any forecast where you
overrode revenue without setting percentages is understating its claim and will
keep doing so until you reopen and re-save it. Worth doing on any you have
already built that way.
