BIGGER
=======
1000px to min(1800px, 96vw), and 96% of the height.

Six weeks is 42 day columns at 88px each. At 1000px the grid was a narrow window
onto a very wide table, and most of what you had asked for was scrolled out of
sight.

The card is now a fixed height with the grid scrolling INSIDE it, rather than the
whole page growing. The header, the tabs and the Download / Close buttons stay put -
before, a long list pushed the buttons below the fold and you had to scroll back for
them.


AND IT NO LONGER SHUTS WHEN YOU CLICK OUTSIDE
===============================================
The backdrop carried onMouseDown={onClose}. A click that began inside the table and
drifted outside it closed the whole thing - and with a project picker and the O/A
toggles in there now, that is real work lost.

The x and Escape close it, like every other modal in the portal. Escape closes the
project picker first when that is open, so pressing it over the picker does not
shut the grid behind it. The picker follows the same rule - Cancel and Escape, no
backdrop close.
