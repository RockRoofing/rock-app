pkg734 - HOTFIX: pkg733 emptied both cash flows
================================================

DEPLOY THIS OVER pkg733. Two files only. Everything else from pkg733 stays.

REPLACE:
  pages/api/business-financials.js
  pages/business-financials/cashflow.js

WHAT I BROKE
------------
pkg733 filtered receivables with `type === 'ACCREC'`.

lib/xero.js:1171 does stamp that field - but only on rows written SINCE it was
added. A store last synced before then carries no type on anything, so the
strict test discarded every receivable. Both cash flows read the same payload,
which is why they went together: no invoices due on the 13-week, and the
12-month with nothing coming in.

I made the guard fail-closed to stop bills getting through, and applied it to a
field that may not be there. That is the same mistake in the opposite direction:
pkg733's whole point was that `i.type && i.type !== 'ACCREC'` failed OPEN on a
missing field. I replaced one dependency on an unreliable field with another.

THE FIX
-------
Normalise, do not filter.

Provenance is the guarantee, not the field. bank:outstanding-receivables is
written ONLY by fetchOutstandingReceivables, which scopes to ACCREC in the WHERE
clause and post-filters on inv.Type. Whatever is in that store is a receivable
whether the row says so or not.

  - Explicit payables (ACCPAY, ACCPAYCREDIT) still excluded, in case the store
    is ever fed from somewhere else.
  - Sales credit notes (ACCRECCREDIT) KEPT - they belong there as negatives.
    pkg733 would have dropped these too.
  - Every row is then STAMPED with its type on the way out, so the strict guards
    downstream have something real to read.

That last part is what makes the strict guards safe. Normalising at the source
is the fix; filtering on a field that may not exist is what broke it.

Tested against all three shapes a stored row can take - legacy untyped, current
typed, and a sales credit note - plus a supplier bill that must not appear.
Legacy rows survive, the bill is excluded, the credit is kept, every row comes
out typed, and nothing is dropped by the page guards.
