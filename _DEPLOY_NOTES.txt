OVERDRAFT
---------
New "Overdraft limit" field. A box appears showing what is available and how much
of the limit is used.

NO DOUBLE COUNTING. Anything already drawn on an overdraft shows as a NEGATIVE
bank balance, so it is already inside the bank total. Headroom is the limit LESS
what is used, not the whole limit on top:

  bank  25,000  limit 20,000  ->  drawn      0  headroom 20,000  max cash  45,000
  bank  -5,000  limit 20,000  ->  drawn  5,000  headroom 15,000  max cash  10,000
  bank -20,000  limit 20,000  ->  drawn 20,000  headroom      0  max cash -20,000

Adding the full limit on top would have shown 45,000 of headroom on a fully drawn
overdraft.


CARD LIMITS - ONE PER CARD
--------------------------
A limit field appears for every card account Xero returns, so headroom is worked
out card by card. The pooled total stays as a fallback and is used only while no
per-card limit is set - nothing already entered is lost.

Per card matters because a pooled figure hides which card is full:

  Amex -9,000 (limit 9,000), Barclaycard -1,000 (limit 15,000)
    per card   14,000   Amex maxed, Barclaycard has the room
    pooled 24k 14,000   same total, no idea Amex is at its limit


ONE BUG FOUND WHILE DOING THIS
------------------------------
The load whitelists the finance config:

    setFinance({ ifLimit: ..., ifDrawn: ..., ccLimit: ... })

Anything not named there is dropped on every refresh, even though the SAVE writes
the whole object. The overdraft and per-card limits had to be added or they would
have appeared to save and been gone on reload - the same shape of fault as the
invoice finance settings whitelist.


NOT DONE - AND WORTH A DECISION
-------------------------------
1. OPENING BALANCE SHOULD STAY BANK-ONLY. It does today, and it should: if the
   facilities are folded into it the 13-week line can never go negative, and
   seeing when you run out is the whole point. The facilities belong as a FLOOR
   LINE on the chart - cross zero and you need to draw, cross the negative
   facility total and the money genuinely is not there. Say the word.

2. THE CASH FLOW RECALCULATES INVOICE FINANCE ITSELF, at
   business-financials.js:751:

       const drawn = Number(ifSettings.drawn) || 0

   That reads the OLD single drawn setting, not the dated history, and knows
   nothing about High Involvement or the eligibility caps. So this page will show
   a different availability from the Invoice Finance page. It should consume the
   IF page's figure rather than keep a simpler copy - worth fixing before you
   rely on the 13-week.
