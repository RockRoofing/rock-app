SORTING
-------
Every column header sorts. Click again to reverse.

  value desc     Wates > Torsion > Alheembouw     (the default)
  spread desc    Torsion > Wates > Alheembouw
  medLate asc    Alheembouw > Torsion > Wates
  name asc       Alheembouw > Torsion > Wates

A new column starts DESCENDING - biggest first is nearly always what you want -
except the customer name, where A-Z is useful and Z-A is not.

The arrow shows only on the column in use. Eight permanent arrows is noise and
tells you nothing about which one is active.

Default stays value descending: the biggest customers are the ones whose payment
behaviour actually moves the forecast.


WORTH SORTING BY SPREAD AT LEAST ONCE
-------------------------------------
It puts the customers you cannot plan around at the top, whatever their median
says. Torsion medians 12 days but swings between 0 and 60 - sorted by "typically
paid" it looks like one of your better payers, and sorted by spread it is the
worst. Those are the ones where a forecast offset will not save you.
