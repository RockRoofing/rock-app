pkg534 - emails read in full, chain behind the toggle
=====================================================

Deploy after pkg533. One changed file: pages/crm.js. No new files.


WHAT CHANGES
------------
    ALWAYS SHOWN, in the CRM green
        what this person actually wrote - greeting, message, sign-off, name.
        No truncation, no button to press.

    BEHIND "read full email", in a lighter green
        the quoted thread underneath - "From: ... Sent: ... " and everything below it.

Your Clarendon Road example now reads in full on the card: "Hi Jennie, I am looking for a
bit of advice... 38 lm to suit a 520mm wall... Many thanks, Niall". Nothing to click.

The button reads "read full email" / "hide earlier emails", and only appears when there IS
a chain underneath - offering it on a message with nothing below promises something and
then shows nothing.


THE BODY NOW LOADS ITSELF
-------------------------
It used to show Graph's 255-character preview and make you press a button per email just
to find out what it said. Each card now fetches its own body as it appears.

THREE AT A TIME. The Emails tab is fifty cards, and fifty simultaneous Graph requests get
throttled - so they go through a queue. Results are cached for the session, so scrolling
back, or opening a deal showing the same email, costs nothing.


WORTH SAYING
------------
This is now a request per email rather than per click. It is queued and cached, so it
should feel quick, but it is more traffic to Microsoft than before.

The proper fix, if reading mail here becomes routine, is storing bodies at sync time -
instant, searchable, and no per-view cost. That is the thing I flagged in pkg464 and it is
looking more worthwhile with every change you have asked for. Say the word.
