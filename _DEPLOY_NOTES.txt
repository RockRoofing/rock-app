PLEASE LOOK AT THIS ON YOUR SCREEN FIRST
-----------------------------------------
20px is a big jump from 13px - roughly 50% wider text. That row holds ten items and some
are long ("Project Management", "Project Financials", "Report app improvement"). On a
normal laptop screen it is likely to run out of room and start scrolling sideways, which
would mean Scorecards and Open Site App drop off the right-hand edge until you scroll.

The row already had overflowX: 'auto' so nothing breaks or overlaps - it just scrolls.

If it does overflow, the options are:
  - drop to 16-17px, which is still clearly bigger but usually still fits
  - shorten a couple of labels (e.g. "Project Mgmt", "Financials")
  - let it wrap onto two lines instead of scrolling

Tell me which and it is a quick change.
