WHAT YOUR SCREENSHOT SHOWED
---------------------------
React errors #425, #418 and #423 on the CRM page. Those are hydration failures, and they
are not cosmetic.

#425 is "text content does not match server-rendered HTML". The cause:

    dateTime()  used  toLocaleString('en-GB')  with no timezone.

Vercel runs in UTC. Your browser runs in London time. Same instant, different text:

    UTC     16 Aug 2026, 23:30
    London  17 Aug 2026, 00:30

Every date on the page disagreed with itself. React then abandons hydration (#418/#423),
and a page that fails to hydrate keeps its HTML but LOSES EVENT HANDLERS in parts of the
tree. Inputs look normal and do nothing.

That is the real reason some comment boxes would not accept typing - the duplicate-key
fix in pkg395 was worth doing, but this was underneath it. It is very likely why the
mention email never fires either: if the handler that calls it was never attached, no
request is made and nothing appears anywhere, which matches exactly what you are seeing.

FIXED:
  - every date is now formatted with timeZone: 'Europe/London', so both sides agree
  - todayISO() is UK date, not the server's. On UTC it was a day behind for an hour every
    evening during BST, so "overdue" was wrong too
  - the CRM page no longer server-renders at all. It reads localStorage, the URL, xlsx and
    the clock - none of which mean anything on a server - so there is nothing left to
    disagree about. This removes the whole class of fault, not just the known instance.

After deploying, reload the CRM and check the console. Those three React errors should be
gone. If they are, try the @mention again before anything else - I expect it to work.
