YOU FOUND IT
------------
Supplier invoices in the Invoices in drill-down is not a display problem. The
receivables STORE contains ACCPAY bills.

    /Invoices?Type=ACCPAY&where=Status=="AUTHORISED"

Xero does not filter on a bare Type query parameter - it belongs inside the where
clause. So both fetches returned BOTH types. There is a defensive filter in the
code now (`if (inv.Type && inv.Type !== type) continue`), but anything stored
BEFORE that guard existed is still in bank:outstanding-receivables, and that is
what you are looking at.

A BILL COUNTED AS MONEY IN IS WRONG TWICE OVER. It inflates the arrears row, and
the same bill is already in Bills out. Removing it takes the overstatement off
both sides - which is very likely most of your 434,338 and a good part of the 700k.

1. FILTERED AT THE POINT OF USE. The cash flow now skips anything that is not
   ACCREC, so a stale store cannot poison the forecast even before you re-sync.

     X Invoices owed contains only sales invoices

ON YOUR OTHER QUESTION - the projected cash balance uses expectedDate first and
falls back to Xero's due date only where no expected date is set. The Invoices in
drill-down marks which is which: "(expected)" or "(due date)".

PRESS SYNC ON INVOICES OWED after deploying. The filter protects the forecast, but
only a re-sync cleans the underlying store - and the Bills to Pay store is worth
syncing too, for the same reason.
