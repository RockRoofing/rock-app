THE CHANGE
===========
"How far ahead" on the Weekly Labour Allocation now offers 1 to 6 weeks instead of
1 to 4.

No server change was needed. Both PDF routes have always accepted up to 26 weeks -
the limit of four was only ever the row of buttons in the modal. With the previous
week ticked as well that is now up to seven pages.

The Weeks dropdown on the Accounts report is raised to 6 at the same time. It is the
same report over the same data and there is no reason for the two to disagree, which
also keeps the new timesheet CSV in step - it takes its date range from that
dropdown.


ONE THING TO BE AWARE OF
=========================
That row of buttons drives BOTH the download and "Send to operatives". Choosing 6
and pressing send will email each operative six weeks of their allocation rather
than four.

That may be exactly what you want, or six weeks out may be too speculative to be
putting in front of them. I have not invented a separate limit for the email,
because a download control that quietly means something different when you press the
button next to it is worse than either choice.

If the email should stay at four while the download goes to six, say so and it is a
small change - the two would then need their own controls, clearly labelled.

The email itself already only sends FUTURE days, so past dates inside the range are
never emailed regardless.
