WHAT CHANGED
------------
The Due box on "Set the next activity" now starts EMPTY. It used to default to today, which
meant an activity could pick up today's date just because nobody looked at it.

"Done & set next" now requires one. Press it without a date and you get, in red:

    Next due date must be set.

The date box outlines in red at the same time, and the message clears the moment you pick
one.


WHY A WARNING RATHER THAN A GREYED-OUT BUTTON
-----------------------------------------------
A disabled button tells you nothing about why it will not work - you are left clicking at
something dead, which is exactly the sort of thing that wastes five minutes. The button
stays live and tells you what is missing.

"Just mark done" is unaffected. If there is no follow-up, that is the button, and it needs
no date.
