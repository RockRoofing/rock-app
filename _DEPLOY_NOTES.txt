WIP IS FULL WIDTH
-----------------
Three things were narrowing it, and all three had to go:

    the page's 24px side padding      -> 8px on these two tabs
    the WIP page's own maxWidth 1400  -> no cap in the embed
    the embed's 24px padding          -> 14px

On a 1920 screen that takes the usable table from about 1,400px to about 1,870px. Nearly
470px more of the columns you were scrolling to reach.

The frames are taller as well - with the header gone there is a lot more room, so
100vh-150px rather than 100vh-220px. Retention gets the same, since the same header was
above it.
