TWO BANNERS
-----------
RED - past due and not released:

  "2 retention releases past its due date and not marked released - GBP 8,000.00"
  "Chase these, or confirm the release by clicking the 1st / 2nd Value cell."

AMBER - no release date at all:

  "1 retention with no release date - GBP 8,000.00"
  "With no date these never show as overdue and never reach the Cash Flow, which
   only schedules dated releases. Set the date on the row."

Two banners rather than one because they are different problems needing different
actions: one is money that should already be in, the other is money that will
NEVER be chased by anything because nothing can go overdue without a date. That
second one is the quieter and more expensive of the two.

Each is COLLAPSED by default with the count and the money in the heading, and a
"show N" link listing the projects. On a register this size an always-open list
pushes the table off screen, and a warning you scroll past is a warning you
ignore.


THE RULES
---------
  - Only halves with a VALUE and NOT confirmed released.
  - Released counts a manual mark OR a half ticked on an application - same rule
    as everything else on the page.
  - COMPLETE projects excluded. Retention on a closed job is not being chased.
  - Computed over the FILTERED view, so a banner never warns about something you
    have filtered away.
  - Overdue list sorted oldest first; no-date list sorted largest first.

  J218  both halves, no dates            -> AMBER
