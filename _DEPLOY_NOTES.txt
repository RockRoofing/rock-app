WHAT CHANGED
1. Lessons Learnt is now STRUCTURED (going forward). In a meeting's minutes, the Lessons
   Learnt section is a list of rows, each with:
     - Lessons Learnt item (short name)
     - Lessons Learnt (the detail / context)
     - Who it's for (tick the departments yourself)
   Add as many as you like. On "Meeting complete" they populate the Lessons Learnt table.

2. The TABLE now has columns: Month | Lessons Learnt item | Lessons Learnt | Who it's for.
   You can still search, filter by department, tweak departments (click chips), add a
   manual lesson, or delete one.

3. THE AI LAYER IS REMOVED. You categorise every lesson yourself. (ANTHROPIC_API_KEY is
   no longer used by this page - you can leave it set or remove it, your call.)

4. Only applies GOING FORWARD. The 20 seeded historical meetings keep their original
   free-text lessons notes, shown read-only under "Recorded notes (historical)" inside
   each old meeting - they are NOT converted and do NOT populate the new table.

CLEARING THE OLD TABLE (do this once after deploy):
   The 132 old auto-generated rows are still in storage. On the Lessons table tab there is
   now a red "Clear table" button - click it once to wipe them. (This clears the table
   only; the minutes are untouched.) After that, the table fills only from meetings you
   complete going forward, plus anything you add manually.
