WHY IT STOPPED WORKING
----------------------
"Required" only counted a project if its Gantt cell was explicitly marked ACTUAL.

    allocation stored as an array of names (the older format)   reads as 'confirmed'
    cell with no status set                                     defaults to 'confirmed'
    cell marked confirmed or provisional                        not counted

So only a cell somebody had retrospectively ticked as actual counted. Everywhere that tick
was not applied - which is most of the Gantt - the week required nothing, and the card had
nothing to measure.


THE RULE NOW, AS YOU DESCRIBED IT
-----------------------------------
Labour allocated to a project on a day that has passed means we worked on it. Provisional,
confirmed or actual - for a week in the past it is the record of what happened.

    one day in a week    = one report
    five days in a week  = one report
    worked 3 of 4 weeks  = three reports

An allocation with nobody on it is not counted - an empty cell is not work.

Weeks are Monday to Sunday, which they already were.


AND IT IS NOW A MONTHLY FIGURE
--------------------------------
Required for a month is the SUM of each week's requirement, not a de-duplicated project
count - a project worked in three weeks needs three reports.

Checked against your example:

    week of 03 Aug   J178, J190   2 reports
    week of 10 Aug   J178         1
    week of 17 Aug   J178         1
    week of 24 Aug   none         0
                                  4 required in August

Completed is tallied the same way, from Weekly Project Reports dated in each week.

The trend line is monthly too. It was a run of WEEKS labelled as though they were months,
which is why it never lined up with the cards beside it.

A week is assigned to the month containing its Thursday - the same key the weeks are
already built on, and the usual convention for a week that straddles a month end.
