THE ARITHMETIC
--------------
  placed by the placement check    625,197
  shown in Invoices in (13wk)      502,486   (512,779 less the 10,293 arrears)
  difference                       122,711

122,711 is EXACTLY the invoice finance drawn figure on your net cash position.

WHAT WAS HAPPENING
------------------
The model has a rule: if no manual balance is marked as invoice finance, it
assumes the drawn balance is NOT in your cash figure, and repays it out of
incoming invoices instead - diverting 122,711 away from Invoices in as the weeks
pass.

Bibby was entered as kind "Bank", so that rule fired. But the balance was ALSO
reducing opening cash. Counted twice. The comment in the code says "doing both
would count it twice" - the check just could not see the account.

WHY CHANGING THE KIND DID NOT FIX IT
-------------------------------------
The dropdown does save 'if'. The problem is where the rule looks.

  the editor writes to      manualBal          (page state, saved in background)
  the rule reads            data.manualBalances (API payload, NOT refetched)

So the change was saved and invisible to the rule until a full reload. Line 1613
already carried a comment about exactly this trap - it had been fixed in one
place and left in three others.

Now fixed: manualBankTotal, hasManualBank and ifAsAccount all read live state,
falling back to the payload only when local state is empty.
