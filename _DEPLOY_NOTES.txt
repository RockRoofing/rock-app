  the Operations Live Project Tasks list:

    - the Person responsible (a portal user) becomes the task's Responsible/assignee,
    - a note records which Lessons Learnt meeting it came from.
  Each action is marked with a green "task" tick once sent, and won't be sent twice.

  PERSON RESPONSIBLE is now a dropdown of portal users (was free text). Pick the user
  who owns each action.
