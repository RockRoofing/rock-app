pkg335 - Activities could be stripped from a deal without being stored
=======================================================================

REPLACES pkg302 through pkg334. Deploy this one only.

CHANGED FILES
-------------
  pages/crm.js
  pages/api/crm.js        recount endpoint
  lib/crmImportMap.js     unchanged since pkg305


A REAL FLAW I INTRODUCED IN pkg331
------------------------------------
Moving activities to their own store meant the deal is now saved WITHOUT them. The order
was: write the activities to their store, then save the stripped deal.

But the deal was saved whether or not the activity write had succeeded. So a single failed
request - a dropped connection, a slow moment - and the activities were removed from the
deal and never written anywhere else. Gone.

The error was logged to the console and nothing else, so it would have looked exactly like
what you are seeing: activities that were there yesterday, missing today.

FIXED: if a deal's activities or notes cannot be written, that deal is NOT saved at all.
It keeps its old baseline and is retried on your next change, so nothing is stripped
without being safely stored first. The badge tells you:

    "1 project(s) could not be fully saved - retrying on your next change."


RECOVERING WHAT LOOKS MISSING
------------------------------
Under "Where are my activities?" there is now a "Recount from stored records" button.

It rebuilds the outstanding list and the kanban counts directly from the per-deal stores,
which are the source of truth. If the list has drifted - a count that never updated, an
activity stored but not listed - this puts it right.

It CANNOT lose anything. It only recounts what is already saved; it does not add, remove
or change a single activity.

Run it once after deploying.

BE AWARE: anything actually lost by the flaw above cannot be recovered by recounting - it
was never written. If you find a project where activities have genuinely vanished, they
will need re-entering, and I am sorry. The fix stops it happening again.


WHY THIS KEEPS HAPPENING
-------------------------
Splitting activities out of the deal was the right call and I still think so - but I made
the change without protecting the moment between "remove from the deal" and "write to the
store". That gap is exactly where data goes missing, and I should have closed it as part of
pkg331 rather than after you noticed.
