THE BASIS HAS CHANGED - AND IT MATTERED
---------------------------------------
Was: fundable = sum of "this cert (net)" across every UNPAID APPLICATION.

The failure mode you spotted is real and already live. thisCertNet comes from
computeApplicationSummary(app, prevGrossFor(app)), and prevGrossFor uses
app.prevCertGross when set, otherwise the previous application's computed gross.
With PREVIOUSLY CERTIFIED BLANK - exactly the state J240 App 4 is in - thisCert
computes as the FULL CUMULATIVE VALUE. On this page that is not a display error,
it is 92,766.50 of phantom debt inflating the funding position by 55,660 at 60%.

Now: the DEBT is the unpaid sales invoice. The matched application is read only
for COMPOSITION - materials on site, variations to date, gross to date - which is
what the caps need and an invoice cannot give. Exactly as you put it: they fund
the invoice, and need the application to show what it consists of.

An invoice has no gross/net ambiguity. It is one number with a real amountDue.


THREE CASES, ALL VISIBLE RATHER THAN SILENT
-------------------------------------------
RETENTION INVOICES - excluded from funding, shown as a purple chip with their
total. Detected by "retention" (or "retn") in the reference or invoice number.
CAVEAT WORTH KNOWING: a retention invoice raised WITHOUT that note will not be
caught and will be funded as ordinary debt. The chip says so on hover.

UNMATCHED INVOICES - no "App N" to match. These ARE counted as debt (they are
real money owed), flagged blue, and the caps are applied using the project's
unpaid applications instead, so they are never funded blind.

APPLIED BUT NOT INVOICED - shown grey, NOT funded. Bibby assign the invoice, so
work applied for and not yet billed is not debt yet. Shown rather than dropped,
because silently vanishing work looks like a bug.

Worked example, 500k contract:

  invoice debt (excl retention)   75,000
  retention invoices              12,500  (1)  excluded
  unmatched invoices               5,000  (1)  counted, capped via unpaid apps
  applied not invoiced            17,766        shown, not funded
  caps excluded                   25,000        materials over the 25% cap
  ELIGIBLE                        50,000
  advance @ 60%                   30,000


ALSO IN THIS PACKAGE - THE THREE BIBBY CAPS (was pkg591)
--------------------------------------------------------
  materials on site   25% of contract value
  variations          25% of contract value
  certified ceiling   90% of contract value

Applied per project BEFORE the advance rate - they reduce approved debt, not the
advance. Measured on the CUMULATIVE position at the evidence application, since
"materials funded to 25% of contract" is a position not an increment. The 90%
ceiling is applied LAST to the figure already reduced by the other two, so money
that is both a variation and above 90% is not counted twice.

All three are editable beside the advance rate - they are facility terms and
will change at review. The save handler whitelists settings, so they had to be
added there too or they would have appeared to save and been gone on reload.
