THE PDF
-------
Laid out like the sheet you sent: our details top left, the header block with Contract,
Variation No, Sub-Contract Ref, Date, Description and Requested by in red, then the priced
items, the total, and the clarifications lettered a to e underneath in red.

    Item  Description                    Quantity  Unit  Rate     Total
    1     Additional VCL to plant deck   120       m2    £22.64   £2,716.50
    2     Perimeter detailing            38        m     £14.20     £539.60
                                                          Total   £3,256.10

IT DOES NOT SHOW THE WORKINGS. Those are how we arrived at the rate and are ours; the
customer gets the rate, the quantity and the total - which is what the old document showed
as well.

Checked against a generated document: the PDF total and the figure on the tracker agree to
the penny, because both come from the same items.


DOWNLOAD
--------
"Download V01 PDF" appears once the variation is raised. It has to be saved first - the
PDF is built server-side from the record, so there is nothing to build until it exists.


SEND
----
"Send to customer" opens the send window, built the same way as Outstanding Invoices:

    TO        the customer's own contacts from the handover, as tick boxes, plus a box
              for anybody not on it
    COPY IN   the Rock team, from the portal directory
    SUBJECT   Variation V01 - J205 Pets Choice
    MESSAGE   written for you from the variation - number, project, sub-contract ref,
              description and value, then "Please confirm your instruction to proceed"

All editable before it goes.

REPLIES COME BACK TO YOU - the portal user who sent it, not a sending address nobody
reads. A customer querying a variation has to reach the person who raised it.

The PDF is attached automatically, named "Variation V01 - J205 Pets Choice.pdf".


ONE THING TO WATCH
------------------
If a project's handover has no customer contacts with email addresses, the To list will be
empty and it says so plainly. Type an address in the box underneath - and it is worth
filling the handover in, because the same contacts now feed "Requested by" as well.
