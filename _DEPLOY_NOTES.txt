DEPLOY NOTES - Contracted Rates for negotiated projects (Phase 2b)
==================================================================

TWO files, both EDITS - just replace them.
  pages/contracted-rates.js       (picker now includes negotiated projects)
  pages/api/contracted-rates.js   (stores negotiated rates separately)

WHAT'S NEW
  The Contracted Rates page previously only listed LIVE projects. It now also lists
  NEGOTIATED projects (Pipedrive "Negotiating" deals), shown with a "NEG" tag in the
  picker. You can upload / build / lock contracted rates for them exactly like live
  projects.

WHERE THEY'RE STORED
  Live projects keep storing rates inside the project record (unchanged). Negotiated
  projects have no project record, so their rates are stored under a separate key
  (crates:negotiated:<dealId>). Everything else - upload/parse, edit, lock, delete,
  totals - works identically. The "Open project" link is hidden for negotiated ones
  (they have no project page); a small "Negotiated project" tag shows instead.

WHY
  This is the foundation for the Commercial Cash Flow hypothetical application (next
  phase), which builds the application FROM contracted rates - so negotiated projects
  need rates too.
