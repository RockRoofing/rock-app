1. WHY THE DATES DO NOT MATCH - NOT A BUG
-----------------------------------------
The project forecast shows the DELIVERY date. The cash flow shows the PAYMENT
date, computed at save time as delivery plus terms:

    payDate: paymentDate(m.deliverDay, m.term || { basis: 'eom', days: 30 })

Default is end of month + 30 days:

    deliver 25/09, eom+30  ->  pays 30/10
    deliver 15/10, eom+30  ->  pays 30/11

So your 70k "due mid-October" is a delivery, and it pays at the end of November -
outside the 13 weeks entirely. That is correct for a cash flow, but nothing on
either screen said which date it was showing.


2. MATERIAL LINES CONTRIBUTING NOTHING - THE REAL FAULT
-------------------------------------------------------
    matItems: (fc.matItems || []).map(m => ({ date: m.payDate, amount: m.amount || 0 }))

`amount` is resolved at SAVE time. A material line can be a PERCENTAGE of the
materials budget (mode: 'pct') and only becomes a figure when saved. So:

  - any line saved before `amount` existed has none, and counted as ZERO
  - any pct line saved without a budget has none, and counted as ZERO
  - any line with no DELIVERY date gets no payDate and never lands in a week

All three were silent. On a four-line example the old code counted 65,630 where
the true figure was 77,630, with 5,040 unplaceable.

Now: falls back to `value` for a plain line, and reports what it cannot resolve
instead of counting it as nil.
