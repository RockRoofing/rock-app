YOUR QUESTION - YES, IT ALREADY WORKS THAT WAY
----------------------------------------------
    invoices   i.expectedDate || i.dueDate
    bills      billOverrides[i.id] || i.payDate || i.dueDate

Expected/planned wins; the due date is the fallback. That was already correct -
the screen just never showed which of the two was in play.


THE FIELD NOW SHOWS IT
----------------------
  nothing set, due 26/08    shows 26/08/26 in LIGHT GREY, plain border
                            - the forecast is using the due date
  expected set 15/09        shows 15/09/26 in normal text, amber border,
                            with an x to clear back to the due date
  no date at all            empty

So you can see what the forecast will do without a greyed default looking like
somebody chose it.

CLICKING IN AND OUT CHANGES NOTHING. The commit only fires when the date differs
from what the forecast is ALREADY using - otherwise merely opening the picker on a
greyed default would have turned it into a saved override.

Clearing goes back to the due date rather than to empty, which is what "clear"
should mean here.
