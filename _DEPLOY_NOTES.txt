1. WHY YOUR TWO EXPORTS DIFFERED
--------------------------------
The app matched Xero TO THE PENNY on bills and credit notes. The whole gap was
four supplier overpayments the app never fetched.

  App total                      307,471.06   118 rows
  Xero - Bill                    307,607.14   117
  Xero - Bill credit note           -136.08     1
  Xero - Bill overpayment          4,699.57     4   <- missing entirely
  Xero total                     312,170.63   122

  307,607.14 - 136.08 = 307,471.06, exactly the app's figure.

The four: Construction Liberation 3,400.00, Caz Roofing 1,000.00, Fixfast 274.92,
UK Postbox 24.65.

CAUSE: fetchOutstandingBills called Invoices?Type=ACCPAY and
CreditNotes?Type=ACCPAYCREDIT. Overpayments are a SEPARATE Xero endpoint
(/Overpayments), not a type of invoice, so they could never appear.

(Your diff also counted the "total" footer row of the app CSV as a bill, which is
why the gap first looked like 302k rather than 4.7k.)


2. OVERPAYMENTS NOW COME IN, NEGATIVE
-------------------------------------
Same treatment as credit notes: money already paid that sits with the supplier
and offsets future bills, so it reduces the total. Badged OVERPAYMENT on the row,
green like a credit, and exported as OVERPAYMENT rather than CREDIT.

Only Type SPEND is taken - RECEIVE is a customer overpayment and does not belong
on a bills page. Only unallocated remaining credit counts.

An overpayment has no due date - it is already paid - so its own date is used.
Leaving it undated would drop it the moment you set a date range.


3. EXPECT A THIRD NUMBER - THIS IS THE IMPORTANT BIT
----------------------------------------------------
  app after this change     302,771.49
  Xero "Bills you need to pay" screen  307,471.06
  Xero raw CSV export       312,170.63

All three are defensible and none of them are wrong:

  The Xero SCREEN excludes overpayments entirely.
  The Xero EXPORT lists them positive, which overstates what you owe.
  The APP now nets them off, which is the true amount you will actually pay.

So the app will no longer tie to either Xero figure, by design. If you would
rather it matched the Xero screen exactly, the alternative is to fetch them and
show them at zero, or not at all - say the word and it is a one-line change.


4. ALSO IN THIS PACKAGE - COST ACCOUNT FILTER
---------------------------------------------
"Cost accounts" multi-select next to the supplier filter, searchable on code or
name, with Clear and Select shown. Only accounts present on the current bills are
listed. A bill shows if ANY of its lines is on a picked account - bills are never
split, because showing part of one would make the Filtered total wrong.

The selection is saved to localStorage, the same approach the CRM uses. NOT a
Redis config key: that is shared, so one person narrowing the filter would
silently narrow it for everybody else.

Credit notes and overpayments now carry lineCodes too. Without that they would
have vanished under any account filter - and since both are NEGATIVE, the
filtered total would have gone UP.
