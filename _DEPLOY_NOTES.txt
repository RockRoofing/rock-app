WHY YOU GOT NOTHING, AND WHY YOU COULD NOT TELL WHY
=====================================================
notifyMentions() returned a bare { sent: 0 } for three completely different
outcomes:

  - the name in the comment matched nobody
  - the only person tagged was you, and nobody is emailed their own comment
  - there are no taggable users at all

The composer collapsed all three, plus a genuine send failure with no names, into
the single word "Saved." So the one thing you needed to know - which of those
happened - was the one thing it did not say.

I have not guessed which of the three it was. The build now tells you.


WHAT CHANGED
-------------
Every outcome now comes back with a reason, and the composer prints it:

  tag a colleague        "Saved. Emailed Dori Balog."
  tag YOURSELF           "Saved. Only you were tagged - you are not emailed your
                          own comment."
  a name that matches
  nobody                 "Saved. No name in the comment matched anyone. Type @ and
                          pick from the list."
  no taggable users      "Saved. Nobody is taggable here. Portal users need an
                          email address and the right role."
  email not configured   "Saved, but the email to X did not send. Email is not
                          configured on the server (RESEND_API_KEY is not set)."
  Resend rejected it     "Saved, but the email to X did not send." followed by
                          exactly what Resend said - usually an unverified sending
                          domain, and it says so plainly.
  some sent, some not    "Saved. Emailed A. 1 did not send."

The composer footer also states how many people are taggable at all. If it reads
"Nobody is taggable", that is the answer on its own and no amount of typing @ will
help - the portal users need email addresses and a post-contract, management or
admin role.

All six branches verified against the real resolver, including that "@Dori" resolves
on first name only where exactly one person has it.


MOST LIKELY, FOR WHAT IT IS WORTH
-----------------------------------
If you tagged yourself while testing, that is the second case and it is working as
intended - the rule that nobody is emailed their own words came over from the CRM.
Tag somebody else, or tell me and I will drop the rule so a self-tag emails you too.

If the footer says nobody is taggable, the portal user records have no email
addresses on them, and that is fixable in user admin rather than in code.
