THE SEARCH
===========
A search box in the deal's top bar, left of Won/Lost. Type, and matching projects
drop down; click one, or press Enter, and it opens.

Opening a deal replaces the whole page - nav and all - so the toolbar search
disappears the moment you are inside one, and getting from one project to another
meant going back to the list and starting again.


DECISIONS WORTH KNOWING
------------------------
IT USES THE SAME MATCHING RULE AS EVERYTHING ELSE. dealHaystack() already defines
what a CRM search looks at, and its own comment says a search that quietly stops
covering a field is the kind of fault nobody reports. So this reads it rather than
listing fields again: title, company, contact, and the whole Details card including
scope of works and general info.

RANKED BY WHERE IT MATCHED, not just alphabetically:

    title starts with it   ->  first
    title contains it      ->  next
    company contains it    ->  next
    anywhere else          ->  last

Searching "oakwood" puts the Oakwood projects above a job that merely mentions
oakwood in its scope of works. Eight results maximum.

WON AND LOST ARE INCLUDED, with the status shown on the right of each row - open
deals show their stage instead. When you are hunting for a project from inside
another one, it is usually an old one.

THE PROJECT YOU ARE ALREADY IN IS EXCLUDED. It is never the answer, and it would
otherwise sit at the top of its own results.

KEYBOARD. Up and down to move, Enter to open, Escape to clear and then to close.
Clicks are bound on mousedown, and the blur is delayed, so a click on a result
cannot be lost to the list closing first - that is the commonest way a box like
this feels broken.

Verified: current deal excluded, won and lost returned, company matches ranked
above free-text ones, no match says so rather than showing an empty box.
