pkg590 - Cash Schedule: split by PERCENTAGE so it tracks the month
===================================================================

Independent of pkg589. Two files.


CHANGED FILES (replace)
-----------------------
  pages/business-financials/cash-schedule.js
  pages/business-financials/cashflow.js


ANSWERING THE QUESTION FIRST
----------------------------
The AMOUNT already comes through per month, correctly. cashflow.js:29 reads
predictedByCodeMonth, which mirrors the Budgets page month by month - the actual
for a month switched to Actual, a typed override where there is one, otherwise
the forecast method, budget last. Sep, Oct and Nov each get their own figure. The
flat budget is only the fallback. And it already flows to Cash Flow, not
eventually.

The TIMING is per code, not per month - "pay on the 28th" applies to every month.
Carry-forward entries are the escape for a one-off shift.

BUT you were right that it does not balance, and the cause is worse than a
rounding gap.


THE DEFECT
----------
In 'multiday' mode, cashflow.js scheduled the TYPED AMOUNTS and never touched
monthlyBudget at all:

    const amount = (Number(d.amount) || 0) * vatMult + adj * share

So the month's budget, forecast and actual were ignored entirely. Type 4,000
across three days and Cash Flow pays 4,000 every month whatever the month says -
and switching a month to Actual changed nothing.

'oneday' and 'even' were fine; both use monthlyBudget.

The editor made it worse: "Balances to budget" compared against the FLAT monthly
budget, so it reported balanced while the months diverged.

Budget 4,000/mo, forecast Sep 4,300 / Oct 4,000 / Nov 3,800:

  AMOUNT basis    Sep 4,000 (out -300)   Oct 4,000 (ok)   Nov 4,000 (out +200)
                  quarter 12,000 vs 12,100
  PERCENT basis   Sep 4,300              Oct 4,000        Nov 3,800
                  quarter 12,100 vs 12,100 - balances every month


THE FIX
-------
"Specific days" now has a basis:

  % OF THE MONTH'S BUDGET  each split is a share, so they always add to that
                           month's figure whatever it is
  FIXED AMOUNTS            paid as typed, whatever the month says

Existing schedules default to FIXED, so nothing already saved changes meaning on
deploy. A "Convert to %" button turns the typed amounts into shares of the budget,
keeping the same split - so switching over is one click and keeps your pattern.

In percent mode each row shows the resulting money next to the percentage at the
current budget, and the footer checks against 100% rather than a cash figure.
Fixed mode carries a warning saying it will not follow the forecast.

VAT: in percent mode the gross-up already sits in monthlyBudget via amountFor(),
so it is NOT applied a second time to each split. In fixed mode it still is, as
before.


WORTH DOING AFTER DEPLOY
------------------------
Any account on "Specific days" is still on fixed amounts and still will not
balance. Open each, press Convert to %, and check the footer says 100%.
