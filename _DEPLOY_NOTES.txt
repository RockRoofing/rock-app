YES - AND IT FIXES A BUG I INTRODUCED
-------------------------------------
pkg610 had cards typed as a positive "amount owed" and flipped the sign
internally. That broke the Xero cross-check, which compares your figure against
Xero's - and Xero holds a card as NEGATIVE, because it is a liability:

    typed  77,156.42   Xero  -77,156.42   gap shown  154,312.84
    on an account that actually AGREED to the penny.

Balances are now taken LITERALLY for both kinds. Negative means owed, for cards
and for an overdrawn bank account alike. Same convention as Xero, so the
cross-check works:

    typed -77,156.42   Xero  -77,156.42   gap  0.00

A card typed as a POSITIVE is now FLAGGED - "in credit?" - rather than silently
reinterpreted. It is far more likely to be the amount owed entered without the
minus, but guessing which is exactly how the cross-check ended up comparing a
positive against a negative. The field is labelled "- = owed" and turns red when
negative.

    Metrobank   bank    2,500.52
    AMEX        card        0.00
    Cap on Tap  card  -77,156.42
    -> card debt 77,156.42, headroom 82,843.58, max cash 191,532.10
