WHAT'S NEW
  In Historic mode you can now jump straight to the OLDEST programmed date instead of
  clicking back one week at a time.

  - A new "<< Oldest" button sits next to the back arrow. Click it and the planner turns
    Historic on (if it isn't already) and jumps to the week containing the earliest
    programmed day found anywhere in the data (labour allocations or water-ingress). Its
    tooltip shows which week that is.
  - From there you can still use the < / > arrows to move week by week, or "Today" to jump
    back to the current week.
  - If there is no historic data at all, the button is greyed out.

  (The back arrow already had no limit - this just gets you to the very start in one click.)
