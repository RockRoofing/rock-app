1. CREDIT NOTE REVERSALS ARE NETTED
------------------------------------
App 2 was raised in Feb 2024, credited out in full in Nov 2025 and re-raised the
same day. Both postings were counted, inflating each column by 642.20. The
account still nets to zero, so nothing looked wrong until you compared against
Retention Owed.

A credit note is now cancelled against the invoice it reverses, matched through
Xero's Allocations (captured in pkg788). Only the overlapping amount and only
where the signs oppose, so a partial credit leaves the remainder and a credit
note that reinforces a deduction is left alone.
