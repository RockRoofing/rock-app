WHAT WAS MISSING - all three of Holly's caps
--------------------------------------------
The page funded 60% of every unpaid application in full. Materials on site,
variations and the last 10% were all being counted as approved debt when Bibby
would not fund them.

  1. MATERIALS ON SITE      fundable to 25% of contract value
  2. VARIATIONS             fundable to 25% of contract value; beyond that needs
                            written instruction
  3. CERTIFIED CEILING      90% of contract approved initially; past that needs
                            further certification as the final account approaches

All three are now applied PER PROJECT, before the advance rate - they reduce what
counts as approved debt, not what is advanced against it.

The API now returns measuredToDate, variationsToDate, materialsOnSite,
grossToDate and contractValue per application, which is what the caps measure
against.


TWO THINGS I WAS CAREFUL ABOUT
------------------------------
CUMULATIVE, NOT PER CERTIFICATE. "Materials on site funded to 25% of contract" is
a POSITION, not an increment, so the caps are measured on the cumulative figures
at the latest unpaid application. The excess is then taken off the unpaid total,
and never by more than is actually outstanding - an excess sitting in an
application already paid is not in the unpaid figure to remove.

THE 90% CEILING IS APPLIED LAST, to the figure already reduced by the other two.
Applying all three to the raw total would double-count money that is both a
variation AND above 90%. Checked: contract 500k, materials at 30% AND certified
to 95% - the materials excess brings gross to exactly 450k, so the ceiling adds
nothing further. 25k excluded, not 50k.

Worked, on a 500k contract with 100k unpaid:

  clean, 50% certified        100,000 eligible   advance  60,000
  materials at 30%             75,000 eligible   advance  45,000
  variations at 30%            75,000 eligible   advance  45,000
  certified to 95%             75,000 eligible   advance  45,000
  excess exceeds the unpaid         0 eligible   advance       0


ON SCREEN
---------
A project reduced by a cap shows an amber "-GBP X not eligible" chip beside its
figure, hovering to give the reasons in full - eg "Materials on site GBP 150,000
over the 25% cap (GBP 125,000) - GBP 25,000 not funded". Without it a cut figure
reads as a smaller application rather than money Bibby will not fund.

The three percentages are EDITABLE next to the advance rate, not hard-coded -
they are facility terms and will change at review.


ONE BUG CAUGHT IN BUILD
-----------------------
The save handler WHITELISTS setting fields, so the three new ones would have
appeared to save and been gone on reload. Added. They use `?? 25` rather than
`|| 25`, because a deliberate 0% cap is a real setting and `||` would quietly
turn it back into 25.


WORTH CHECKING
--------------
Holly's answer on the facility limit - "your current account can reach a max
500k before being capped" - describes a cap on the DRAWN balance. The page caps
the calculated advance, which is the same thing while you are drawing to the
maximum but not identical if you are not. If you want it to cap drawn + new
advance instead, that is a small change - tell me how Bibby actually apply it.
