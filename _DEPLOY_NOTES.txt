WHAT CHANGED
- Each LESSON now also has a "Person responsible" (a portal user) alongside item,
  detail and the department tags. It shows as a new "Responsible" column in the table.

- A meeting CANNOT BE MARKED COMPLETE unless, for every lesson that has been started:
    * the Lessons Learnt item is filled in,
    * the Lessons Learnt detail is filled in,
    * a person responsible is chosen, and
    * at least one department is ticked (categorised);
  and every meeting action that has text must have a person responsible.

- If you click "Meeting complete" with anything missing, a red warning box lists exactly
  what needs completing (e.g. "Lesson 2: no person responsible", "Action 1: no person
  responsible") and completion is blocked until it's fixed. This is enforced on the
  server too, not just the screen.

- DRAFTS STILL AUTOSAVE as you work - the validation only blocks COMPLETION, so you never
  lose half-finished notes. Completely blank lesson rows are ignored (they don't block
  you); only rows you've started need finishing.
