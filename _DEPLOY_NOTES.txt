1. THE CARETS
-------------
At 9px grey they were nearly invisible whether open or shut, and with two rows
expanded you could not tell which. Now:

    closed   9px, #bbb
    open    12px, BOLD, #1a1a19

One shared Caret component drives all seven columns, so they cannot drift apart
again - which is how the overheads one ended up styled differently from the rest.


2. FINANCING & TAX
------------------
    Item                    Type                   Date        Amount
    PAYE/CIS arrears        liability repayment    22/09/26    -5,000
    Van HP - Ford Transit   recurring commitment   01/09/26    -1,200
    Corporation tax         liability repayment    30/09/26      -410
    Total (3)                                                  -6,610

THE TYPE COLUMN MATTERS. A liability repayment reduces what you owe and never
touches the P&L; a recurring commitment is ordinary spend. Two different things
sharing one column, and nothing said which was which.


3. MATERIALS OUT
----------------
    Project                Delivered   Pays        Amount
    J190 - Russell Hill    05/10/26    15/12/26   -42,441
    J244 - Waungron        01/10/26    30/11/26    -5,000

TWO DATES, DELIBERATELY. Delivered is when the cost is INCURRED - what the P&L and
the application use. Pays is delivery plus that supplier's terms, which is what
this column schedules. They are routinely six to ten weeks apart and the column
only ever showed one of them. "est" flags a payment date that was assumed because
the line carried no term.


A BUG CAUGHT IN BUILD
---------------------
Balance sheet items carry `label`, commitments carry `name`. The drill read only
`label`, so every vehicle commitment would have shown as "(unnamed)". Reads both.
