DEPLOY NOTES - Project Process: checklist ticks no longer undo themselves
=========================================================================

ONE file, EDIT. Replace in GitHub.

  EDIT  pages/operations/project-process.js

PROBLEM
On the role checklists, ticking items could make them tick/untick themselves - especially
when ticking several boxes quickly. Cause: every tick re-fetched the whole board and re-synced
the open card from the server; a fast second tick would race that refresh and get overwritten
by the older server state.

FIX
- Ticking a box now updates instantly and saves in the background, WITHOUT re-fetching the
  board mid-session. No more race, no more self-undoing ticks.
- The open card only re-syncs from the server when you open a DIFFERENT card (not on every
  background change), so an in-flight refresh can never stomp your ticks.
- The board's traffic light / progress count refreshes ONCE when you close the card (X or click
  outside), so it stays accurate.
- Adding an item now merges (keeps your current ticks, just appends the new item) instead of
  replacing the whole list.

Everything else (notes, due date, assignee, chat, reorder, edit, delete) works as before.

NO vercel.json change. No cron. BUILD: Compiled successfully.
