pkg846 - GP margin drill-down: show what the headline is actually made of
=========================================================================

ONE FILE:  pages/scorecard-crm.js

Not part of the multi-tenancy conversion. That continues at pkg847.


THE FAULT
---------
The card and its drill-down were two implementations of the same rule.

  Headline:   invoiced and costs AS AT THAT MONTH'S VALUATION DATE
  Drill-down: raw running totals TO TODAY

Same formula in both - total profit / total invoiced - but different inputs, so
your August card read 2.2% while the four rows underneath added up to 26.3%.

The page already computed the correct rows, as `eomRows`, used them for the
headline, and then handed the drill-down `liveProjects` instead.

Three things followed from that:
  1. rows were not capped at the valuation date
  2. rows included projects the headline EXCLUDES (an in-progress job with no
     valuation date is left out of the figure, but was still listed at full
     value)
  3. there was no totals row, so the disagreement was invisible until someone
     added the column up by hand


WHAT IT DOES NOW
----------------
- The drill-down shows the same rows the headline is built from: invoiced and
  costs as at that month's valuation date.
- A new VALUATION DATE column, so you can see where each project's figures
  stop and why. This is also the visual marker that the deploy landed.
- A TOTALS ROW at the bottom. It must equal the card that opened it. If it ever
  does not, one of them is wrong and you can see which.
- Projects that are NOT counted are listed separately underneath, with the
  reason, instead of silently padding the table.
- The card now says "3 projects counted - 1 not counted" rather than
  "4 live projects", which was counting projects the figure did not include.


COLUMNS REMOVED: Labour and Materials
-------------------------------------
Deliberate. Those two cannot be shown on the valuation-date basis without
re-deriving the labour/materials split from the cost lines, and that rule
already lives in two other places (account categorisation, and the 320/321 rule
in cost-reconcile). Writing a third copy here is exactly the fault that caused
this in the first place.

Total Costs is shown, correctly capped. If you want the split back, say so and
I will wire it to the existing categorisation rather than write a new one.


ALSO FIXED WHILE IN THIS FILE
-----------------------------
The drill-down modal closed on a backdrop click, against your standing rule.
It now closes on the x and on Escape only.

WHAT TO CHECK AFTER DEPLOYING
-----------------------------
1. Open your GP margin card for Aug 26. The table should now have a Valuation
   date column and a Total row.
2. THE TOTAL ROW MUST MATCH THE CARD. Profit, Invoiced, Costs, and the
   percentage. If it does not, tell me the two sets of numbers.
3. Check the valuation dates shown look right for those projects.
4. Look at what is listed under "Not counted" - if a project is there that you
   would expect to be counted, its valuation date is missing for that month.
5. Check an estimator whose card looked correct, and confirm it still does.
6. Press Escape to close the modal. Click the grey area behind it and confirm
   it stays open.

On whether 2.2% is right: with this deployed you will be able to see for
yourself. J244 carrying 99k of materials against 357 of labour looks like a
delivery landing before the valuation date with the invoicing following after,
which would make August genuinely bad rather than wrong. The valuation date
column will show whether that is what happened.
