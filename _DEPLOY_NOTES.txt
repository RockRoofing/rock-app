1. THE 507k "ACTUAL SALES" - DRAFTS WERE IN IT
------------------------------------------------
    if (!a.dueDate || !a.thisCert) continue
    note(..., 'actual', `App ${a.appNumber}${a.status === 'draft' ? ' (draft)' : ''}`, ...)

It knew perfectly well the application was a draft - it printed "(draft)" in the
label - and counted it as an actual sale anyway. An application you have started
but not sent has certified nothing and invoiced nothing.

Drafts are now excluded. The row is also RENAMED to what it actually is:

    "Applied for / month (sent apps, gross)"

You are right that it is not an invoiced figure - it is the certificate gross,
which is the correct P&L basis but is NOT the same as what has been invoiced. The
label said "Actual Sales" and invited exactly that reading.


2. THE OVERHEADS GAP - FOUND, AND IT IS A DIFFERENT ACCOUNT LIST
-----------------------------------------------------------------
    Aug  budgets 54,370   P&L 42,962   short 11,408
    Sep  budgets 54,870   P&L 43,462   short 11,408
    Oct  budgets 55,861   P&L 54,910   short    951
    Nov  budgets 46,134   P&L 45,183   short    951

Identical within each pair - a fixed SET of accounts missing, not a rounding.

    Budgets page   seeds from catConfig - every account categorised as overheads
    Cash flow      seeds from ohBudgets - only accounts with a BUDGET TYPED IN

So any overhead account without a budget was missing from the Forecast P&L, the
Forecast Balance Sheet and the 12-month entirely, while the Budgets page counted
it. The two pages can only agree if they count the same accounts, and now they do.

That also explains why the gap CHANGES between September and October - the missing
accounts use a different forecast method in those months.


AFTER DEPLOYING, the four forecast months should match the Budgets page exactly.
If they still differ, the remaining gap is a forecast-method difference rather
than a missing account, and that is worth knowing on its own.
