WHAT THIS DOES
  The O&Ms page now has a "Build O&M Manual" button (Rock Roofing only) that pulls
  everything together into ONE professional PDF and shows it in a viewer on the page.

  The manual contains, in order:
    Front cover  - Rock Roofing logo, "Operation & Maintenance Manual", project name,
                   project number, project address, Rock Roofing details, and the Main
                   Contractor's company + address (taken from the Internal Handover Minutes
                   customer company/address).
    Contents     - lists each section and the page number it starts at, and how many pages
                   it runs to (page numbers correctly account for multi-page documents).
    Sections     - each preceded by a cover sheet titled with the section and its page
                   number, then the merged documents:
                     1. Technical Submittal (current revisions)
                     2. Rock Roofing Construction Issue Drawings (only drawings marked
                        Construction Issue)
                     3. Calculations (current revisions)
                     4. Leak Test Certificates
                     5. Warranties
                   Sections with no documents are skipped automatically.

  The built manual shows in an on-page VIEWER (scroll through all pages). You can DOWNLOAD
  it, but it is view-only - no mark-up. Customers can view and download; only Rock Roofing
  can build/rebuild. Rebuild after adding/approving more documents to refresh it.
