1. PORTAL USERS AS RECIPIENTS
------------------------------
The Renewal notifications panel now lists every active portal user who has an email
address, with a tick box each. Tick someone and they are added to the weekly list; untick
to remove. The typed-email box is still there for anyone outside the portal.

Ticking stores the EMAIL, not a link to the user account. That is deliberate:

  - if the person is later renamed in the portal, the email keeps working
  - if they are removed from the portal, the notification does NOT silently stop - the
    address stays on the list until you take it off, so nobody quietly falls out of the
    loop without anyone noticing

The tick boxes reflect the current list, so a portal user you added by typing their
address shows as already ticked.


2. SEND NOW - THERE ARE NOW TWO
--------------------------------
  "Send weekly list now"           - the summary to the recipient list. This one already
                                     existed in pkg300; it is just labelled more clearly
                                     now that there are two.

  "Send operative reminders now"   - NEW. Emails every operative whose CSCS or Working at
                                     Height is overdue or due within 6 weeks, immediately.

The operative one IGNORES the normal once-a-week throttle, so someone reminded two days
ago will be emailed again. The confirm box says so before it sends.

Afterwards it reports what happened - e.g. "Sent 7 operative reminders. 2 skipped - no
email address on their Site App user record." That skipped count is worth watching: it is
the only way to spot people who can never receive these.
