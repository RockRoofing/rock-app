WHAT CHANGED
------------

1. "Switch to Actual on the Budgets tab" banner REMOVED from the Forecast P&L.

2. DRILL-DOWNS. Click any month heading marked "forecast" on the Forecast P&L
   and the projects behind it appear under the table: revenue, materials,
   labour, gross and margin % per project, with a total row that ties to the
   column above it.

   Only FORECAST months have this. Xero returns a single cost-of-sales total
   for a closed month, so an ACTUAL month has nothing to break down, and a
   MANUAL month is a figure you typed. Those headings are not clickable.

   A project with revenue and no cost shows "none" in red rather than a dash -
   that is a forecast missing its cost, not a job that costs nothing.

3. MANUAL MONTH FIGURES. New panel on the Forecast P&L above the table.
   Pick a month, type revenue / materials / labour, save. Cost of sale is
   taken as materials plus labour unless you type it explicitly.

   - The month badges as MANUAL (blue) instead of forecast or ACTUAL.
   - "Back to forecast" clears it. Emptying every box does the same.
   - Overheads are NOT overridable - you said Budgets has them right, and
     they keep coming from the Budgets grid either way.
   - A typed 0 is kept as a real figure, not treated as blank.
   - If a manual month is ALSO switched to Actual on Budgets, the typed
     figures win and the row says so rather than silently choosing.

   Stored in config:pl-manual-months.

4. ONE MODEL, THREE PAGES BECAME TWO. The Forecast P&L and the Forecast
   Balance Sheet each rebuilt monthly revenue and cost of sale from the
   project forecasts, with the supersede test, the valuation-date cap and the
   undated-line rule written out separately in each file. Forecast Margin was
   a third copy. That is now lib/forecastMonths.js, read by both remaining
   pages - so your August figures reach the balance sheet without being
   stored twice, and a future fix to the accrual rules lands in one place.

   On the balance sheet a MANUAL month is deliberately NOT treated as an
   actual month: re-basing onto Xero is what an actual month does, and the
   reason you typed August by hand is that Xero's August is not usable.

5. Forecast gross margin page removed. Everything it showed is on the
   Forecast P&L now that the drill-downs are there. Its "margin above 35% is
   not credible" warning is gone with it, as asked.

6. Store Audit removed (pkg721).
