WHAT CHANGED
Each date cell in the H&S Training Matrix can now hold an attachment (the training certificate /
CSCS card image or PDF):
  - Click a cell to open the editor. As well as the expiry date / "Does not expire", there is now
    a "Certificate / card" section with "+ Attach file" (images or PDF).
  - Once attached, the file name shows with a link; you can remove or replace it.
  - Cells with an attachment show a small paperclip in the top-right corner. Click the paperclip
    to open the certificate in a new tab (without opening the editor) - so you can check the CSCS
    card matches at a glance.
  - A cell can hold just an attachment, just a date, or both.

Files upload straight to secure blob storage (same mechanism as the design certificates).

NO vercel.json change. No cron. No middleware change. BUILD: Compiled successfully.
