THE CHANGE
----------
  Forecasted Income / month   ->  Forecasted Sales / month
  Actual Income / month       ->  Actual Sales / month

And, more importantly, they now bucket by the TRANSACTION date - the valuation
date the work was applied for - not by the date the money lands.

An August valuation paid in October is AUGUST sales. Before, that 88,128.18 sat
in October because that is when the cash arrived.

The cash rows above are untouched. "Sales in", "Retention in" and "Total IN /
week" still work off payment dates, because they answer a different question -
when the money is in the bank. The two must not be mixed and are now computed
from separate maps: cashByDay by payment date, the Sales rows off the detail
index by transaction date.


WHICH DATE IS USED AS THE TRANSACTION DATE
------------------------------------------
  Actual application     its valuation date (valDate, falling back to appDate)
  Forecast, project or
  weekly/fortnightly     the application date the cycle produced
  Forecast, per
  application            the end of the calendar month being valued, or the
                         period end for an older forecast with no monthly split


RETENTION IS NO LONGER IN THESE ROWS
------------------------------------
It used to be added to the Income row. A retention release is cash arriving
against work sold months ago, not a new sale, so counting it as sales in the
month it is released overstates that month and understates the one the work was
actually valued in.

It has not been lost - it is still on "Retention in" and in "Total IN / week",
where it belongs. Tell me if you would rather it stayed on the sales row.


THE HOVER NOW SHOWS BOTH DATES

    val 2026-08-28 -> cash 2026-10-14  J251 - Ellesmere Port forecast       19,800.00

The cash date is only shown where it differs from the valuation date.


ONE THING THIS WILL CHANGE ON SCREEN
------------------------------------
Money will MOVE MONTHS the first time you look. Anything on a 30 or 60 day term
shifts one or two months earlier, into the month it was valued. That is the
point, but it will not look like your old figures - expect August to change even
before any double-counting is dealt with.

The two causes of the inflated figures are still worth reading off the hover:
a DRAFT with "Previously certified (gross)" blank contributing its full
cumulative value (marked "(draft)"), and a project appearing as both "App N" and
"forecast" where the forecast runs past the application it overlaps.
