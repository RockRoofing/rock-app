1. NUMBERING FOLLOWS THE TRACKER
----------------------------------
The next number is now the highest on the TRACKER plus one, whatever raised it and whether
or not it has been sent.

I had this following sends in pkg517, so an abandoned draft did not burn a number. You are
right that it was wrong: the tracker is the register, and handing out a number that
already exists on it gives you two V03s on one project - which is worse than a gap in the
sequence.

It also no longer renumbers at send time, so a draft somebody has already referred to in
an email keeps the number they quoted.


2. THE EMAIL SAYS IT PLAINLY
------------------------------
Added above the button, in bold:

    We are unable to proceed without your instruction via the below instruct button.

On the first send and on the automatic reminder. Bolded by matching the line itself, so it
stays bold even if the rest of the message is edited before sending.


3. THE LOGO WAS SQUASHED
--------------------------
The file is SQUARE - 565 x 565 - and was being drawn 92 wide by 46 tall. Half its proper
height, which is why it looked wrong.

Now scaled from the image: fixed height, width follows. Correct whatever the file is
replaced with later.

THE WIP PDF HAD THE SAME FAULT - 70 x 34 on the same square logo - and is fixed too. The
application PDF was already scaling properly and is untouched.


4. THE FOOTER NAMES WHO RAISED IT
-----------------------------------
    Raised 19/08/2026 at 14:32 by Roman Jarosz
    0121 827 0433   ·   roman@rockroofing.co.uk
    Any queries on this variation, please contact us using the details above.

"Produced from the Rock Roofing portal" told the customer nothing they could act on. A
variation they want to query should carry the name and number of the person who priced it.

Recorded on the variation when it is raised, so the document still names the right person
months later - and WHOEVER RAISED IT FIRST STAYS ON IT. Editing a variation does not
quietly reassign it to whoever happened to open it.
