HOW IT WORKS
------------
Nothing is copied or synced. The Ops page reads design:rock-drawings:<projectNo> LIVE, so
the Design Portal is the single source of truth. Approve a drawing or mark it Construction
Issue in the Design Portal and the Ops page and Site App reflect it immediately - there is
no sync job to wait for and nothing to go stale.

Only the NEWEST revision of each drawing is shown. Superseded revisions stay in the Design
Portal as history and never reach Ops or site. (If a drawing somehow has every revision
marked superseded, the newest is still shown rather than the drawing disappearing.)

Where a stamped copy exists, that is what is shown and downloaded - so the APPROVED /
CONSTRUCTION ISSUE stamp is on the PDF itself, not just the label on screen.


1. OPS > PROJECTS > DRAWINGS
-----------------------------
Upload and Delete are GONE. View and Download only.

Two banners across the top:
  green   "X of Y approved"              + how many still awaiting customer approval
  blue    "X of Y Construction Issue"    + how many are not released to site

Every drawing shows as a card with its first page as a thumbnail and three labels:
  Rev A / B / C
  Approved            or  Not approved
  Construction Issue  or  Not for site


2. SITE APP > DRAWINGS
-----------------------
Now shows ONLY drawings marked Construction Issue. Operatives cannot see anything that has
not been released for construction.

Empty state reworded - it used to say "No drawings uploaded for this project yet", which
would now be misleading. It says drawings have not been issued for construction.

RAMS in the Site App are untouched and still come from project-files.


*** READ THIS BEFORE DEPLOYING ***
-----------------------------------
Any drawings previously UPLOADED under Ops > Projects > Drawings will DISAPPEAR from both
the Ops page and the Site App the moment this goes live.

They are not deleted - they are still stored - but nothing displays them any more, because
both screens now read the Design Portal instead.

So on any live project where operatives currently get their drawings from an Ops upload
rather than the Design Portal, site will lose access until those drawings exist in the
Design Portal's Rock Drawings AND are marked Construction Issue.

WORTH CHECKING FIRST: pick two or three live jobs and confirm their drawings are in the
Design Portal and marked Construction Issue before you push this.

If it turns out a lot of projects rely on the old uploads, tell me and I can either:
  - add a "Previously uploaded" section on the Ops page so the old files stay reachable
    and can be moved across, or
  - write a one-off migration that copies them into the Design Portal.

Neither is difficult - but it is much better to decide before site loses drawings than
after.


ALSO WORTH KNOWING
------------------
Drawings only carry the stamp on the PDF if their status was set AFTER the stamping work
went live. Anything approved or marked Construction Issue before that shows the correct
LABELS on the Ops page (those are read live), but the PDF itself will not carry a stamp
until the status is toggled. Select all -> Unmark -> Mark Construction Issue on the Design
Portal's Rock Drawings page re-stamps a whole project.
