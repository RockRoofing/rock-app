DEPLOY NOTES - Minutes: auto-save + multi-open sections
=======================================================

THREE files, all EDITS - just replace them.
  pages/handover/form.js        (Internal Handover Minutes)
  components/PreStartForm.js     (Pre-Start Minutes)
  components/ProjectConcerns.js  (Project Concern meeting minutes)

1. AUTO-SAVE (all three)
   While completing the minutes, the form now auto-saves a DRAFT in the background a
   moment after you stop typing - so clicking away won't lose the work. A small
   "Auto-saving.../Draft auto-saved" note shows by the buttons. It never sends and never
   finalises; your Save / Complete / Save & send buttons work exactly as before.
   - IHM auto-saves once the Project Name + RR Project Number are filled (the record is
     keyed by project number).
   - Pre-Start auto-saves while editing (not once the minutes are 'sent'/locked).
   - Project Concern auto-saves the meeting as you edit; the first auto-save captures
     the new meeting id so later saves update the same record.

2. MULTI-OPEN SECTIONS (Internal Handover Minutes)
   The IHM section accordion now lets multiple sections stay open at once. Opening one
   no longer closes another - each section opens/closes only when you click its own
   header. (Pre-Start and Project Concern already show all sections at once, so this
   applies to the IHM.)
