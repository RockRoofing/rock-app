IT IS NOT THE EXPECTED DATES
----------------------------
That money is not overdue invoices at all. It is the "applied for but not yet
invoiced" guard I added in pkg670, and it was claiming the lot.

    invoiced = invByProject[fc.projectNo] || 0
    short    = certified - invoiced

RECEIVABLES DO NOT RELIABLY CARRY projectNo. The Xero sync attaches it where it
can and leaves it blank otherwise. Where it was blank the lookup returned ZERO, so
"certified less invoiced" became "certified less nothing" - and the ENTIRE
certified value of every applied-for period was dumped into week 1 as money
awaiting an invoice.

    certified 500,000, invoiced 480,000  ->      20,000   correct
    certified 500,000, lookup fails      ->     500,000   what was happening

No amount of setting expected dates would have touched it, because it was never
reading them.


THE FIX - PROVE THE MATCH FIRST
-------------------------------
Matched on project NAME as well as number, and where a project has no matchable
invoices at all the guard now says NOTHING rather than assuming the worst. It
cannot tell "fully invoiced" from "could not match", and guessing between them is
what caused this.

THE CARRY-FORWARD HAD THE SAME FAULT. An unmatched project read certified as nil
and carried its whole period into the next one. Same guard applied.

The cost side was always fine - bills do carry b.project, so that lookup has
always matched.


AFTER DEPLOYING, the arrears row should fall to genuinely overdue invoices only,
and the day-one spike should go with it. The reconciliation panel will then show a
much smaller "collection of debt that existed on day one" - and if the trading
movement is still around -18,000, that is a real figure worth looking at.
