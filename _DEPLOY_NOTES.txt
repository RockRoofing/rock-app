EMAIL 1 - YOUR OWN ACTIVITIES (per person)
--------------------------------------------
Goes only to people who have something due today or overdue, listing only their own.
Nothing due, no email.


EMAIL 2 - PROJECTS WITH NO ACTIVITY SET (new)
-----------------------------------------------
Goes to EVERY pre-contract and admin user, all getting the same list. It leads with:

    All projects must have an activity set. Please check your projects and update
    accordingly.

Then every OPEN project with nothing outstanding against it - project, customer, stage, and
whoever owns it (estimator, or sales person if there is no estimator). Projects with nobody
assigned show "nobody assigned" in amber, since those are the ones most likely to sit
untouched.

Won and lost projects are excluded. If every open project has an activity, NO EMAIL IS
SENT - same principle as the first one.

Verified against a worked example: projects with an outstanding activity are left out, the
won job is left out, and an unowned project is flagged.


BOTH AT 01:00 UK, FOR NOW
--------------------------
Both go out on the same run, at the top of the 01:00 hour UK, holding through the clock
change. That is for your testing - say when you want the daily one moved back to the
morning and I will change it. The projects report might reasonably stay overnight.

TESTING WITHOUT WAITING:
  ?dryRun=1   both report what they WOULD send - who, and how many - and send nothing
  ?force=1    both send immediately

The response comes back as { personal: {...}, noActivity: {...} } so you can see how each
one got on separately.

One is not allowed to break the other: if the projects report fails, the personal emails
still go, and the error is reported alongside them.
