THE CAUSE
---------
The sending address was chosen like this:

    COMMERCIAL_FROM_EMAIL || ACCOUNTS_FROM_EMAIL || FORMS_FROM_EMAIL

COMMERCIAL_FROM_EMAIL is not set, so everything fell through to accounts receivable.

Now NOTIFY_FROM_EMAIL, the same address the portal's other notifications use.

ACCOUNTS_FROM_EMAIL is out of the chain entirely rather than left further down it. It was
never the right address for a variation, and as a fallback it comes straight back the
moment another variable is unset - which is precisely how this happened.


I CHANGED ALL THREE, NOT JUST THE REMINDER
--------------------------------------------
You asked about the reminders. The first send and the instruction confirmation had the
same fault and the same address, and a variation arriving from accounts receivable invites
the wrong reading of it - it is a request to authorise works, not a demand for payment.

Say if you would rather the first send came from somewhere else and I will split them.

Replies are unchanged: they still go to the person who raised the variation.


LEFT ALONE, DELIBERATELY
------------------------
    application-send    still accounts receivable - it IS a payment application
    chase-email-send    still accounts receivable - it IS chasing an invoice

Those two are correct as they are.
