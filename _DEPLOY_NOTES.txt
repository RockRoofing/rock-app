ONE FIELD THAT WOULD HAVE BEEN WRONG
------------------------------------
The obvious choice was thisCert.subTotal - the Sub-Total row on the certificate.
It is wrong, and plausibly so.

subTotal is `base - mcd`, where base is only the MCD-ABLE part. On a job where
MCD applies to the contract sum but not to variations or materials on site, those
parts are held in a separate `after` figure and subTotal omits them entirely.

  MCD 2.5% on everything          gross 120,000  subTotal 117,000  netBeforeRet 117,000
  MCD 2.5% on contract sum only   gross 120,000  subTotal  97,500  netBeforeRet 117,500

The second line is a 100,000 contract with a 20,000 variation. subTotal loses the
whole variation - 20,000 short, on a row that would have looked entirely
reasonable. netBeforeRet is the whole account after the discount, and that is
what is used.

On J240, MCD is 0% and all three agree, so this would not have shown up there.


OLDER FORECASTS
---------------
A forecast stores its NET figure, so only the retention is added back - the MCD
stays deducted. Exact, not an estimate: net 111,625.00 recovers 117,500.00, which
matches netBeforeRet to the penny. No re-saving needed.
