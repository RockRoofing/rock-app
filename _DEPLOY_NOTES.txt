ABOUT THE "OVERHEADS OUT" COLUMN (your question)
   That column is ONLY the scheduled overheads from the Cash Schedule - not all
   outgoings. Total weekly out = Bills + Overheads + Vehicles + VAT + CIS (which is
   what the Net column reflects). The new totals row makes the split clear.

   WATCH-OUT: "Depreciation Expense" (code 416) is currently scheduled in your
   overheads, but depreciation is a NON-CASH accounting entry - it doesn't actually
   leave the bank. Including it overstates cash-out by ~£1,101/mo. Consider setting
   its timing to "- not in forecast -". (Bad debts / Profit-Loss on Disposal are
   already not set - good.)
