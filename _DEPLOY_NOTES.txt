TWO FAULTS
----------

1. THE STRADDLE TEST WAS MISSING. It existed before pkg729 and I removed it when
   rebuilding around application dates. A forecast had to still be running at
   month end:

     fc.from <= 30 Nov AND fc.to >= 30 Nov

   Without it, a Renshaw period running 14-30 September was given a window from
   its own start through to 30 November - two and a half months of cost in what
   should be a few days. Renshaw House appeared three times, Winnersh from
   9 September, Bradford Works from 21 November.

   A period that finished in September has no unclaimed work at the year end. It
   was claimed in September.

2. LABOUR ALREADY INSIDE THE WINDOW WAS BEING PRO-RATED. That is why the table
   showed "already in Nov" LARGER than "accrued cost" - Bradford Works 5,255
   against 3,311, Winnersh 11,002 against 3,000. Already-in cannot exceed
   accrued; the two were measured on different bases.

   A labour line carries the END of its instalment window. A line dated on or
   before the 30th is wholly inside the window and now counts at FACE VALUE.
   Only the first line dated AFTER month end straddles the boundary, and only
   that one is pro-rated - across its own instalment, from the previous line's
   date, not across the whole forecast.

   The cost top-up is therefore exactly the straddling share, and can never be
   negative or exceed the accrual.
