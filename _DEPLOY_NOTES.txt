pkg452 - one timeline, and Complete activity on the deal
========================================================

Deploy after pkg451. One changed file: pages/crm.js. No new files.


1. ONE TIMELINE INSTEAD OF TWO LISTS
------------------------------------
Email and history were two separate stacks - all the mail, then all the history. Following
what actually happened on a project meant reading both and holding the dates in your head:
a reply and the note written about it sat a screen apart.

They are now one list, newest first, with filter chips across the top:

    Everything 24 | Emails 11 | Activities 6 | Notes 4 | Changes 3

The counts are live. "Changes" is the catch-all - stage moves, value edits, won/lost,
mentions - so a new kind of history entry turns up there rather than disappearing from
every filter.

Switching a filter hides rows. It never reorders or reloads, so you do not lose your place.

The Email section above is unchanged and still there. That is deliberate: it is where you
work THROUGH mail - allocate, move, remove - while the timeline is for reading what
happened in order. Same data, two jobs.


2. COMPLETE ACTIVITY, THE SAME EVERYWHERE
-------------------------------------------
Pressing Done on an activity in the deal used to just tick it. No note of what happened,
no follow-up. The Activities page has always asked both.

The deal now opens the same modal:

    What happened? (call notes, email summary, outcome)
    Set the next activity - what, when, who

Two routes to the same action behaving differently is how outcomes go unrecorded - Edita
completing from the Activities page left a record, the same person completing from the
deal left nothing.


3. "WHAT HAPPENED" IS VISIBLE IN THE TIMELINE
-----------------------------------------------
A recorded outcome now shows as its own block under the activity:

    Activity completed: Call
    ┃ WHAT HAPPENED
    ┃ Spoke to Dave, pricing agreed, sending revised quote Friday

Rather than being dropped in as plain text indistinguishable from the activity's own
description. Applies to completions from both screens.


ONE THING WORTH KNOWING
-----------------------
The follow-up activity is created a moment AFTER the completion is recorded, deliberately.
Created in the same instant they share a timestamp and the timeline sorts them
arbitrarily - so "completed the call" could appear below "call them again", which reads
backwards.
