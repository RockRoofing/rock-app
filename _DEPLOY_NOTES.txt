1. PROJECT CASH FLOWS TABLE
---------------------------
Below the forecast: every project contributing to the 13 weeks, with sales in,
materials out, labour out, net and COST %.

Built from the same fcBreak rows the weekly drill-down uses, so it agrees with the
Project sales / Materials / Labour columns by construction rather than by luck.

Cost % is red below 50% and a missing schedule reads "none" - so a forecast with
income and no cost against it is obvious across the whole quarter, not just in the
week you happen to open.


2. CIS MARKED AS ESTIMATED
--------------------------
A week whose CIS came partly from FORECAST labour now shows "est", the same as
VAT. CIS from a bill already ticked in Xero is not marked - that is real.

Hovering says which: "Includes CIS predicted from forecast labour" or "From bills
ticked as CIS labour".


3. RISK - PAY ME LATER (WEEKS)
------------------------------
Delays every RECEIPT by N weeks: invoices, retention and forecast sales.

    0 weeks   an invoice due 10/09 lands in the week of 10/09
    2 weeks   ... the week of 24/09
    4 weeks   ... the week of 08/10

MONEY OUT IS NOT MOVED, and that asymmetry is the whole point. Customers paying
late does not make your suppliers, your labour or HMRC wait. Shifting both would
slide the entire picture and show you nothing.

The box turns red while a delay is applied, so a risk run cannot be mistaken for
the plan. It does not touch any stored date.


4. THE PDF - NOT BUILT, AND HERE IS WHY
---------------------------------------
"PDF every tab into one document with tick options" is a build in itself, not a
change to an existing page. There are two honest routes:

  A. PRINT STYLESHEET. A /business-financials/report page that renders the ticked
     tabs one after another, styled for print, and you use the browser's Print to
     PDF. Fastest, looks like the screen, and page breaks need care. No new
     dependency.

  B. SERVER-SIDE with pdf-lib, which you already use. Full control of layout,
     headers, page numbers, and it can be emailed or scheduled. But every tab's
     figures have to be re-derived server-side - the tables are currently built in
     the browser from several API calls each - so it is the larger piece by some
     distance.

B is the better product and roughly a day's work; A gets you something usable much
sooner. Tell me which and I will scope it properly rather than start it half-built
at the end of a long session.
