to the Budgets call and missed the CASHFLOW call - which is the one the Forecast
P&L, Forecast Balance Sheet and 12-month all read. So August kept showing a

THE REAL ANSWER TO YOUR QUESTION - STOP CHASING SYMPTOMS
----------------------------------------------------------
New "Reconcile" tab. It counts your three sources of truth ONCE and runs the
checks that must pass before anything built on them can be right. Every one is
arithmetic, not opinion:

  Cost as a share of forecast sales    is cost missing from the forecasts
  Schedules add up to period revenue   is money vanishing in the spread (Winnersh)
  Forecasts match the invoice ledger   can netting work at all (the 434,338)
  Forecasts carry valuation dates      are periods cut at the right point
  Invoices have expected dates         is the timing real or assumed
  Invoices and bills carry a project   can they be netted, or double-counted

A failing check NAMES THE FAULT rather than the symptom. Both bugs you hit today -
Winnersh's 90% spread and the 434,338 - would have shown up here as a red line
instead of you spotting a missing payment three screens away.

Below the checks: every forecast as STORED, with revenue against scheduled sales,
materials, labour and cost %. Red where a schedule does not tie or a cost is nil.

  Bills CSV          supplier, project, due, planned, CIS, total, still due
