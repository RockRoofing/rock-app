pkg367 - email sync every 15 minutes
====================================

  vercel.json

NEW FILES
---------
  NONE.


WHAT CHANGED
------------
One line. The CRM email sync moves from hourly to every 15 minutes:

    "schedule": "0 * * * *"      ->   "schedule": "*/15 * * * *"

Worst case an email now takes 15 minutes to appear in the CRM instead of 60. Average wait
drops from about 30 minutes to about 7.

Nothing else in the file is touched - the other 11 crons are exactly as they were.


BEFORE YOU PASTE
----------------
vercel.json is a whole-file replacement. If you have edited it directly in GitHub since
you sent me the repo, that edit will be lost. Worth a glance at the file in GitHub first -
it should have 12 crons, ending with crm-email-sync.


COST
----
Each run is one Microsoft Graph call per mailbox and returns fetched:0 when there is
nothing new. Four mailboxes, four times an hour, is about 380 calls a day. Well inside
Graph's limits and negligible on Vercel Pro.


STILL OUTSTANDING FROM THE LAST CONVERSATION
--------------------------------------------
The "last run" timestamp race - the sync remembers the clock time of the run rather than
the newest message it saw, so an email landing mid-run can fall through the gap. Rare, and
it existed at hourly too, but running four times as often means four times as many
windows. The fix is small and lives in lib/crmEmailSync.js. Not included here because you
asked for the schedule on its own - say the word and it is a five minute job.
