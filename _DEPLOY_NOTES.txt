DEPLOY NOTES - Fix scheduled send time (UTC vs UK time)
=======================================================

TWO files, both EDITS - just replace them.
  lib/outstandingInvoicesReport.js     (weekly report schedule gate fixed)
  pages/api/cron/notifications.js       (notifications evaluated in UK time)

WHY THE AUTO-SEND DIDN'T FIRE
  The cron ticks hourly in UTC, but the schedule was being compared against UK local
  time with an exact-hour match. During British Summer Time UK is UTC+1, so the hours
  never lined up - e.g. the cron's "11:00" tick is really 12:00 UK, so an 11:00 UK
  schedule was always missed. It also used a fragile way to read UK time.

FIX
  - UK day + hour are now read reliably (Intl parts), and the weekly report fires when
    it's the right UK day and the UK hour is AT OR AFTER the target hour (then a
    once-per-day guard prevents repeats). So the correct hour is no longer missed by the
    UTC/BST offset, and a slightly late cron tick still catches the day.
  - The notifications cron now also evaluates "today" in UK time (correct day near
    midnight).

AFTER DEPLOY
  Set the weekly report schedule to the UK day + hour you want on the Outstanding
  Invoices page. It will send on the next cron tick at/after that hour on that day.
  (Manual "Send now" was already working - this only affects the automatic schedule.)

  Note: because it now fires "at or after" the target hour, if you set a time that has
  already passed TODAY on the chosen day, it will send on the very next cron tick (top of
  the next hour) rather than waiting a week.
