WHAT THE SPLIT ALREADY TELLS US
-------------------------------
  project sales     857,011
  materials out     130,679    15.2% of sales
  labour out        224,686    26.2%
  total cost        355,365    41.5%
  implied margin                58.5%

A roofing subcontract runs 75-85% cost. At 80% the cost on 857,011 would be
685,609 - so ROUGHLY 330,000 OF COST IS MISSING from the project forecasts. The
cash flow is counting that income and not the spend against it, which is most of
your 668k.

The other half of it: invoices in 550,352 plus project sales 857,011 is 1,407,363
in 13 weeks, an annualised 5.6m. And the project sales all fall in the last eight
weeks - 107,126 a week of NEW work on top of the existing debtors.

Both point at the forecasts themselves, not at the cash flow arithmetic.

no spend is obvious at a glance.

That tells you exactly which projects to fix in Commercial, rather than either of
us guessing again.


WHAT I EXPECT YOU TO FIND
-------------------------
Several projects with large sales and either no materials or no labour scheduled.
Those forecasts were built to model REVENUE and the cost side was never filled in
- which does not matter on the project cash flow page, where you are looking at
one job's income, but matters enormously here.

If that is what you see, the fix is in the project forecasts, not this page. Send
me a screenshot of the drill-down and we can decide whether to fill them in, or to
apply a default cost percentage where none is scheduled.
