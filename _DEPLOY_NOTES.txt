PROBABLY THIS, AND YOU CAN CHECK IT IN ONE LOOK
------------------------------------------------
Today is 31 August. A forecast whose period ENDS ON OR BEFORE TODAY has its sales
dropped - that is the rule you and I agreed: if a period has passed without being
applied for, you have not earned the money, so it is not future cash.

So if you have just built forecasts for the period that has JUST ENDED, they will
be saved correctly and still show nothing. That is the rule working, not a fault -
but nothing on the page said so, which is the actual problem.


THREE GATES, ALL PREVIOUSLY SILENT
----------------------------------
  1. no `accrual` data      the API file has not been deployed
  2. sales dropped          period already applied for, OR period ended before today
  3. outside the window     its cash falls beyond the next 13 weeks

The Cash Flow page now lists every forecast that is NOT in the figures, and why:

    3 forecasts not in the figures above.
      J190 - Russell Hill - period ended 31/08/26 - sales drop once a period has
        passed without being applied for
      J248 - Renshaw House - already applied for (application to 31/08/26) - its
        money is a real invoice now
      J251 - Winnersh - its cash falls outside the next 13 weeks

The Forecast Margin page does the same for its empty state, and distinguishes the
cases: no forecasts at all, versus all of them missing accrual data because the
API is not deployed, versus forecasts with no monthly sales spread saved.


SO, IN ORDER
------------
1. Deploy this and open the Cash Flow. Read the amber list.
2. If it says "no accrual data", pages/api/business-financials.js has not gone up -
   that is the file that carries the accrual dates the P&L and Margin both need.
3. If it says "period ended", the forecast is for a period that has passed. Raise
   the application, or move the forecast period forward.
