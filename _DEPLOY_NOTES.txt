    as the bookkeeping page; shows matched/unassigned counts when done.
  - Retentions Due -> "Sync from Xero" (POST /api/sync-invoices). Retention figures
    come from invoices (account 612), so this refreshes them.
  - Budgets        -> "Sync figures from Xero" (POST /api/sync-benchmark). Refreshes
    the P&L benchmark that drives the actuals/predicted spend.
