THE INSTRUCT BUTTON
-------------------
Every variation email now carries a green "Instruct variation V01" button.

It opens a page showing what they are instructing - project, ref, description, value, and
a link to the PDF - with a name box and an Instruct button.

NOT A ONE-CLICK INSTRUCTION, deliberately. A link that instructed on click would be
quicker, and would also mean a mis-click, an email scanner or a link preview could commit
us to work. There is a page, and a button on it.

ONE EMAIL PER RECIPIENT, each with its own link carrying that person's address. Four
people on one email would share one link and the instruction could only ever say "somebody
at the customer clicked it".


WHAT GETS RECORDED
------------------
    when it was instructed
    the name they typed
    THE EMAIL ADDRESS THE LINK WAS ISSUED TO - not one typed on the page
    their IP address and browser

The signed link is what makes this evidence rather than a name in a box. Tested: a token
edited to point at a different variation is rejected.


IT UPDATES EVERYWHERE
---------------------
Instructing sets instructed: 'yes' - the field the tracker, the anticipated final account,
applications, cash flow and project financials all already read. So it updates all of them
with no further work, and the dashboard cache is cleared so it shows immediately.


REMINDERS
---------
MANUAL: on a variation already sent, the button becomes "Send reminder to customer" and
the email rewrites itself as a chase - "Following up on variation V01, sent on 19/08/2026.
We have not yet received your instruction."

AUTOMATIC: a cron at 9am on weekdays chases anything sent 3 WORKING days ago and not
instructed. Working days, so a Thursday send is chased the following Tuesday rather than
landing on a Sunday.

SENT ONCE. Something still not instructed after that is a conversation to have, not
another email to ignore.

Test it safely first:  /api/cron/variation-reminders?dry=1
That reports exactly what it would send, and sends nothing.
