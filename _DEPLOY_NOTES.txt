ONE THING TO NOTE ABOUT xero/callback.js
----------------------------------------
That is where Xero sends the user back after authorising. Wrapped, it resolves
the customer from the address it was called on, which is right - a customer
connecting their own Xero must land on their own subdomain. Worth remembering
when the first outside customer connects Xero: the redirect URI registered with
Xero has to match the address they came from, not Rock's. That is configuration,
not code, but it will bite if nobody is expecting it.
