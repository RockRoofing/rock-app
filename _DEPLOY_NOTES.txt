THE TICK BOX
------------
Beside the application number and period:

    Application  14  — August 2026   [x] Final Account

Tick it and nothing structural changes. It keeps its number, its period, its dates and its
place in the sequence - because it IS an application for payment, the last one. Anything
else would have broken the numbering.


WHAT CHANGES ON THE PDF
-----------------------
    title       "Final Account" instead of "Application for Payment"
    banner      "FINAL ACCOUNT - this is the last application on this project"
    footer      "Final Account 14" instead of "Application 14"
    filename    "Final Account 14 - J178 Gas Lane.pdf"

The banner says it twice on purpose. A document titled Final Account that still reads
"Application no. 14" invites the question, so the page answers it.

The filename matters more than it looks - that PDF ends up in somebody's Downloads folder
next to eleven others called "Application n".

The emailed attachment is named the same way, so the copy the customer receives matches
the one the QS has on file.


IN THE LIST
-----------
A FINAL ACCOUNT badge beside the period, so it can be picked out without opening every
application to find which one it was.


ONE SMALL THING WORTH THE MINUTE
----------------------------------
The extra banner line needed extra vertical space in the PDF. Without it the project name
printed on top of it - the cursor moves 30pt after the title and the banner sits 36pt
down. Now 44pt when the flag is set, checked at both settings.
