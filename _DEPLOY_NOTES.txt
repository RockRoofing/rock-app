pkg750 - Card headroom missing from the availability line
==========================================================

DEPLOY OVER pkg749 (contains 742-749). Four files.

REPLACE:
  pages/api/business-financials.js
  pages/business-financials/cashflow.js
  pages/business-financials/monthly.js
  pages/business-financials/invoice-finance.js

WHAT WAS WRONG
--------------
Cards WERE in the dashed line - avail = closing + cards + IF - but the card
figure was computed twice, from two different sources:

  "Max cash available" box   built its card list from your MANUAL balances
  the dashed line            built its own from data.balances.accounts (XERO'S)

Your cards are entered by hand, so Xero's list did not flag them as cards,
anyPerCard came out false, and the line fell back to the pooled "Card limit"
box - which is 0.

So the box showed 82,844 of card headroom and the graph showed none, off the
same two numbers. That is why the line looked like it excluded them.

THE FIX
-------
One calculation, at component scope, read by both. Manual balances first, Xero
as the fallback - the same precedence the rest of the page uses.

Checked against your actual figures: AMEX 60,000 limit with nil owed, plus
Capital on Tap 100,000 limit with 77,156.42 owed = 82,843.58. That is the
82,844 the box has been showing, and it is now in the line too.

WHAT TO CHECK
-------------
  - The dashed total-available line should jump by about 82,844 across the
    whole window.
  - Hover any week: "Card headroom" in the tooltip should read 82,844, and
    total available should equal closing cash + that + still-drawable IF.
  - It should match the "Max cash available" box at week 0.

If you set a per-card limit and the two ever disagree again, tell me - they read
one figure now, so they cannot.
