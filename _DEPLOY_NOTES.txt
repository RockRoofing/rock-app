pkg737 - Find the missing 127k
===============================

DEPLOY OVER pkg736. One file.

REPLACE:
  pages/business-financials/cashflow.js

THE MECHANISM
-------------
Invoice placement reads:

    if (isArrears(d))              { ... }
    else if (inWkIn(d, offsetFor(i))) { ... }

Two branches and NO else. An invoice matching neither is dropped in silence.
Three ways that happens:

  - no due date AND no expected date
  - a date after the end of the 13-week window
  - a date pushed past the window by the payment-performance offset

Nothing counted them, nothing reported them, and Invoices in simply read lower
than the ledger. That is the 127k, or most of it.

WHAT THIS ADDS
--------------
A red strip above the table whenever anything fails to land:

  "X of invoices owed never landed in any week (N of M). Total owed Y, of which
   Z you excluded. Click for the list and the reason for each."

Per invoice: customer, invoice number, the date used, the offset applied, the
amount, and WHY it is not in the forecast. Your invoice due tomorrow will be in
that list with the reason next to it.

TWO THINGS FIXED WHILE IN THERE
--------------------------------
The offset rule was defined inside the week loop, so the reconciliation could
not see it. Hoisted to ONE definition that both use - a second copy is how these
pages have drifted before. Moved below `usePerf` rather than above it, so there
is no temporal dead zone for whoever edits this next.

The week rows carried weekStart but NOT weekEnd. My first cut of the "after the
horizon" test read r.weekEnd, which did not exist - so it would have silently
reported every such invoice as "no date" instead. weekEnd is now set on the row,
and the horizon is taken as the latest week rather than by array position.

ON PAYMENT PERFORMANCE
----------------------
You said that is not the cause. It may not be - but the strip names the offset
per invoice, so if a 30-day median is pushing September invoices into December
it will say so explicitly rather than either of us assuming. If the Offset
column reads "-" throughout, you were right and it is the dates.

NOTHING ABOUT THE FIGURES CHANGES. This only reports what was already being
dropped.
