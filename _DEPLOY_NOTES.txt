THE REVENUE OVERRIDE
--------------------
"Revenue this period" is now an editable box, same as Labour and Materials on
site. Click Override, type a figure, Reset to go back. The calculated figure
stays visible underneath and the box takes a teal border so an overridden
forecast is obvious.

All three overrides now share one component rather than three near-copies.


WHAT IT DRIVES, AND WHAT IT DELIBERATELY DOES NOT
-------------------------------------------------
DRIVES: the month spread, the sales payment schedule, the sales-in cash stream,
and the revenueThisPeriod figure saved on the forecast - which is what the cash
flow chart reads.

DOES NOT: rewrite the percentages. Gross to date, the labour position and the
materials position all still show the true measured position, and the NEXT
forecast still carries on from the real numbers.

That separation matters. If a typed revenue figure fed back into the
percentages, one adjustment would silently move what the following forecast
believes has already been claimed, and the error would compound down the chain.
So the override says "this is the cash I expect to receive for this period",
not "this is how much work got done".

thisCertTotal is still saved as the CALCULATED certificate value, untouched, so
the measured position remains on the record next to whatever you typed.


WHY IT WAS NEEDED
-----------------
The calculation - increment over the last certificate, less MCD and retention -
is the right default and still only a model. A QS disputes a percentage, a claim
is part-certified, an application lands late and slips a month. Good starting
suggestion, poor rule.
