BACKGROUND (the gap you spotted)
--------------------------------
The P&L overhead figures are NET of VAT, and balance-sheet payments (like vehicle
finance principal) never hit the P&L at all. So the forecast could understate real
cash out. Two fixes:

1) "+VAT" TICK BOX PER OVERHEAD (Cash Schedule)
   Not all overheads carry VAT (wages, insurance don't; fuel, rent, subs do). Tick
   "+VAT" on the lines that do, and the forecast grosses that line up by 20% for the
   cash-out timing. The input VAT still nets off at the VAT return (VAT Refund page),
   so this only affects WHEN the cash moves, not the net total - no double count.
   The "Predicted / mo" column shows the inc-VAT figure when ticked (with the net
   underneath).

2) RECURRING CASH COMMITMENTS (Cash Schedule, new section)
   For vehicle finance / HP and similar: payments that leave the bank but aren't in
   the P&L (only depreciation + interest are). Add each one (description, amount/mo,
   day of month, optional start/end month). They flow into the 13-week forecast on
   the day you set, shown in a new "Vehicles / commitments" column.

WHERE TO GO
   Cash Schedule page: tick +VAT on the relevant overheads; add your vehicles under
   "Recurring cash commitments"; Save. The 13-week Cash Flow updates automatically.

NOTE: "Specific days" overhead schedules use the exact Ł amounts you enter, so the
+VAT gross-up applies to "spread evenly" and "one day" modes; for specific-days,
enter the gross figures directly if needed.
