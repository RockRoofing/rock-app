CHANGES
1. The viewer now says "Loading technical submittal" (was "Loading drawing").

2. MULTI-PAGE: the whole document is now scrollable - all pages stack in a scrollable
   window (pages render as you scroll, so even a 200-page PDF stays responsive). There's
   also a page box + Prev/Next to jump quickly. Click a page to make it the "active" page
   for markup (it gets a purple outline); marks are saved per page.

3. APPROVAL:
   - New "Approval" column: "To Be Approved" (orange) or "Approved" (green).
   - When you upload a Tech Sub (or a new revision), you must first pick the CUSTOMER who
     needs to approve it. They are emailed to review / comment / approve.
   - An "Approve" button appears on the table row and in the viewer. The assigned customer
     (or internal staff) can approve; once approved it shows who approved it, and the team
     is emailed.
   - Making a new revision resets it to "To Be Approved" and re-notifies the approver.

4. Buttons: "Add revision" is now "Add New Revision", and "Open" is now a solid button so
   it's clearly separate from the other row actions.
