THE CHANGE
===========
"Personnel on site working on behalf of Rock" now lists only the people allocated to
that project in the Planner, instead of every operative on the Site App roster.

The diary already read the Planner - it pre-selects whoever is on the Gantt for the
diary date. It just never narrowed the LIST you picked from, so choosing today's gang
meant scrolling past the whole company.


DECISIONS WORTH KNOWING
------------------------
ALL DATES ON THE PROJECT, not just the diary date. Somebody who turns up a day early,
or is pulled across to help, is still on this job - and the date may not even be
filled in when the picker is first opened. Narrowing to a single day would have hidden
people who are genuinely there.

ONLY THE CREW FIELD. "Insert your name" is the same field type, and it is NOT
narrowed - the person filling the diary in is not always on the plan for it, and
locking them out of signing it would be worse than a long list. The crew field is
identified by the same label rule the file already uses to decide where to pre-select
from, rather than a second rule of my own.

A PROJECT WITH NOBODY PLANNED ON IT falls back to the full roster. An empty picker
would make the diary uncompletable, which is a worse failure than a long list.

AND THERE IS A WAY PAST IT. Above the search box: "Showing the 4 on this project.
Show everyone." Somebody turning up who was not on the plan happens often enough that
this has to be a default, not a wall. Anyone already selected stays selected whether
they are on the project or not.

Verified against both day-cell shapes - the legacy array and the newer
{ status, unnamed, entries } object - since allocations hold a mix of the two.
