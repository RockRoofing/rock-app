--------------

  "Placement check: 45 receivables, N placed (£X), M not placed (£Y),

  Invoice | Customer | Date used | Offset | Amount | Landed in

  NOT PLACED                    matched no week and no arrears

  EXCLUDED - type (none)        wrong type on the row
