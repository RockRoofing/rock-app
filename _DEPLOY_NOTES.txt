pkg414 - the two "priced" cards had no drill-down
=================================================

Deploy after pkg413. One changed file: pages/scorecard-crm.js. No new files.


THE CAUSE
---------
On the estimator scorecards, "Value priced - existing customers" and "Total value of work
priced" had no drillKey at all. Clicking them did nothing because there was nothing
wired up to open.

The data was there the whole time. Both figures are computed from lists of value-change
events - monthChanges and existingChanges - and both lists were built, summed, and then
thrown away rather than returned. The equivalent card on Edita's page had its drill-down
because it happened to be written later.

Both now open the same event list the sales page uses: date, project, old value, new
value, and the change.


WHILE I WAS IN THERE
--------------------
I checked every card on both scorecards rather than just the two you reported. One more
was missing: "Average value of projects priced" on Edita's page - added in pkg412, and I
had computed the list of projects behind it and not returned it. Same fault, one package
old. Now wired.

Every card on both pages now opens its detail except two:

    Emails sent externally
    Calls made

Those are stored as monthly TALLIES, not as the underlying messages or calls - a
deliberate decision, so there is genuinely no detail to show. If you ever want to click
into them, that is a different job: it would mean storing every message and call record
rather than a count, which is a lot of data to keep to answer a question nobody may ask.
