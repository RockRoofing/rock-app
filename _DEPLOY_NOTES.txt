WHAT'S NEW
  Rock Drawings is now a full thumbnail page (like Handover Docs), with per-drawing status,
  revisions and approval.

  - THUMBNAILS: one card per drawing (image preview, or PDF first-page preview). Each has a
    tick box, View, Download, and (Rock Roofing only) actions.
  - REVISIONS: older revisions show as GREYED cards stacked BEHIND the current one, with an
    "N old revs" note and a "Rev A/B/C" tag on the front card.
  - STATUS: "In Review" -> "Approved". Only the assigned CUSTOMER can approve (green record
    with name/company/role/email/phone/date+time on the drawing). Approve is hidden from
    Rock Roofing; internal users see "Awaiting customer approval".
  - CONSTRUCTION ISSUE: Rock Roofing only - a button to mark/unmark a drawing as
    Construction Issue (blue tag).
  - MARK SUPERSEDED: Rock Roofing only. Marking superseded REQUIRES uploading a new
    revision - it opens the approver picker then the file picker; you cannot supersede
    without providing the new drawing.
  - VIEW: opens a near-fullscreen viewer. Comments sit at the TOP (light-blue, @mentions
    notify immediately). The ONLY way to close the viewer is the X (clicking outside does
    nothing). Mark up and download inside.
  - NOTIFY PROJECT USERS (top button, Rock Roofing only): emails everyone on the project
    that drawings have been uploaded. In the pop-up you tick who needs to APPROVE - they're
    asked to review + approve; everyone else is just told drawings are up for review.
  - DOWNLOAD: "Download all" / tick + "Download selected" (single zip).

  CUSTOMER PERMISSIONS: customers can view, comment, mark up, download and approve (where
  they're the approver). They cannot upload, revise, supersede, mark construction issue, or
  delete - on this and every other design page (verified server-side).
