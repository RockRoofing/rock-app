WHY THE CHANGE FROM THE LAST VERSION
------------------------------------
Your Xero bill screenshot showed that Xero ALREADY applies the CIS deduction: a
Ł1,250 gross labour bill shows "Amount Due Ł1,000" (net of the 20%). My previous
version wrongly deducted a further 20% (paying Ł800). Corrected:

  - A CIS-flagged bill now pays its FULL Amount Due (the Ł1,000 net) on the payment
    date - exactly what leaves your bank.
  - The 20% CIS to HMRC is added as a SEPARATE outflow on the 22nd of the following
    month. Because Amount Due is already net, CIS = net x 0.25 (Ł1,000 -> Ł250),
    which reconciles to your Xero bill exactly.

WHAT YOU DO
   In the Bills to pay table (bottom of Cash Flow), tick "CIS" on labour bills.
   The amount cell shows the bill's full amount plus a small "+Ł.. CIS to HMRC"
   note. The forecast's new "CIS to HMRC" column shows the 20% landing on the 22nd
   of the following month.

WHY IT MATTERS
   The subcontractor payment was already correct (Amount Due is net). What was
   genuinely missing was the CIS payment to HMRC - a real outflow that wasn't in
   the forecast at all. This adds it. Rate 20%, all flagged bills. Flags saved.
