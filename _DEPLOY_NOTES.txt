YOU DIAGNOSED IT - THE END OF MONTH CYCLE NEVER SET A VALUATION DATE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
    return { month: mk, date, amount: rev * pct }     <- no appDate

So a forecast on that cycle had no valuation date NO MATTER HOW OFTEN IT WAS
SAVED. My banner was telling you to do something that could not work, which is
worse than not warning at all.

On an end-of-month cycle the valuation IS the month end - that is what the cash
date is derived from - so it can simply be stated:

    J248, period 02/11-13/11
      before  no appDate -> valuation fell back to the period end, 13/11
      after   30/11

    and on J244's four-month period:
      Sep -> valued 30/09,  Oct -> 31/10,  Nov -> 30/11,  Dec -> 31/12
      before, ALL FOUR were cut off at the period end, 18/12

Save J248 and 8335 once more after deploying and the banner should clear.

MATERIALS PAID ON DELIVERY - ALREADY SUPPORTED
-----------------------------------------------
No new option needed. Every materials LINE has its own delivery date and its own
term, which is what "Materials terms are set per line below (per supplier)" means.

    Renshaw materials delivered 14/09
      days + 0    ->  paid 14/09   (on delivery)
      days + 7    ->  paid 21/09
      EOM + 30    ->  paid 30/10   (normal terms)

So for Renshaw and Kilburn: set the first delivery line to days + 0 and leave the
later lines on their normal term. First app paid on delivery, everything after on
