1. SPECIFIC-DAYS NOW RESPECT +VAT
   Previously the +VAT tick only grossed up "spread evenly" and "one day" modes.
   Now "Specific days" splits are ALSO grossed up by 20% when the row is +VAT
   ticked. So a VAT overhead scheduled on specific days now lands at the gross
   (inc-VAT) amount in the forecast, matching what the "Predicted / mo" column says.
   IMPORTANT: enter the NET figures in the specific-day boxes (the 20% is added for
   you). If you previously typed gross figures into a +VAT specific-days line, halve
   them back to net or untick +VAT, or it'll double up.

2. CLICK-TO-EXPAND OVERHEAD BREAKDOWN (Cash Flow)
   Click any week's "Overheads out" figure to expand a breakdown showing each
   overhead line (code, name, amount inc VAT where applicable) that makes up that
   week's total. Click again to collapse.

3. TOTALS ON THE CASH SCHEDULE
   A totals row at the bottom of the overheads table shows the monthly total of all
   scheduled overheads, both inc VAT and split as "net + VAT".
