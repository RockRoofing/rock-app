THE CARD
--------
A fifth card, "Retention Outstanding": the halves NOT yet ticked as Received,
across the projects shown.

  Total Retention        gross - 1st Value + 2nd Value, ignores payment
  Retention Outstanding  the same, less the halves ticked Received

Red while there is anything to chase, green at zero. Both cards now carry a
tooltip saying which is which, because two money figures side by side with no
explanation is how the confusion started.

calcBalance() - which does exactly this - has been sitting in retention.js since
it was written and was never called anywhere. It is now.


BOTH CARDS SUM THE FILTERED VIEW
--------------------------------
Worth knowing: these two use allEntries, so filtering the table changes them. The
"In Defects Liability" and "Live Projects" counts beside them deliberately use
the whole register and do NOT change with the filters. That is intentional, but
it does mean the five cards are not all describing the same set of projects.
