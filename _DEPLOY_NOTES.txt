THE CHANGE
===========
One word: `wide` on the page's OperationsShell.

The shell already takes that flag and its own comment says it is for full-width
pages - tables and planning grids - and twenty-five other Operations pages pass it.
Site App Users did not, so eleven columns were being squeezed into the default
1100px cap, pushing Projects, Mobile and Email into a sideways scroll that is easy
to miss on a page which is nothing but a table.

No new styling, no override of the shell. It just uses the flag that was already
there for exactly this.
