1. OVERDUE BILLS WERE VANISHING TOO
-----------------------------------
    const billsOut = data.bills.filter(i => inWk(...))

Same fault as the invoices had. A bill past its due date sits BEFORE week 1 and
was dropped entirely - so the forecast was flattered TWICE over: the debt
disappeared from Invoices in AND the bill disappeared from Bills out.

Both now land in week 1, and only week 1 - not repeated down the table.

    invoice due 12/06   week 1 INCLUDED    week 5 no
    bill    due 01/07   week 1 INCLUDED    week 5 no


2. OVERDUE IS FLAGGED, NOT SILENT
---------------------------------
Any bill or invoice past its date with no confirmed planned/expected date shows
an amber row and "OVERDUE - confirm date". It is being paid or collected in week 1
whether or not that is realistic, so it needs a date you actually intend.

Setting a date clears the flag.


3. NEW "INVOICES OWED" TABLE, AND THE DATES ARE SHARED
------------------------------------------------------
Mirrors the Invoices Owed page - customer, invoice, due date, amount, expected
payment date.

The expected date posts to the SAME endpoint that page uses, which writes
invoice:meta. So a date set here appears there and vice versa, with no syncing
logic to drift out of step. Keyed by invoice number, as that page keys it.


4. EXCLUDE FROM THE FORECAST
----------------------------
An "In forecast" tick on every bill and every invoice. Untick and it is left out
of the forecast entirely - a disputed bill, or an invoice you do not expect within
the window. The row greys out so it is obvious it is not counted.

Stored in config:cashflow-excluded, keyed by bill id or invoice number. Absent
means included, so nothing changes for anything you have not deliberately
excluded. The totals row counts only what is in the forecast.


A BUG CAUGHT IN BUILD - WORTH RECORDING
---------------------------------------
Adding a key to the Promise.all in business-financials.js without adding a name to
the destructuring shifted EVERY variable after it by one - balancesStore was bound
to config:cashflow-excluded, financeCfg to config:manual-balances, and so on down.
It compiles perfectly and silently corrupts a dozen features. I now check that
block pairs name-to-key every time I touch it.
